## S1-U10-L01 — Big Review 1: Past Narration (Retrieval-Based)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1–U3, U8.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and use *was/were*, regular + irregular past, and *did/didn't* accurately.
- The learner can narrate a connected past account with sequence.
- The learner can self-correct the top past-tense errors (did+base, -ed endings, irregulars).

**Target language:** all Stage-1 past language. **Grammar:** past simple (all forms) — retrieval + consolidation.
**Accent Lab focus:** -ed endings (/t/,/d/,/ɪd/) + irregular clarity under connected speech. **Arabic support:** ~20%.
**Mindset focus:** look back — you can now tell stories you couldn't at Stage 0.

**Materials:** past-tense retrieval deck; a picture-story prompt; the rubric card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid retrieval: 10 irregular pasts + 5 regulars (with endings) | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A model past narration mixing all past forms | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-surface the three big errors (did+base, -ed sounds, irregular forms) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | -ed sort speed-round + 3 hard irregulars; record-and-compare | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build a picture-story in pairs (past + sequence) | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Narrate a real past event (6–8 sentences); record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self-correct against the top-3 error list | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a past narration; self-check the -ed endings | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — retrieval deck + skeleton. Stretch — add reported speech / a feeling + follow-up.
**Success check:** accurate connected past narration. **Queue:** feeds the L05 summative.
**Coach notes:** retrieval > re-teaching here — make them produce; correct only the patterned past errors.

## S1-U10-L02 — Big Review 2: Future, Comparisons, Quantity, Advice (Retrieval-Based)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U4–U7.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and use *going to/will*, comparatives/superlatives, quantifiers, and *should/have to*.
- The learner can combine several structures in a short talk.
- The learner can self-correct the top errors in each area.

**Target language:** future, comparison, quantity, advice/obligation. **Grammar:** integrated retrieval.
**Accent Lab focus:** long-adjective stress, *than* /ð/, *have to* → "hafta". **Arabic support:** ~20%.
**Mindset focus:** you now handle time (past + future), degree, and advice — that's real range.

**Materials:** mixed retrieval deck; a "plans + opinions" prompt; the rubric card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid: 1 example each — plan, prediction, comparative, superlative, quantifier, advice | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A model talk combining plans + a comparison + advice | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Common errors per area (I going to / more big / the most biggest / much people / should to) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Long-adjective stress + *than* /ð/; "hafta" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Prompt task: "Compare two plans + advise a friend" in pairs | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk ~1.5 min combining ≥3 areas; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self-correct against the per-area error list | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a mixed talk (plan + comparison + advice) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — retrieval deck + frames. Stretch — sustain a 2-minute mixed talk.
**Success check:** accurate use of ≥3 Stage-1 areas in one talk. **Queue:** feeds the L05 summative.
**Coach notes:** this is where the whole stage integrates — reward learners who *combine* structures naturally.

## S1-U10-L03 — Accent Clinic: Fix Your Top 3 Sounds (-ed Focus)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: all units.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can identify their personal top-3 weak sounds (from the tracker flags).
- The learner can produce them more accurately in words and connected speech.
- The learner can self-monitor and self-correct.

**Target language:** each learner's flagged sounds (commonly -ed endings, /θ/–/ð/, clusters, long-adj stress).
**Grammar:** (pronunciation clinic). **Accent Lab focus:** personalized — the top-3 list per learner; record-and-compare.
**Arabic support:** ~20%. **Mindset focus:** self-monitoring is the skill that keeps improving you after the course.

**Materials:** each learner's tracker weak-sound flags; minimal-pair + -ed sort sets; record-and-compare setup; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Learners state their top-3 flagged sounds | 4 min |
| 2 | Input (perceive) | Model contrasts for each learner's targets | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-cue the articulation for each target (esp. the -ed rule) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab (produce) | Targeted drills: word → phrase → sentence; **record-and-compare** | 9 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Read a short passage seeded with each learner's sounds | 4 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak freely for 1 min; self-flag any slips | 2 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to Stage-0 accent clinic notes — measurable gains | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a record-and-compare of your #1 target sentence | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — focus on 1 sound + intelligibility. Stretch — self-correct in spontaneous speech.
**Success check:** clearer production of the top-3 targets vs earlier recordings. **Queue:** residual sounds carry into Stage 2 (B1) refinement.
**Coach notes:** honesty guardrail — celebrate *clarity gains*, never demand "native". Compare to their own past, not to natives.

## S1-U10-L04 — Practice Conversation: The A2 Exchange (Narration + Role-Play Mock)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 10 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can sustain a mixed A2 conversation: a short narration + a role-play + Q&A.
- The learner can ask and answer across tenses and functions.
- The learner can self-assess readiness for the summative.

**Target language:** integrated Stage-1 language. **Grammar:** all Stage-1 structures in interaction.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity + interaction rhythm. **Arabic support:** ~15% (mostly English).
**Mindset focus:** rehearse like it's real — the summative is a celebration, not a trap.

**Materials:** an A2 conversation prompt set (narration + role-play); the rubric card; peer-feedback sheet; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid mixed retrieval (past + future + advice + comparison) | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | A model A2 exchange (narration + a role-play turn + Q&A) | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | The rubric dimensions; what a strong A2 answer sounds like | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Quick personal top-1 sound tune-up | 3 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Full mock in pairs: narrate → role-play → Q&A; peer feedback with the rubric | 10 min |
| 6 | Free output | One learner does a mock with the coach (others observe) — optional | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Rubric-based, forward-looking (what to polish before L05) | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Self-assess with the rubric; prepare your summative topics | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — provide the prompt + frames + prep time. Stretch — improvise with fewer supports.
**Success check:** a sustained mixed A2 exchange at/near threshold on the rubric. **Queue:** directly precedes the summative.
**Coach notes:** keep it low-stakes and encouraging; this mock removes summative anxiety and previews the format exactly.

## S1-U10-L05 — Stage-1 Summative Assessment + A2 Milestone Speaking Sample

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 10 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can demonstrate the **A2 exit criteria**: narrate past events, talk about plans, compare,
  give advice, and handle a role-play — connected, intelligible, at the A2 threshold.
- The learner completes the **Stage-1 summative quiz** (grammar/vocab recognition + production).
- The learner records the **A2 milestone speaking sample** on the standard prompt for the proof timeline.

**Target language:** all Stage-1 language. **Grammar:** full Stage-1 range (assessed).
**Accent Lab focus:** clarity across the stage's target sounds (scored on the Pronunciation dimension — *clear*, not *native*).
**Arabic support:** ~10% (English-first). **Mindset focus:** celebrate the A2 milestone; look ahead to B1 (Intermediate).

**Materials:** the **Stage-1 summative quiz** (Google Form/portal); the extended speaking task (narration +
role-play + Q&A); the **standard speaking-sample prompt** (before/after protocol §2); the 5-dimension rubric;
consent record (for using the clip per tier).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up | Light retrieval; settle nerves; restate that this is a milestone, not a filter | 4 min |
| 2 | Summative quiz | Grammar/vocab recognition + short production (all Stage-1 targets) | 10 min |
| 3 | Speaking task setup | Explain the 3 parts (narration + role-play + Q&A) + the rubric | 3 min |
| 4 | Accent tune-up | 60-second personal top-1 sound warm-up | 2 min |
| 5 | Speaking task | Learner performs the extended A2 task (recorded) | 8 min |
| 6 | A2 milestone sample | Record the **standard prompt** sample ("Tell me about yourself, your family, and your typical day" + a past/future extension) | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Rubric-based result; A2 determination; specific wins + next-stage targets | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Save the milestone clip (with consent); set B1 goal; carry residual weak sounds forward | 2 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — allow prep notes + a slightly shorter task (still covering the criteria). Stretch — longer, less-supported performance.
**Success check (Stage-1 summative):** meets the **A2 threshold** across the rubric (esp. Fluency, Accuracy, Interaction, Pronunciation); quiz passed at the set band. **Queue:** weak sounds + any shaky structures carry into Stage 2 (B1) review.
**Coach notes:** pair the A2 milestone sample with the Day-1 and Stage-0 clips — the three-point timeline is powerful, motivating proof (with consent, a flagship testimonial asset). Frame B1 as the next climb: opinions, storytelling with detail, coping independently.

## Unit 10 — end-of-unit / Stage-1 summative check

- **Quiz (Google Form/portal):** mixed Stage-1 grammar/vocab (past, future, comparison, quantity, modals) + short production items.
- **Speaking task:** the L05 extended A2 task + the standard-prompt milestone sample, scored on the 5-dimension rubric (A2 threshold).
- **Feeds:** Cohort Tracker — A2 determination, milestone-clip link, residual weak-sound flags for Stage 2; before/after proof timeline (Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2).

## Stage 1 — completion & handoff

- **A2 achieved** when the learner meets the exit criteria (roadmap §4) on the summative + speaking task.
- **Proof:** Day-1 → Stage-0 "after" → **A2 milestone** speaking samples (paired, with consent).
- **Next:** **Stage 2 — Intermediate (B1)** (roadmap §5): present perfect vs past, conditionals, relative
  clauses, opinions + storytelling with detail, connected-speech accent focus (linking, weak forms, reductions),
  mindset "breaking the plateau & going immersive". Build just ahead of the cohort per design §3.4.
