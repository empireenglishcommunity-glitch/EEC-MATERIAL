# Stage 1 — Elementary: Unit Map (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 5.2 (build A2 as the cohort climbs) · **Requirements:** R2, R1
> **Reads with:** `curriculum/01-cefr-roadmap.md` (§4 Stage 1), `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`,
> `methodology/01-eec-methodology-handbook.md`, `curriculum/03-accent-lab-syllabus.md` (Stage-1 focus),
> `curriculum/04-mindset-and-accountability-track.md`.
> **Purpose:** The complete structure of Stage 1 (Elementary, A2) — the level the cohort receives after
> Stage 0. This map defines the units, their objectives, and the lessons within each, so lessons can be
> authored consistently and reviewed as a whole. Built to fit the roadmap spine and the lesson template.

## 1. Stage 1 overview

- **CEFR:** A2 · **EEC label:** Elementary
- **Entry criterion:** A1 achieved (Stage 0 exit or placement).
- **Guided-hour budget:** ~100 hrs (cumulative ~180–200) · **Cohort length:** ~10–14 weeks @ ~7 hrs/week.
- **Big promise of this stage:** *"You can now talk about your past, your plans, and your world —
  tell a simple story, make plans, compare things, give advice, and handle everyday situations in English."*
- **Arabic support:** moderate (~40%), fading across the stage — explanations shift toward English.
- **Vocabulary target:** ~1,000–1,500 words cumulative (past-life events, travel, food/quantity, health,
  feelings, describing people/places, work basics).
- **Skill weighting:** speaking ●●● · listening ●●● · reading ●● · writing ●●.

### Structure
- **10 units** (no separate orientation module — learners are continuing) · **Unit 10 = consolidation/assessment.**
- **~5 lessons per unit** (≈ 50 lessons total), each ~25–40 min, built on the Lesson Template.
- **Weekly rhythm:** ~1 unit/week; recorded lessons during the week + 1–2 live speaking sessions.

### Sequencing logic
The stage is built around **time**: master the **past** first (was/were → regular → irregular + questions
→ storytelling), because narrating past events is the biggest A2 unlock and the clearest proof of growth.
Then the **future** (plans/predictions), then the tools to **describe and compare** (comparatives/
superlatives), then the everyday domains that need new grammar — **quantity/food**, **advice/health**,
**travel**, **feelings & everyday situations** — closing with consolidation and the A2 summative. Every
unit recycles Stage-0 language in warm-ups and adds to the spaced-review queue.

### What's new vs Stage 0
- **Tense range opens up:** past simple and future are the headline additions (Stage 0 was present-only).
- **More independence:** Arabic support roughly halves; learners narrate, plan, and role-play at length.
- **Longer output:** speaking tasks move from 4–6 sentences to short narratives and multi-turn role-plays.

## 2. Cross-stage threads (Stage-1 slice)

- **Accent Lab focus (from `curriculum/03` §6):** the **-ed past endings** (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) ⭐ new-and-critical;
  consolidate **/θ/ and /ð/**; the **consonant-cluster / no-vowel-insertion** fix (reinforced); **word stress
  in longer words**; **intro to sentence rhythm** (stressed content words vs squeezed function words); light **schwa** intro.
- **Mindset focus (Consistency & Momentum — `curriculum/04`):** habit-stacking & environment design;
  tracking practice; recovering from missed days without quitting; building a personal English input diet
  (music/shows/podcasts at level); handling the "I'm not improving fast enough" dip.
- **Assessment:** end-of-unit formative checks (quiz + speaking task); **Unit 10 summative** (quiz + extended
  speaking task: a short narration + a role-play) scored on the 5-dimension rubric at the **A2 threshold**;
  record an A2 "milestone" speaking sample to pair with the Stage-0 and (future) graduation clips.

## 4. Unit detail + lesson lists

> Each lesson is authored with `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`. Lesson IDs: `S1-U{n}-L{nn}`.
> Objectives are the "By the end, the learner can…" for that lesson.

### Unit 1 — The Past (1): Was & Were
- **Can-do (unit):** talk about the past with *to be*; say where you/others were; describe past states and
  feelings; use past time expressions.
- **Vocab:** past time expressions (yesterday, last night/week/year, ago, in 2019), places, feelings (tired, happy, busy).
- **Grammar/function:** *was/were* (affirmative, negative *wasn't/weren't*, questions *Was/Were…?*); *There was/were*.
- **Accent Lab:** /w/ in *was/were*; weak *was* (/wəz/) vs strong; word-stress review.
- **Mindset:** momentum — recommit to your daily practice slot for a new level.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U1-L01 — *I was, you were* (past of *to be*, affirmative) + past time expressions
  - S1-U1-L02 — Negatives & questions: *wasn't / weren't / Was you…? → Were you…?*
  - S1-U1-L03 — *There was / There were* (describing a past place)
  - S1-U1-L04 — How was it? Talking about a past experience (feelings & states)
  - S1-U1-L05 — Unit speaking task: "Where were you last weekend?" (record) + review

### Unit 2 — The Past (2): Regular Verbs
- **Can-do:** say what you did (regular verbs); sequence past actions; talk about yesterday/last week.
- **Vocab:** common regular verbs (work, play, watch, cook, study, call, walk, clean, visit, finish), sequence words.
- **Grammar/function:** past simple regular (affirmative); *-ed* spelling rules; *ago*; then/after that/later.
- **Accent Lab:** ⭐ **the -ed endings**: /t/ (worked), /d/ (played), /ɪd/ (visited) — sort & produce.
- **Mindset:** habit-stacking — attach 20 min of English to an existing daily habit.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U2-L01 — "Yesterday I worked" — past simple regular (affirmative)
  - S1-U2-L02 — The **-ed sound**: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/ (Accent Lab focus lesson)
  - S1-U2-L03 — Spelling rules for *-ed* (stop→stopped, study→studied)
  - S1-U2-L04 — Sequencing a day: first / then / after that / later
  - S1-U2-L05 — Unit speaking task: "What did you do yesterday?" (record) + review

### Unit 3 — The Past (3): Irregular Verbs & Questions
- **Can-do:** use high-frequency irregular past verbs; ask and answer past questions; tell a short story.
- **Vocab:** irregular verbs (go→went, have→had, do→did, see→saw, eat→ate, buy→bought, get→got, come→came, make→made, take→took).
- **Grammar/function:** irregular past; *did/didn't* (questions & negatives); *Did you…? Yes I did / No I didn't.*
- **Accent Lab:** stress on the main verb in past sentences; sentence rhythm in a short narrative.
- **Mindset:** environment design — put English cues where you'll see them.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U3-L01 — Common irregular verbs (1): go/have/do/see/eat
  - S1-U3-L02 — Common irregular verbs (2): buy/get/come/make/take
  - S1-U3-L03 — Past questions & negatives: *did / didn't*
  - S1-U3-L04 — Telling a story: a small event from your week (with sequence + past verbs)
  - S1-U3-L05 — Unit speaking task: "Tell me about your last holiday/day off" (record) + review

### Unit 4 — Future Plans
- **Can-do:** talk about plans and intentions; make predictions; offer and decide in the moment.
- **Vocab:** future time expressions (tomorrow, next…, soon, tonight), plans/goals vocab.
- **Grammar/function:** *be going to* (plans/intentions & evidence-based predictions); *will* (instant
  decisions, offers, predictions/opinions); *going to* vs *will* (intro contrast).
- **Accent Lab:** *going to → gonna* (receptive reduction); stress & linking in *"I'm going to…"*.
- **Mindset:** set your A2 goal; visualize the specific real-world win you're working toward.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U4-L01 — "I'm going to…" — plans & intentions
  - S1-U4-L02 — Asking about plans: *"What are you going to do…?"*
  - S1-U4-L03 — "I'll…" — decisions, offers, and predictions with *will*
  - S1-U4-L04 — *going to* vs *will* (choosing which; light contrast)
  - S1-U4-L05 — Unit speaking task: "My plans for next month" (record) + review

### Unit 5 — Making Comparisons
- **Can-do:** compare people, places, and things; say which is the best/biggest; express preferences with reasons.
- **Vocab:** adjectives for comparison (big/small, cheap/expensive, fast/slow, good/bad, comfortable, beautiful), places/cities.
- **Grammar/function:** comparatives (*-er / more*); superlatives (*-est / the most*); irregular (good/better/best,
  bad/worse/worst); *(not) as … as*; *too / enough* (intro).
- **Accent Lab:** word stress in longer adjectives (*ex**pen**sive, com**for**table*); /ð/ in *than*; *-er/-est* endings.
- **Mindset:** track progress — compare yourself today to your Day-1 self (the only fair comparison).
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U5-L01 — Comparatives: *"Cairo is bigger than…"* (-er)
  - S1-U5-L02 — Longer & irregular comparatives: *more expensive, better, worse*
  - S1-U5-L03 — Superlatives: *"the biggest / the most beautiful"*
  - S1-U5-L04 — *as … as*, *too*, and *enough* (expressing degree & preference)
  - S1-U5-L05 — Unit speaking task: "Compare two cities / two things you like" (record) + review

### Unit 6 — Food, Quantity & Restaurants
- **Can-do:** talk about food and quantities; order a meal; ask about and describe amounts.
- **Vocab:** food & dishes, containers/quantities (a bottle of, a piece of, a cup of), restaurant language.
- **Grammar/function:** countable/uncountable; *some/any*; *much/many/a lot of/a few/a little*;
  *How much/How many…?*; ordering (*Could I have…? / I'll have…*).
- **Accent Lab:** clusters in food words (*breakfast, vegetables*); polite rising intonation; clear prices.
- **Mindset:** real-world reps — order or shop in English this week (use it, don't just study it).
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U6-L01 — Countable vs uncountable; *some / any*
  - S1-U6-L02 — *much / many / a lot of / a few / a little*
  - S1-U6-L03 — *How much? / How many?* + quantities & containers
  - S1-U6-L04 — At the restaurant: ordering politely (*Could I have… / I'll have…*)
  - S1-U6-L05 — Unit speaking task: role-play ordering a meal (record) + review

### Unit 7 — Health & Advice
- **Can-do:** describe how you feel / symptoms; give and ask for advice; talk about obligations & rules.
- **Vocab:** body parts, common symptoms (headache, a cold, a temperature), remedies, health habits.
- **Grammar/function:** *should / shouldn't* (advice); *have to / don't have to* & *must* (obligation/necessity);
  *"What's the matter? / You should…"*.
- **Accent Lab:** /θ/–/ð/ consolidation (*health, teeth, mouth, breathe*); stress in advice sentences.
- **Mindset:** recovering from a bad/low week without guilt — the "never miss twice" rule.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U7-L01 — Body & symptoms: *"What's the matter?"*
  - S1-U7-L02 — Giving advice: *should / shouldn't*
  - S1-U7-L03 — Obligation & necessity: *have to / don't have to / must*
  - S1-U7-L04 — Health habits & a visit to the doctor/pharmacy (role-play prep)
  - S1-U7-L05 — Unit speaking task: give a friend health advice (record) + review

### Unit 8 — Travel & Transport
- **Can-do:** make requests and ask permission; handle transport/booking; talk about a past trip; state rules.
- **Vocab:** transport, travel & booking (ticket, platform, gate, reservation), places & landmarks.
- **Grammar/function:** *can/could* (polite requests & permission; past ability *could*); *have to* for travel
  rules; recycling past simple for a trip narration.
- **Accent Lab:** linking in fixed phrases (*Could I…, a ticket to…*); sentence rhythm; clear vowels in place names.
- **Mindset:** input diet — switch your travel content / a show to English at your level.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U8-L01 — Transport & getting around (vocab + *How do I get to…?*)
  - S1-U8-L02 — Requests & permission: *Can / Could I…?*
  - S1-U8-L03 — Booking & rules: buying a ticket; *you have to / you can't* (travel rules)
  - S1-U8-L04 — A past trip: narrate a journey (past simple recycled + sequence)
  - S1-U8-L05 — Unit speaking task: "Tell me about a trip you took" (record) + review

### Unit 9 — People, Feelings & Everyday Situations
- **Can-do:** describe how people do things and how they feel; contrast routine vs now; manage phone calls & small talk.
- **Vocab:** feelings/emotions, personality adjectives, adverbs of manner (carefully, quickly, well), phone phrases.
- **Grammar/function:** adverbs of manner (+ *good/well*); present continuous vs present simple (extend/contrast);
  phone & small-talk functions (*Can I speak to…? / How's it going?*).
- **Accent Lab:** -ing endings review (/ŋ/); intonation of feelings and friendly small talk.
- **Mindset:** thinking in English — catch yourself translating and swap to direct English.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U9-L01 — Feelings & personality (describing how people are)
  - S1-U9-L02 — Adverbs of manner: *"She speaks English well / carefully"*
  - S1-U9-L03 — *usually* vs *now*: present simple vs continuous (extend)
  - S1-U9-L04 — On the phone & small talk (functional role-play)
  - S1-U9-L05 — Unit speaking task: a 2-minute chat about your week & feelings (record) + review

### Unit 10 — Putting It Together (A2 Consolidation & Assessment)
- **Can-do:** narrate past events, talk about plans, compare, give advice, and handle a role-play — connected,
  intelligible, at the A2 threshold; demonstrate A2 exit criteria.
- **Grammar/function:** integrated review of all Stage-1 language (past, future, comparison, quantity, modals).
- **Accent Lab:** overall clarity + self-monitoring; revisit each learner's weakest sounds (esp. -ed endings, /θ/–/ð/).
- **Mindset:** from "I studied English" to "I use English"; celebrate the A2 milestone; readiness for B1.
- **Lessons:**
  - S1-U10-L01 — Big review 1: past narration (was/were + regular + irregular + questions)
  - S1-U10-L02 — Big review 2: future, comparisons, quantity, advice (retrieval-based)
  - S1-U10-L03 — Accent clinic: fix your top 3 sounds (with -ed endings focus)
  - S1-U10-L04 — Practice conversation: the A2 exchange (narration + role-play mock)
  - S1-U10-L05 — **Stage-1 summative assessment** + A2 milestone speaking sample

## 5. Assessment placement (links to Task 1.6)

- **Before Unit 1:** confirm A1 exit / A2 readiness (Stage-0 summative or placement).
- **End of each unit:** short formative check (quiz + speaking task) — feedback, not gatekeeping.
- **Unit 10, L05:** **summative** (quiz + extended speaking task on the rubric) → A2 determination; record
  the **A2 milestone "after" sample** and pair with the Day-1 and Stage-0 clips for cumulative proof.

## 6. Spaced-review threading

- Every unit's warm-ups retrieve prior units and **Stage 0** (e.g., Unit 4 future warm-ups recycle Unit 1–3 past;
  Unit 6 quantity recycles Stage-0 food/there is-are).
- New target language enters the review queue and returns at ~next lesson → ~3 days → ~1 week → later units.
- **-ed endings, irregular verbs, and /θ/–/ð/** are deliberately re-drilled across units (they need many reps).
- Unit 10 is retrieval-heavy (the testing effect consolidates the whole stage).

## 7. Build order (recommendation)

Build in teaching order: **Unit 1 → Unit 2 → …** so the cohort can continue seamlessly from Stage 0, with
later units completed just ahead of the cohort (design §3.4). Author every lesson with the Lesson Template;
obey the design checklist each time. The **past-tense block (Units 1–3)** is the priority — it's the biggest A2 unlock.
