# Stage 1 · Unit 8 — Travel & Transport (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 5.2 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage1/00-stage1-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~30% (fading). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can get around and ask for
> transport help, make polite requests / ask permission (*can/could*), buy a ticket and state travel rules
> (*have to / can't*), and narrate a past trip — recorded.
> **Grammar spine:** *can/could* (requests, permission, past ability) + *have to* (rules) + past-simple
> recycled · **Accent Lab:** linking in fixed phrases, sentence rhythm, clear vowels in place names · **Mindset:** input diet.

**Unit lessons:** L01 transport & getting around · L02 requests & permission (*Can/Could I…?*) ·
L03 booking & rules · L04 a past trip (narration) · L05 speaking task + review.

**Assets to produce:** transport flashcards, a station/airport dialogue clip (American model), request
frames, a ticket/booking prop, a map for "how do I get to…?", a Google Form per lesson.

## S1-U8-L01 — Transport & Getting Around ("How Do I Get To…?")

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 8 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Stage-0 directions (U8).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can name common transport and travel places.
- The learner can ask *"How do I get to…?"* and understand a simple answer.
- The learner can describe how they usually travel.

**Target language:** transport (bus, metro, train, taxi, plane); places (station, stop, platform, airport);
*How do I get to…? / Take the… / Get off at…*. **Grammar:** present simple for habitual travel; question forms.
**Accent Lab focus:** clear vowels in place names; linking "get-off-at". **Arabic support:** ~30%.
**Mindset focus:** input diet — set your maps/travel apps or a travel video to English.

**Materials:** transport flashcards; a station clip; a simple map; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: Stage-0 directions (turn left/right, near) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "How do I get to the station?" "Take the metro and get off at…" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Transport vocab; *How do I get to…?*; *take/get off*; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Linking in "get off at"; clear place-name vowels | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Map task: ask/answer "how do I get to…?" for 3 places | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Describe how you travel to work/school (2–3 sentences); record one | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check transport vocab + question form | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "I usually go to ___ by ___." + one "How do I get to…?" | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — flashcards + frame. Stretch — give a 2-step route with sequence words.
**Success check:** correct transport vocab + a "how do I get to…?" exchange. **Queue:** transport recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** by + transport ("by bus/metro", but "on foot") is a small fixed-phrase set — teach as chunks.

## S1-U8-L02 — Requests & Permission: *Can / Could I…?*

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 8 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can make polite requests and ask permission with *Can/Could I…?* and *Could you…?*.
- The learner can respond to requests (*Sure. / Of course. / Sorry, you can't.*).
- The learner can handle common travel requests.

**Target language:** *Can/Could I…? Could you…?*; responses; travel requests (help with a bag, directions,
a window seat). **Grammar:** *can/could* for requests/permission (*could* = more polite). **Accent Lab focus:**
polite rising intonation; linking "Could-I", "Could-you" /kʊdʒə/. **Arabic support:** ~30%.
**Mindset focus:** politeness travels — a small phrase opens doors.

**Materials:** request frames; a travel-help dialogue clip; role-play cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: transport + "how do I get to…?" (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Could you help me with my bag?" "Sure!" "Can I sit here?" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *Can/Could I…?* (permission) vs *Could you…?* (request); responses; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Polite rising intonation; linking "could you" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Role-play requests from cards; give polite responses; swap | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Make 3 polite requests in a travel scenario; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check request form + politeness | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 2 polite requests you might make while travelling | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — request frames. Stretch — soften with "Excuse me, could you possibly…?".
**Success check:** polite, correct requests + suitable responses. **Queue:** can/could recycles into U10; links to U6 ordering.
**Coach notes:** flat intonation makes *could* sound like a demand — model the friendly rise repeatedly.

## S1-U8-L03 — Booking & Rules: Buying a Ticket; *You Have To / You Can't*

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 8 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can buy a ticket (ask price, times, single/return).
- The learner can state travel rules with *have to / can't*.
- The learner can handle a short booking exchange.

**Target language:** ticket language (a ticket to…, single/return, platform, gate, what time…); rules (*you have to
check in, you can't smoke*). **Grammar:** *have to* (obligation, from U7) + *can't* (prohibition) in travel context;
*How much/What time* (from U6). **Accent Lab focus:** clear times/prices; *can't* (/kænt/) vs *can*. **Arabic support:** ~30%.
**Mindset focus:** plan your reps like a trip — know your next 3 steps.

**Materials:** a ticket/booking prop; a booking dialogue clip; a rules card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: requests (L02) + how much/what time (U6/Stage-0) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "A return ticket to Alexandria, please. What time does it leave?" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Booking language; travel rules (*have to/can't*); brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | *can* vs *can't* (final /t/); clear times/prices | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Role-play buying a ticket; state 2 travel rules | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Book a ticket + say one rule of the place; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check booking language + can/can't clarity | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a short booking exchange or 2 travel rules | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — booking-phrase card. Stretch — handle a complication (sold out → alternative).
**Success check:** a coherent booking exchange + a correct rule. **Queue:** have to/can't recycle into U10.
**Coach notes:** *can/can't* distinction is hard for Arabic speakers (vowel + final /t/). Drill minimal contrast ("I can go / I can't go").

## S1-U8-L04 — A Past Trip: Narrate a Journey

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 8 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03; U1–U3 (past).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can narrate a past trip using past simple + sequence + travel vocab.
- The learner can describe where they went, how, and what they did.
- The learner can react to a peer's trip and ask a past question.

**Target language:** trip narration (went, took, stayed, visited, saw); travel vocab recycled; *There was/were* (U1).
**Grammar:** past simple (U1–U3) recycled in a travel context. **Accent Lab focus:** narrative rhythm; clean *-ed*/irregulars.
**Arabic support:** ~25%. **Mindset focus:** notice how much past-tense you now control vs a few weeks ago.

**Materials:** a "my last trip" model clip; a trip-skeleton card (where/how/who with/what you did/how it was); Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past verbs (U2–U3) + there was/were (U1) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Model narrates a trip: "Last year I went to… I took the train… There were…" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Trip-narration shape; combine travel vocab + past; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Narrative rhythm; keep endings/irregulars clean | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build a trip from the skeleton card; peer adds a question | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Narrate a real past trip (5–6 sentences); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check past forms + travel vocab | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 5–6 sentence "my last trip" + ask a classmate one question | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — skeleton card + verb bank. Stretch — add feelings + a superlative ("the best part was…").
**Success check:** a coherent 5–6 sentence past-trip narration. **Queue:** trip narration recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** this deliberately reactivates the past block (U1–U3) in a rich context — the spaced-review payoff.

## S1-U8-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Tell Me About a Trip You Took" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 8 · ~35 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can narrate a trip (7–9 sentences) mixing past narration + travel language + a request/rule reference.
- The learner can answer follow-up questions and ask one.
- The learner records their **Unit-8 trip narration**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-8 language. **Grammar:** past + can/could + have to consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; place-name vowels; can/can't; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~25% (fading). **Mindset focus:** you can now handle travel + tell the story after — real independence.

**Materials:** a model trip narration; a checklist card (where/how / past verbs / a request or rule / feeling / a question handled);
recording prompt; Unit-8 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: transport, can/could, have to/can't, past verbs | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean trip narration (7–9 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist of a good trip narration | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (can/can't / place vowels) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice + one follow-up each | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full trip narration + answer one live follow-up | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the narration; complete Unit-8 quiz | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 7 sentences with the card. Stretch — include a request you made + a rule + 2 follow-up answers.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 8):** connected 7–9 sentence trip narration with correct past + travel language (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A2). **Queue:** trip narration recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** rich, personal, and integrative — a strong candidate for a mid-A2 proof clip alongside earlier milestones.
