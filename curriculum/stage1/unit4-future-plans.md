# Stage 1 · Unit 4 — Future Plans (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 5.2 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage1/00-stage1-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~35% (fading). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can talk about plans and
> intentions with *be going to*, make decisions/offers/predictions with *will*, ask about others' plans,
> and choose between the two — recorded.
> **Grammar spine:** *be going to* + *will* (+ light contrast) · **Accent Lab:** *going to → gonna*
> (receptive), stress & linking in *"I'm going to…"* · **Mindset:** goal-setting for A2.

**Unit lessons:** L01 *"I'm going to…"* (plans) · L02 asking about plans · L03 *"I'll…"* (will) ·
L04 *going to* vs *will* · L05 speaking task + review.

**Assets to produce:** a "my plans" clip (American model), a *going to*/*will* function card, a
future-time-expression set, minimal audio for *gonna* (receptive), a Google Form per lesson.

## S1-U4-L01 — "I'm Going To…" — Plans & Intentions

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 4 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1–U3 (past); present *to be*.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can express future plans/intentions with *be going to + base verb*.
- The learner can use future time expressions (tomorrow, next week, soon, tonight).
- The learner can state 3–4 real personal plans.

**Target language:** *be going to*; future time expressions; plan/goal verbs (visit, start, buy, travel, study, look for).
**Grammar:** subject + *am/is/are* + *going to* + base verb. **Accent Lab focus:** linking + stress in *"I'm **going** to **study**"*;
receptive *gonna*. **Arabic support:** ~35%. **Mindset focus:** set your A2 goal — one concrete, real-world target.

**Materials:** *going to* structure slide; future-time cards; a "my plans" clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past (U3) — "What did you do yesterday?" → pivot to "And tomorrow?" | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Tonight I'm going to cook. Next week I'm going to visit my family." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Structure: *am/is/are + going to + base verb*; future time words; brief Arabic note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Linking/stress in "I'm going to…"; hear (not force) *gonna* | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Substitution: "Tomorrow I'm going to ___." (swap verbs/times) | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | State 3 true plans (tonight / this week / this year); record one | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *going to + base verb* (not "going to studied"); note linking | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "This week I'm going to ___, ___, and ___." | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — give the structure slide + verb bank. Stretch — add a reason ("because I want to…").
**Success check:** 3 correct *going to* plans, intelligible. **Queue:** *going to* recycles every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** two errors to watch: dropping *be* ("I going to…") and using a past/‑s verb after *to*. Fix the base-verb rule now (parallels the *did + base* rule from U3).

## S1-U4-L02 — Asking About Plans: "What Are You Going To Do…?"

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 4 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can ask about future plans (yes/no + wh- with *going to*).
- The learner can give short answers and follow-ups.
- The learner can hold a short plans mini-interview.

**Target language:** *Are you going to…? What/Where/When are you going to…?*; short answers (*Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.*).
**Grammar:** question forms of *be going to*. **Accent Lab focus:** weak *are you* /əjə/ (receptive); question intonation.
**Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** say your goal out loud to someone — commitment device.

**Materials:** question frames; a "weekend plans" dialogue clip; interview cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: L01 plans — 2–3 learners share a plan | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "What are you going to do this weekend?" "I'm going to relax and see friends." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Question inversion (*are you going to…?*); wh- front position; short answers | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Question intonation; natural weak forms (receptive) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Pair drill: ask/answer plan questions; add one follow-up each | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Mini-interview: ask a partner 3 plan questions; report one; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check question form + short answers | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: ask a classmate about their weekend plans + answer one | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — provide question frames. Stretch — 2 follow-up questions + a reaction.
**Success check:** correct plan questions + short answers in an exchange. **Queue:** plan questions recycle in U8 (travel plans) & U10.
**Coach notes:** encourage genuine curiosity — real plans make the drill communicative, not mechanical.

## S1-U4-L03 — "I'll…" — Decisions, Offers & Predictions with *will*

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 4 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use *will/'ll* for instant decisions, offers, and predictions/opinions.
- The learner can make negatives with *won't*.
- The learner can respond in the moment ("I'll help you.", "I think it'll rain.").

**Target language:** *will/'ll/won't*; offer/decision/prediction language (I'll get it, I'll help, I think… will…).
**Grammar:** *will + base verb* (all persons); contractions. **Accent Lab focus:** the contraction *'ll* (I'll, he'll);
clear *won't* (/oʊ/). **Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** visualize the win — picture using English successfully.

**Materials:** *will* function card (decision/offer/prediction); situation prompts; a short clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: *going to* plans + plan questions (L01–L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip with offers/decisions: "The bag is heavy." "I'll carry it." + a prediction | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Three uses of *will* (decide now / offer / predict); *'ll* + *won't*; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Contract *'ll* (don't say full "will" every time); clear *won't* | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | React to situation prompts with an offer/decision ("The phone's ringing." → "I'll get it.") | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Make 3 predictions about next year + offer help in 1 scenario; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *will + base verb*; encourage contractions | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 2 predictions ("I think… will…") + one offer | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — give the function card + frames. Stretch — combine ("I think it'll be hard, but I'll try.").
**Success check:** correct *will* for a decision/offer + a prediction. **Queue:** *will* recycles in U7 (advice: "you'll feel better") & U10.
**Coach notes:** keep the three uses simple and situational; avoid deep future-form theory at A2 — usage over rules.

## S1-U4-L04 — *Going To* vs *Will* (Choosing Which)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 4 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can choose *going to* (planned) vs *will* (decided now / predicted) in common situations.
- The learner can talk about a mix of plans and spontaneous decisions.
- The learner can self-correct the choice with a simple rule of thumb.

**Target language:** both forms, contrasted. **Grammar:** *going to* (already-decided plan) vs *will* (decide at the
moment / prediction). **Accent Lab focus:** natural rhythm switching between the two forms. **Arabic support:** ~30%.
**Mindset focus:** progress check — you now handle past AND future in English.

**Materials:** a "plan vs decide-now" sorting activity; mixed-situation prompts; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: one *going to* plan + one *will* offer from earlier lessons | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Short dialogue mixing both ("I'm going to travel this summer." … "Great, I'll book with you!") | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Rule of thumb: **already planned → going to**; **deciding now / predicting → will**; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Switch cleanly between "I'm going to…" and "I'll…" in one turn | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Sort situations into going to / will; justify the choice with a partner | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk ~1 min about your summer: plans (going to) + spontaneous bits (will); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check the choice; accept reasonable overlaps (both sometimes fine) | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: one plan (going to) + one prediction (will) about this year | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — provide the rule card + sorted examples. Stretch — explain your choice in English.
**Success check:** appropriate form choice in most situations. **Queue:** the contrast recycles in U10.
**Coach notes:** don't over-police overlaps — many contexts accept both. Reward communication + a reasonable choice, not textbook purity.

## S1-U4-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "My Plans for Next Month" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 4 · ~35 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about future plans + predictions (6–8 sentences) using *going to* + *will* appropriately.
- The learner can ask a partner about their plans and react.
- The learner records their **Unit-4 future account**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-4 language. **Grammar:** *going to* + *will* consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; linking in "going to"; contractions ('ll); each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~25% (fading). **Mindset focus:** plans in English make the goal feel real — commit to one this month.

**Materials:** a model "my plans" account; a checklist card (2+ going-to plans / 1+ will prediction / a time expression / a reason);
recording prompt; Unit-4 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: going to, plan questions, will (3 uses), the contrast | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean "my plans" account (6–8 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist of a good future account | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (linking / 'll / a past ending still weak) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice + one plan question each | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full "my plans for next month" account | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the account; complete Unit-4 quiz | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 6 sentences with the card. Stretch — add a prediction + a *because*-reason + a follow-up answer.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 4):** connected 6–8 sentence future account, appropriate forms (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A2). **Queue:** future account recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** learners now move fluidly between past (U1–U3) and future (U4) — a big range gain. Note it to keep motivation high.
