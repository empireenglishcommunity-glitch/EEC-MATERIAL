## S1-U2-L01 — "Yesterday I Worked" — Past Simple Regular (Affirmative)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 2 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1 (was/were).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the past simple of regular verbs (verb + *-ed*) for all persons.
- The learner can say 3–4 things they did yesterday/last week.
- The learner can use *ago* with a time period.

**Target language:** common regular verbs (work, play, watch, cook, study, call, walk, clean, visit, finish,
start, want, help, talk, listen); *ago*. **Grammar:** past simple regular, affirmative, same form for all persons
("I/he/we **worked**"). **Accent Lab focus:** intro to the *-ed* endings (full drill in L02); stress the main verb.
**Arabic support:** ~40%. **Mindset focus:** habit-stacking — attach English to something you already do daily.

**Materials:** regular-verb bank card; "yesterday" timeline graphic; a short narration clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: U1 was/were — "Where were you yesterday?" | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Yesterday I worked, then I cooked dinner and watched TV." (text + light gloss) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Rule: regular verb + *-ed*, **same for all persons**; contrast with present ("I work / I worked") | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Quick taste of the 3 *-ed* sounds (worked/played/visited) — full sort next lesson | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Substitution: "Yesterday I ___." with verb-bank cards; add *ago* ("two days ago") | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 true things you did yesterday; record one | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check the *-ed* is added (not dropped); note pronunciation for L02 | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "Yesterday I ___, ___, and ___." (3 regular verbs) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — give the verb bank + frame. Stretch — add a time expression + a *because*-reason.
**Success check:** 3 correct regular past sentences, intelligible. **Queue:** regular past + verb bank recycle every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** the big early error is dropping *-ed* ("Yesterday I work"). Insist on the ending in writing/speaking; pronunciation is fixed in L02.

## S1-U2-L02 — The *-ed* Sound: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/ ⭐ (Accent Lab focus lesson)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 2 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can hear and produce the three *-ed* endings: /t/ (worked), /d/ (played), /ɪd/ (visited).
- The learner can sort regular verbs into the three sound groups.
- The learner can pronounce past regular verbs correctly in sentences.

**Target language:** *-ed* verbs across the three groups. **Grammar:** (pronunciation of the past regular).
**Accent Lab focus:** ⭐ **the rule** — after voiceless sounds → **/t/** (worked, watched, stopped); after voiced
sounds/vowels → **/d/** (played, cleaned, listened); after /t/ or /d/ → **/ɪd/** (visited, wanted, started).
**Arabic support:** ~35% (the rule taught partly in Arabic, drilled in English). **Mindset focus:** small daily reps beat one long session.

**Materials:** the **3-column sort** worksheet + audio; minimal pairs of endings; record-and-compare setup; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 5 regular verbs from L01 in a past sentence | 4 min |
| 2 | Input (perceive) | Listen to grouped sets; hear /t/ vs /d/ vs /ɪd/ — perception before production | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | The rule (voiceless→/t/, voiced/vowel→/d/, /t,d/→/ɪd/); Arabic explanation of "voiced/voiceless" | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab (produce) | Sort 15 verbs into 3 columns; produce each; **record-and-compare** to the model | 8 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Say sentences mixing all three: "I **worked**, **played**, and **visited**…" | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | Retell yesterday using ≥3 verbs from different groups; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check the three endings; flag learners' habitual group errors for the queue | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a voice note using one verb from each group; label which sound each is | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — focus only on avoiding an extra vowel (not "visit-ED-uh"); master /t/ vs /d/ first. Stretch — self-correct in connected speech.
**Success check:** correctly produces the three endings in a short retell. **Queue:** *-ed* endings re-drilled in every following unit (needs many reps).
**Coach notes:** Arabic speakers often add a full vowel to every *-ed* ("worked" → "work-ed"). The /ɪd/ group is ONLY after /t,d/; everywhere else it's a single consonant. This is a high-value, high-frequency fix — return to it often.

## S1-U2-L03 — Spelling Rules for *-ed* (stop→stopped, study→studied)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 2 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can spell regular past verbs correctly (double consonant; *y*→*i*; drop-*e*).
- The learner can write 4–5 correct past sentences.
- The learner can self-check spelling using the rule card.

**Target language:** verbs showing each rule (stop→stopped, plan→planned; study→studied, try→tried;
live→lived, arrive→arrived; play→played). **Grammar:** *-ed* spelling rules. **Accent Lab focus:** spelling ≠ sound
(stopped is still /t/) — keep the L02 sounds while spelling changes. **Arabic support:** ~35%.
**Mindset focus:** track your reps — a simple tick each day you practice.

**Materials:** spelling-rule card (3 rules + "just add -ed"); a short writing worksheet; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: say + spell 3 regular verbs (past) from L01–L02 | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Read a short past paragraph; underline the *-ed* verbs and notice spelling changes | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | The rules: double final consonant (CVC); *y*→*ied*; drop silent *e*; else just *-ed* | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Remind: spelling change doesn't change the /t/,/d/,/ɪd/ sound (stopped=/t/, studied=/d/) | 3 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Convert present→past (spoken + written); peer-check spelling with the card | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Write + say 4 sentences about last week using different spelling patterns; record one | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check spelling + that sounds stayed correct | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Write 4 past sentences (one per rule) and post; add a voice note | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — give the rule card + examples. Stretch — write a 4–5 sentence mini-diary of last weekend.
**Success check:** correct spelling across the rule types + correct sounds. **Queue:** spelling rules recycle whenever new regular verbs appear.
**Coach notes:** don't overload — most learners quickly get "just add -ed"; spend time on *y*→*ied* and the doubling rule (CVC), which cause the most errors.

## S1-U2-L04 — Sequencing a Day: first / then / after that / later

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 2 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can order past actions using sequence words.
- The learner can narrate a simple sequence (a day, a routine done yesterday) in 4–6 sentences.
- The learner can listen to a sequence and put events in order.

**Target language:** first, then, after that, next, later, finally; recycled regular verbs. **Grammar:** sequencing +
past simple regular. **Accent Lab focus:** pausing/rhythm between clauses; light stress on sequence words.
**Arabic support:** ~35%. **Mindset focus:** stack it — do your English right after a fixed daily anchor.

**Materials:** sequence-word cards; a scrambled-events listening; a "my day" model clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 4 regular past verbs + correct endings (L02–L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Model narrates yesterday in sequence; learners order the events | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Sequence words + where they go; comma/pause; brief Arabic note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Rhythm: "First I **worked**, then I **cooked**…" — beat on the verbs | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Reorder scrambled sentences into a story; say it with sequence words | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Narrate your yesterday in 4–6 sequenced sentences; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check sequencing + endings; note fluency | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 4–6 sentence "my day yesterday" using sequence words | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — provide the sequence-word cards + verb bank. Stretch — add times ("At 7 I…") and a feeling.
**Success check:** a coherent, ordered 4–6 sentence past narration. **Queue:** sequencing recycles into U3 (storytelling) & U8 (trip narration).
**Coach notes:** sequencing is the scaffold for storytelling in U3 — get them comfortable chaining actions now.

## S1-U2-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "What Did You Do Yesterday?" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 2 · ~35 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can narrate what they did (6–8 sentences) with correct regular past + endings + sequencing.
- The learner can ask a partner about their day and respond.
- The learner records their **Unit-2 "my day" narration**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-2 language. **Grammar:** past simple regular consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity with correct *-ed* endings; each learner's flagged group error.
**Arabic support:** ~30% (fading). **Mindset focus:** you can now tell what you did — narration is a big A2 step.

**Materials:** a model "my day" narration; a checklist card (3+ verbs / correct endings / 2+ sequence words / a time or feeling);
recording prompt; Unit-2 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: regular verbs, the 3 endings, sequence words | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean "yesterday" narration (6–8 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist of a good narration | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their weakest *-ed* group (or other flagged sound) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice (pairs) | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full "what I did yesterday" narration | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback (endings focus) | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the narration; complete Unit-2 quiz | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 6 sentences with the card. Stretch — add a *because*-reason + a question to a partner.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 2):** connected 6–8 sentence narration, correct regular past + endings (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A2). **Queue:** narration recycles into U3 and U10.
**Coach notes:** celebrate that they can now *narrate*; the endings won't be perfect yet — that's fine, keep re-drilling. Pairs well as a proof clip.
