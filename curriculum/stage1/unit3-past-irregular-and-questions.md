# Stage 1 · Unit 3 — The Past (3): Irregular Verbs & Questions (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 5.2 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage1/00-stage1-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~35% (fading). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can use high-frequency
> irregular past verbs, ask and answer past questions with *did/didn't*, make past negatives, and tell a
> short true story — recorded.
> **Grammar spine:** irregular past + *did/didn't* (questions & negatives) · **Accent Lab:** main-verb
> stress + sentence rhythm in past narratives · **Mindset:** environment design.

## S1-U3-L01 — Common Irregular Verbs (1): go / have / do / see / eat

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U2 (regular past).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use five high-frequency irregular past forms (went, had, did, saw, ate).
- The learner can mix regular + irregular verbs in a short past account.
- The learner can recognize that irregular verbs don't take *-ed*.

**Target language:** go→went, have→had, do→did, see→saw, eat→ate; recycled regular verbs & time expressions.
**Grammar:** irregular past (affirmative); contrast with regular. **Accent Lab focus:** stress the main verb
("I **went** to work"); clear vowel in *went/saw/ate*. **Arabic support:** ~35%. **Mindset focus:** environment design — put an English cue where you'll see it daily.

**Materials:** irregular-verb card (set 1); a short "yesterday" clip mixing regular + irregular; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 4 regular past verbs + endings (U2) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I went to work, I had lunch with a friend, and I saw a good film." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Irregular = special past form, **no -ed**; present→past pairs; brief Arabic note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the verb; clear vowels (went/saw/ate); rhythm of a 3-action sentence | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Substitution/matching: build past sentences with set-1 verbs | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 true past sentences using ≥2 irregular verbs; record one | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check irregular forms (not "goed/eated"); note for the queue | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "Yesterday I went ___, had ___, and saw ___." | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — give the verb card. Stretch — combine with regular verbs + a time/sequence word.
**Success check:** correct use of ≥2 set-1 irregular verbs. **Queue:** set-1 irregulars re-drilled every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** over-regularization ("goed", "eated") is expected and normal — correct warmly and re-expose; frequency builds the memory.

## S1-U3-L02 — Common Irregular Verbs (2): buy / get / come / make / take

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use five more irregular past forms (bought, got, came, made, took).
- The learner can narrate a short past sequence using set-1 + set-2 verbs.
- The learner can self-check irregular forms against the reference card.

**Target language:** buy→bought, get→got, come→came, make→made, take→took; recycled verbs. **Grammar:** irregular past
(affirmative), consolidation. **Accent Lab focus:** the /ɔː/ in *bought*; clusters in *made/took*; main-verb stress.
**Arabic support:** ~35%. **Mindset focus:** reps compound — ten verbs now, ten more soon.

**Materials:** irregular-verb card (set 2); a "shopping trip" clip; matching game; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: set-1 irregulars (went/had/did/saw/ate) in sentences | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I went to the market, bought vegetables, and made dinner." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Set-2 present→past pairs; add to the growing irregular list; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | /ɔː/ in *bought*; keep clusters clean (*made, took*); stress the verb | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Chain a story with mixed set-1/2 verbs; peer builds the next sentence | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Narrate a small real event (a purchase, a visit) in 3–4 sentences; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check set-2 forms; flag persistent errors | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3–4 sentences about something you bought/made recently | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — reference card + frame. Stretch — 5-sentence mini-story with sequence words.
**Success check:** correct use of ≥3 set-1/2 irregular verbs in connected speech. **Queue:** set-2 irregulars re-drilled + carried to U8/U10.
**Coach notes:** ten irregulars is plenty for A2 core; add more only as they appear naturally. Prioritize fluency over an exhaustive list.

## S1-U3-L03 — Past Questions & Negatives: *did / didn't*

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can ask past questions with *did* (+ base verb) and give short answers.
- The learner can make past negatives with *didn't* (+ base verb).
- The learner can hold a past-tense mini-interview.

**Target language:** *Did you…? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.*; *I didn't + base verb*; wh- past questions
(*What did you do? Where did you go?*). **Grammar:** *did/didn't* + **base form** (the verb loses its past ending
after did). **Accent Lab focus:** weak *did* /dɪd/→/dəd/ in wh-questions; clear *didn't* (/t/); rising vs falling intonation.
**Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** never miss twice — one missed day is fine, two starts a habit.

**Materials:** *did/didn't* question frames; short-answer cards; an interview clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 5 irregular past verbs (affirmative) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "What did you do yesterday?" "I went out." "Did you see Ali?" "No, I didn't." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | The rule: *did/didn't + base verb* (NOT past form) — "Did you **go**?" not "Did you went?"; wh- questions | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *did* in wh-questions; clear *didn't*; question intonation | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Pair drill: ask/answer *Did you…?*; convert affirmatives to negatives | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Mini-interview: 3 past questions to a partner; report one answer; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check base-verb-after-did; short answers; note errors | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: ask a classmate a past question + answer one, using *did/didn't* | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — provide question frames + short-answer cards. Stretch — add wh- follow-ups ("Why? Who with?").
**Success check:** correct *did/didn't + base verb* in questions and negatives. **Queue:** did/didn't recycles heavily (U4–U10 warm-ups).
**Coach notes:** the #1 A2 error — keeping the past form after *did* ("Did you went?"). Drill the "did takes the base verb" rule relentlessly; it's worth the time.

## S1-U3-L04 — Telling a Story: A Small Event From Your Week

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can tell a short true story (5–7 sentences) with past verbs + sequence.
- The learner can use basic story language (*One day…, Suddenly…, In the end…*).
- The learner can listen to a peer's story and ask one past question.

**Target language:** story connectors (One day, First, Then, Suddenly, In the end); mixed regular + irregular past.
**Grammar:** past narration (integration). **Accent Lab focus:** narrative rhythm — beat on content verbs, pause at
connectors; keep endings clean. **Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** input diet — pick one English show/podcast at your level.

**Materials:** a model 6-sentence story clip; a story-skeleton card (beginning/middle/end); picture prompts; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: did/didn't questions + irregular verbs (L01–L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Model tells a small real story (missed a bus, met a friend); learners note the sequence | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Story shape (beginning → what happened → end) + connectors; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Narrative rhythm: stress verbs, pause at connectors; clean *-ed* + irregulars | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build a story from picture prompts together; then in pairs | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell your own small true story (5–7 sentences); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check past forms + flow; a peer asks one past question | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 5–7 sentence story voice note; ask one question on a classmate's story | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — use the story-skeleton card + connectors list. Stretch — add direct speech ("She said, 'Hello!'") or a feeling.
**Success check:** a coherent 5–7 sentence past story with connectors + mostly-correct past forms. **Queue:** storytelling recycles into U8 (trip) & U10.
**Coach notes:** stories are the A2 fluency engine — prioritize communication and flow; correct only patterned errors (did+past, missing endings) via delayed feedback.

## S1-U3-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Tell Me About Your Last Holiday / Day Off" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 1 / A2 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can narrate a past experience (7–9 sentences) using regular + irregular past + questions + connectors.
- The learner can answer follow-up past questions.
- The learner records their **Unit-3 story** — a clear step up in past-tense range.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-3 language. **Grammar:** past simple (all forms) consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity — *-ed* endings, irregular vowels, weak *did*, narrative rhythm.
**Arabic support:** ~25% (fading). **Mindset focus:** you can tell stories now — the heart of real conversation.

**Materials:** a model holiday/day-off narration; a checklist card (setting / 3+ past verbs / did-question handled / connector / feeling);
recording prompt; Unit-3 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: irregular verbs, did/didn't, connectors | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean holiday/day-off narration (7–9 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist of a good past account | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their weakest past sound (endings / a hard irregular) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice + one follow-up question each | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full holiday/day-off narration + answer one live follow-up | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the narration; complete Unit-3 quiz | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 7 sentences with the card. Stretch — include reported speech + 2 follow-up answers.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 3):** connected 7–9 sentence past narration with correct forms + handled question (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A2). **Queue:** full narration recycles into U10; past block now complete.
**Coach notes:** this closes the past-tense block (U1–U3) — the biggest A2 milestone. Compare informally to their Stage-0 clips; the growth is motivating.
