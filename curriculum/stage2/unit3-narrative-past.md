# Stage 2 · Unit 3 — Narrative Past: Past Continuous & *used to* (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 5.2 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage2/00-stage2-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~15% (light). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can describe past situations in
> progress, tell interrupted-action stories (*while/when*), and talk about past habits/states now changed
> (*used to*) — recorded.
> **Grammar spine:** past continuous (was/were + -ing) + past simple contrast + *used to* · **Accent Lab:**
> *was/were* weak forms, -ing linking · **Mindset:** stop translating — think in English.

**Assets to produce:** a "what were you doing when…?" clip (American model), a timeline graphic
(background vs event), a *used to* then/now card, minimal audio for weak *was/were*, a Google Form per lesson.

## S2-U3-L01 — Past Continuous: "I Was Doing…"

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: A2 past simple; Stage-0 present continuous.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the past continuous (was/were + -ing) to describe actions in progress in the past.
- The learner can describe what was happening at a past moment.
- The learner can set a background scene.

**Target language:** past continuous; time phrases (at 8 o'clock, this time yesterday, all morning). **Grammar:**
*was/were + -ing*; +/−/?. **Accent Lab focus:** weak *was* /wəz/, *were* /wə/; -ing linking. **Arabic support:** ~15%.
**Mindset focus:** picture the scene in English — describe it directly, don't translate word-by-word.

**Materials:** a timeline graphic (a moment in the past); a "scene" clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past simple (A2) — "Yesterday I worked." | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "At 8pm I was cooking, my kids were doing homework." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *was/were + -ing* = in progress at a past time; +/−/?; brief gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *was/were*; link "was-cooking / were-doing" | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe a past moment (multiple people in progress); pair check | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say what you were doing at 3 past times; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check was/were + -ing | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "At ___ yesterday, I was ___." (3 moments) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — timeline + frame. Stretch — describe a busy scene (3+ people in progress).
**Success check:** correct past continuous for actions in progress. **Queue:** recycles into L02/L04.
**Coach notes:** keep past continuous meaning "in progress / background" distinct from past simple "completed" — the contrast is L02.

## S2-U3-L02 — *While / When*: Interrupted Actions

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can combine past continuous (background) + past simple (interrupting event).
- The learner can use *while* (+ continuous) and *when* (+ simple).
- The learner can tell a short interrupted-action story.

**Target language:** *while/when*; background + event verbs. **Grammar:** past continuous + past simple with *while/when*.
**Accent Lab focus:** clause rhythm; pause between background and event. **Arabic support:** ~15%.
**Mindset focus:** think in English while you tell it — narrate, don't translate.

**Materials:** background-vs-event timeline; a "when suddenly…" clip; story prompts; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past continuous (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I was walking home when it started to rain." "While I was cooking, the phone rang." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Long action (continuous) interrupted by short action (simple); *while* + continuous, *when* + simple | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Rhythm: beat on the two verbs; brief pause at the clause break | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Combine background + event with while/when; reorder prompts | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell a short interrupted-action story (3–4 sentences); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tense pairing + while/when | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "I was ___ when ___." + "While I was ___, ___." | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — timeline + frames. Stretch — a 5-sentence story with two interruptions.
**Success check:** correct continuous+simple pairing with while/when. **Queue:** narrative tense mix recycles into L04, U11.
**Coach notes:** the classic errors — using simple for the background ("I walked when it started") or continuous for the event. Anchor to the timeline: long line = continuous, dot = simple.

## S2-U3-L03 — *used to*: Past Habits & States

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use *used to* for past habits/states that are no longer true.
- The learner can contrast past (used to) with now.
- The learner can talk about how they/things have changed.

**Target language:** *used to + base verb*; change language (now, these days, not anymore). **Grammar:** *used to*
(+ / − *didn't use to* / ? *Did you use to…?*). **Accent Lab focus:** *used to* → /juːstə/ (reduction). **Arabic support:** ~15%.
**Mindset focus:** "I used to avoid English — now I use it" — name your change.

**Materials:** a then/now card; a "how life has changed" clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: interrupted-action stories (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I used to live in a village. I didn't use to like coffee, but now I love it." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *used to* = repeated past habit/state, not now; forms; brief gloss | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Reduction *used to* → "useta"; keep it smooth | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Then/now pairs: "I used to…, but now I…"; pair check | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 ways your life/habits used to be different; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *used to* form + base verb | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "I used to ___, but now I ___." (2 changes) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — then/now card. Stretch — add a reason for the change.
**Success check:** correct *used to* + a clear then/now contrast. **Queue:** *used to* recycles into U11.
**Coach notes:** "used to" (past habit) vs "be used to" (accustomed) — teach only *used to* here; flag the other as later. Watch the base-verb rule ("used to lived" is wrong).

## S2-U3-L04 — Setting a Scene in a Story

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can set a story scene (background with past continuous) then narrate events (past simple).
- The learner can weave in *used to* and time phrases.
- The learner can tell a richer story than at A2.

**Target language:** scene-setting + narrative language. **Grammar:** integrated narrative tenses (continuous +
simple + used to). **Accent Lab focus:** narrative rhythm + connected speech across the story. **Arabic support:** ~10%.
**Mindset focus:** notice you're telling richer stories now — that's B1 range appearing.

**Materials:** a model narrated story (scene → events); a story-skeleton card; picture prompts; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past continuous + while/when + used to | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Model story: sets the scene (continuous), then events (simple), with a *used to* aside | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Story layers: background (continuous) vs events (simple); brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Connected-speech rhythm across sentences; keep endings clean | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build a story from prompts (scene → events) in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell a short scene-set story (5–6 sentences); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tense layering + flow | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 5–6 sentence story that sets a scene then narrates events | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — skeleton card + frames. Stretch — add feelings + a *used to* background detail.
**Success check:** a layered narrative (background + events) that flows. **Queue:** scene-setting recycles into U11/U12.
**Coach notes:** this is where narrative tenses integrate — prioritize a smooth, well-layered story over perfect forms; correct patterned errors via delayed feedback.

## S2-U3-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "A Memorable Moment" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 3 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can narrate a memorable past moment (8–10 sentences): scene (continuous) + events (simple) + a *used to* detail.
- The learner can answer follow-up questions and ask one.
- The learner records their **Unit-3 narrative**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-3 language. **Grammar:** narrative tenses consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; was/were weak forms; narrative rhythm; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~10% (mostly English). **Mindset focus:** your storytelling has depth now — a clear B1 signal.

**Materials:** a model memorable-moment narrative; a checklist card (scene / events / while-when / a used-to detail / a feeling);
recording prompt; Unit-3 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: past continuous, while/when, used to | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean memorable-moment narrative (8–10 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (was/were / -ing linking) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice + one follow-up each | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full narrative + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the narrative; complete Unit-3 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 8 sentences with the card. Stretch — two interruptions + a reflection ("I'll never forget it").
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 3):** connected 8–10 sentence narrative with layered tenses (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at B1). **Queue:** narrative recycles into U11/U12.
**Coach notes:** a strong proof-clip candidate — memorable, personal, and clearly more advanced than the A2 trip narration.
