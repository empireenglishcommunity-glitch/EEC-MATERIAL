## S2-U11-L01 — Telling a Detailed Story (Tense Mix)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 11 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1–U3 (perfect/narrative past).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can tell a detailed story mixing past simple, past continuous, and present perfect naturally.
- The learner can set a scene, narrate events, and reflect ("I've never forgotten it").
- The learner can hold listeners' attention with connectors + detail.

**Target language:** narrative connectors (one day, suddenly, in the end) + reflection (*I've always…, I'd never…*).
**Grammar:** integrated narrative tenses. **Accent Lab focus:** connected-speech rhythm across a long turn; the beat.
**Arabic support:** ~5%. **Mindset focus:** tell your real stories in English — that's using the language, not studying it.

**Materials:** a detailed-story model clip; a story-arc card (scene → events → twist → reflection); Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past continuous + past simple + present perfect | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Model tells a detailed story (scene-set, events, reflection) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | How tenses combine in a rich narrative; the story arc; brief note (English) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Rhythm/linking across a long turn; keep it flowing | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build a detailed story from an arc card in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell a detailed real story (8–10 sentences); record | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tense mixing + flow | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a detailed story voice note | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — story-arc card + connector list. Stretch — add reported speech + a reflection + a twist.
**Success check:** a detailed, well-layered story with mixed tenses. **Queue:** detailed storytelling feeds L05 + U12 summative.
**Coach notes:** this is the payoff of the whole tense system — reward rich, flowing narration; correct only patterned errors via delayed feedback.

## S2-U11-L02 — Talking About Work & Study

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 11 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk in detail about their work/study (responsibilities, routines, opinions).
- The learner can describe experience (present perfect) + daily tasks (present) + goals (future).
- The learner can answer common work/study interview-style questions.

**Target language:** work/study vocab (responsibilities, deadlines, colleagues, skills, degree, major). **Grammar:**
integrated tenses + opinion. **Accent Lab focus:** clear professional vocab; connected speech. **Arabic support:** ~5%.
**Mindset focus:** English is a career multiplier — practice your work story.

**Materials:** work/study vocab set; an "about my job" model clip; interview-question cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: present perfect (experience) + present simple (routine) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: someone describes their job (what they do, have done, want to do) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Combining tenses for a work profile; useful vocab; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Clear professional vocab; connected speech | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Answer work/study questions in pairs (mini-interview) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk ~1.5 min about your work/study; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tense range + vocab | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an "about my work/study" voice note | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — vocab set + question cards. Stretch — handle unexpected follow-up questions.
**Success check:** a coherent, multi-tense work/study talk. **Queue:** work topic recycles into U12 + Stage 3.
**Coach notes:** high real-world value (job interviews, networking). Encourage learners to build a reusable "work story."

## S2-U11-L03 — Technology, Media & City Life

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 11 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can discuss familiar abstract-ish topics (technology, media, city life) with opinions + reasons.
- The learner can use topic vocab and connectors to build a short argument.
- The learner can compare and evaluate (recycling U5 comparatives, U9 connectors).

**Target language:** topic vocab (social media, apps, traffic, public transport, cost of living). **Grammar:**
opinion + connectors + comparison (integration). **Accent Lab focus:** connected speech in longer opinion turns.
**Arabic support:** ~5%. **Mindset focus:** consume these topics in English (news, videos) to feed your output.

**Materials:** topic vocab sets; opinion prompts; a model topic-talk clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: opinion + connectors (U9) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a short opinion on social media / city life (with reasons + examples) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Topic vocab + building a balanced short argument; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Connected speech across longer turns | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Give opinions on 2 topics with reasons + a comparison | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk ~1.5 min on one topic (opinion + 2 reasons + example); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check topic vocab + argument structure | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a short opinion on a topic | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — vocab set + opinion frames. Stretch — present both sides then conclude.
**Success check:** a structured opinion on a familiar topic with reasons. **Queue:** topics feed U12 + Stage 3.
**Coach notes:** these topics preview the B2 professional/abstract range — keep them familiar and opinion-based at B1.

## S2-U11-L04 — Handling an Unexpected Topic (Fluency Strategies)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 11 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can keep talking when unsure (fillers, paraphrase, buy time, ask to clarify).
- The learner can handle a topic they didn't prepare.
- The learner can avoid freezing/switching to Arabic.

**Target language:** fluency strategies (*Let me think…, What I mean is…, It's a kind of…, Could you repeat that?*).
**Grammar:** functional (all stage language available). **Accent Lab focus:** natural fillers; keep-going rhythm.
**Arabic support:** ~5% (English-only). **Mindset focus:** breaking the plateau — talk *through* the gaps, don't stop.

**Materials:** a fluency-strategy card; surprise-topic cards; a model "thinking aloud" clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: hedging + fillers (U10) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: speaker handles a hard question with fillers + paraphrase | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Strategies: buy time, paraphrase, generalize, ask to clarify; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Natural fillers ("well…, you know…, I mean…"); keep the beat | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Draw a surprise topic; talk 60–90s using strategies; swap | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Handle an unseen prompt for ~1 min; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Did they keep going in English? | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a "surprise topic" attempt (no prep) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — strategy card + easier topics. Stretch — harder/abstract surprise topics.
**Success check:** sustains speech on an unprepared topic without stopping/switching to Arabic. **Queue:** strategies feed U12 + Stage 3.
**Coach notes:** this is the anti-freeze lesson — the plateau-breaker. Praise "keeping going" over accuracy; the strategies are the transferable skill.

## S2-U11-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Detailed Story + Discuss a Topic" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 11 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can tell a detailed story AND discuss a real topic (two parts, ~4–5 min total), integrating the stage.
- The learner can respond to questions and keep going with strategies.
- The learner records their **Unit-11 integrated performance** (a summative rehearsal).

**Target language:** integrated Stage-2 language. **Grammar:** full stage range in use.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity + connected speech + the beat; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~5% (near-immersion). **Mindset focus:** you're now a *user* of English — this is what B1 feels like.

**Materials:** a model integrated performance; a checklist card (detailed story w/ tense mix / topic opinion + reasons / a fluency strategy / respond to a question);
recording prompt; Unit-11 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: tense mix + opinion + strategies | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean integrated performance (story + topic) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their top flagged sound | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse both parts in pairs; Q&A each | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the integrated performance (story + topic) + answer a question | 8 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach forward-looking feedback for the summative | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the performance; complete Unit-11 quiz; note summative readiness | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep time. Stretch — less prep, longer performance, harder topic.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 11):** an integrated ~4–5 min performance (story + topic) at/near the B1 threshold (rubric: all dimensions). **Queue:** directly rehearses the U12 summative.
**Coach notes:** this is the final rehearsal before the summative — its purpose is to remove anxiety and reveal any last gaps to polish in U12.
