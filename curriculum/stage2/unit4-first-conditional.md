## S2-U4-L01 — First Conditional: *if* + present, *will*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 4 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: A2 *will/going to*.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the 1st conditional (*If* + present simple, + *will* + base verb).
- The learner can talk about real/likely future situations and their results.
- The learner can produce 3–4 first-conditional sentences.

**Target language:** 1st conditional; everyday cause→result contexts (weather, plans, study, work). **Grammar:**
*If* + present simple, *will* + base verb; comma when *if* starts. **Accent Lab focus:** weak *'ll* contraction;
present tense in the *if*-clause (NOT *will*). **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** set one small **English-only zone** (e.g., your commute).

**Materials:** cause→result cards; a "what will you do if…?" clip; the two-clause structure slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: A2 *will* for predictions/decisions | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "If it rains, I'll stay home. If I study hard, I'll pass." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Structure: *If* + present, *will* + base; result is real/likely; **no *will* in the if-clause**; brief gloss | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *'ll* ("I'll stay"); present verb in if-clause | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Match causes→results; build 1st conditionals; flip clause order | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 real conditionals about your week/goals; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check "no will after if" + *'ll* | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 sentences: "If I ___, I'll ___." | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — structure slide + cause/result cards. Stretch — add *maybe/probably* + a second result.
**Success check:** 3 correct 1st conditionals (present in if-clause, will in result). **Queue:** 1st conditional recycles every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** the #1 error is *will* in the if-clause ("If I will study…"). Drill "present after if, will in the result" — it also sets up the contrast with the 2nd conditional in U5.

## S2-U4-L02 — Future Time Clauses: *when / as soon as / before / after*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 4 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about the future with time clauses (*when/as soon as/before/after* + present).
- The learner can sequence future events.
- The learner can talk about plans that depend on timing.

**Target language:** *when, as soon as, before, after, until* + present (referring to future). **Grammar:** present
tense after future time words (same "no will" rule as *if*). **Accent Lab focus:** weak *'ll* in the main clause;
linking. **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** stack English into a fixed daily moment ("as soon as I wake up, I…").

**Materials:** future-time-clause card; a "future plan sequence" clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 1st conditional (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I'll call you when I arrive. As soon as I finish, I'll start the next task." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Present after *when/as soon as/before/after* (future meaning); brief gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *'ll*; link "when-I-arrive" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Sequence future events with time words; combine two actions | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Describe a future sequence (3 steps) with time clauses; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check "no will after when/as soon as" | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "When I ___, I'll ___. As soon as ___, I'll ___." | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — the time-clause card. Stretch — a 4-step future plan.
**Success check:** correct present-after-time-word + will in main clause. **Queue:** future time clauses recycle into U11.
**Coach notes:** same rule as the 1st conditional (present, not will, in the time clause) — reinforce them together for transfer.

## S2-U4-L03 — *unless / if not*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 4 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use *unless* (= if not) in real conditionals.
- The learner can express conditions/warnings.
- The learner can rephrase *if not* ↔ *unless*.

**Target language:** *unless*; warning/condition contexts. **Grammar:** *unless* + present = *if…not*. **Accent Lab focus:**
sentence stress on the condition; connected speech. **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** "you won't improve unless you practice" — own the condition.

**Materials:** unless⇄if-not cards; a short clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 1st conditional + time clauses (L01–L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "You won't pass unless you study." (= if you don't study) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *unless* = *if…not*; when to use it; brief gloss | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the condition; keep *'ll/won't* clear | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rephrase if-not ↔ unless; make warnings/conditions | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Give 2 conditions using *unless* (about goals/plans); record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check unless = if not logic | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 2 *unless* sentences | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — unless⇄if-not cards. Stretch — combine unless + a time clause.
**Success check:** correct *unless* meaning + form. **Queue:** unless recycles into U9/U11.
**Coach notes:** the double-negative logic ("unless you don't…") is a common trap — keep examples simple and positive-conditioned.

## S2-U4-L04 — Real Plans & Promises

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 4 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can make realistic plans with conditions and consequences.
- The learner can make and respond to promises/commitments.
- The learner can talk through a decision with if/when.

**Target language:** planning + promising language (*If…, I'll definitely…; I promise I'll…*). **Grammar:**
integrated 1st conditional + time clauses + unless. **Accent Lab focus:** emphasis on commitment words; connected speech.
**Arabic support:** ~10%. **Mindset focus:** make a real English-practice promise to yourself (with a condition).

**Materials:** decision-scenario cards; a planning dialogue clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 1st conditional + when/as soon as + unless | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: someone plans an event with conditions ("If enough people come, we'll…") | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Chaining conditions + consequences into a plan; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Emphasis on key commitment words; smooth linking | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Plan a scenario in pairs using if/when/unless | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk through a real plan with 3 conditions; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check conditional accuracy + planning flow | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a mini-plan with 2 conditions | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — scenario card + frames. Stretch — negotiate a plan with a partner (react to conditions).
**Success check:** a coherent conditional plan using ≥2 structures. **Queue:** planning talk recycles into U11.
**Coach notes:** integrates the unit — reward realistic, connected planning; correct only patterned conditional errors.

## S2-U4-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Plans & Consequences" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 4 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about future plans with conditions (8–10 sentences): 1st conditional + time clauses + unless.
- The learner can discuss consequences and respond to a partner's conditions.
- The learner records their **Unit-4 plans-and-consequences talk**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-4 language. **Grammar:** 1st conditional + future time clauses consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; weak *'ll*; present-in-if-clause; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~10% (mostly English). **Mindset focus:** you can now reason about the future in English — real independence.

**Materials:** a model plans-and-consequences talk; a checklist card (2+ 1st conditionals / a time clause / an unless / a consequence);
recording prompt; Unit-4 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: 1st conditional, when/as soon as, unless | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean plans-and-consequences talk (8–10 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (weak 'll / linking) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice + react to conditions | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full talk + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-4 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 8 sentences with the card. Stretch — chain 3 conditions + consequences + a follow-up.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 4):** connected 8–10 sentence conditional talk (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at B1). **Queue:** conditional talk recycles into U5 (contrast) & U12.
**Coach notes:** ends the "real future" block; U5 pivots to the unreal (2nd conditional) — flag the contrast to prime it.
