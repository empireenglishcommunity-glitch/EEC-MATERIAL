## S2-U10-L01 — Agreeing & Adding

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 10 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U9 (opinions/connectors).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can agree with a range of natural phrases and add to a point.
- The learner can respond to an opinion, not just state one.
- The learner can keep a discussion moving.

**Target language:** agreement (*I agree, exactly, that's true, good point, I think so too*) + adding (*and also, plus, what's more*).
**Grammar:** functional (integrates U9 connectors). **Accent Lab focus:** warm agreement intonation; emphasis on *exactly/absolutely*.
**Arabic support:** ~5%. **Mindset focus:** discussion is a two-way street — respond, don't just wait to talk.

**Materials:** agreement/adding phrase card; a discussion clip; opinion prompts; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: give a quick structured opinion (U9) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I think prices are too high." "I totally agree, and also…" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Agreement phrases + adding a point; responding to build on an idea; brief note (English) | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Emphatic agreement ("**Exactly!**"); warm intonation | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | React to opinions with agreement + an added point; swap | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Agree with + build on 3 opinions from prompts; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check natural agreement + adding | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an agreement + added point on a classmate's opinion | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — phrase card. Stretch — agree + add + a personal example.
**Success check:** natural agreement + a relevant added point. **Queue:** agreement language recycles into L04/L05.
**Coach notes:** learners often only state opinions and don't respond. Train "listen → react → add" — the core of discussion.

## S2-U10-L02 — Disagreeing Politely

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 10 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can disagree politely (soften + give a reason).
- The learner can acknowledge before disagreeing (*I see your point, but…*).
- The learner can hold a respectful difference of opinion.

**Target language:** polite disagreement (*I'm not sure I agree, I see your point but…, actually, I'd say…*) + reason.
**Grammar:** functional + U9 contrast connectors. **Accent Lab focus:** softening intonation (don't sound harsh);
stress the contrast. **Arabic support:** ~5%. **Mindset focus:** disagreeing in English is safe — practice it low-stakes.

**Materials:** polite-disagreement phrase card; a clip; controversial-but-light prompts; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: agreement + adding (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I see your point, but I'm not sure — actually, I'd say the opposite, because…" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Acknowledge → soften → disagree → reason; brief note (English) | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Soft, non-aggressive intonation; contrast stress | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Disagree politely with prompts + give a reason; swap | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Politely disagree with 3 statements + reasons; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check politeness + reason | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a polite disagreement + reason | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — phrase card + the acknowledge→disagree frame. Stretch — disagree, give 2 reasons, propose an alternative.
**Success check:** polite disagreement + a clear reason. **Queue:** disagreement language recycles into L04/L05.
**Coach notes:** flat intonation can make disagreement sound rude — model the softening rise. Emphasize acknowledging first ("I see your point").

## S2-U10-L03 — Hedging & Softening

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can hedge/soften claims (*I think, maybe, probably, it depends, sort of, in a way*).
- The learner can sound less absolute and more natural.
- The learner can express uncertainty.

**Target language:** hedges (*I think/guess, maybe, probably, it depends, kind of/sort of, in my experience*).
**Grammar:** functional. **Accent Lab focus:** natural rhythm of hedges; they're usually unstressed. **Arabic support:** ~5%.
**Mindset focus:** hedging buys thinking time — a real fluency tool, not weakness.

**Materials:** hedging phrase card; a clip; strong-claim → hedged-claim set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: agree/disagree (L01–L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "It depends, really. I think it's probably better, but I'm not totally sure." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Hedges soften claims + buy time; where they go; brief note (English) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Unstressed hedges; smooth into the sentence | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Soften strong claims with hedges; use "it depends" + a condition | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Give 3 hedged opinions on tricky topics; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check natural hedging | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 hedged opinions | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — hedging card. Stretch — "it depends on…" + explain the conditions.
**Success check:** natural hedging that softens claims. **Queue:** hedging recycles into L04/L05 & U11.
**Coach notes:** hedging is a secret fluency weapon (it buys processing time and sounds native). Encourage it heavily — many learners over-commit to absolute statements.

## S2-U10-L04 — A Short Structured Discussion / Debate

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 10 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can take part in a short structured discussion (opinion + agree/disagree + hedge + reasons).
- The learner can take turns and respond to others.
- The learner can stay in English under mild time pressure.

**Target language:** integrated discussion language. **Grammar:** functional + U9 connectors + opinions.
**Accent Lab focus:** interaction rhythm; turn-taking intonation. **Arabic support:** ~5% (English-only run).
**Mindset focus:** speaking under pressure without freezing — use hedges + fillers to keep going.

**Materials:** balanced-topic cards; a structured-discussion format card (open → opinions → respond → wrap); a fluency-strategy card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: agree/disagree/hedge phrases | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Model a short structured discussion (2–3 people, turn-taking) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Discussion structure + turn-taking + staying-in-English strategies; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Turn-taking intonation; fillers (well, I mean, you know) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Small-group discussion on a balanced topic (roles + phrases) | 12 min |
| 6 | Free output | 2-minute paired discussion; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Peer feedback (did everyone respond + justify?) | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a reflection + one strong point you made | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — format card + phrase cards. Stretch — chair the discussion (invite others, summarize).
**Success check:** active participation with agree/disagree + reasons, staying in English. **Queue:** discussion feeds L05 + the summative.
**Coach notes:** this is the summative format in miniature — keep it English-only, low-stakes, and reward participation + reactions over perfect grammar.

## S2-U10-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Discuss a Topic With a Partner" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 10 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can sustain a ~3–4 min discussion: state + justify an opinion, agree/disagree politely, hedge, and respond.
- The learner can take turns and keep the discussion going in English.
- The learner records their **Unit-10 discussion**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-10 language. **Grammar:** discussion functions consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; (dis)agreement + softening intonation; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~5% (near-immersion). **Mindset focus:** you can hold a real discussion in English — the core B1 "cope independently" win.

**Materials:** a model discussion; a checklist card (state opinion + reason / agree or disagree politely / a hedge / a response to partner / stay in English);
recording prompt; Unit-10 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: agree/disagree/hedge/opinion phrases | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean paired discussion | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (softening / emphasis intonation) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the discussion in pairs; swap topics | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record a ~3–4 min paired discussion | 8 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the discussion; complete Unit-10 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + phrase cards + prep time. Stretch — chair the discussion / handle a challenging counter-argument.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 10):** a sustained ~3–4 min discussion with opinions + (dis)agreement + reasons, in English (rubric: Interaction + Fluency + Coherence at B1). **Queue:** discussion skills feed U11/U12 (summative).
**Coach notes:** this is the closest task to the B1 summative — prioritize interaction (turn-taking, responding, hedging) over accuracy. It's a flagship confidence moment.
