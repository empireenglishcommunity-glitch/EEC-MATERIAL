> **Spec task:** 5.2 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage2/00-stage2-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~15% (light). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can talk about recent actions

## S2-U2-L01 — *just / already / yet*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 2 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1 (present perfect form).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use *just* (very recent), *already* (sooner than expected), and *yet* (until now, in −/?).
- The learner can talk about recently completed actions.
- The learner can ask *"Have you … yet?"* and answer.

**Target language:** *just/already/yet*; recent-action verbs (finish, eat, arrive, send, do). **Grammar:**
adverb position (*just/already* before participle; *yet* at end of −/?). **Accent Lab focus:** weak, quick *just/already*;
*yet* clear at the end. **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** the plateau is a stretch — trust the reps, keep showing up.

**Materials:** *just/already/yet* slide; a "recent actions" clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: present perfect form + participles (U1) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I've just finished. She's already left. Have you eaten yet?" | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Meaning + position of *just/already/yet*; +/−/? patterns; brief gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Quick weak *just/already*; final clear *yet* | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Substitution + transform (+ → ? with *yet*); pair check | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 recent things (just/already) + ask a partner "…yet?"; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check adverb position | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "I've just ___, I've already ___. Have you ___ yet?" | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — position card. Stretch — combine with time detail ("I've already done it — I finished an hour ago" preview of the contrast).
**Success check:** correct use + placement of *just/already/yet*. **Queue:** recycles into L03/L04.
**Coach notes:** *yet* only in negatives/questions is the common slip. Contrast *already* (positive) vs *yet* (−/?) directly.

## S2-U2-L02 — *for / since* (How Long…?)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 2 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use *for* (period) and *since* (starting point) with the present perfect.
- The learner can ask and answer *"How long have you…?"*.
- The learner can talk about ongoing states/situations (unfinished time).

**Target language:** *for* (two years, a long time) / *since* (2019, last month, I was a child); state verbs
(live, work, know, have, be). **Grammar:** present perfect for unfinished time; *for* + duration vs *since* + point.
**Accent Lab focus:** weak *for* /fə/ and *since*; *'ve lived* linking. **Arabic support:** ~15%.
**Mindset focus:** measure duration, not perfection — "I've practiced for 6 weeks" is the win.

**Materials:** a for/since timeline card; a "how long" dialogue clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: just/already/yet (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I've lived here for five years." "How long have you known him?" "Since 2015." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *for* + period vs *since* + point; present perfect = still true now; brief gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *for* /fə/; link "I've-lived / I've-known" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Sort time phrases (for/since); ask/answer *How long have you…?* | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say how long you've done 3 things (job, city, hobby); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check for/since choice + present perfect | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "I've ___ for ___ and ___ since ___." | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — timeline card + phrase sort. Stretch — add *How long…?* questions to a partner.
**Success check:** correct for/since + present perfect for unfinished time. **Queue:** for/since recycle into L03 & U11.
**Coach notes:** Arabic speakers often use present simple for these ("I live here since…"). Anchor: unfinished time up to now → present perfect. This is a high-value fix.

## S2-U2-L03 — Present Perfect **vs** Past Simple (the Core Contrast)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 2 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02; A2 past simple.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can choose present perfect (no specific time / unfinished) vs past simple (finished time).
- The learner can switch to past simple when a time is given or asked for details.
- The learner can tell about experiences then add specifics correctly.

**Target language:** contrast pairs ("I've been to Italy." → "I went in 2019."). **Grammar:** present perfect
(indefinite/unfinished) vs past simple (definite/finished time). **Accent Lab focus:** clean switching between
"I've + participle" and past-simple forms. **Arabic support:** ~15% (the rule glossed once). **Mindset focus:** stop translating — feel which "time frame" you're in.

**Materials:** a decision card (time given? finished? → past simple); mixed-sentence sort; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: present perfect (experience + for/since) vs a past-simple sentence | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Have you ever been to Italy?" "Yes — I've been twice. I went last summer with my family." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | The rule: **no/indefinite time or unfinished → present perfect; specific finished time → past simple**; brief gloss | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Switch cleanly mid-turn; keep -ed/participles clean | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Sort mixed sentences; do "experience → detail" chains (perfect → past) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk about an experience, then add when/where in past simple; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check the switch; note habitual errors | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: an experience (present perfect) + a detail (past simple) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — the decision card + sorted examples. Stretch — a short monologue that naturally moves between the two.
**Success check:** correct choice in most items + a clean "experience→detail" switch. **Queue:** the contrast is re-drilled every remaining unit — the single most important B1 tense skill.
**Coach notes:** this is THE B1 grammar hinge. Don't expect mastery in one lesson — the anchor phrase "specific finished time = past simple" plus lots of contrastive reps over the stage is what builds it.

## S2-U2-L04 — News & Recent Events

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 2 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can share and react to recent news (present perfect for the news, past simple for details).
- The learner can ask *"What's new? / Have you heard…?"*.
- The learner can hold a short "catching up" conversation.

**Target language:** news/recent-events language (*Guess what! Have you heard…? I've just…*). **Grammar:**
present perfect (announce) → past simple (details) in real discourse. **Accent Lab focus:** intonation of news
(excitement/emphasis); connected speech. **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** English is for your real life — talk about your actual news.

**Materials:** a "catching up" dialogue clip; news prompt cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: perfect-vs-past switch (L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Guess what — I've got a new job!" "Really? When did you start?" "Last week." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Announce with present perfect → give details with past simple; news openers; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Excited/emphatic intonation; link "I've-got" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Share a piece of news, partner asks for details (past simple) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell 2 pieces of real recent news + take questions; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check announce→details pattern | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post real recent news (perfect) + 1 detail (past) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — news openers + frames. Stretch — react + ask 2 follow-up questions.
**Success check:** a coherent "news + details" exchange with correct tense switch. **Queue:** the discourse pattern recycles into U11.
**Coach notes:** this makes the L03 contrast communicative and real — reward natural reactions and follow-up questions as much as tense accuracy.

## S2-U2-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "What's New / What You've Done Lately" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 2 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about recent life (8–10 sentences): present perfect (just/already/yet, for/since) + past-simple details.
- The learner can catch up with a partner (ask + react + follow up).
- The learner records their **Unit-2 "lately" talk**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-2 language. **Grammar:** present perfect vs past simple consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; weak *for/since*; news intonation; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~10% (mostly English). **Mindset focus:** you can now talk about "lately" — a natural, high-frequency conversation unlocked.

**Materials:** a model "lately" talk; a checklist card (2+ present perfect / for-since / a just-already-yet / a past-simple detail / a question);
recording prompt; Unit-2 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: just/already/yet, for/since, perfect-vs-past | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean "what I've done lately" talk (8–10 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (for/since / a participle) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer catch-up (ask + react) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full "lately" talk + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-2 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 8 sentences with the card. Stretch — sustain a 2-way catch-up with follow-ups.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 2):** connected 8–10 sentence "lately" talk with correct perfect/past choice (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Interaction at B1). **Queue:** the contrast + "lately" discourse recycle into U11 & U12.
**Coach notes:** the present-perfect-vs-past contrast is the unit's true target — score it, flag it, and keep re-drilling it across the stage; it won't be perfect yet.
