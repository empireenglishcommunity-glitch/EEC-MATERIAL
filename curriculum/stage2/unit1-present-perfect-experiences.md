## S2-U1-L01 — Present Perfect Form: *have/has + past participle*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 1 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: A2 past simple.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the present perfect (have/has + past participle) for all persons.
- The learner can understand that the present perfect links past to now (experience, no specific time).
- The learner can produce 3–4 present perfect experience sentences.

**Target language:** present perfect; experience verbs (visit, try, meet, see, eat, travel, do). **Grammar:**
*have/has + past participle*; the concept "sometime in my life, time not important". **Accent Lab focus:**
contractions *I've / he's / she's*; don't drop the auxiliary. **Arabic support:** ~15% (concept glossed).
**Mindset focus:** the intermediate **plateau** — progress feels slower now; that's normal, not failure.

**Materials:** present-perfect structure slide; a short experiences clip; past-participle starter card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: A2 past simple ("I visited… yesterday") — set up the contrast | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I've visited Turkey. She's tried sushi. We've met famous people." | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Form *have/has + past participle*; meaning = experience, no specific time; brief Arabic gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Contractions *I've/he's*; don't say the full "have" every time; link "I've-eaten" | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Substitution: "I've + (participle)…"; swap experiences | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 true experiences (no time); record one | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check auxiliary + participle; note contractions | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 experiences: "I've ___, I've ___, and I've never ___." | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — participle card + frame. Stretch — add detail without a specific time ("a few times / once").
**Success check:** 3 correct present perfect experience sentences. **Queue:** present perfect recycles every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** the core confusion is present perfect vs past simple — hold the line "no specific time" here; the explicit contrast is U2-L03. Expect dropped auxiliary ("I visited many countries" for experience) — gloss the difference in Arabic once, then drill in English.

## S2-U1-L02 — Past Participles (Regular + Key Irregulars)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 1 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01; A2 irregular pasts.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form past participles of regular verbs (= *-ed*) and high-frequency irregulars.
- The learner can distinguish past simple vs past participle where they differ (went/gone, saw/seen).
- The learner can use participles accurately in present perfect sentences.

**Target language:** participles — regular (worked, visited) + irregulars (been, gone, done, seen, eaten, had,
made, taken, met, written, given). **Grammar:** past participle formation; the 3-column verb table (base/past/participle).
**Accent Lab focus:** -ed endings recycled (/t/,/d/,/ɪd/) on regular participles; clear irregular participles.
**Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** small daily reps rebuild momentum on the plateau.

**Materials:** the 3-column verb table (base/past/past participle); a matching game; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: present perfect form (L01) with 3 verbs | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Read short experience texts; underline participles; notice past≠participle (saw/seen) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Regular participle = *-ed*; irregular participles (3rd column); the base/past/participle table | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | -ed endings on regular participles; clear irregulars (been/seen/done) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Fill the 3rd column; make present perfect sentences with mixed participles | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 experiences using irregular participles; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check participle forms (not "I've saw/went") | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Learn 10 participles; post 3 sentences using them | 2 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — the 3-column card. Stretch — a short paragraph mixing 5 participles.
**Success check:** correct participles (esp. irregular) in present perfect. **Queue:** participles re-drilled U2, U8 (passive uses them too).
**Coach notes:** "I've saw / I've went" (using the past, not the participle) is the top error. The 3-column table is the anchor — drill the 3rd column relentlessly; it also powers the passive in U8.

## S2-U1-L03 — *Have You Ever…?* + *ever / never*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 1 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can ask about experiences with *"Have you ever…?"* and give short answers.
- The learner can use *ever* (questions) and *never* (negative experience).
- The learner can hold an experiences mini-interview.

**Target language:** *Have you ever + participle?*; *Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. / I've never…*. **Grammar:**
question + negative of present perfect; *ever/never* position. **Accent Lab focus:** linking "have-you-ever";
question intonation. **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** progress check — you're asking real questions now.

**Materials:** *Have you ever…?* prompt cards; an interview clip; short-answer cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: participles (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Have you ever been to Europe?" "No, I haven't, but I've been to Turkey." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Question form; *ever* in Qs, *never* in negatives; short answers; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Link "have-you-ever" (/həvjuˈɛvər/); rising intonation | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Pair drill: ask *Have you ever…?* → short answer + follow-up | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Mini-interview: 3 experience questions; report one; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check question/negative forms + short answers | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: ask a classmate a *Have you ever…?* + answer one | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — prompt cards + short-answer cards. Stretch — add "When?" to switch to past simple ("Yes — I went last year").
**Success check:** correct *Have you ever…?* + short answer + a *never* sentence. **Queue:** ever/never recycle into U2 & U11.
**Coach notes:** great place to preview the perfect↔past pivot: when we add a *time*, we switch to past simple ("Yes, I went in 2019"). Flag it lightly now; U2-L03 formalizes it.

## S2-U1-L04 — *Been vs Gone*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 1 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can distinguish *has been* (went and came back) from *has gone* (went and is still there).
- The learner can talk about people's whereabouts/experiences accurately.
- The learner can use both in short exchanges.

**Target language:** *have/has been to* vs *have/has gone to*. **Grammar:** the been/gone contrast. **Accent Lab focus:**
*'s been* /zbɪn/ linking; clear /iː/ vs /ɒ/ (been/gone). **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** stop translating — grab the meaning directly.

**Materials:** a been/gone contrast graphic; a short dialogue clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: *Have you ever been to…?* (L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "She's been to Dubai (she's back)." vs "She's gone to Dubai (she's there now)." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *been* = went & returned / experience; *gone* = went & still away; brief gloss | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | "'s been" linking; been (/iː/) vs gone (/ɒ/) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Choose been/gone for situations; describe where people are | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say where you've been + where someone has gone; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check been/gone choice | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "I've been to ___. My friend has gone to ___." | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — the contrast graphic. Stretch — a mini-story using both.
**Success check:** correct been/gone choice in context. **Queue:** been/gone recycle into U11.
**Coach notes:** keep it situational and concrete (back vs still there). Don't over-explain — one clear image fixes it.

## S2-U1-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Life Experiences" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 1 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about their experiences (7–9 sentences): present perfect + ever/never + been/gone, with a couple of past-simple details.
- The learner can interview a partner about experiences and react.
- The learner records their **Unit-1 experiences talk**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-1 language. **Grammar:** present perfect (experience) consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; contractions ('ve/'s); "have-you-ever" linking; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~10% (mostly English). **Mindset focus:** you're now discussing life beyond routine — a real B1 step.

**Materials:** a model experiences talk; a checklist card (3+ experiences / ever-never / been-gone / a past-simple detail / a question to a partner);
recording prompt; Unit-1 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: present perfect form, participles, Have you ever…?, been/gone | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean experiences talk (7–9 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist of a good experiences talk | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (contractions / a hard participle) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer interview (ask back) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full experiences talk + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-1 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 7 sentences with the card. Stretch — weave in a past-simple detail when giving a time + a follow-up.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 1):** connected 7–9 sentence experiences talk, correct present perfect (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at B1). **Queue:** experiences talk recycles into U2 & U12.
**Coach notes:** this is the first clearly-B1 task (talking about life, not just routine). Reassure learners on the plateau — the growth is real even when it feels slow.
