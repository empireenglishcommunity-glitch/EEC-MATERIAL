## S2-U12-L01 — Big Review 1: Perfect + Narrative Past (Retrieval)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 12 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1–U3.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and use present perfect (experience/recent), the perfect-vs-past contrast, past continuous, and *used to*.
- The learner can narrate + discuss experiences with correct tense choice.
- The learner can self-correct the top tense errors.

**Target language:** all Stage-2 perfect/narrative language. **Grammar:** perfect + narrative past (retrieval + consolidation).
**Accent Lab focus:** connected speech + contractions across tenses. **Arabic support:** 0%. **Mindset focus:** look back to A2 — you now handle the whole time system.

**Materials:** perfect/narrative retrieval deck; a mixed-tense prompt; the rubric card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid retrieval: present perfect vs past simple contrast items | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A model that mixes experience (perfect) + story (narrative past) | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-surface the big errors (perfect/past choice, backshift-free storytelling) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Contractions + connected speech across tenses; record-and-compare | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | "Tell about an experience + the story behind it" in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak ~1.5 min mixing perfect + narrative past; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self-correct against the top tense-error list | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a mixed perfect + narrative-past talk | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — retrieval deck + frames. Stretch — add reported speech + reflection.
**Success check:** accurate tense choice across perfect + narrative past. **Queue:** feeds the L05 summative.
**Coach notes:** retrieval > re-teaching — make them produce; the perfect-vs-past choice is still the top thing to reinforce.

## S2-U12-L02 — Big Review 2: Conditionals + Relatives + Reported + Passive (Retrieval)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 12 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U4–U8.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and use 1st/2nd conditionals, relative clauses, reported speech, and the passive.
- The learner can combine several structures in a short talk.
- The learner can self-correct the top errors in each area.

**Target language:** conditionals, relatives, reported, passive. **Grammar:** integrated retrieval.
**Accent Lab focus:** weak forms/reductions across structures. **Arabic support:** 0%. **Mindset focus:** you now have the grammar to say complex things — use it.

**Materials:** mixed retrieval deck; a "describe + hypothesize + report" prompt; rubric card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid: 1 example each — conditional, relative clause, reported statement, passive | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A model combining a relative clause + a conditional + a reported bit | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Common errors per area (will-after-if, doubled subject, reported word order, missing *be*) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak forms/reductions across the structures | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Prompt task combining ≥3 structures in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak ~1.5 min using ≥3 Stage-2 structures; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self-correct against the per-area error list | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a talk combining ≥3 structures | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — retrieval deck + frames. Stretch — combine 4+ structures fluently.
**Success check:** accurate use of ≥3 Stage-2 structures in one talk. **Queue:** feeds the L05 summative.
**Coach notes:** integration is the goal — reward learners who combine structures naturally; don't chase isolated perfection.

## S2-U12-L03 — Discussion Clinic: Opinions + Connected Speech

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 12 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U9–U11.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can give + justify opinions, agree/disagree, and hedge fluently.
- The learner can improve connected speech (linking, weak forms, reductions) + self-monitor.
- The learner can identify and target their top 3 remaining weak sounds.

**Target language:** opinion/discussion functions + connected speech. **Grammar:** functional review.
**Accent Lab focus:** personalized top-3 connected-speech/prosody targets; record-and-compare. **Arabic support:** 0%.
**Mindset focus:** self-monitoring keeps you improving after the course — build the habit now.

**Materials:** each learner's tracker flags; opinion prompts; connected-speech drill set; record-and-compare setup; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Learners state their top-3 flagged sounds/features | 4 min |
| 2 | Input (perceive) | Model connected speech for each learner's targets | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-cue linking/weak forms/reductions + opinion structure | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab (produce) | Targeted drills → opinion turns; record-and-compare | 9 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Give structured opinions with clean connected speech | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | 90-second opinion; self-flag slips | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to earlier recordings — measurable gains | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a record-and-compare of an opinion turn | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — focus on 1 feature + intelligibility. Stretch — self-correct in spontaneous discussion.
**Success check:** clearer connected speech + structured opinions vs earlier. **Queue:** residual features carry into Stage 3 (B2) refinement.
**Coach notes:** honesty guardrail — celebrate clarity/naturalness gains, never demand "native." Compare to the learner's own past recordings.

## S2-U12-L04 — B1 Mock Discussion (8–12 min)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 12 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can sustain an 8–12 min discussion: opinions + justification + (dis)agreement + narration, in English.
- The learner can take turns, respond, and use fluency strategies.
- The learner can self-assess readiness for the summative.

**Target language:** integrated Stage-2 language. **Grammar:** full range in interaction.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity + interaction rhythm. **Arabic support:** 0% (English-only). **Mindset focus:** rehearse like it's real — the summative is a celebration.

**Materials:** the mock discussion prompt set; the rubric card; peer-feedback sheet; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid mixed retrieval (opinion + tense + discussion phrases) | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | A model 8–12 min discussion excerpt; note the rubric dimensions | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | What a strong B1 discussion sounds like (interaction + coherence) | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Quick personal top-1 feature tune-up | 3 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Full mock discussion in pairs/small groups (8–12 min); peer feedback w/ rubric | 15 min |
| 6 | Feedback | Rubric-based, forward-looking (what to polish before L05) | 3 min |
| 7 | Assignment | Self-assess with the rubric; prepare summative topics | 2 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — prompt + phrase cards + prep time. Stretch — chair the discussion / handle challenges with less support.
**Success check:** a sustained 8–12 min discussion at/near the B1 threshold. **Queue:** directly precedes the summative.
**Coach notes:** this is the exact summative format — keep it English-only and encouraging; it removes anxiety and previews the assessment.

## S2-U12-L05 — B1 Summative Assessment + B1 Milestone Speaking Sample

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 12 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment, English-only) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can demonstrate the **B1 exit criteria**: express/justify opinions, tell detailed stories,
  handle hypotheticals & reported speech, and sustain an 8–12 min discussion, largely in English.
- The learner completes the **Stage-2 summative quiz** (grammar/vocab recognition + production).
- The learner records the **B1 milestone speaking sample** on the standard prompt (extended) for the proof timeline.

**Target language:** all Stage-2 language. **Grammar:** full Stage-2 range (assessed).
**Accent Lab focus:** clarity + connected speech across the discussion (scored on Pronunciation — *clear*, not *native*).
**Arabic support:** 0% (full immersion). **Mindset focus:** celebrate the B1 milestone; look ahead to B2 (Confident — the flagship graduation).

**Materials:** the **Stage-2 summative quiz**; the extended **opinion + discussion** task (8–12 min); the
**standard speaking-sample prompt** (before/after protocol §2, extended for B1: opinions + a story + a hypothetical);
the 5-dimension rubric; consent record (for using the clip per tier).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up | Light retrieval; settle nerves; restate this is a milestone, not a filter | 4 min |
| 2 | Summative quiz | Grammar/vocab recognition + short production (all Stage-2 targets) | 12 min |
| 3 | Speaking task setup | Explain the discussion task + the rubric (English-only) | 3 min |
| 4 | Accent tune-up | 60-second personal top-1 feature warm-up | 2 min |
| 5 | Speaking task | Extended **opinion + discussion** (8–12 min), recorded | 14 min |
| 6 | B1 milestone sample | Record the **standard prompt** (extended): about yourself + an opinion + a story + a hypothetical | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Rubric-based result; B1 determination; specific wins + next-stage (B2) targets | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Save the milestone clip (with consent); set B2 goal; carry residual weak features forward | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — allow prep notes + a slightly shorter discussion (still covering the criteria). Stretch — longer, less-supported, more abstract topics.
**Success check (Stage-2 summative):** meets the **B1 threshold** across the rubric (esp. Fluency, Coherence, Interaction, Accuracy, Pronunciation); quiz passed at the set band. **Queue:** residual weak features + any shaky structures carry into Stage 3 (B2).
**Coach notes:** extend the proof timeline — Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → **B1** milestone samples (with consent, a powerful testimonial). Frame B2 as the flagship climb: 20–30 min professional-topic conversation, full tense range, refinement of the accent — the graduation benchmark.
