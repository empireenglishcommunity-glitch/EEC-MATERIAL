## S2-U8-L01 — Present Simple Passive

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 8 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1 (past participles).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the present simple passive (*is/are + past participle*).
- The learner can describe facts/processes where the doer is unknown/unimportant.
- The learner can convert simple active sentences to passive.

**Target language:** present simple passive; process/fact contexts (products, places, routines). **Grammar:**
*is/are + past participle*. **Accent Lab focus:** weak *is/are*; clear past participles (links to U1). **Arabic support:** ~12%.
**Mindset focus:** progress ≠ perfection — every mistake shows you what to fix next.

**Materials:** active↔passive card; a "facts about things" clip; participle refresher; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past participles (U1) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Coffee is grown in Brazil. These phones are made in China." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *is/are + past participle*; focus on the action/thing, not the doer; brief gloss | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *is/are*; clear participle (grown/made) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Convert active→passive (present); describe facts | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 passive facts about products/places; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *is/are + participle* | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 present-passive facts | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — active↔passive card + participle list. Stretch — describe a simple routine process passively.
**Success check:** 3 correct present-passive sentences. **Queue:** passive recycles every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** the participle is the engine (from U1's 3rd column). If participles are shaky, re-drill them here — the passive won't work without them. Common error: forgetting *be* ("Coffee grown in Brazil").

## S2-U8-L02 — Past Simple Passive

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 8 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the past simple passive (*was/were + past participle*).
- The learner can describe past events/results where the doer is unknown/unimportant.
- The learner can convert active past sentences to passive.

**Target language:** past simple passive; history/events/results contexts. **Grammar:** *was/were + past participle*.
**Accent Lab focus:** weak *was/were*; clear participles. **Arabic support:** ~12%. **Mindset focus:** look how much grammar you've stacked — keep going.

**Materials:** a "history/facts" clip; active↔passive (past) card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: present passive (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "The pyramids were built thousands of years ago. It was made by hand." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *was/were + past participle*; when to use (result/event, doer unknown); brief gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *was/were*; clear participle (built/made) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Convert active→passive (past); describe past results | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 past-passive facts (a building, an invention, an event); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *was/were + participle* | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 past-passive facts | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — the past active↔passive card. Stretch — mini-paragraph about how a place was built/founded.
**Success check:** 3 correct past-passive sentences. **Queue:** past passive re-drilled in L04.
**Coach notes:** learners often reuse the base verb ("was build"). Anchor to the participle (was **built**). Keep contexts concrete (famous buildings/inventions).

## S2-U8-L03 — *by* + Agent (When to Include It)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 8 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can add the agent with *by* when the doer is important.
- The learner can decide when *by* is needed vs unnecessary.
- The learner can talk about famous works/creations.

**Target language:** *by* + agent; creators/famous works. **Grammar:** passive + *by* + agent. **Accent Lab focus:**
weak *by* /baɪ/ unstressed; linking. **Arabic support:** ~12%. **Mindset focus:** own your reps — you're the "agent" of your progress.

**Materials:** famous-works cards (art/books/inventions); a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: present + past passive (L01–L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "This book was written by a famous author." vs "English is spoken here." (no agent) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Add *by* only when the doer matters; omit when unknown/obvious; brief gloss | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *by*; link "written-by-a" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Add/omit *by*; describe famous works with by + agent | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Say 3 "X was made/written/directed by Y" facts; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *by* + agent + when to include | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 "by + agent" sentences (favourite works/creators) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — famous-works cards. Stretch — explain why some sentences don't need *by*.
**Success check:** correct *by* + agent + sensible inclusion/omission. **Queue:** by-agent recycles into L04.
**Coach notes:** over-using *by* (adding it when the doer is obvious/unknown) is common — teach that we often omit it; that's the point of the passive.

## S2-U8-L04 — Processes, Facts & News (Active ↔ Passive)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 8 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe a simple process/sequence in the passive.
- The learner can choose active vs passive appropriately.
- The learner can talk about how something is made/done.

**Target language:** process language (first, then, next + passive); active/passive choice. **Grammar:** integrated
passive (present + past) + sequencing. **Accent Lab focus:** connected speech across a process; weak auxiliaries. **Arabic support:** ~10%.
**Mindset focus:** describe a real process from your work/life in English.

**Materials:** a "how it's made" process clip; a process card set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: passive forms + by-agent | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a short process ("First the beans are picked, then they're dried…") | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Passive + sequence words for processes; active vs passive choice; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Connected speech across steps; weak auxiliaries | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe a process (steps) in the passive; pair check | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Describe how something is made/done (4–5 steps); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check passive + sequencing + choice | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 4–5 step process in the passive | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — process card + sequence words. Stretch — a real process from your job/hobby.
**Success check:** a coherent passive process (4–5 steps). **Queue:** process description recycles into U11/U12.
**Coach notes:** processes make the passive purposeful and communicative — reward a clear, sequenced description over form-perfection.

## S2-U8-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Describe How Something Is Made/Done" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 8 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe a process/product (8–10 sentences) using present & past passive + by-agent + sequence.
- The learner can answer follow-up questions and ask one.
- The learner records their **Unit-8 process description**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-8 language. **Grammar:** simple passive consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; weak auxiliaries + *by*; participles; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~10% (mostly English). **Mindset focus:** you can now explain how things work in English — a professional-level skill emerging.

**Materials:** a model process description; a checklist card (present passive / past passive / a by-agent / sequence / 4+ steps);
recording prompt; Unit-8 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: present/past passive, by-agent, process sequencing | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean process description (8–10 sentences) | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (participle / weak auxiliary) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer practice + one follow-up each | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full process description + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the description; complete Unit-8 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 8 sentences with the card. Stretch — mix present (how it's made now) + past (how it was invented) + by-agent.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 8):** connected 8–10 sentence passive process description (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at B1). **Queue:** passive recycles into U11/U12.
**Coach notes:** completes the "grammar for complex ideas" block (U6–U8). From here the stage shifts to discourse/opinions (U9–U11) — the priority for the B1 "cope independently" outcome.
