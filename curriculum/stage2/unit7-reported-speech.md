## S2-U7-L01 — *say vs tell*

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 7 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: A2/B1 past simple.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can choose *say* (no person needed) vs *tell* (+ person).
- The learner can report simple statements at a basic level.
- The learner can avoid the common *say/tell* errors.

**Target language:** *say / tell*; reporting verbs. **Grammar:** *say (that)…* vs *tell someone (that)…*.
**Accent Lab focus:** weak *that* /ðət/ in reporting frames; linking "told-me". **Arabic support:** ~12%.
**Mindset focus:** output ramp — say a bit more than feels comfortable each session.

**Materials:** say/tell rule card; a short reporting clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: past simple + a quote ("He said…") | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "She said she was tired. She told me she was tired." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *say* (+ no person) vs *tell* (+ person); *tell me/him/us*; brief gloss | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *that*; link "told-me/said-that" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Choose say/tell; report simple statements | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 things people said/told you today; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check say/tell choice | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 sentences using say + tell correctly | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — rule card + frames. Stretch — report a short real exchange.
**Success check:** correct say/tell in 3 sentences. **Queue:** say/tell recycle every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** "she said me / she told that" are the classic errors. Anchor: *tell* needs a person, *say* doesn't. Drill the pair.

## S2-U7-L02 — Reported Statements (Backshift)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 7 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report statements with backshift (present→past, will→would, can→could).
- The learner can change pronouns/time words as needed.
- The learner can report what someone said earlier.

**Target language:** backshift set (am/is→was, are→were, do→did, will→would, can→could). **Grammar:** reported
statements with backshift. **Accent Lab focus:** smooth reporting frames; weak forms. **Arabic support:** ~12% (backshift glossed once).
**Mindset focus:** mistakes are data — report anyway, refine later.

**Materials:** a backshift table (present→past); a direct↔reported card; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: say/tell (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I'm tired." → "She said she was tired." "I'll call." → "He said he'd call." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Backshift one step back; pronoun + time changes; brief gloss | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Reporting-frame rhythm; weak *that* | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform direct→reported (statements); pair check | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 statements someone made recently; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check backshift + pronouns | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 reported statements (from real quotes) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — backshift table. Stretch — report a 3-sentence mini-conversation.
**Success check:** correct backshift + pronoun changes in reported statements. **Queue:** backshift re-drilled in L03–L04.
**Coach notes:** don't demand perfect backshift — B1 target is the main shifts (present→past, will→would). Reassure: even natives keep present when the fact is still true; keep it simple.

## S2-U7-L03 — Reported Questions

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 7 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report yes/no questions (with *if/whether*) and wh- questions.
- The learner can use statement word order in reported questions (no inversion, no *do/did*).
- The learner can report what someone asked.

**Target language:** *asked (me) if/whether…*; *asked (me) wh- + statement order*. **Grammar:** reported questions
(no inversion; backshift). **Accent Lab focus:** falling intonation (it's a statement now); weak forms. **Arabic support:** ~12%.
**Mindset focus:** speak up more — reporting questions helps you re-tell real interactions.

**Materials:** reported-question frames; a clip; a "what did they ask?" set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: reported statements (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Where do you live?" → "She asked me where I lived." "Are you ok?" → "He asked if I was ok." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *if/whether* for yes/no; wh- word + **statement order**; no *do/did*; backshift; brief gloss | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Falling (statement) intonation; weak forms | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform questions→reported; pair check | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 questions someone asked you; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check word order (no inversion) + if/whether | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 reported questions | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — frames + the "statement order" reminder. Stretch — report a short Q&A exchange.
**Success check:** correct reported questions (statement order, if/whether). **Queue:** reported questions recycle into U12.
**Coach notes:** the top error is keeping question word order/*do* ("She asked me where do I live"). Drill "reported question = statement order" hard.

## S2-U7-L04 — Reporting Advice & Requests (*asked me to…*)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 7 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report requests/commands/advice with *asked/told/advised + object + to + base verb*.
- The learner can report negative requests (*asked me not to…*).
- The learner can retell instructions.

**Target language:** *asked/told/advised me to…*; *not to…*. **Grammar:** reporting with *to* + infinitive.
**Accent Lab focus:** weak *to* /tə/; linking "asked-me-to". **Arabic support:** ~10%.
**Mindset focus:** re-tell what you're told in English — a practical daily rep.

**Materials:** request/advice cards; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: reported statements + questions | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Please wait." → "She asked me to wait." "Don't be late." → "He told me not to be late." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *asked/told + object + to + base*; negative *not to*; brief gloss | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *to*; link "told-me-to" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform requests/advice→reported; pair check | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 requests/pieces of advice you received; record | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *to*-infinitive pattern | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 reported requests/advice (incl. one negative) | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — pattern card. Stretch — report a set of instructions in sequence.
**Success check:** correct *asked/told + to + base* (incl. *not to*). **Queue:** reporting requests recycles into U12.
**Coach notes:** this pattern is high-frequency and reliable — often easier than backshift. Reward fluent use.

## S2-U7-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Report a Conversation" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 2 / B1 · Unit 7 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report a short conversation (8–10 sentences): say/tell + statements + a question + a request.
- The learner can retell what people said and asked coherently.
- The learner records their **Unit-7 reported conversation**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-7 language. **Grammar:** reported speech consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity; reporting-frame rhythm; weak *that/to*; each learner's flagged sound.
**Arabic support:** ~10% (mostly English). **Mindset focus:** re-telling conversations = a real, useful narrative skill.

**Materials:** a short source dialogue to report; a model reported version; a checklist card (say+tell / 2+ reported statements / a reported question / a reported request);
recording prompt; Unit-7 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: say/tell, backshift, reported Qs, reported requests | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch/read a short dialogue + its reported version | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break the reported version into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills a flagged sound (weak that/to) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Report the given dialogue in pairs; then a real one | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record a report of a real recent conversation | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the reported conversation; complete Unit-7 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — report the provided dialogue with frames. Stretch — report a real conversation with backshift + a question + a request.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 7):** a coherent reported conversation using ≥3 reported forms (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at B1). **Queue:** reported speech recycles into U11/U12.
**Coach notes:** the "telephone/gossip" game is a fun way to practice — keep it light; backshift won't be flawless and that's fine at B1.
