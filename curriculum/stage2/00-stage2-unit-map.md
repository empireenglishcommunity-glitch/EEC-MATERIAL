## 1. Stage 2 overview

- **CEFR:** B1 · **EEC label:** Intermediate
- **Entry criterion:** A2 achieved (Stage 1 exit or placement).
- **Guided-hour budget:** ~150–200 hrs · **Cohort length:** ~16–24 weeks @ ~7 hrs/week.
- **Big promise of this stage:** *"You can now express and justify opinions, tell detailed stories, handle
  hypotheticals, and cope independently in English — you start functioning in English, not translating into it."*
- **Arabic support:** light (~15%), fading to **none (immersion)** by the end — instructions become English-only.
- **Vocabulary target:** ~2,000–2,500 words cumulative (opinions, work/study, abstract-ish topics, connectors, collocations).
- **Skill weighting:** speaking ●●● · listening ●●● · reading ●●● · writing ●●.

### Structure
- **12 units** (bigger stage → more units than A2) · **Unit 12 = consolidation/assessment.**
- **~5 lessons per unit** (≈ 60 lessons total), each ~35–45 min, built on the Lesson Template.
- **Weekly rhythm:** ~1 unit/1–1.5 weeks; recorded lessons + 1–2 live discussion sessions (English-only).

### Sequencing logic
B1 is the **"tense range + independence"** stage. First complete the tense system that A2 opened: **present
perfect** (experience + recent past) and how it contrasts with the past simple (U1–U2), then **narrative
past** (past continuous + used to, U3). Next, the reasoning tools: **conditionals** (real then hypothetical,
U4–U5), **relative clauses** (U6), **reported speech** (U7), and the **passive** (U8) — the grammar that lets
learners talk about complex, connected ideas. Then the discourse layer: **connectors + opinions** (U9) and
**discussion skills** (U10), consolidated through **detailed storytelling & real-world topics** (U11), closing
with consolidation and the **B1 summative** (U12). Every unit recycles A2/Stage-0 language and adds to the queue.

### What's new vs A2
- **Perfect aspect + narrative tenses**: the tense system becomes complete enough for real storytelling.
- **Reasoning & abstraction**: conditionals, opinions with justification, hypotheticals, reported speech.
- **Immersion**: Arabic support goes from light to none; lessons and live sessions move to English-only.
- **Longer output**: from A2 role-plays/narratives to **opinion + discussion** turns (8–12 min by the summative).

## 2. Cross-stage threads (Stage-2 slice)

- **Accent Lab focus (from `curriculum/03` §6 — Stage 2 = connected speech):** **linking** (join final
  consonant → next vowel), **weak forms** (and/to/of/can → /ən, tə, əv, kən/), **reductions**
  (gonna/wanna/gotta), sentence **intonation** (yes/no vs wh vs lists vs emphasis/contrast), and refining
  **stress-timing** (the American "beat" — stress content words, squeeze function words); perfect-tense
  contractions (I've/he's been). Residual segmental clean-up (esp. -ed endings, /θ/–/ð/) continues per learner.
- **Mindset focus ("Breaking the Plateau & Going Immersive" — `curriculum/04`):** pre-teach the intermediate
  plateau (normal, not failure); push into fuller immersion; **stop translating in your head**; build
  thinking-in-English habits; ramp input + output; create English-only zones; handle frustration/comparison.
- **Assessment:** end-of-unit formative checks (quiz + speaking task); **Unit 12 summative** (quiz + extended
  **opinion + discussion** speaking task, 8–12 min, English-only) on the 5-dimension rubric at the **B1
  threshold**; record a **B1 milestone speaking sample** to extend the proof timeline (Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → B1).

## 4. Unit detail + lesson lists

> Each lesson is authored with `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`. Lesson IDs: `S2-U{n}-L{nn}`.
> Objectives are the "By the end, the learner can…" for that lesson. Instructions increasingly **English-only**.

### Unit 1 — Present Perfect (1): Experiences
- **Can-do:** talk about life experiences; ask/answer *"Have you ever…?"*; distinguish *been/gone*.
- **Grammar/function:** present perfect (have/has + past participle) for indefinite experience; *ever/never*; *been vs gone*.
- **Accent Lab:** contractions *'ve/'s*; linking "have you ever". **Mindset:** the intermediate plateau is normal.
- **Lessons:** L01 form (have/has + past participle) · L02 past participles (regular + key irregulars) ·
  L03 *Have you ever…?* + *ever/never* · L04 *been vs gone* · L05 speaking task ("life experiences") + review

### Unit 2 — Present Perfect (2): Recent & Unfinished + vs Past Simple
- **Can-do:** talk about recent actions and unfinished time; contrast present perfect with past simple.
- **Grammar/function:** *just/already/yet*; *for/since* (duration); present perfect **vs** past simple (finished vs unfinished time).
- **Accent Lab:** weak *for/since*; *'ve been* linking. **Mindset:** trust the process on the flat stretch.
- **Lessons:** L01 *just/already/yet* · L02 *for/since* (How long…?) · L03 present perfect vs past simple (the core contrast) ·
  L04 news & recent events · L05 speaking task ("what's new / what you've done lately") + review

### Unit 3 — Narrative Past: Past Continuous & *used to*
- **Can-do:** describe past situations in progress; interrupted actions; past habits/states now changed.
- **Grammar/function:** past continuous (was/were + -ing); past simple vs continuous (*while/when*); *used to*.
- **Accent Lab:** *was/were* weak forms; -ing linking. **Mindset:** stop translating — think in English.
- **Lessons:** L01 past continuous (was doing) · L02 *while/when* (interrupted actions) · L03 *used to* (past habits) ·
  L04 setting a scene in a story · L05 speaking task ("a memorable moment") + review

### Unit 4 — First Conditional & Future Time Clauses
- **Can-do:** talk about real future possibilities and their results; plan with conditions.
- **Grammar/function:** 1st conditional (*if* + present, *will*); *when/as soon as/unless/before/after* + present.
- **Accent Lab:** weak *will/'ll*; linking across the *if*-clause. **Mindset:** create small English-only zones.
- **Lessons:** L01 1st conditional (if + present, will) · L02 *when/as soon as* (future time clauses) ·
  L03 *unless/if not* · L04 making real plans & promises · L05 speaking task ("plans & consequences") + review

### Unit 5 — Hypotheticals: Second Conditional (+ *wish*)
- **Can-do:** talk about unreal/imaginary situations and their results; express wishes.
- **Grammar/function:** 2nd conditional (*if* + past, *would*); *I wish* + past (intro); *would/could*.
- **Accent Lab:** *would/'d* reduction; sentence stress on content words. **Mindset:** imagine your English future.
- **Lessons:** L01 2nd conditional form · L02 real vs unreal (1st vs 2nd conditional) · L03 *If I were you…* (advice/hypothetical) ·
  L04 *I wish…* (intro) · L05 speaking task ("if I could… / dreams") + review

### Unit 6 — Relative Clauses (Defining)
- **Can-do:** give more information about people, things, and places in one sentence.
- **Grammar/function:** defining relative clauses (*who/which/that/where/whose*); omitting the object pronoun.
- **Accent Lab:** intonation grouping; slight pause at clause boundaries. **Mindset:** upgrade your input diet.
- **Lessons:** L01 *who/which/that* (people & things) · L02 *where/whose* · L03 leaving out the relative pronoun ·
  L04 defining people/places/things in detail · L05 speaking task ("describe a person/place who/that…") + review

### Unit 7 — Reported Speech (Intro)
- **Can-do:** report what people said and asked.
- **Grammar/function:** *say/tell*; backshift (present → past); reported statements & questions; *asked/told + to*.
- **Accent Lab:** weak *that/to*; linking in reporting frames ("she said that…"). **Mindset:** output ramp — speak more than feels comfortable.
- **Lessons:** L01 *say vs tell* · L02 reported statements (backshift) · L03 reported questions · L04 reporting advice/requests (*asked me to…*) ·
  L05 speaking task ("report a conversation") + review

### Unit 8 — The Passive (Intro)
- **Can-do:** describe processes, facts, and results where the doer is unknown/unimportant.
- **Grammar/function:** present & past simple passive (*is/are/was/were + past participle*); *by* + agent.
- **Accent Lab:** weak *is/are/was/were* + *by*; clear past participles. **Mindset:** progress ≠ perfection (mistakes are data).
- **Lessons:** L01 present simple passive · L02 past simple passive · L03 *by* + agent (when to include it) ·
  L04 processes, facts & news (active↔passive) · L05 speaking task ("describe how something is made/done") + review

### Unit 9 — Connecting Ideas & Expressing Opinions
- **Can-do:** connect ideas smoothly; express and justify opinions.
- **Grammar/function:** connectors (*however, although, because, so, so that, in addition*); opinion frames (*In my opinion…, I think… because…*).
- **Accent Lab:** list intonation; contrast stress on *however/although*. **Mindset:** from facts to opinions — own your voice.
- **Lessons:** L01 *because/so/so that* (reason & result) · L02 *although/but/however* (contrast) · L03 adding & sequencing ideas ·
  L04 expressing + justifying an opinion · L05 speaking task ("give your opinion on…") + review

### Unit 10 — Discussion Skills: Agreeing/Disagreeing & Justifying
- **Can-do:** take part in a discussion — agree, disagree politely, give reasons, hedge.
- **Grammar/function:** (dis)agreement language (*I agree/I'm not sure/I see your point but…*); hedging (*maybe, I think, it depends, sort of*).
- **Accent Lab:** emphasis + intonation of (dis)agreement; softening intonation. **Mindset:** speaking under pressure without freezing.
- **Lessons:** L01 agreeing & adding · L02 disagreeing politely · L03 hedging & softening · L04 a short debate/discussion (structured) ·
  L05 speaking task ("discuss a topic with a partner") + review

### Unit 11 — Storytelling with Detail & Real-World Topics
- **Can-do:** tell a detailed story and talk fluently about real everyday/work topics.
- **Grammar/function:** integrated narrative tenses (perfect + continuous + simple) + connectors + opinion; topic vocab (work, technology, media, city life).
- **Accent Lab:** full connected-speech integration; the American beat/rhythm. **Mindset:** use English in real life (calls, content, work).
- **Lessons:** L01 telling a detailed story (tense mix) · L02 talking about work/study · L03 talking about technology/media & city life ·
  L04 handling an unexpected topic (fluency strategies) · L05 speaking task ("tell a detailed story + discuss a topic") + review

### Unit 12 — Putting It Together (B1 Consolidation & Assessment)
- **Can-do:** demonstrate the **B1 exit criteria** — express/justify opinions, tell detailed stories, handle
  hypotheticals & reported speech, and sustain an 8–12 min discussion, largely in English.
- **Grammar/function:** integrated review of all Stage-2 language.
- **Accent Lab:** overall clarity + self-monitoring; connected speech + prosody. **Mindset:** "learner → user"; readiness for B2 (the flagship).
- **Lessons:** L01 big review 1 (perfect + narrative past) · L02 big review 2 (conditionals + relatives + reported + passive) ·
  L03 discussion clinic (opinions + connected speech) · L04 B1 mock discussion (8–12 min) · L05 **B1 summative + milestone speaking sample**

## 5. Assessment placement (links to Task 1.6)

- **Before Unit 1:** confirm A2 exit / B1 readiness (Stage-1 summative or placement).
- **End of each unit:** short formative check (quiz + speaking task) — feedback, not gatekeeping.
- **Unit 12, L05:** **summative** (quiz + extended opinion+discussion task, 8–12 min, English-only) → B1
  determination; record the **B1 milestone "after" sample** and extend the proof timeline (Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → **B1**).

## 6. Spaced-review threading

- Every unit's warm-ups retrieve prior Stage-2 units + **A2/Stage-0** (e.g., U2 contrasts present perfect with
  the A2 past simple; U11 re-integrates the whole tense system; conditionals recycle *will* from A2-U4).
- New target language enters the review queue (~next lesson → ~3 days → ~1 week → later units).
- **Present perfect vs past simple, conditionals, and connected-speech reductions** are deliberately re-drilled
  across units (they need many reps). Unit 12 is retrieval-heavy (testing effect consolidates the whole stage).

## 7. Build order (recommendation)

Build in teaching order **Unit 1 → Unit 12**, so the cohort continues seamlessly from A2, with later units
completed just ahead of the cohort (design §3.4). Author every lesson with the Lesson Template and obey the
design checklist. The **present-perfect block (U1–U2)** and the **conditionals (U4–U5)** are the priority
grammar; the **discussion/opinion block (U9–U11)** is the priority for the B1 "cope independently" outcome.
