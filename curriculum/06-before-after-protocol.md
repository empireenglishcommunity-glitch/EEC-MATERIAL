# Before/After Speaking-Sample Protocol (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.7 (completes Phase 1) · **Requirement:** R3 (Assessment/Progress), supports R6 (content) & R13 (KPIs)
> **Reads with:** `curriculum/05-assessment-suite.md`, `operations/02-cohort-tracker.md`,
> `brand/01-brand-foundations.md` (§10 proof standards).
> **Purpose:** The exact, repeatable process for capturing each learner's speaking on a **fixed prompt**
> at the start and end of a stage (and at milestones), getting **consent**, **storing** it safely, and
> turning the paired clips into EEC's strongest proof asset. This is the operational spine of the
> "results = marketing" flywheel — done ethically.

---

## 1. Why this matters

The paired **Day-1 ↔ graduation** clip does three jobs at once:
1. **Measurement** — a fair before/after on the *same* prompt shows real, objective progress.
2. **Motivation** — learners hearing their own transformation is the most powerful retention fuel.
3. **Marketing (the flywheel)** — with consent, real transformations are proof no ad can buy, and they
   lower acquisition cost cohort over cohort.

Because the prompt is fixed and identical at both ends, the comparison is honest — not cherry-picked.

---

## 2. The standard prompts (fixed & reused)

Use the **same prompt** at Day-1 and graduation so the comparison is valid.

- **Primary prompt (Stage 0 baseline & graduation):**
  > *"Tell me about yourself, your family, and your typical day."*
- **Beginner fallback (true zero — Day 1 only, for those who can't attempt the primary):**
  > *"What's your name? Where are you from? What do you do?"*
  > *(At graduation, everyone attempts the primary prompt — that contrast is the point.)*
- **Per-stage prompts (for later stages):** each stage uses a fixed prompt at the level's ceiling
  (e.g., **B2 flagship:** *"Talk about your work/studies and discuss a topic you care about"* — a 20–30
  min everyday+professional conversation). Define and freeze each stage's prompt when the stage is built.

**Milestone captures (optional but recommended):** the unit L05 recordings we already built (self-intro,
family, routine…) form an ongoing portfolio between the two bookend samples — great for mid-cohort
motivation and content.

---

## 3. Capture protocol

**When:**
- **Day-1 sample:** during placement / first week (Assessment Suite §3).
- **Graduation sample:** at the stage summative (e.g., Stage 0 Unit 10 L05).
- **Milestones:** each unit's L05 (already built into the curriculum).

**How (keep it simple, phone-first):**
1. Quiet-ish space; phone or laptop camera (or audio-only if the learner prefers early on).
2. Coach/learner states a **slate**: learner ID + date + "Day 1" or "Graduation" (helps organization).
3. Read the **fixed prompt**; learner responds naturally (~1–3 min at Stage 0; longer at higher stages).
4. **One take, minimal pressure** — this is a baseline, not a performance. Reassure (affective filter).
5. Save immediately with the standard filename (§5) and log the link in the Cohort Tracker.

**Format:** video preferred (more compelling proof + shows confidence/body language); audio acceptable
if the learner is camera-shy early. Aim to match format across before/after for a clean comparison.

---

## 4. Consent (ethical, non-negotiable)

We **never** publish or share a learner's clip/quote without explicit, informed, opt-in consent
(brand foundations §10). Consent is **layered** so a learner can say yes to measurement but no to marketing.

**Consent tiers (learner chooses):**
- **Tier 0 — Internal only:** clip used only for the learner's own measurement/feedback. *(Default; always allowed.)*
- **Tier 1 — Community:** may be shared inside the private EEC community (e.g., celebration).
- **Tier 2 — Marketing:** may be used publicly (social, sales pages, ads), with/without name as specified.

**Consent record captures:** learner name + ID, date, tier granted, whether name/face may be shown,
channels allowed, and the right to **withdraw** at any time (with what happens on withdrawal). Store the
signed/*digital* consent; mark `consent_to_use` in the Cohort Tracker (extend to note the tier).

**Rules:**
- Minors: not in the beachhead; if ever relevant, require guardian consent + stricter handling (later phase).
- Withdrawal: on request, stop future use and remove from active assets where feasible.
- Never imply a result is typical if it isn't — show representative outcomes + the effort behind them (honesty, GC-5).

## 5. Storage & organization

**Where:** a structured cloud drive (e.g., Google Drive), access-restricted to the EEC team.

**Folder structure:**
```
/EEC-Samples/
  /CohortC1/
    /EEC-001/
      EEC-001_2026-08-01_day1.mp4
      EEC-001_2026-10-15_graduation.mp4
      EEC-001_consent.pdf
    /EEC-002/ ...
  /CohortC2/ ...
```
**Filename convention:** `{learnerID}_{YYYY-MM-DD}_{day1|graduation|unitNN}.{ext}`

**Linking:** paste the file links into the Cohort Tracker fields `day1_sample_link` and
`graduation_sample_link` (Task 0.5). Keep raw files private; only *approved, consented* edits get published.

**Privacy/security:** restrict sharing to the team; don't store more PII than needed; honor withdrawal;
review access when team changes.

---

## 6. Producing the paired proof clip (for Tier-2 consented learners)

1. Trim both clips to the strongest comparable ~20–40 seconds.
2. Layout: **Day 1** then **Now** (label clearly with dates/CEFR level, e.g., "Day 1 — beginner" → "10 weeks — A1").
3. Keep it **honest**: same prompt, real audio, note the timeframe and that it took consistent effort.
4. Add brand-compliant framing (brand §8): clean template, EEC mark, subtitles (Arabic + English) for reach.
5. Get the learner's **final approval** on the edit before publishing (respect + trust).

**Usage:** flagship social content, sales-page proof, ad creative, and community celebration — feeding
the funnel (content engine, Task 2.1/2.2) and lowering CAC (the proof flywheel, strategy §13).
