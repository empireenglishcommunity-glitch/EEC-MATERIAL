> for each new lesson. Part B is a **fully worked Stage-0 example** so authors see exactly how to fill it.
> Consistency here is what makes the whole curriculum feel like one coherent system.

---

## How to use

1. Copy **Part A** into a new file per lesson (suggested path: `curriculum/stageN/unitX/lesson-YY.md`).
2. Fill every field. If a field is "n/a," say why.
3. Obey the **design checklist** at the end — a lesson isn't done until every box is true.
4. Keep each lesson to one Learning Loop and ~20–40 minutes of learner time.

# PART B — WORKED EXAMPLE (Stage 0)

*This shows the template filled for a real beginner lesson. Use it as the quality/style benchmark.*

## 1. Lesson metadata
- **Lesson ID:** S0-U1-L02
- **Stage / CEFR level:** Stage 0 — Foundations / Pre-A1→A1
- **Unit / theme:** Unit 1 — Introducing Yourself
- **Lesson title:** "Hello, I'm… — Saying who you are"
- **Target duration:** ~30 min self-paced; practiced again in the weekly live speaking session
- **Format:** recorded lesson + live reinforcement
- **Prerequisites:** S0-U1-L01 (greetings: hello/hi/good morning; the alphabet & sound intro)

## 2. Objectives (can-do statements)
- By the end, the learner can **introduce themselves** with name, country, and job in simple sentences.
- By the end, the learner can **ask a person's name** and respond ("What's your name?" / "I'm…").
- By the end, the learner can **produce their first spoken self-introduction** on video.

## 3. Target language
- **Vocabulary (new):** name, country, job, from; a few jobs (teacher, engineer, student, doctor);
  a few countries (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE).
- **Grammar / structure (target):** verb **to be** (I am / I'm; you are / you're); "My name is…";
  "I'm from…"; "I'm a/an + job."
- **Function(s):** introducing yourself; asking and giving personal information.
- **Accent Lab focus:** the **/aɪ/** sound in "I" and the clear **/m/** in "I'm"; light stress on the
  key word ("I'm a **teacher**").

## 4. Spaced-review carry-forward
- **Review items:** greetings from L01 (hello / good morning); the letters practiced in L01.
- **New items added to queue:** to-be (I'm / you're), "I'm from…", jobs vocabulary.

## 5. Arabic-support level
- **Explanation language:** ~70% Arabic (concepts and instructions explained in Arabic to reduce fear).
- **Instruction language:** mostly Arabic with English models; learner **outputs in English**.

## 6. Materials & assets
- Input asset: 60–90s video of 3 short self-introductions (varied people; American-accent models).
- Slides: sentence frames ("My name is ___." "I'm from ___." "I'm a ___.").
- Accent Lab model audio: "I / I'm / I'm a teacher" (slow + natural).
- Worksheet: fill-the-frame + a 3-question mini-quiz (Google Form).
- Recording prompt: "Introduce yourself in 3 sentences."

## 7. Lesson flow (the Learning Loop)

| # | Phase | Content for this lesson | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | **Warm-up / recall** | Learner recalls greetings from L01 out loud; quick "say hello 3 ways" | 3 min |
| 2 | **Input** | Watch 3 short self-introductions; meaning supported with on-screen text + Arabic gloss | 7 min |
| 3 | **Notice** | Highlight "My name is… / I'm from… / I'm a…"; brief Arabic explanation of *to be* | 5 min |
| 4 | **Accent Lab** | Drill "I → I'm → I'm a teacher"; minimal focus on /aɪ/ + /m/; record-and-compare | 4 min |
| 5 | **Guided output** | Fill sentence frames aloud with own info; substitution drill (swap country/job) | 6 min |
| 6 | **Free output** | Learner records a 3-sentence self-introduction (their real info) | 3 min |
| 7 | **Feedback & self-record** | Compare to model; note 1 thing to improve; keep the recording as Day-1-style sample | 2 min |
| 8 | **Assignment + queue** | Homework: introduce yourself to one real person (or in the community channel) | 1 min |

## 8. Differentiation
- **Support:** provide the full written frames + audio model; allow listening again before recording.
- **Stretch:** add a 4th sentence ("I live in…") and ask a partner a question in the live session.

## 9. Assessment / success check
- **Success:** learner produces 3 correct, intelligible self-intro sentences (rubric: Accuracy +
  Pronunciation + Fluency at A1 threshold).
- **Formative check:** 3-question form (match to-be forms; choose correct frame).

## 10. Homework & review-queue additions
- **Assignment:** post a 20–30s self-introduction voice note in the cohort WhatsApp/community.
- **Added to queue:** to-be, "I'm from…", jobs → return in L03 warm-up, then ~3 days, then ~1 week.

## 11. Coach / facilitator notes
- **Expected Arabic-transfer errors:** dropping "am/is" ("I teacher"), /p/–/b/ if a job has /p/,
  adding a vowel before clusters. Correct the **target** (to-be) now; note others for later (Handbook §11).
- **Live tie-in:** in the weekly session, learners introduce themselves in pairs (breakout rooms), then
  a few share to the group; coach gives group-level delayed feedback.
