## 2. Placement quiz — item bank + scoring

**Purpose:** assign a starting level. ~15 items, ordered easy→hard (A1→B1). Pair with the 2–3 min
speaking sample (Assessment Suite §3); **the speaking sample overrides the quiz** on disagreement.

**Scoring → provisional level (from Assessment Suite §3):** 0–30% → Pre-A1/A1 · 31–55% → high-A1/A2 ·
56–75% → A2/B1 · 76%+ → B1+.

**A1-level items**
1. "Hello, ___ name is Omar." → a) my ✅ b) me c) I
2. "She ___ a teacher." → a) am b) is ✅ c) are
3. "I ___ from Egypt." → a) is b) are c) am ✅
4. "There ___ two books on the table." → a) is b) are ✅ c) am
5. "___ you like coffee?" → a) Do ✅ b) Are c) Is
6. Choose the plural: "one box, two ___" → a) boxs b) boxes ✅ c) box
7. "He ___ to work every day." → a) go b) goes ✅ c) going

**A2-level items**
8. "Yesterday I ___ to the market." → a) go b) going c) went ✅
9. "She is ___ than her brother." → a) tall b) taller ✅ c) tallest
10. "I ___ TV when you called." → a) watched b) was watching ✅ c) watch
11. "There isn't ___ milk." → a) some b) any ✅ c) a
12. "You ___ smoke here (it's not allowed)." → a) mustn't ✅ b) don't have c) can

**B1-level items**
13. "If it rains, we ___ at home." → a) stay b) will stay ✅ c) stayed
14. "I've lived here ___ 2019." → a) since ✅ b) for c) ago
15. "He said he ___ tired." → a) is b) was ✅ c) will be

*(Add a couple more B1/B2 items later to sharpen the top band. Keep as single-answer multiple choice.)*

## 3. Per-unit formative quizzes — Stage 0

Low-stakes checks (feedback + spaced review, not gatekeeping). ~4 items each; pair with the unit's L05
speaking task. ✅ = correct.

### Unit 0 — Welcome & Sounds
1. Which word has the /p/ sound? a) big b) **pen ✅** c) van
2. "Can you repeat, please?" is used to… a) **ask someone to say it again ✅** b) say goodbye c) say thanks
3. The number after "twelve" is… a) **thirteen ✅** b) thirty c) twenty
4. Which is the /v/ sound? a) fan b) **very ✅** c) ban

### Unit 1 — Introducing Yourself
1. "___ your name?" a) **What's ✅** b) Where c) Who
2. "I'm ___ engineer." a) a b) **an ✅** c) the
3. "Where are you from?" → "I'm ___ Cairo." a) in b) **from ✅** c) at
4. "This is Sara. ___ is a doctor." a) He b) **She ✅** c) It

### Unit 2 — Family & People
1. "This is my brother. ___ name is Ali." a) Her b) **His ✅** c) Your
2. "I ___ two sisters." a) **have ✅** b) has c) am
3. "She ___ a big family." a) have b) **has ✅** c) is
4. "How old are you?" → "I'm twenty-five ___ old." a) year b) **years ✅** c) age

### Unit 3 — My Things & Places
1. "There ___ three chairs." a) is b) **are ✅** c) am
2. "The keys are ___ the table." a) **on ✅** b) in c) under (picture-dependent — accept per image)
3. Plural of "watch": a) watchs b) **watches ✅** c) watch
4. "It's ___ apple." a) a b) **an ✅** c) the

### Unit 4 — Daily Routine
1. "She ___ up at 7." a) get b) **gets ✅** c) getting
2. "What time ___ you start work?" a) **do ✅** b) does c) are
3. "I ___ drink coffee (100% of the time)." a) never b) **always ✅** c) sometimes
4. "It's 7:30 = half past ___." a) six b) **seven ✅** c) eight

### Unit 5 — Free Time & Ability
1. "I like ___." a) **swimming ✅** b) swim c) to swimming
2. "She ___ drive." a) can ✅ b) cans c) is can  → **a ✅**
3. "___ you like football?" a) Do ✅ b) Are c) Does → **a ✅**
4. "I ___ play the guitar (not able)." a) don't b) **can't ✅** c) not

### Unit 6 — Food & Shopping
1. "I'd ___ a tea, please." a) **like ✅** b) want c) liking
2. "There isn't ___ sugar." a) some b) **any ✅** c) a
3. "___ is it?" (price) a) **How much ✅** b) How many c) What time
4. "Can I ___ the menu, please?" a) **have ✅** b) has c) having

### Unit 7 — Describing People & Things
1. Correct order: a) **a big red car ✅** b) a car big red c) a red car big
2. "She ___ got long hair." a) **has ✅** b) have c) is
3. "It's ___ (color of the sky)." a) **blue ✅** b) blue color c) a blue
4. "He is ___ (not short)." a) **tall ✅** b) taller c) tallest

### Unit 8 — Places & Directions
1. "___ a bank near here?" a) **Is there ✅** b) There is c) Are there
2. "Turn ___." (opposite of right) a) **left ✅** b) straight c) up
3. "Go ___ the street." a) **along ✅** b) in c) at
4. "The pharmacy is ___ to the bank." a) **next ✅** b) on c) under

### Unit 9 — Happening Now
1. "Right now, I ___ studying." a) am ✅ b) is c) are → **a ✅**
2. "-ing of 'run'": a) runing b) **running ✅** c) runn
3. "She ___ TV now." a) watch b) **is watching ✅** c) watches
4. "I ___ usually work on Friday, but today I ___ working." a) **don't / am ✅** b) am / don't c) not / am

### Unit 10 — Putting It Together (mixed review)
1. "I'm from Egypt and I ___ a student." a) **am ✅** b) is c) are
2. "He ___ up at 6 and ___ to work." a) **gets / goes ✅** b) get / go c) getting / going
3. "There ___ a bed and two windows in my room." a) **is ✅** b) are c) am
4. "I like ___ and I can ___." a) **cooking / swim ✅** b) cook / swimming c) to cook / swimming
