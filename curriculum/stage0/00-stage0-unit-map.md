# Stage 0 — Foundations: Unit Map (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.3 (this is the map; lessons follow) · **Requirements:** R2, R1
> **Reads with:** `curriculum/01-cefr-roadmap.md`, `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`,
> `methodology/01-eec-methodology-handbook.md`
> **Purpose:** The complete structure of Stage 0 (Foundations, Pre-A1→A1) — the level the founding
> cohort receives first. This map defines the units, their objectives, and the lessons within each,
> so lessons can be authored consistently and reviewed as a whole before full build-out.

---

## 1. Stage 0 overview

- **CEFR:** Pre-A1 → A1 · **EEC label:** Foundations
- **Guided-hour budget:** ~80–100 hrs · **Cohort length:** ~10–14 weeks @ ~7 hrs/week
- **Big promise of this stage:** *"You will speak your first real English sentences and survive basic
  everyday interactions — and you'll have it on video."*
- **Arabic support:** heavy (~70%), fading slightly by the last units.
- **Vocabulary target:** ~500–800 high-frequency words.
- **Skill weighting:** speaking ●●● · listening ●●● · reading ● · writing ●.

### Structure
- **1 orientation module (Unit 0)** + **10 content units** + **1 consolidation/assessment unit**.
- **~5 lessons per unit** (≈ 55–60 lessons total), each ~20–40 min, built on the Lesson Template.
- **Weekly rhythm:** ~1 unit/week; recorded lessons during the week + 1–2 live speaking sessions.

### Sequencing logic
Sounds & survival first (confidence + the ear) → identity (to be) → immediate world (family, objects,
places) → daily life (routines, time) → preferences & ability → needs (food, shopping) → description →
getting around → "happening now" → consolidation. Grammar stays **present-focused** (past tense is
Stage 1/A2). Every unit adds to the **spaced-review queue** and recycles earlier units in warm-ups.

## 3. Unit detail + lesson lists

> Each lesson is authored with `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`. Lesson IDs: `S0-U{n}-L{nn}`.
> Objectives are the "By the end, the learner can…" for that lesson.

### Unit 0 — Welcome & Sounds
- **Can-do (unit):** understand classroom instructions; recognize and begin producing key English sounds
  Arabic speakers find hard; say basic survival phrases.
- **Vocab:** classroom words (listen, repeat, say, again), survival phrases (yes/no, please/thank you,
  I don't understand, how do you say…?), the alphabet.
- **Grammar/function:** none formal; imperatives for classroom use; polite basics.
- **Accent Lab:** English vowel system overview; /p/–/b/, /v/–/f/, hard /g/ (foundational contrasts).
- **Mindset:** identity + how EEC works + the promise + how to practice.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U0-L01 — Welcome & how this journey works (orientation + mindset kickoff; set your goal)
  - S0-U0-L02 — The English alphabet & letter sounds
  - S0-U0-L03 — Sounds Arabic speakers must master (1): /p/ vs /b/, /v/ vs /f/
  - S0-U0-L04 — Survival phrases: staying in the conversation ("Sorry?", "How do you say…?")
  - S0-U0-L05 — Numbers 0–20 & saying them clearly

### Unit 1 — Introducing Yourself
- **Can-do:** introduce yourself (name, country, job); ask someone's name; greet appropriately.
- **Vocab:** greetings, name/country/job, common jobs, common countries.
- **Grammar/function:** *to be* (I'm/you're); "My name is…"; "I'm from…"; "I'm a/an…".
- **Accent Lab:** /aɪ/ in "I", clear /m/ in "I'm"; light content-word stress.
- **Mindset:** the daily practice habit; consistency > intensity.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U1-L01 — Greetings & "How are you?"
  - S0-U1-L02 — "Hello, I'm…" — saying who you are *(worked example in the Lesson Template)*
  - S0-U1-L03 — "Where are you from?" — countries & nationalities (basic)
  - S0-U1-L04 — "What do you do?" — jobs
  - S0-U1-L05 — Unit 1 speaking task: introduce yourself (record) + review

### Unit 2 — Family & People
- **Can-do:** talk about your family; say who people are; use this/that.
- **Vocab:** family members, people, numbers 20–100 (for ages).
- **Grammar/function:** possessive adjectives (my/your/his/her); 's; this/that/these/those; have/has.
- **Accent Lab:** /θ/–/ð/ (this/these/mother); plural/possessive -s sounds.
- **Mindset:** overcoming fear/embarrassment of speaking.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U2-L01 — My family (family vocab + my/your)
  - S0-U2-L02 — "This is my…" — introducing people; his/her
  - S0-U2-L03 — Have/has: "I have two brothers"
  - S0-U2-L04 — Ages & numbers 20–100
  - S0-U2-L05 — Unit speaking task: describe your family (record) + review

### Unit 3 — My Things & Places
- **Can-do:** name everyday objects; say what there is in a room/place; locate things.
- **Vocab:** common objects, rooms, places in a town.
- **Grammar/function:** there is/are; a/an/the; regular plurals; prepositions of place (in/on/under/next to).
- **Accent Lab:** consonant clusters without vowel insertion (desk, book**s**, next).
- **Mindset:** designing a practice-friendly environment.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U3-L01 — Everyday objects & a/an
  - S0-U3-L02 — Plurals (and their sounds)
  - S0-U3-L03 — "There is / There are"
  - S0-U3-L04 — Where is it? Prepositions of place
  - S0-U3-L05 — Unit speaking task: describe your room (record) + review

### Unit 4 — Daily Routine
- **Can-do:** describe your daily routine; tell the time; say how often you do things.
- **Vocab:** daily activities, times of day, days of the week.
- **Grammar/function:** present simple (I/you/we/they + he/she/it); telling time; frequency adverbs.
- **Accent Lab:** 3rd-person -s endings (/s/,/z/,/ɪz/); intro to sentence rhythm/stress.
- **Mindset:** tracking practice; recovering after a missed day (no guilt spiral).
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U4-L01 — My day: daily activities (present simple I/you)
  - S0-U4-L02 — He/she + verb-s ("She works…")
  - S0-U4-L03 — What time is it? Telling the time
  - S0-U4-L04 — How often? always/usually/sometimes/never
  - S0-U4-L05 — Unit speaking task: describe your typical day (record) + review

### Unit 5 — Free Time & Ability
- **Can-do:** talk about hobbies and likes/dislikes; say what you can/can't do.
- **Vocab:** hobbies, free-time activities, sports.
- **Grammar/function:** like/love/hate + -ing; can/can't for ability; do you like…?
- **Accent Lab:** linking sounds; stressing content words in a sentence.
- **Mindset:** building a personal English input diet (music/shows/podcasts at level).
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U5-L01 — "I like…" likes & dislikes (+ -ing)
  - S0-U5-L02 — Hobbies & free-time vocab
  - S0-U5-L03 — "Can you…?" ability
  - S0-U5-L04 — Asking questions: "Do you like…?" mini-conversations
  - S0-U5-L05 — Unit speaking task: talk about your free time (record) + review

### Unit 6 — Food & Shopping
- **Can-do:** talk about food; order and ask prices; make simple requests politely.
- **Vocab:** food & drink, shops, money/prices.
- **Grammar/function:** some/any; would like; countable/uncountable (intro); how much/how many.
- **Accent Lab:** clear numbers/prices; polite rising intonation for requests.
- **Mindset:** getting real-world speaking reps (order in English, use it outside class).
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U6-L01 — Food & drink vocab; I like/eat…
  - S0-U6-L02 — some/any; countable vs uncountable (intro)
  - S0-U6-L03 — "I'd like…" ordering & requests
  - S0-U6-L04 — Prices & shopping: "How much is it?"
  - S0-U6-L05 — Unit speaking task: role-play a café/shop (record) + review

### Unit 7 — Describing People & Things
- **Can-do:** describe appearance and objects using adjectives; say what you have got.
- **Vocab:** adjectives (size/colour/quality), appearance words.
- **Grammar/function:** adjective + noun order; have got; colours.
- **Accent Lab:** adjective stress; /w/ and /r/ sounds.
- **Mindset:** progress mindset — noticing and celebrating small wins.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U7-L01 — Colours & basic adjectives
  - S0-U7-L02 — Describing things (adjective + noun)
  - S0-U7-L03 — Describing people's appearance
  - S0-U7-L04 — have got: "I've got brown eyes"
  - S0-U7-L05 — Unit speaking task: describe a person you know (record) + review

### Unit 8 — Places & Directions
- **Can-do:** ask for and give simple directions; talk about places in a town.
- **Vocab:** places in town, direction words.
- **Grammar/function:** imperatives (go/turn); Is there a…? prepositions of movement (to/into/across).
- **Accent Lab:** intonation of instructions; clear vowels in direction words.
- **Mindset:** confidence in real interactions (asking strangers, being okay with mistakes).
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U8-L01 — Places in town
  - S0-U8-L02 — "Is there a…near here?"
  - S0-U8-L03 — Giving directions (imperatives)
  - S0-U8-L04 — Following & giving a route (prepositions of movement)
  - S0-U8-L05 — Unit speaking task: give directions (record) + review

### Unit 9 — Happening Now
- **Can-do:** describe what is happening now; contrast "now" vs "usually".
- **Vocab:** action verbs, clothes (common context for "wearing").
- **Grammar/function:** present continuous (now); brief contrast with present simple.
- **Accent Lab:** -ing endings; stress contrast (now vs usually).
- **Mindset:** thinking in English — reducing in-head translation.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U9-L01 — "I'm …-ing" present continuous (now)
  - S0-U9-L02 — What are they doing? (describing pictures/scenes)
  - S0-U9-L03 — now vs usually (present continuous vs present simple, intro)
  - S0-U9-L04 — What are you wearing? (clothes + continuous)
  - S0-U9-L05 — Unit speaking task: describe what's happening (record) + review

### Unit 10 — Putting It Together (Consolidation & Assessment)
- **Can-do:** hold a simple multi-topic self-introduction + Q&A; demonstrate A1 exit criteria.
- **Grammar/function:** integrated review of all Stage-0 language.
- **Accent Lab:** overall clarity + self-monitoring; revisit each learner's weakest sounds.
- **Mindset:** from learner to speaker; celebrating the transformation; readiness for A2.
- **Lessons:**
  - S0-U10-L01 — Big review 1: identity, family, things, routine (retrieval-based)
  - S0-U10-L02 — Big review 2: free time, food, description, directions, now
  - S0-U10-L03 — Accent clinic: fix your top 3 sounds
  - S0-U10-L04 — Practice conversation: the A1 exchange (mock)
  - S0-U10-L05 — **Stage-0 summative assessment** + graduation "after" speaking sample

## 4. Assessment placement (links to Task 1.6)

- **Before Unit 0:** placement test + **Day-1 speaking sample** (baseline proof).
- **End of each unit:** short formative check (quiz + speaking task) — feedback, not gatekeeping.
- **Unit 10, L05:** **summative** (quiz + speaking task on the rubric) → A1 determination; record the
  **graduation "after" sample** and pair with Day-1 for transformation proof.

## 5. Spaced-review threading

- Every unit's warm-ups retrieve prior units (e.g., Unit 4 warm-ups recycle to-be, family, there is/are).
- New target language enters the review queue and returns at ~next lesson → ~3 days → ~1 week → later units.
- Unit 10 is deliberately retrieval-heavy (the testing effect consolidates the whole stage).

---

## 6. Build order (recommendation)

Build in teaching order: **Unit 0 → Unit 1 → …** so the founding cohort can start after Units 0–2 are
ready, with later units completed just ahead of the cohort (design §3.4). Author every lesson with the
Lesson Template; keep an eye on the design checklist each time.
