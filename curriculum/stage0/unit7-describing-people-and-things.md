# Stage 0 · Unit 7 — Describing People & Things (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.3 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage0/00-stage0-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~35% (fading), instructions/explanations in Egyptian Arabic; learner outputs in English.
> **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can describe things and people using adjectives and *have got* —
> and can record a description of a person they know.
> **Grammar spine:** adjective + noun order · colours · *have got* (for features/possessions) ·
> **Accent Lab:** adjective stress + the /w/ and /r/ sounds · **Mindset:** progress mindset — noticing
> and celebrating small wins.

**Unit lessons:** L01 Colours & basic adjectives · L02 Describing things (adjective + noun) ·
L03 Describing people's appearance · L04 have got ("I've got brown eyes") · L05 Unit speaking task:
describe a person you know + review.

**Assets to produce:** colour + adjective flashcards, object/person images, a "describe someone" clip
(American model), /w/ and /r/ minimal audio, have got frames, a Google Form per lesson, community prompts.

## S0-U7-L01 — Colours & Basic Adjectives

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 7 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Unit 6.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can name colours and basic adjectives (big/small, old/new, good/bad, nice, beautiful, tall/short).
- The learner can use *"It's + adjective"* (*"It's red. It's big."*).
- The learner can ask *"What colour is it?"*

**Target language:** colours; basic opposite adjectives; *"It's ___." / "What colour is it?"*
**Grammar:** adjective after *to be*. **Accent Lab focus:** /r/ in *red/brown/orange*; /w/ in *white/yellow*.
**Arabic support:** ~35%. **Mindset focus:** notice a small win from last week — name it out loud.

**Materials:** colour + adjective flashcards; objects to describe; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: order something politely (U6) + one object (U3). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "It's a red car. It's big and new."; text + Arabic gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Colours + opposite adjectives; *"It's + adjective"*; *"What colour is it?"* | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | /r/ (red, brown) vs /w/ (white, yellow) — lip rounding for /w/, no roll for /r/. | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe flashcards: *"It's + colour + adjective."*; ask colours. | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: describe 3 objects around you (colour + adjective); record one. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check adjective use + /r/,/w/; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a voice note: describe 3 things by colour + one adjective. Colour/adjective quiz form. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — colour only ("It's blue"). Stretch — colour + 2 adjectives.
**Success check:** learner uses ≥3 colours/adjectives intelligibly. **Queue:** colours/adjectives feed L02–L05.
**Coach notes:** /r/ vs /w/ can blur for Arabic speakers on some words — the rounding cue for /w/ helps. Keep it light and visual.

## S0-U7-L02 — Describing Things (Adjective + Noun)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 7 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can put adjectives **before** the noun (*"a big house", "a beautiful car"*) — key word-order fix.
- The learner can describe objects with 2 adjectives (*"a small old phone"*).
- The learner can compare simply (*"This one is better/nicer."* — basic, optional).

**Target language:** adjective + noun phrases; a/an + adjective + noun. **Grammar:** adjective **before** noun
(English order, unlike Arabic). **Accent Lab focus:** stress the noun; smooth adjective+noun linking.
**Arabic support:** ~35%. **Mindset focus:** progress is built one small correct sentence at a time.

**Materials:** object images; adjective+noun cards; frames slide; Google Form (word-order fix).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: colours/adjectives from L01. | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "It's a beautiful old house. I have a new black bag."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | **Adjective goes BEFORE the noun** in English (contrast with Arabic order); explain (Arabic) clearly. | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the noun (*"a big HOUSE"*); link adjective+noun smoothly. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build phrases from images: *"a + adjective + noun"*; then 2 adjectives. | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: describe 3 of your things with adjective + noun; record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Target adjective–noun order (key error) + noun stress. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: describe 3 things using adjective + noun. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — one adjective + noun. Stretch — 2 adjectives + a simple comparison.
**Success check:** correct adjective–noun order in ≥3 phrases. **Queue:** adjective+noun recycles in L03–L05.
**Coach notes:** Arabic places the adjective *after* the noun, so expect "a house big". This is a signature word-order fix — drill it explicitly and warmly; it recurs at every level.

## S0-U7-L03 — Describing People's Appearance

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 7 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe appearance (tall/short, young/old, hair and eye words).
- The learner can use *He/She is + adjective* and describe height/build.
- The learner can ask *"What does he/she look like?"* (basic).

**Target language:** appearance adjectives; hair (long/short/black/blonde), height/build; *"He/She is ___."*
**Grammar:** to be + adjective for people; recycle he/she. **Accent Lab focus:** stress in descriptive
sentences; clear /r/ in *short/hair*.
**Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** celebrate: you can now paint a picture of a person in English.

**Materials:** people images (varied); appearance flashcards; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: adjective + noun phrases (L02). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "She is tall. She has long black hair."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Appearance adjectives; *He/She is + adjective*; preview *has/have got hair* (→ L04). | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Sentence stress on key describing words; /r/ in *short/hair*. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe people in images: height + one feature. | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: describe a famous person or a friend (2–3 sentences); record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check to-be + adjective + he/she; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: describe a person in 2–3 sentences (appearance). | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — height + one adjective. Stretch — 4-sentence portrait (height, build, hair, eyes).
**Success check:** learner describes a person in ≥2 sentences intelligibly. **Queue:** appearance feeds L04 (have got) + L05.
**Coach notes:** keep descriptions respectful and neutral. Model varied, positive descriptions.

## S0-U7-L04 — have got: "I've Got Brown Eyes"

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 7 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use **have got / has got** for features and possessions (*"I've got brown eyes. She's got a car."*).
- The learner can use contractions (I've got / she's got).
- The learner can ask *"Have you got…?"* and answer (*"Yes, I have. / No, I haven't."*).

**Target language:** have got/has got + features/possessions; Have you got…? **Grammar:** have got
(= have) — contractions + questions. **Accent Lab focus:** contractions *I've /aɪv/, she's /ʃiːz/*; linking.
**Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** two ways to say "have" — you're gaining flexibility.

**Materials:** features flashcards; have got frames; contraction audio; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: describe a person's appearance (L03) + have/has from U2. | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I've got green eyes. He's got short hair. Have you got a pet?"; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | have got/has got (= have/has); contractions; questions Have you got…?; explain (Arabic) it equals have. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Contractions: *I've got, she's got*; don't drop the /v/ /z/; link "got a". | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe features: *"I've got ___. My friend has got ___."* | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: ask *"Have you got…?"* (a car/pet/brother); answer; record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check have got/has got + contractions; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: describe 2 of your features with have got. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — "I've got…" only. Stretch — mix I've/he's got + a question.
**Success check:** correct have got/has got in ≥3 statements. **Queue:** have got recycles into U10 review.
**Coach notes:** link back to U2 have/has — reassure learners that "have" and "have got" mean the same for possession; the contraction /v/ often gets dropped, so drill it.

## S0-U7-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: Describe a Person You Know (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 7 · ~30 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe a person they know in 6–8 connected sentences (appearance + features + a like).
- The learner can ask another person to describe someone.
- The learner records a **"describe someone I know"** piece (portfolio proof).

**Target language:** integrated Unit-7 language. **Grammar:** adjective+noun order, to be + adjective, have got consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** adjective–noun order + /r/,/w/ + contractions; each learner's flagged weak sound.
**Arabic support:** ~30%. **Mindset focus:** notice your growth — a full portrait in English, from zero 8 weeks ago.

**Materials:** a model person-description; a checklist card; recording prompt; Unit-7 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Chain recall: colours/adjectives, adjective+noun order, appearance, have got. | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a model description of a person (6–7 sentences). | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break into learned frames; build the "good person description" checklist. | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their weakest sound flagged in U7 (often /r/,/w/ or adjective order). | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the full description with the card; peer practice (pairs). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record: describe a person you know (appearance + features + one thing they like). | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level delayed feedback. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the description; complete Unit-7 quiz; celebrate progress. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 6 sentences with the card. Stretch — describe two people and compare them simply.
**Success check (Unit-7 summary):** connected 6–8 sentence person description, intelligible, correct adjective order + have got (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A1). **Queue:** person description recycles into Unit 10 review.
**Coach notes:** ~8 weeks in — descriptive fluency is a clear, visible gain. This clip pairs well with the Day-1 sample; log with consent.
