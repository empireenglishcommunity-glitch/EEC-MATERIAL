# Stage 0 · Unit 3 — My Things & Places (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.3 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage0/00-stage0-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~55% (fading), instructions/explanations in Egyptian Arabic; learner outputs in English.
> **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can name everyday objects, say what there is in a room or place,
> and locate things — and can describe their own room in a short recorded piece.
> **Grammar spine:** a/an/the · regular plurals · there is / there are · prepositions of place
> (in/on/under/next to/behind/in front of) · **Accent Lab:** consonant clusters *without* vowel insertion
> (desk, books, next, chairs) · **Mindset:** designing a practice-friendly environment.

**Unit lessons:** L01 Everyday objects & a/an · L02 Plurals (and their sounds) · L03 There is / There are ·
L04 Where is it? Prepositions of place · L05 Unit speaking task: describe your room + review.

**Assets to produce:** object flashcards, a labelled "room" image, a "my room" clip (American model),
consonant-cluster audio, prepositions visual, a Google Form per lesson, community voice-note prompts.

## S0-U3-L01 — Everyday Objects & a/an

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Unit 2.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can name common everyday objects (book, pen, phone, table, chair, bag, key, cup…).
- The learner can use **a/an** correctly with singular objects.
- The learner can ask *"What's this?"* and answer *"It's a/an ___."*

**Target language:** everyday objects; *"What's this?" / "It's a/an ___."* **Grammar:** a (consonant sound) / an (vowel sound); it's.
**Accent Lab focus:** final consonants pronounced clearly (boo**k**, ba**g**, cu**p**) — no added vowel.
**Arabic support:** ~55%. **Mindset focus:** set up a small "English corner" at home (environment cues).

**Materials:** object flashcards; a/an rule card (revisit from U1-L04); clip; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: a/an + jobs from U1-L04 ("I'm an engineer"). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: someone shows objects — "This is a pen. It's a phone."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Object words; *"What's this?" → "It's a/an ___."*; re-explain a vs an (vowel sound). | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Clear final consonants: book/bag/cup/key — say the last sound, no "book-uh". | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Flashcard drill: *"What's this?" "It's a ___."*; include an-words (an apple, an umbrella). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: hold up 3 real objects near you and name them; record one. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check a/an + clear final consonants; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a voice note naming 5 objects on your desk (*"It's a ___."*). Objects quiz form. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 3 objects with *"a"* only. Stretch — mix a/an with 6 objects.
**Success check:** learner names ≥5 objects with correct a/an. **Queue:** objects + a/an recycle in L02–L05.
**Coach notes:** Arabic speakers may add a vowel after final consonants ("booku") — pair this with the cluster work in L02. Praise clean endings.

## S0-U3-L02 — Plurals (and Their Sounds)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form regular plurals (book→books, box→boxes) and a few common irregulars (man/men, child/children).
- The learner can pronounce plural -s as /s/, /z/, /ɪz/ correctly.
- The learner can say how many objects there are (*"two books, three chairs"*).

**Target language:** plurals of Unit-3 objects; numbers + plural nouns. **Grammar:** regular plural rules (+s, +es); key irregulars.
**Accent Lab focus:** the three plural sounds — book**s** /s/, pen**s** /z/, box**es** /ɪz/ — and **consonant clusters** (desks, books) without inserted vowels.
**Arabic support:** ~55%. **Mindset focus:** tiny daily reps beat one long session.

**Materials:** plural-sound audio (3 groups); cluster words audio; number+object cards; Google Form (choose the -s sound).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: name 3 objects with a/an (L01). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Listen: "one book, two books; one box, two boxes"; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | The +s / +es rule; the 3 sounds (/s/,/z/,/ɪz/); a couple of irregulars (man/men). | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Sort words by -s sound; drill clusters: desk**s**, book**s**, chair**s** — no "deskuh". | 6 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Number + plural: *"two pens, three books, four boxes."* | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: count objects around you (*"I have two ___ and three ___."*); record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check plural sounds + no vowel insertion; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: count 5 things around you in plural. Plural-sound quiz form. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — regular +s only. Stretch — add box/boxes (/ɪz/) + one irregular.
**Success check:** correct plural forms with intelligible -s sounds. **Queue:** plurals feed there are (L03) and U6 (food quantities).
**Coach notes:** vowel-insertion in clusters is a signature Arabic-transfer error — this is the unit's core Accent Lab win. Use the "say the cluster, don't break it" cue.

## S0-U3-L03 — There Is / There Are

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can say what exists in a place using **there is** (singular) and **there are** (plural).
- The learner can ask *"Is there a…?" / "Are there any…?"* (basic) and answer.
- The learner can describe a room's contents (*"There is a bed. There are two chairs."*).

**Target language:** there is/are; rooms & furniture (bed, table, chair, window, door, sofa).
**Grammar:** there is + singular / there are + plural; Is there…? Are there…?
**Accent Lab focus:** /ð/ in *there*; weak-form "there are" linking.
**Arabic support:** ~55%. **Mindset focus:** review your goal — is your daily habit sticking?

**Materials:** labelled room image; furniture flashcards; clip; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: plurals from L02 (say 3 plural nouns with clear -s). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip/description of a room: "There is a bed. There are two windows."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | there is (one) vs there are (many); questions Is there…?/Are there…?; explain (Arabic). | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | /ð/ in *there*; link "there are" naturally. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Look at the room image: make 5 sentences (there is/are); ask *"Is there a…?"* | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: describe your real room — 3 sentences; record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check is/are agreement + /ð/; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: 3 things in your room using there is/are. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — there is only (singular). Stretch — add questions + "there isn't/aren't".
**Success check:** correct there is/are agreement in ≥3 sentences. **Queue:** there is/are recycle in L04–L05 and U8 (places in town).
**Coach notes:** common error: "there is two chairs" — target agreement now; it recurs across the course.

## S0-U3-L04 — Where Is It? Prepositions of Place

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use prepositions of place (in, on, under, next to, behind, in front of, between).
- The learner can ask and answer *"Where is the ___?" / "It's on the ___."*
- The learner can locate objects in a room.

**Target language:** prepositions of place; *"Where is/are…?" / "It's/They're + preposition."*
**Grammar:** preposition phrases; it's/they're + location. **Accent Lab focus:** clear vowels in
*on/under*; linking "in front of".
**Arabic support:** ~50%. **Mindset focus:** speaking about your real space makes practice easy.

**Materials:** prepositions visual (a box + a ball in different positions); room image; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: there is/are about the room image (L03). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip/visual: "The keys are on the table. The bag is under the chair."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Present prepositions with the box+ball visual; *"Where is…?" → "It's ___."* | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Clear /ɒ/ in *on*, /ʌ/ in *under*; link "in-front-of". | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Ask/answer about the room image: *"Where is the lamp?" "It's next to the bed."* | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: ask where 3 real objects are in your space; answer + record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check preposition choice + clarity; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: say where 3 things are in your room (with prepositions). | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 3 prepositions (in/on/under). Stretch — add between / behind / in front of.
**Success check:** correct location answers for ≥3 objects. **Queue:** prepositions feed U8 (directions).
**Coach notes:** learners often overuse "in" — contrast in/on/under explicitly with the visual.

## S0-U3-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: Describe Your Room (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 3 · ~30 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe their room in 5–7 connected sentences (objects + there is/are + locations).
- The learner can ask another person about their room.
- The learner records a **"my room"** description (portfolio proof).

**Target language:** integrated Unit-3 language. **Grammar:** a/an + plurals + there is/are + prepositions consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** clusters + plural -s + /ð/ "there"; each learner's flagged weak sound.
**Arabic support:** ~50%. **Mindset focus:** compare to week 1 — you're describing a whole scene now.

**Materials:** a model "my room" description; a checklist card; recording prompt; Unit-3 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Chain recall: objects (a/an), plurals, there is/are, prepositions. | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a model "my room" description (6 sentences). | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break the model into learned frames; build the "good description" checklist. | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their one weakest sound flagged in U3 (usually a cluster or -s). | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the full room description with the card; peer practice (pairs). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the room description (objects + there is/are + where things are). | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level delayed feedback. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the room description; complete Unit-3 quiz; celebrate progress. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 5 sentences with the card. Stretch — add colours/adjectives (preview U7) and how many.
**Success check (Unit-3 summary):** connected 5–7 sentence room description, intelligible, correct there is/are + prepositions + plural -s (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A1). **Queue:** room description recycles into Unit 10 review.
**Coach notes:** By now the founding cohort is ~4 weeks in; this "my room" clip shows visible growth from the Day-1 sample. Log it (with consent) in the Cohort Tracker.
