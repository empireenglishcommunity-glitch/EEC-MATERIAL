# Stage 0 · Unit 1 — Introducing Yourself (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.3 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage0/00-stage0-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~70% (heavy), instructions/explanations in Egyptian Arabic; learner outputs in English.
> **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can fully introduce themselves in English — greet, give their
> name, country, and job, and ask a person for the same — and has it recorded on video.
> **Grammar spine:** verb **to be** (I'm / you're / he's / she's) · **Accent Lab:** /aɪ/ "I'm", word
> stress · **Mindset:** the daily habit; consistency > intensity.

**Unit lessons:** L01 Greetings & "How are you?" · L02 "Hello, I'm…" · L03 "Where are you from?" ·
L04 "What do you do?" · L05 Unit speaking task + review.

**Assets to produce:** dialogue videos (American models), sentence-frame slides, country/job flashcards,
minimal audio for /aɪ/ and stress, a Google Form per lesson, community voice-note prompts.

## S0-U1-L01 — Greetings & "How are you?"

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / Pre-A1→A1 · Unit 1 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Unit 0.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can greet at different times (hello / hi / good morning / afternoon / evening).
- The learner can ask *"How are you?"* and answer (*"I'm fine, thanks. And you?"*).
- The learner can say goodbye (bye / goodbye / see you).

**Target language:** hello, hi, good morning/afternoon/evening; how are you?; fine/good/great/not bad;
thanks; goodbye/bye/see you. **Grammar:** *to be* (I'm), fixed phrases.
**Accent Lab focus:** clear /h/ in *hello/how*; friendly **rising→falling** intonation.
**Arabic support:** ~70%. **Mindset focus:** the daily habit — pick your 20-minute slot.

**Materials:** greeting dialogue video; time-of-day graphic (morning/afternoon/evening); frames slide.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Recall Unit 0: say your name + one survival phrase. | 3 min |
| 2 | Input | Watch 2 short greeting dialogues; text + Arabic gloss; note *"How are you?"* | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Explain (Arabic) which greeting for which time; *"How are you?"* + answers. | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Drill /h/ (hello, how) — don't drop it; friendly intonation of *"How are you?"* | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Pair frames: A: *"Hi, how are you?"* B: *"I'm fine, thanks. And you?"* (swap roles) | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: greet 2 classmates properly for the current time of day; record one. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to model; check the /h/ isn't dropped. | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Greet someone in English today; post a voice note: greeting + *"How are you?"* + answer. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — one greeting (hi) + one answer (fine). Stretch — add *"Nice to meet you."*
**Success check:** learner performs a full greeting exchange intelligibly. **Queue:** greetings recycle in every warm-up; *to be* enters queue.
**Coach notes:** Arabic speakers sometimes drop /h/ or use only "hello" — encourage time-appropriate greetings and keep it warm.

## S0-U1-L02 — "Hello, I'm…" — Saying Who You Are

*(This is the fully worked example in `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` Part B — reproduced here as the unit's L02 so the unit file is complete.)*

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / Pre-A1→A1 · Unit 1 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can introduce themselves with name, country, and job in simple sentences.
- The learner can ask *"What's your name?"* and respond.
- The learner can produce their first spoken self-introduction on video.

**Target language:** name/country/job; *"My name is… / I'm… / I'm from… / I'm a/an + job."*
**Grammar:** *to be* (I'm/you're). **Accent Lab focus:** /aɪ/ in "I", clear /m/ in "I'm"; stress the key word (*"I'm a **teacher**"*).
**Arabic support:** ~70%. **Mindset focus:** consistency > intensity.

**Materials:** 3 self-introduction clips (American models); frames slide (*My name is ___ / I'm from ___ / I'm a ___*); model audio "I / I'm / I'm a teacher"; recording prompt; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Recall greetings (L01): say hello + how are you. | 3 min |
| 2 | Input | Watch 3 short self-introductions; meaning supported with text + Arabic gloss. | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Highlight *"My name is… / I'm from… / I'm a…"*; brief Arabic explanation of *to be*. | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Drill *"I → I'm → I'm a teacher"* (/aɪ/ + /m/); record-and-compare. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Fill frames aloud with own info; substitution (swap country/job). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Record a 3-sentence self-introduction (real info). | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to model; note 1 thing to improve; keep the recording. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 20–30s self-introduction voice note in the community. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — full written frames + audio; allow re-listening. Stretch — add *"I live in…"*.
**Success check:** 3 correct, intelligible self-intro sentences (rubric: Accuracy + Pronunciation + Fluency at A1 threshold). **Queue:** *to be*, "I'm from…", jobs → return L03 warm-up, ~3 days, ~1 week.
**Coach notes:** expect dropped "am/is" ("I teacher") — correct the *to be* target now; note other errors for later (Handbook §11).

## S0-U1-L03 — "Where Are You From?" — Countries & Nationalities

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 1 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can ask and answer *"Where are you from?"* / *"I'm from ___."*
- The learner can name several countries and a few nationalities (basic).
- The learner can ask a classmate and report (*"He's from Egypt."*).

**Target language:** countries (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, England, America…); *"Where are you from?"*;
*"I'm from ___."*; intro to nationality (Egyptian). **Grammar:** *to be* extended (he's/she's from).
**Accent Lab focus:** clear /r/ (from, are); stress on the country name.
**Arabic support:** ~60% (starting to fade). **Mindset focus:** review your goal — are you practicing daily?

**Materials:** map/flags visual; country flashcards; dialogue clip; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: say your L02 self-intro (name + job). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: people saying where they're from; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *"Where are you from?"* → *"I'm from ___."*; add *he's/she's from*; Egyptian (nationality) intro. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | /r/ in *from/are*; stress the country (*"I'm from **E**gypt"*). | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Substitution with flashcards: swap countries; then *"He's from ___."* | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: ask 2 classmates *"Where are you from?"*, then report to the group; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check /r/ and *to be*; note anything for the queue. | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: *"Hi, I'm ___. I'm from ___."* + tag where a classmate is from. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — just *"I'm from ___."* Stretch — *"I'm Egyptian, from Cairo."*
**Success check:** correct question + answer with clear country stress. **Queue:** he's/she's + from recycle in U2.
**Coach notes:** don't overload nationalities (they're irregular) — one is enough at A1; focus on the pattern.

## S0-U1-L04 — "What Do You Do?" — Jobs

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 1 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can ask and answer *"What do you do?"* / *"I'm a/an ___."*
- The learner can name common jobs and use **a/an** correctly.
- The learner can talk about someone else's job (*"She's a doctor."*).

**Target language:** jobs (teacher, engineer, doctor, student, nurse, driver, accountant…); *"What do you do?"*;
*"I'm a/an ___."* **Grammar:** *to be* + a/an (article before jobs); he's/she's a…
**Accent Lab focus:** a vs an (*an engineer*); stress on the job word.
**Arabic support:** ~60%. **Mindset focus:** small wins compound — you can already introduce yourself!

**Materials:** job flashcards; dialogue clip; a/an rule card; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: *"I'm ___, I'm from ___."* (L02–L03). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: people saying their jobs; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *"What do you do?"* → *"I'm a/an ___."*; explain (Arabic) **a** before consonant, **an** before vowel sound. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Drill *"a teacher / an engineer"*; stress the job. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Flashcard substitution; then *"He's a driver / She's a doctor."* | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: ask *"What do you do?"*, answer, report; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check a/an and the *to be*; queue any weak sounds. | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: *"I'm a/an ___."* + one classmate's job. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — provide the a/an card. Stretch — *"I'm a student and I want to be an engineer."* (preview want to)
**Success check:** correct *"I'm a/an + job"* with right article. **Queue:** a/an + jobs recycle in U3 (a/an with objects).
**Coach notes:** common error: omitting the article ("I engineer") — this is the lesson to fix it gently and repeatedly.

## S0-U1-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: Introduce Yourself (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 1 · ~30 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can give a connected self-introduction: greeting + name + country + job (4–6 sentences).
- The learner can ask another person the same questions.
- The learner records their **Unit-1 self-introduction** (a visible milestone vs the Day-1 clip).

**Target language:** integrated Unit-1 language. **Grammar:** *to be* consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity — /aɪ/, /r/, a/an, stress; each learner's flagged weak sound.
**Arabic support:** ~50% (fading). **Mindset focus:** notice how far you've come in ~2 weeks.

**Materials:** a model 5-sentence self-introduction; a simple checklist card; recording prompt; Unit-1 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid recall: greeting, name, from, job — as a chain. | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean model self-introduction (5 sentences). | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break the model into the frames learned; the "checklist" of a good intro. | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their **one** weakest sound flagged in U0–U1. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the full intro with the checklist card; peer practice (pairs). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the full self-introduction (greeting + name + from + job + one extra). | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check against the checklist; coach gives group-level delayed feedback. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the self-intro video/voice note; complete Unit-1 quiz; celebrate the milestone. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 4 sentences with the card. Stretch — add *"I live in ___"* / *"Nice to meet you."*
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 1):** connected 4–6 sentence self-intro, intelligible, correct *to be* and a/an (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A1). **Queue:** whole self-intro recycles into Unit 2 warm-ups and Unit 10 review.
**Coach notes:** This recording is a key proof asset — with consent, it pairs beautifully with the graduation clip later. Make the milestone feel big.
