# Stage 0 · Unit 9 — Happening Now (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.3 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage0/00-stage0-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~25% (nearly immersion), instructions/explanations mostly English with brief
> Arabic for hard points; learner outputs in English.
> **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can describe what is happening now and contrast it with what
> usually happens — and can narrate a live scene.
> **Grammar spine:** present continuous (am/is/are + -ing) for now · contrast with present simple (usually)
> · **Accent Lab:** -ing endings (/ɪŋ/) + stress contrast (now vs usually) · **Mindset:** thinking in
> English — reducing in-head translation.

**Unit lessons:** L01 "I'm …-ing" (present continuous now) · L02 What are they doing? (scenes) ·
L03 now vs usually (continuous vs simple) · L04 What are you wearing? (clothes + continuous) ·
L05 Unit speaking task: describe what's happening + review.

**Assets to produce:** action-verb flashcards, "busy scene" images (many people doing things), a
present-continuous clip (American model), clothes flashcards, -ing audio, a Google Form per lesson, community prompts.

## S0-U9-L01 — "I'm …-ing" — Present Continuous (Now)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 9 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Unit 8.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can say what they are doing right now using **am/is/are + -ing** (*"I'm studying English."*).
- The learner can form the -ing of common verbs (spelling: study→studying, run→running, write→writing).
- The learner can use contractions (I'm / he's / they're + -ing).

**Target language:** action verbs; *"I'm/He's/They're + -ing (now)."*; time markers now/at the moment.
**Grammar:** present continuous (be + -ing) for actions in progress. **Accent Lab focus:** clean -ing
/ɪŋ/ (recycled U5); linking *"I'm-doing"*.
**Arabic support:** ~25%. **Mindset focus:** narrate your actions in English in your head (thinking in English).

**Materials:** action flashcards; -ing spelling card; clip; frames slide; Google Form (make the -ing form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: give a short direction (U8) + a like with -ing (U5). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Right now, I'm sitting at my desk. I'm drinking coffee."; text + brief gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | be + -ing for NOW; the -ing spelling rules (double letter, drop -e); contractions. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Clean -ing /ɪŋ/ (sitting, drinking); link *"I'm-sitting"*. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Say what you're doing now (*"I'm ___-ing."*); make -ing forms from base verbs. | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: say 3 things you're doing now (real); record one. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check be + -ing (don't drop the be verb) + clean -ing; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a voice note: 2 things you're doing right now. -ing quiz form. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — "I'm + -ing" only. Stretch — I'm/he's/they're + -ing across a small scene.
**Success check:** learner forms ≥3 present-continuous sentences intelligibly. **Queue:** present continuous feeds L02–L05.
**Coach notes:** the classic Arabic-transfer error is dropping the *be* verb ("I studying") — target it; also watch -ing spelling. Contrast with present simple comes in L03 (don't confuse yet).

## S0-U9-L02 — What Are They Doing? (Describing Scenes)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 9 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe what other people are doing now (*"He's reading. They're playing."*).
- The learner can ask *"What are you/they doing?"* and answer.
- The learner can describe a busy scene (several actions at once).

**Target language:** action verbs (3rd person continuous); *"What is/are ___ doing?"*
**Grammar:** present continuous questions + he/she/they. **Accent Lab focus:** *"What-are-they"* linking
(→ /wɒtə(r)ðeɪ/); clean -ing.
**Arabic support:** ~25%. **Mindset focus:** describe scenes around you in English (a daily thinking-in-English drill).

**Materials:** busy-scene images; a "what's happening" clip; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: say what you're doing now (L01). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip/scene: "Look — he's cooking, she's talking on the phone, they're watching TV."; text + brief gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Questions: *"What is he doing?" → "He's ___-ing."*; multiple actions in a scene. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Link *"what-are-they-doing"*; keep -ing clean. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe a busy-scene image: 4–5 sentences (he's/she's/they're + -ing). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: A describes a scene, B guesses/asks *"What is ___ doing?"*; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check question form + he/she/they + -ing; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: describe what people are doing in a photo (real or given). | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 3 sentences about a scene. Stretch — 5 sentences + a question exchange.
**Success check:** learner describes ≥4 actions in a scene + asks a question. **Queue:** feeds L03–L05.
**Coach notes:** great "thinking in English" practice — encourage narrating real surroundings. Watch dropped *be* again.

## S0-U9-L03 — now vs usually (Present Continuous vs Present Simple)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 9 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can contrast an action **now** (continuous) with a habit **usually** (simple).
- The learner can choose the right form with time markers (now/at the moment vs usually/every day).
- The learner can say *"I usually…, but right now I'm…"*.

**Target language:** now/at the moment vs usually/every day; both tenses side by side.
**Grammar:** present continuous vs present simple (the core A1 contrast). **Accent Lab focus:** stress the
time marker (*"RIGHT now" / "USUALLY"*) to signal meaning.
**Arabic support:** ~25% (brief Arabic for the concept). **Mindset focus:** you're handling two tenses —
real grammatical control is forming.

**Materials:** two-column visual (usually vs now); contrast clip; frames slide; Google Form (choose the tense).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: a routine (present simple, U4) + a now action (L01). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I usually drink tea, but right now I'm drinking coffee."; text + brief gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | now (continuous) vs usually (simple); time markers; explain (brief Arabic) the difference. | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the time marker to cue meaning; clean -ing vs base verb. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Make contrast sentences: *"I usually ___, but right now I'm ___-ing."* | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: say one habit + what you're doing differently right now; record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tense choice (the key skill) + form; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: 1 "usually" sentence + 1 "right now" sentence. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — separate sentences (one of each). Stretch — combine with "but" in one sentence.
**Success check:** learner correctly contrasts now vs usually in ≥2 sentences. **Queue:** the contrast recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** this is the highest-order grammar point in Stage 0 — keep examples vivid and personal. Don't over-explain; let the time markers do the work.

## S0-U9-L04 — What Are You Wearing? (Clothes + Continuous)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 9 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can name common clothes (shirt, trousers, dress, shoes, jacket, hat).
- The learner can say what they/others are wearing now (*"I'm wearing a blue shirt."*).
- The learner can combine colours/adjectives (U7) with clothes.

**Target language:** clothes vocab; *"I'm/He's wearing ___."*; colours + clothes. **Grammar:** present
continuous with *wear*; adjective + noun recycled (U7). **Accent Lab focus:** clear clothes words
(shirt /ʃɜːrt/, shoes /ʃuːz/); /ʃ/ sound.
**Arabic support:** ~25%. **Mindset focus:** you're combining old + new language freely now — that's fluency forming.

**Materials:** clothes flashcards; people images; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: now vs usually (L03) + a colour+noun (U7). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I'm wearing a black jacket and blue jeans."; text + brief gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Clothes vocab; *wearing + colour + item*; explain any new words. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | /ʃ/ in *shirt/shoes*; clear clothes words. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Say what you're wearing (colour + item); describe a person in an image. | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: describe what you and a classmate are wearing; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check continuous + adjective order (U7) + /ʃ/; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: describe what you're wearing today (2 items with colours). | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 2 items ("I'm wearing a shirt"). Stretch — full outfit + describe someone else.
**Success check:** learner describes ≥2 clothing items with colours intelligibly. **Queue:** feeds U10 review.
**Coach notes:** nice integration of U7 (adjective+noun order) with U9 (continuous). Reinforce adjective-before-noun once more.

## S0-U9-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: Describe What's Happening (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 9 · ~30 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can narrate a scene in 6–8 sentences (what people are doing now + what they're wearing +
  a now-vs-usually contrast).
- The learner can ask another person what's happening / what they're doing.
- The learner records a **"what's happening now"** narration (portfolio proof).

**Target language:** integrated Unit-9 language. **Grammar:** present continuous + the now/usually contrast consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** clean -ing + linking + /ʃ/; each learner's flagged weak sound.
**Arabic support:** ~20% (near immersion). **Mindset focus:** you're narrating live English — you're nearly at A1 exit.

**Materials:** a busy-scene image or short video; a model narration; a checklist card; recording prompt; Unit-9 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Chain recall: present continuous, questions, now vs usually, clothes. | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a model scene narration (6–7 sentences). | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break into learned frames; build the "good scene narration" checklist. | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their weakest sound flagged in U9 (often -ing or dropped *be*). | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the narration with the card; peer practice (pairs). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record: narrate a scene (what people are doing + wearing + one now-vs-usually contrast). | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level delayed feedback. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the narration; complete Unit-9 quiz; celebrate — Stage 0 assessment is next! | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 6 sentences describing actions only. Stretch — full narration + contrast + a question exchange.
**Success check (Unit-9 summary):** connected 6–8 sentence narration, intelligible, correct present continuous + basic now/usually contrast (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation + Interaction at A1). **Queue:** narration recycles into Unit 10 review.
**Coach notes:** ~10 weeks in — learners now narrate live action and juggle two tenses. This clip shows near-A1-exit fluency; log with consent. Prepare them for Unit 10 (consolidation + the summative + the graduation "after" sample).
