# Stage 0 · Unit 5 — Free Time & Ability (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 1.3 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage0/00-stage0-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** ~45% (fading), instructions/explanations in Egyptian Arabic; learner outputs in English.
> **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can talk about hobbies and likes/dislikes, and say what they
> can and can't do — and can record a short "my free time" piece.
> **Grammar spine:** like/love/hate + -ing · can / can't for ability · Do you like…? / Can you…? ·
> **Accent Lab:** linking sounds + stressing content words in a sentence (rhythm) · **Mindset:** building
> a personal English input diet (music/shows/podcasts at your level).

**Unit lessons:** L01 "I like…" (likes & dislikes + -ing) · L02 Hobbies & free-time vocab · L03 "Can you…?"
(ability) · L04 Asking questions: "Do you like…?" mini-conversations · L05 Unit speaking task: talk about
your free time + review.

**Assets to produce:** hobby flashcards, a "my free time" clip (American model), like/love/hate scale
visual, can/can't audio, linking-and-stress audio, a Google Form per lesson, community voice-note prompts.

## S0-U5-L01 — "I Like…" — Likes & Dislikes (+ -ing)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 5 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Unit 4.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can express likes/dislikes with like/love/hate (+ noun and + verb-ing).
- The learner can say *"I like football. I love reading. I don't like cooking."*
- The learner can use *don't like* for dislikes.

**Target language:** like/love/hate; don't like; common activities (reading, cooking, swimming, watching TV).
**Grammar:** like + noun / like + verb-ing; negative *don't like*. **Accent Lab focus:** -ing endings
(/ɪŋ/, not /ɪn/ or /ɪŋg/); linking *"I-like"*.
**Arabic support:** ~45%. **Mindset focus:** find English content you *enjoy* — like it and you'll do it.

**Materials:** activity flashcards; like/love/hate scale; clip; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 2 frequency sentences from U4 ("I always…"). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I love reading. I like football. I don't like cooking."; text + Arabic gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | like/love/hate scale; like + -ing; negative *don't like*; explain (Arabic). | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | -ing endings (readi**ng**, swimmi**ng**) — clean /ŋ/; link *"I-like"*. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Say 3 likes + 2 dislikes with -ing (*"I like watching TV. I don't like running."*). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: tell a partner 2 things you love and 1 you hate; record one. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check like + -ing form + clean -ing sound; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a voice note: 2 likes + 1 dislike (with -ing). Likes quiz form. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — like + noun only ("I like football"). Stretch — like/love/hate + -ing across 5 activities.
**Success check:** learner expresses ≥3 likes/dislikes intelligibly. **Queue:** like + -ing recycles in L04 questions and U9 (-ing).
**Coach notes:** common errors: "I like to cooking" or dropping -ing; and /ɪn/ for /ɪŋ/. Target the -ing form and sound gently.

## S0-U5-L02 — Hobbies & Free-Time Vocabulary

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 5 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can name common hobbies and free-time activities (sports, music, games, going out).
- The learner can say what they do in their free time (*"In my free time, I…"*).
- The learner can use *play / go / do* with activities (play football, go swimming, do yoga — basic).

**Target language:** hobbies vocab; *play + game/sport*, *go + -ing*, *do + activity*; "In my free time…".
**Grammar:** collocations play/go/do (introductory). **Accent Lab focus:** stress the activity word;
linking *"go-out"*.
**Arabic support:** ~45%. **Mindset focus:** pick ONE English show/podcast to start your input diet this week.

**Materials:** hobby flashcards grouped by play/go/do; clip; frames slide; Google Form (match verb → activity).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: your likes from L01. | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "In my free time, I play football and go swimming."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Group activities by play/go/do; "In my free time…"; explain (Arabic) the collocations simply. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the content word (*"play FOOTball"*); link *"go-out"*. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Say what you do: *"I play…, I go…, I do…"* (3–4 activities). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: describe your free time in 3 sentences; record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check play/go/do choice + stress; queue weak items. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: 3 free-time activities (using play/go/do). Match-verb quiz form. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 2 activities with "I like…". Stretch — full play/go/do set + frequency (from U4).
**Success check:** learner names ≥3 hobbies with reasonable collocations. **Queue:** hobbies recycle in L03–L05.
**Coach notes:** don't over-drill play/go/do rules at A1 — accept and gently model correct collocations; the goal is communication.

## S0-U5-L03 — "Can You…?" — Ability

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 5 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can say what they can and can't do (*"I can swim. I can't drive."*).
- The learner can ask *"Can you…?"* and answer (*"Yes, I can. / No, I can't."*).
- The learner can talk about others' abilities (*"She can cook well."*).

**Target language:** can/can't + base verb; ability verbs (swim, drive, cook, sing, play the guitar…).
**Grammar:** can/can't (same for all persons — no -s!); Can you…? short answers. **Accent Lab focus:**
weak *can* /kən/ vs strong *can't* /kænt/ in natural speech.
**Arabic support:** ~40%. **Mindset focus:** "I can't… yet" — add "yet" and keep a growth mindset.

**Materials:** ability flashcards; can/can't audio (weak vs strong); frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: free-time activities from L02. | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I can swim but I can't drive. Can you cook?"; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | can/can't + base verb; **no -s with he/she** (She can cook, not "cans"); short answers. | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | weak *can* vs strong *can't*; hear the difference (I can /kən/ swim vs I can't /kænt/ swim). | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Say 3 things you can + 2 you can't; ask a partner *"Can you…?"* | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: find one thing you both can do and one neither can; record. | 3 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check no-3rd-person-s with can + can/can't distinction; queue. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: 2 things you can + 1 you can't do. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — I can / I can't only. Stretch — he/she can + a follow-up question.
**Success check:** correct can/can't ability statements + question. **Queue:** can recycles in U6 (requests) and U8 (directions help).
**Coach notes:** two wins here — no -s on modals ("she can cook") and hearing weak/strong can. The mindset "…yet" reframe is powerful; use it.

## S0-U5-L04 — Asking Questions: "Do You Like…?" Mini-Conversations

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 5 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can ask **Do you like…?** questions and give short answers (*"Yes, I do. / No, I don't."*).
- The learner can keep a short conversation going (question → answer → follow-up).
- The learner can ask *"What do you like doing?"* (basic).

**Target language:** Do you like + -ing?; short answers; follow-ups (*"Me too. / Really? Why?"*).
**Grammar:** present simple questions with *do*; short answers. **Accent Lab focus:** question intonation
(rising for yes/no); linking *"do-you"* → /djə/.
**Arabic support:** ~40%. **Mindset focus:** conversation is a skill — a few good questions carry you far.

**Materials:** question prompt cards; a mini-dialogue model; frames slide; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: can/can't statements (L03) + a like (L01). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Model mini-dialogue: "Do you like football?" "Yes, I do! Do you?" "No, I don't."; text + gloss. | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Do you like…? + short answers; keeping it going ("Me too", "Really?"); explain (Arabic). | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Rising yes/no intonation; natural *"d'you like…?"* linking. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Card drill: ask/answer *"Do you like…?"* in pairs; add one follow-up each. | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output | Live/pairs: a 4-line conversation (Q → A → follow-up → A); record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check question form + short answers + intonation. | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: ask 2 classmates *"Do you like…?"* in the community and reply to theirs. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — ask/answer with the card. Stretch — 6-line conversation with 2 follow-ups.
**Success check:** learner asks a correct Do-you-like question + gives short answers in a mini-exchange. **Queue:** question forms feed U6 (shopping Qs) and U8.
**Coach notes:** this is the first real back-and-forth conversation — celebrate it. Common error: "You like…?" without *do*; and answering "Yes, I like" instead of "Yes, I do".

## S0-U5-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: Talk About Your Free Time (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 5 · ~30 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about their free time in 6–8 connected sentences (likes, hobbies, abilities).
- The learner can have a short conversation asking a partner about their free time.
- The learner records a **"my free time"** piece (portfolio proof).

**Target language:** integrated Unit-5 language. **Grammar:** like + -ing, can/can't, do-questions consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** linking + content-word stress + -ing; each learner's flagged weak sound.
**Arabic support:** ~40%. **Mindset focus:** you can now hold a mini-conversation — that's real progress.

**Materials:** a model "my free time" description + mini-dialogue; a checklist card; recording prompt; Unit-5 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Chain recall: likes (+ -ing), hobbies (play/go/do), can/can't, Do you like…? | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a model "my free time" description + a short conversation. | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break into learned frames; build the "good free-time talk" checklist. | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills their weakest sound flagged in U5 (often -ing or can/can't). | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the description + a Q&A with the card; peer practice (pairs). | 6 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record: describe your free time (likes + hobbies + abilities) AND ask/answer one question. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level delayed feedback. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the free-time piece; complete Unit-5 quiz; celebrate progress. | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — 6 sentences with the card. Stretch — full description + a 4-line conversation with a partner.
**Success check (Unit-5 summary):** connected 6–8 sentence free-time talk + a short exchange, intelligible, correct like + -ing and can/can't (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation + Interaction at A1). **Queue:** free-time talk recycles into Unit 10 review.
**Coach notes:** ~6 weeks in — the Interaction dimension now appears (mini-conversations). This clip shows conversational ability emerging; log with consent.
