## S0-U10-L01 — Big Review 1: Identity, Family, Things, Routine

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: Units 0–9.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and reuse language from Units 1–4 (self-intro, family, objects/places, routine).
- The learner can speak for ~1 minute combining these topics.
- The learner can self-correct common errors (to-be, a/an, he/she -s, there is/are).

**Target language:** integrated review (Units 1–4). **Grammar:** to be, possessives, have/has, there is/are,
present simple + time/frequency. **Accent Lab focus:** rapid recall of earlier targets (/p/–/b/, /θ/–/ð/,
plural/3rd-person -s). **Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** see how much you can already say — proof of progress.

**Materials:** retrieval prompt cards; a "talk for a minute" timer; error self-check card; Google Form (mixed review).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid-fire retrieval: name, from, job, family count, one routine sentence. | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Short model that weaves Units 1–4 together (self-intro → family → routine). | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | The self-check card: the 4 errors to watch (to-be, a/an, he/she -s, there is/are). | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Quick recall drill of earlier sounds; each learner flags their weakest. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Card-prompted mini-talks across the 4 topics; peers catch errors kindly. | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | "Talk for 1 minute about you + your family + your day"; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check with the card; coach group-level notes. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post the 1-minute talk; complete the mixed-review form. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — prompts + 45 seconds. Stretch — 90 seconds, no card.
**Success check:** learner sustains ~1 minute across Units 1–4 topics. **Queue:** flags weak areas for L03 accent clinic + L04 mock.
**Coach notes:** this is retrieval practice (the testing effect) — resist re-teaching; let them recall and self-correct. Note each learner's recurring errors for the clinic.

## S0-U10-L02 — Big Review 2: Free Time, Food, Description, Directions, Now

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and reuse language from Units 5–9 (free time, food/shopping, description, directions, now).
- The learner can perform short functional exchanges (order, ask directions, describe, say what's happening).
- The learner can keep a conversation going with questions and follow-ups.

**Target language:** integrated review (Units 5–9). **Grammar:** like + -ing, can, would like, adjective+noun,
have got, imperatives, present continuous. **Accent Lab focus:** recall of -ing, /l/–/r/, clusters, intonation.
**Arabic support:** ~15%. **Mindset focus:** these are real-life skills — you can function in English now.

**Materials:** function-station prompts (café, street, describe-a-person, scene); Google Form (mixed review).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid retrieval: a like, a can/can't, a "how much", a direction, a "right now". | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | A model that chains Units 5–9 functions in a short scene. | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | The "keep it going" toolkit: questions + follow-ups (Do you like…?, What are you doing?). | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Recall drill: -ing, /l/–/r/, clusters; each learner targets their weakest. | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rotate function stations (order / directions / describe / scene) in pairs. | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Pick 2 functions and perform them in a short exchange; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check; coach group-level notes. | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post one function exchange; complete the mixed-review form. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 1 station with prompts. Stretch — 3 stations, no prompts, with follow-ups.
**Success check:** learner performs ≥2 functional exchanges intelligibly with interaction. **Queue:** flags weak areas for L03 + L04.
**Coach notes:** emphasize conversation management (survival phrases + questions). This is where "functional A1" becomes visible.

## S0-U10-L03 — Accent Clinic: Fix Your Top 3 Sounds

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can identify their 3 weakest sounds (from the queue flags across Units 0–9).
- The learner can apply the fix technique for each (articulation cue + minimal pairs + record-and-compare).
- The learner can produce those sounds more clearly in words and a sentence.

**Target language:** the learner's personal target sounds (commonly /p/–/b/, /v/–/f/, /θ/–/ð/, clusters,
-ing, /l/–/r/). **Accent Lab focus:** personalized — each learner's flagged three. **Arabic support:** ~15%.
**Mindset focus:** targeted practice on weaknesses = deliberate practice; this is how accents improve.

**Materials:** each learner's weak-sound flags (from the Cohort Tracker); the Accent Lab technique cards;
minimal-pair audio bank; record-and-compare tool.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Learner reviews their flagged sounds from Units 0–9. | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Listen to the model for each target sound (minimal pairs). | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-teach the articulation cue for each of the learner's 3 sounds (mouth position). | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab (core) | Cycle each sound: perceive → produce in isolation → words → phrase; record-and-compare. | 9 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Put all 3 sounds into one sentence each; refine. | 4 min |
| 6 | Free output | Record a short paragraph loaded with the 3 target sounds. | 2 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to Day-1/earlier samples; note audible improvement. | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Daily 5-min drill on the 3 sounds before the summative. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 2 sounds, more listening. Stretch — 3 sounds + connected-speech/prosody polish.
**Success check:** measurable clarity improvement on the learner's 3 target sounds. **Queue:** these sounds carry into Stage 1.
**Coach notes:** personalization is the point — use each learner's actual data. Celebrate audible gains; this directly improves the Pronunciation rubric score on the summative.

## S0-U10-L04 — Practice Conversation: The A1 Exchange (Mock)

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 10 · ~30 min · recorded + live (mock assessment) · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can sustain the full A1 conversation (greet → self-intro → family → routine → free time →
  a functional exchange → close) with a partner.
- The learner can ask and answer questions naturally, using survival phrases when needed.
- The learner is ready (and calm) for the summative in L05.

**Target language:** all Stage-0 language. **Grammar:** integrated. **Accent Lab focus:** overall clarity +
the learner's 3 target sounds. **Arabic support:** ~10% (near full immersion). **Mindset focus:** exam calm —
it's a conversation, not a test; you've done this all term.

**Materials:** the A1 conversation model + question bank; the 5-dimension rubric sheet (so learners know
the criteria); recording setup.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Quick full self-intro + one function (recall). | 4 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a full A1 conversation model; point out the rubric dimensions in action. | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Walk through the rubric (Fluency/Accuracy/Pronunciation/Range/Interaction) in plain terms. | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Final quick drill of the learner's 3 target sounds. | 3 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Full mock conversation in pairs; coach observes, gives delayed feedback. | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Second run, aiming to improve one rubric dimension; record. | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Rubric-based feedback (strengths + one focus for the real thing). | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Light rehearsal + rest; the summative is next session. | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — shorter exchange with a question bank. Stretch — longer, spontaneous, no bank.
**Success check:** learner completes the full A1 exchange; knows the rubric. **Queue:** none — this preps the summative.
**Coach notes:** reduce anxiety (affective filter) — frame the summative as a friendly conversation. Give each learner one concrete, encouraging focus point.

## S0-U10-L05 — Stage-0 Summative Assessment + Graduation "After" Sample

**Metadata:** Stage 0 / A1 · Unit 10 · ~30–40 min · live (or recorded) assessment · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner demonstrates the **A1 exit criteria** (per `curriculum/01-cefr-roadmap.md` §3).
- The learner completes the Stage-0 summative (quiz + speaking task on the 5-dimension rubric).
- The learner records the **graduation "after" speaking sample** on the **same prompt as the Day-1
  sample**, producing the before/after proof pair.

**Assessment components:**
1. **Written/recognition quiz (Google Form):** a mixed Stage-0 check (to-be, a/an, plurals, present simple
   incl. he/she -s, there is/are, some/any, can, would like, adjective order, present continuous,
   now-vs-usually, prices, directions vocab).
2. **Speaking task (the A1 exchange):** greet → self-introduce → talk about family, routine, free time →
   one functional exchange (order / directions) → describe what's happening now → close. ~5–8 minutes.
3. **Graduation sample:** the learner records the **standard prompt** (identical to Day-1) — e.g.,
   *"Tell me about yourself, your family, and your typical day."* — for the before/after pair.

**Scoring (5-dimension rubric, 0–5 each — from Handbook §16 / Task 1.6):**
Fluency · Accuracy · Pronunciation/Accent · Range · Interaction. **A1 pass threshold:** a defined minimum
across dimensions (finalized in the assessment suite, Task 1.6).

**Flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Settle | Brief, warm reassurance (near-English); it's a conversation. | 2 min |
| 2 | Quiz | Learner completes the recognition quiz (Google Form). | 8 min |
| 3 | Speaking task | The A1 exchange with the coach (or paired + coach-observed); scored on the rubric. | 8–12 min |
| 4 | Graduation sample | Record the standard prompt for the before/after pair. | 3 min |
| 5 | Result & feedback | Coach gives the CEFR determination + warm, specific feedback + next-stage preview. | 4 min |
| 6 | Celebrate & log | Issue the Stage-0 certificate; log everything in the Cohort Tracker (with consent). | — |

**Recording in the Cohort Tracker (Task 0.5 fields):** `end_level`, `final_speaking_score`, `cefr_gain`,
`graduation_sample_link`, and (with consent) prepare the paired Day-1↔graduation clip for testimonials.

**Success check (Stage-0 completion):** learner meets the A1 exit criteria and has a documented CEFR gain
from the Day-1 baseline. **Next:** progress to Stage 1 (Elementary / A2) or the community for continued practice.
**Coach notes:** This is the proof moment the whole strategy is built around — the paired before/after clip
is your strongest marketing asset. Make graduation feel like the achievement it is: from zero to a real
English conversation in ~one term.
