## S4-U1-L01 — Reference & Substitution

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 1 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 connectors/cohesion.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can avoid repetition using reference (this/that/these/such) and substitution (one/ones, do so, so/not).
- The learner can keep long stretches of speech cohesive and economical.
- The learner can track what pronouns/references point to.

**Target language:** reference (*this/that/such/the former/the latter*), substitution (*one/ones, do so, so/neither*).
**Accent Lab focus:** unstressed reference words; smooth linking. **Mindset:** mastery is in the details — reduce repetition to sound polished.

**Materials:** a cohesive-devices card; a repetitive→cohesive rewrite set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 connectors | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: cohesive speech ("I considered two options; the latter seemed better, so I went with it.") | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Reference + substitution to avoid repetition; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Reference words unstressed; link smoothly | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rewrite repetitive passages using reference/substitution | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak ~1.5 min on a topic, minimizing repetition; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check cohesion + clear reference | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a cohesive short talk (no obvious repetition) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — devices card + rewrites. Stretch — the former/the latter + such usage.
**Success check:** cohesive speech using reference/substitution. **Queue:** cohesion recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** ambiguous reference ("it/this" with unclear antecedent) is the risk — reward clarity of reference over cleverness.

## S4-U1-L02 — Discourse Markers for Managing Talk

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 1 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use discourse markers to manage a conversation (add, contrast, digress, return, conclude).
- The learner can signal attitude and organize long turns naturally.
- The learner can sound like a fluent, organized speaker.

**Target language:** discourse markers (*mind you, having said that, that said, on that note, anyway, as I was saying,
to be fair, come to think of it*). **Accent Lab focus:** marker intonation (often set off by a slight pause).
**Mindset:** organized speech = authority.

**Materials:** a discourse-marker functional set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: reference/substitution (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip rich in discourse markers managing a talk | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Markers by function (add/contrast/digress/return/conclude); brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Marker prosody; slight pause | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Insert appropriate markers into a talk; digress + return | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk ~1.5 min using ≥4 markers naturally; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check marker appropriateness | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a short talk using ≥4 discourse markers | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — functional set. Stretch — use markers to digress then return smoothly.
**Success check:** ≥4 discourse markers used appropriately. **Queue:** markers recycle into U7/U9.
**Coach notes:** over-use sounds unnatural — teach a small, high-value set used well over a long list.

## S4-U1-L03 — Ellipsis & Economy

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 1 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can omit repeated/understood elements naturally (ellipsis).
- The learner can speak economically like a native.
- The learner can recognize elliptical speech in fast input.

**Target language:** ellipsis (auxiliary/verb-phrase omission: "I can, but she can't.", "Been busy?"), reduced answers.
**Accent Lab focus:** natural rhythm of elliptical speech. **Mindset:** economy = fluency.

**Materials:** a full↔elliptical set; a natural-speech clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: discourse markers (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: natural elliptical exchanges | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | What can be omitted (understood elements); brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Rhythm of elliptical speech | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Reduce full sentences to natural elliptical forms | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Hold a natural exchange using ellipsis; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check natural, clear ellipsis | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an elliptical exchange example | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — full↔elliptical set. Stretch — a fast, natural dialogue.
**Success check:** natural ellipsis without loss of clarity. **Queue:** ellipsis recycles into U6/U7.
**Coach notes:** balance economy with clarity — ellipsis should sound natural, not clipped/confusing.

## S4-U1-L04 — Cohesion Across a Long Turn

**Objectives (can-do):**

| 6 | Free output | Deliver a cohesive 2-min turn on a prompt; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check cohesion across the whole turn | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — a complex topic, less prep.

## S4-U1-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "A Cohesive 3-Minute Talk" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 1 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can deliver a cohesive ~3-min talk integrating reference, substitution, markers, and ellipsis.
- The learner can field questions while maintaining cohesion.
- The learner records their **Unit-1 cohesive talk**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-1 toolkit. **Accent Lab focus:** cohesive prosody; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** mastery — the details that make speech sound advanced.

**Materials:** a model talk; a checklist card (reference/substitution / ≥3 markers / natural ellipsis / seamless flow);
recording prompt; Unit-1 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: the cohesion toolkit | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean cohesive ~3-min talk | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills cohesive prosody + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer Q&A | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~3-min cohesive talk + field a question | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-1 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — an abstract topic, spontaneous Q&A.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 1):** a cohesive ~3-min talk integrating the toolkit (rubric: Coherence + Fluency + Range at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** cohesion is the first clear C1 marker beyond B2 — reward seamless, listener-friendly extended speech.
