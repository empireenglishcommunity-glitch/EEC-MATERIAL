## S4-U5-L01 — Cleft Sentences (It-Cleft, What-Cleft)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 5 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 full grammar.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use it-clefts (*It was X that…*) and what-clefts (*What I need is…*) to focus/emphasize.
- The learner can highlight the key element of a sentence.
- The learner can add emphasis without just raising volume.

**Target language:** it-cleft (*It was the delay that caused the problem*), what-cleft (*What surprised me was…*).
**Grammar:** cleft structures for focus. **Accent Lab focus:** stress on the focused element. **Mindset:** structure carries emphasis — precision.

**Materials:** a cleft-structure card; a neutral→cleft transform set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 emphasis (stress) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "What really matters is consistency. It was the team that made it work." | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | It-cleft + what-cleft to focus the key part; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the focused element | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform neutral sentences into clefts to shift focus | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Emphasize 3 points using clefts; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check cleft form + focus | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 cleft sentences emphasizing different elements | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — cleft card + transforms. Stretch — combine clefts in a persuasive point.
**Success check:** correct it-/what-clefts that shift focus. **Queue:** clefts recycle this unit + U9 (persuasion).
**Coach notes:** clefts are the most useful marked structure for spoken emphasis — prioritize them; reward natural, purposeful use.

## S4-U5-L02 — Inversion After Negative Adverbials

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 5 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use inversion after negative/limiting adverbials (*Never have I…, Not only… but also…, Rarely does…*).
- The learner can add formal emphasis/drama.
- The learner can recognize inversion in formal input.

**Target language:** inversion triggers (*Never, Rarely, Seldom, Not only, No sooner, Little did…*). **Grammar:**
subject-auxiliary inversion after fronted negatives. **Accent Lab focus:** emphatic stress + formal prosody.
**Mindset:** formal flourish, used sparingly, impresses.

**Materials:** an inversion-triggers card; a formal/emphatic clip; a transform set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: clefts (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Never have I seen such dedication. Not only did we finish, but we exceeded the target." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Fronted negative → invert subject/auxiliary; triggers; brief note | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Emphatic + formal prosody | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform neutral → inverted with triggers | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Make 3 emphatic inverted statements; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check inversion form | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 inverted sentences | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — triggers card. Stretch — *No sooner… than…, Little did I know…*.
**Success check:** correct inversion after negative adverbials. **Queue:** inversion recycles into U9/U10.
**Coach notes:** inversion is formal/dramatic — teach it as an occasional flourish, not everyday speech. Accuracy matters (auxiliary placement); over-use sounds affected.

## S4-U5-L03 — Fronting & Emphatic *do*

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 5 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can front elements for emphasis/cohesion (*This I can't accept.*).
- The learner can use emphatic *do/did* (*I do understand, She did call*).
- The learner can emphasize contrast/insistence naturally.

**Target language:** fronting; emphatic *do/does/did*. **Grammar:** marked word order + emphatic auxiliary.
**Accent Lab focus:** strong stress on emphatic *do* + fronted element. **Mindset:** insist/contrast with precision.

**Materials:** a fronting/emphatic-do card; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: inversion (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I do appreciate it. That, I completely agree with." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Fronting for emphasis/cohesion; emphatic *do*; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the emphatic *do* / fronted element | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Add emphatic *do* + front elements for contrast | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Emphasize/insist on 3 points (do + fronting); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check emphatic forms | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 2 emphatic-*do* + 1 fronted sentence | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — card. Stretch — combine with clefts for layered emphasis.
**Success check:** correct emphatic *do* + fronting. **Queue:** recycles into U9.
**Coach notes:** emphatic *do* is very natural and high-value in speech (concession/insistence) — reward it; fronting is more marked, use sparingly.

## S4-U5-L04 — Subjunctive & Formal Structures

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 5 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use the subjunctive/mandative (*I suggest (that) he be…, It's essential that…*).
- The learner can use formal structures (*Should you need…, Were I to…, inversion in conditionals*).
- The learner can sound appropriately formal in high-register contexts.

**Target language:** mandative subjunctive (*recommend/insist/essential + that + base*), formal conditional inversion
(*Should you…, Were I…, Had I known…*). **Grammar:** subjunctive + formal inversion. **Accent Lab focus:** formal prosody.
**Mindset:** command the formal register when it counts.

**Materials:** a subjunctive/formal-structures card; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: emphatic forms (L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "It's essential that everyone be informed. Should you have questions, contact us." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Mandative subjunctive + formal conditional inversion; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Formal prosody | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Produce subjunctive + formal-conditional sentences | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 formal statements (subjunctive/formal inversion); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check subjunctive/formal forms | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 formal/subjunctive sentences | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — structures card. Stretch — a short formal announcement using several.
**Success check:** correct subjunctive/formal structures. **Queue:** feeds U8 + exam writing.
**Coach notes:** these are register-specific (formal writing/announcements) — teach for recognition + appropriate production; not for casual speech.

## S4-U5-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Emphasize & Impress Accurately" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 5 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can deliver a persuasive/emphatic ~3-min talk using clefts, inversion, fronting, and emphatic *do* accurately.
- The learner can emphasize key points through structure, not just volume.
- The learner records their **Unit-5 emphatic performance**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-5 marked structures. **Accent Lab focus:** stress/emphasis in marked structures; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** precision as craft — impressive *and* accurate.

**Materials:** a model emphatic talk; a checklist card (a cleft / an inversion / emphatic do / accurate + purposeful / natural delivery);
recording prompt; Unit-5 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: clefts, inversion, fronting, emphatic do, subjunctive | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean emphatic/persuasive talk | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills emphasis in a marked structure + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer feedback on accuracy + effect | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~3-min emphatic talk | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-5 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — layer several marked structures naturally.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 5):** an emphatic ~3-min talk using ≥2 marked structures accurately + purposefully (rubric: Accuracy + Range at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U9/U10.
**Coach notes:** the goal is *accurate + purposeful* emphasis, not maximum marked structures. Over-use sounds affected — reward tasteful, correct emphasis.
