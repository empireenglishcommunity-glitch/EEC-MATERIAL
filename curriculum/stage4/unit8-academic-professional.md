## S4-U8-L01 — Complex Argumentation & Evidence

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 8 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 argument + U5 emphasis.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can build a multi-layered argument (thesis → arguments → evidence → counter → rebuttal → conclusion).
- The learner can support claims with different evidence types.
- The learner can structure a rigorous case.

**Target language:** argumentation framework + evidence language (studies show, in practice, by contrast, this is borne out by).
**Accent Lab focus:** authoritative, measured delivery. **Mindset:** rigorous thinking → rigorous English.

**Materials:** an argumentation-framework card; a model complex argument; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 structured argument | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Model complex argument (thesis→evidence→counter→rebuttal→conclusion) | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Multi-layer structure + evidence types; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Authoritative, measured prosody | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build a rigorous argument on a prompt; peer checks the layers | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Argue a complex position (~2 min) with evidence + counter; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check argument rigor + evidence | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a complex, evidenced argument | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — framework card. Stretch — a nuanced position with multiple counters.
**Success check:** a multi-layered, evidenced argument. **Queue:** argumentation feeds U9 + exam writing/speaking.
**Coach notes:** rigor + structure over volume — reward the counter+rebuttal move (steel-manning) as a mark of sophistication.

## S4-U8-L02 — Academic Hedging & Caution

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 8 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can hedge academic claims appropriately (avoid overstatement).
- The learner can qualify, limit, and cautiously assert.
- The learner can sound scholarly and credible.

**Target language:** academic hedges (*tends to, may suggest, to some extent, it could be argued, arguably, broadly speaking*).
**Accent Lab focus:** measured hedging prosody. **Mindset:** intellectual honesty = credibility.

**Materials:** an academic-hedging set; an overstated→hedged transform set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |

| 4 | Accent Lab | Measured, credible prosody | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Hedge overstated claims; calibrate certainty | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Make 3 appropriately-hedged claims on a topic; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check hedging appropriateness | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 hedged academic claims | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — hedging set. Stretch — calibrate fine degrees of caution.
**Success check:** appropriately hedged academic claims. **Queue:** feeds exam writing + U9.
**Coach notes:** connects to U3 nuance + B2 hedging — overstatement undermines credibility; teach calibrated caution.

## S4-U8-L03 — High-Stakes Presenting (Advanced)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 8 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

## S4-U8-L04 — Defending Under Expert Questioning

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 8 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can field challenging expert questions and defend their position.
- The learner can concede fairly, clarify, and hold ground under pressure.
- The learner can stay composed and precise in Q&A.

**Target language:** Q&A-defense language (*That's a fair challenge; to clarify…; I'd push back gently on that; the evidence suggests…*).
**Accent Lab focus:** composed, precise prosody under pressure. **Mindset:** composure = credibility.

**Materials:** a Q&A-defense set; tough-question cards; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |

| 5 | Guided output | Partner asks tough questions; learner defends | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Present a claim + field 2–3 expert questions; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check composure + defense quality | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — defense phrase set. Stretch — rapid, hostile questioning.
**Success check:** composed, precise defense under challenge. **Queue:** feeds L05 + exam speaking + U9.
**Coach notes:** composure under pressure is the differentiator — model calm, precise responses; reward regulation as much as language.

## S4-U8-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Deliver + Defend a Complex Position" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 8 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can present a complex, evidenced position (~3 min) with academic hedging + impactful delivery, then defend it in Q&A.
- The learner can integrate argument + register + composure.
- The learner records their **Unit-8 present-and-defend**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-8 skills. **Accent Lab focus:** authoritative delivery + composed Q&A; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** this is the professional/academic peak — English as career leverage.

**Materials:** a model present-and-defend; a checklist card (thesis / evidence + counter/rebuttal / academic hedging / impactful delivery / composed Q&A);
recording prompt; Unit-8 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: argumentation, hedging, presenting, defense | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean present-and-defend | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills authoritative delivery + a residual sound | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse presentation; peers prepare expert questions | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Deliver the ~3-min position + defend in Q&A; record | 8 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the present-and-defend; complete Unit-8 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — less prep, hostile Q&A, abstract topic.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 8):** a complex evidenced position + composed defense (rubric: Range + Coherence + Interaction at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U10 + exam prep.
**Coach notes:** the academic/professional peak — a flagship C1 proof clip. Assess argument rigor + delivery + composure together.
