## 1. Stage 4 overview

- **CEFR:** C1 · **EEC label:** Advanced (+ optional Exam Track: TOEFL iBT / IELTS)
- **Entry criterion:** B2 achieved (Stage 3 flagship graduate or placement).
- **Guided-hour budget:** ~200+ hrs · **Cohort length:** ~24+ weeks @ ~7 hrs/week (self-paced-friendly for graduates).
- **The C1 promise (headline):** *"Use English flexibly, fluently, and spontaneously for social, academic,
  and professional purposes — understand demanding, implicit, and idiomatic language, and express yourself
  with precision, nuance, and near-native naturalness."*
- **Arabic support:** none — full immersion (as from B1 onward).
- **Vocabulary target:** ~5,000+ words cumulative (idiomatic, academic, domain-specific, low-frequency + nuance).
- **Skill weighting:** all four skills near-balanced and advanced; strong emphasis on **listening to demanding
  input**, **spontaneous production**, and **stylistic control**.

### Structure
- **C1 core: 10 units** (Unit 10 = consolidation + C1 summative), **~5 lessons each (≈ 50 lessons)**, on the Lesson Template.
- **Exam Track (optional): 2 modules** — **TOEFL iBT** and **IELTS** — format familiarization, per-section
  strategies, timed practice, and full mock tests with band/score reporting (`stage4/exam-track-toefl.md`, `stage4/exam-track-ielts.md`).
- Each lesson ~45–55 min. Weekly rhythm flexible; graduates often take Stage 4 part-time.

### Sequencing logic
C1 is **"mastery, nuance, and naturalness,"** not new core grammar. First the **discourse + lexical layer**
(cohesion, idiom/natural phrasing, nuance/implicature — U1–U3) that separates advanced from upper-intermediate.
Then **stylistic + grammatical sophistication** (register mastery, advanced structures — U4–U5). Then the
**receptive + productive stretch** (understanding demanding input, spontaneous extended discourse — U6–U7).
Then **high-level application** (advanced academic/professional communication, persuasion/debate on sensitive
topics — U8–U9). Unit 10 consolidates and runs the **C1 summative**. The **Exam Track** can run in parallel or
after, for candidates who need TOEFL/IELTS certification.

### What's new vs B2
- **Idiomatic + low-frequency range**: idioms, nuanced vocabulary, connotation, tone.
- **Implicit meaning**: reading between the lines, inference, humor/irony, understatement.
- **Stylistic control**: shifting register/tone deliberately for effect (academic, creative, colloquial, diplomatic).
- **Sophisticated grammar for effect**: inversion, cleft sentences, fronting, emphatic/subjunctive structures.
- **Near-native prosody**: individual polish (aspirational ceiling — GC-5).

## 4. C1 core unit detail + lesson lists

> Each lesson is authored with `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`. Lesson IDs: `S4-U{n}-L{nn}`. English-only (immersion).

### Unit 1 — Advanced Cohesion & Discourse
- **Can-do:** produce highly cohesive extended speech/text; use discourse markers and referencing naturally.
- **Focus:** cohesive devices (reference, substitution, ellipsis), discourse markers (mind you, having said that, that said, on that note), signposting long turns.
- **Lessons:** L01 reference & substitution (this/that/such/one/do so) · L02 discourse markers for managing talk · L03 ellipsis & economy · L04 cohesion across a long turn · L05 speaking task ("give a cohesive 3-min talk") + review

### Unit 2 — Idioms & Natural Phrasing
- **Can-do:** understand and use common idioms, advanced phrasal verbs, and fixed expressions appropriately.
- **Focus:** high-frequency idioms (by theme), multi-word phrasal verbs, binomials (back and forth), collocation depth; register-appropriate idiom use.
- **Lessons:** L01 idioms by theme (work, emotions, time) · L02 advanced phrasal verbs · L03 fixed expressions & binomials · L04 using idioms appropriately (register/overuse) · L05 speaking task ("tell a story with natural idiom") + review

### Unit 3 — Nuance, Implicature & Reading Between the Lines
- **Can-do:** grasp implicit meaning; interpret tone, connotation, irony, and understatement; imply rather than state.
- **Focus:** implicature, connotation vs denotation, hedging for implication, irony/understatement, indirectness.
- **Lessons:** L01 connotation & word choice · L02 implying vs stating (indirectness) · L03 irony, understatement & humor · L04 interpreting tone in real input · L05 speaking task ("say it indirectly / read the subtext") + review

### Unit 4 — Stylistic Control & Register Mastery
- **Can-do:** shift deliberately between academic, professional, colloquial, and creative registers for effect.
- **Focus:** register features across styles; tone-shifting; nominalization (academic); vividness (creative); diplomacy (professional).
- **Lessons:** L01 academic style (nominalization, hedging, precision) · L02 colloquial & creative style · L03 diplomatic/professional register (advanced) · L04 shifting register for effect · L05 speaking task ("one idea, three styles") + review

### Unit 5 — Sophisticated Grammar (Inversion, Cleft, Fronting, Emphasis)
- **Can-do:** use marked structures for emphasis, focus, and stylistic effect accurately.
- **Focus:** inversion (negative adverbials: *Never have I…*), cleft sentences (*It was X that…, What I need is…*), fronting, emphatic *do*, subjunctive/formal structures.
- **Lessons:** L01 cleft sentences (it-cleft, what-cleft) · L02 inversion after negative adverbials · L03 fronting & emphatic *do* · L04 subjunctive & formal structures · L05 speaking task ("emphasize & impress accurately") + review

### Unit 6 — Understanding Demanding Input
- **Can-do:** follow fast, authentic, varied-accent speech, lectures, and implicit content; take notes; infer.
- **Focus:** listening to authentic/fast speech, various accents, lectures/podcasts, catching reductions, note-taking, inferring the unsaid.
- **Lessons:** L01 fast/connected authentic speech · L02 varied accents & registers · L03 lectures/podcasts + note-taking · L04 inferring meaning from demanding input · L05 speaking task ("summarize + respond to a demanding source") + review

### Unit 7 — Spontaneous Fluency & Extended Discourse
- **Can-do:** speak fluently and spontaneously at length; think on your feet; hold long, engaging turns.
- **Focus:** extended monologue, spontaneity strategies, storytelling with flair, self-repair without stopping, pacing.
- **Lessons:** L01 extended monologue (structure + flow) · L02 thinking on your feet (spontaneity) · L03 storytelling with flair · L04 self-repair & pacing (natural fluency) · L05 speaking task ("5-min spontaneous talk") + review

### Unit 8 — Academic & Professional Communication (Advanced)
- **Can-do:** build complex arguments, hedge academically, present authoritatively, and turn written content into polished speech.
- **Focus:** complex argumentation, academic hedging/caution, high-stakes presenting, Q&A defense, report/essay-to-speech.
- **Lessons:** L01 complex argumentation & evidence · L02 academic hedging & caution · L03 high-stakes presenting (advanced) · L04 defending under expert questioning · L05 speaking task ("deliver + defend a complex position") + review

### Unit 9 — Debate, Persuasion & Sensitive Topics
- **Can-do:** persuade, use rhetorical technique, and handle sensitive/controversial topics diplomatically at C1.
- **Focus:** rhetoric & persuasion (ethos/pathos/logos-lite), advanced diplomacy, nuance in disagreement, handling sensitive topics gracefully.
- **Lessons:** L01 rhetorical & persuasive technique · L02 advanced diplomacy & face-saving · L03 nuance in disagreement (steel-manning) · L04 handling sensitive/controversial topics · L05 speaking task ("persuade + handle pushback on a sensitive topic") + review

### Unit 10 — Putting It Together (C1 Consolidation & Assessment)
- **Can-do:** demonstrate C1 — flexible, fluent, spontaneous, nuanced English across social/academic/professional purposes, with near-native naturalness.
- **Focus:** integrated review + C1 summative + near-native polish clinic + self-monitoring for life.
- **Lessons:** L01 big review 1 (cohesion + idiom + nuance + register) · L02 big review 2 (sophisticated grammar + demanding input + persuasion) · L03 near-native polish clinic · L04 C1 mock (extended, wide-ranging) · L05 **C1 summative + milestone sample**

## 5. Exam Track (optional add-on) — modules

> For candidates needing certification. Not part of the core B2 promise. Files:
> `stage4/exam-track-toefl.md` (TOEFL iBT) and `stage4/exam-track-ielts.md` (IELTS Academic + General).

- **Shared approach:** diagnostic → format familiarization → per-section strategies → timed skill drills →
  full timed mock(s) → scored feedback (band/score) → targeted improvement loop. Reuses C1 core skills;
  adds test-specific technique + time management.
- **TOEFL iBT:** Reading, Listening, Speaking (integrated + independent), Writing (integrated + academic discussion); 0–120 scoring.
- **IELTS:** Listening, Reading (Academic/General), Writing (Task 1 + Task 2), Speaking (3 parts); 0–9 band scoring.
- **Delivery:** can run alongside C1 units or as a focused block after C1; graduates may take only the exam module.

## 6. Assessment placement

- **Before Unit 1:** confirm B2 / C1 readiness.
- **End of each C1 unit:** formative check (quiz + speaking task).
- **Unit 10, L05:** **C1 summative** (C1-level rubric — demanding, wide-ranging spoken performance) + a C1 milestone sample.
- **Exam Track:** official-style **mock TOEFL/IELTS** with **band/score reporting** + a targeted improvement plan.

## 7. Spaced-review threading

- Every unit recycles B2/B1 range; idioms/collocations/nuance are cumulative and revisited.
- New target language enters the review queue; Unit 10 is retrieval-heavy + integrative.
- Exam-track technique is drilled through repeated timed practice + mock cycles.

## 8. Build order (recommendation)

Build the C1 core in teaching order **Unit 1 → Unit 10**, then the **Exam Track** modules. Author every C1
lesson with the Lesson Template. Keep the GC-5 honesty framing throughout: Stage 4 is the advanced/optional
tier beyond the flagship B2 graduation; near-native is a ceiling, not a promise; the Exam Track is a
specialized add-on for candidates who need certification.
