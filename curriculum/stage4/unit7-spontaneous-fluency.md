# Stage 4 · Unit 7 — Spontaneous Fluency & Extended Discourse (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 6.1 (roadmap Stage 4) · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage4/00-stage4-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** none (immersion). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can speak fluently and
> spontaneously at length — think on their feet, tell stories with flair, and self-repair without stopping — recorded.
> **Focus spine:** extended monologue + spontaneity + storytelling flair + self-repair/pacing · **Accent Lab:**
> fluency + prosody over long stretches · **Mindset:** trusting yourself unscripted.

L04 self-repair & pacing · L05 speaking task ("5-min spontaneous talk") + review.

(hook/pacing/payoff), a self-repair phrase set, a model fluent-speaker clip (American), a Google Form per lesson.

## S4-U7-L01 — Extended Monologue (Structure + Flow)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 7 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1 cohesion.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can deliver a structured extended monologue (3–4 min) that flows.
- The learner can organize spontaneously (open → develop → close) without a script.
- The learner can sustain coherence over length.

**Target language:** monologue structuring + cohesion (from U1). **Accent Lab focus:** prosody + pacing across a long turn.
**Mindset:** length + flow = advanced control.

**Materials:** extended-monologue prompts; a model long turn; a light structure card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: cohesion toolkit (U1) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Model 3–4 min monologue; note its shape | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Spontaneous structuring (open/develop/close); brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Pacing + prosody over a long turn | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Deliver a 3-min monologue with light structure; peer feedback | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Deliver a 3-min monologue on a prompt; record | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check flow + structure | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 3-min monologue | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — structure card + prep. Stretch — 4 min, minimal prep.
**Success check:** a coherent, flowing 3-min monologue. **Queue:** extended discourse recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** the goal is sustained flow, not perfection — reward keeping-going + coherence. Light structure beats scripting.

## S4-U7-L02 — Thinking on Your Feet (Spontaneity)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 7 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can respond to unexpected prompts/questions immediately and coherently.
- The learner can use time-buying + structuring strategies in real time.
- The learner can stay composed without preparation.

**Target language:** spontaneity strategies (buy time, frame the answer, generalize then specify). **Accent Lab focus:**
composed prosody under pressure; natural fillers. **Mindset:** trust your English — you have enough to improvise.

**Materials:** impromptu topic cards; a model impromptu response; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: monologue structuring (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a composed impromptu answer (frame → develop → close) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Real-time framing + time-buying strategies; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Composed prosody; natural fillers | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Draw a card, respond 60–90s with no prep; swap | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Respond to an unseen prompt ~90s; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Did they stay composed + coherent? | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an impromptu response (no prep) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — a few seconds to think + easier topics. Stretch — abstract topics, zero prep.
**Success check:** composed, coherent impromptu response. **Queue:** spontaneity feeds L05 + U9 + the C1 summative.
**Coach notes:** this is the anti-freeze/anti-script lesson — reward composure + flow over polish. Build the habit of framing before diving in.

## S4-U7-L03 — Storytelling With Flair

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 7 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can tell an engaging story with a hook, pacing, vivid detail, and a payoff.
- The learner can use suspense, emphasis, and idiom for engagement.
- The learner can hold an audience.

**Target language:** storytelling craft (hook, pacing, vivid verbs/idiom, payoff) + delivery. **Accent Lab focus:**
dynamic prosody (pace/pause/emphasis) for engagement. **Mindset:** be a compelling speaker, not just a correct one.

**Materials:** a storytelling-craft card; a compelling-story clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: vivid/colloquial style (U4) + idiom (U2) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a well-told story (hook → build → payoff) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Storytelling craft: hook, pacing, vivid detail, payoff; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Dynamic prosody for engagement (pace/pause/emphasis) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Craft + tell a story with a hook + payoff; peer feedback | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell an engaging ~2-min story; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check engagement + delivery | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an engaging short story | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — craft card + prep. Stretch — an impromptu compelling story.
**Success check:** an engaging story with clear craft + dynamic delivery. **Queue:** feeds L05.
**Coach notes:** engagement is a real C1 (and life) skill — reward hook/pacing/delivery; this is where "correct" becomes "compelling."

## S4-U7-L04 — Self-Repair & Pacing (Natural Fluency)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 7 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can self-repair smoothly (rephrase, correct, clarify) without stopping.
- The learner can pace speech naturally (pausing for effect, not hesitation).
- The learner can sound fluent even when adjusting mid-sentence.

**Target language:** self-repair phrases (*or rather, what I mean is, let me rephrase, sorry, I should say*).
**Accent Lab focus:** natural pausing vs hesitation; smooth repair. **Mindset:** fluency includes graceful recovery.

**Materials:** a self-repair phrase set; a model fluent-repair clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: spontaneity strategies (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a fluent speaker self-repairing smoothly | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Self-repair phrases; pausing for effect vs hesitation; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Smooth repair; effective pausing | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Practice mid-sentence repair without stopping | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak ~2 min, self-repairing gracefully as needed; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check smoothness of repair + pacing | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a talk demonstrating graceful self-repair | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — repair phrase set. Stretch — repair fluently on a difficult topic.
**Success check:** graceful self-repair + natural pacing. **Queue:** feeds L05 + the C1 summative.
**Coach notes:** even advanced speakers self-repair — the skill is doing it *smoothly*. Reframe hesitation as controlled pausing.

## S4-U7-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "5-Minute Spontaneous Talk" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 7 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can give a ~5-min spontaneous talk: structured, engaging, self-repaired, well-paced.
- The learner can field questions and continue fluently.
- The learner records their **Unit-7 spontaneous talk**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-7 fluency skills. **Accent Lab focus:** prosody/pacing over 5 min; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** trusting yourself unscripted — the heart of real fluency.

**Materials:** topic prompts (minimal prep); a model spontaneous talk; a checklist card (structure / engagement / graceful repair / pacing / ~5 min);
recording prompt; Unit-7 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: monologue structure, spontaneity, storytelling, repair | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean ~5-min spontaneous talk | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 3 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills pacing/prosody + a residual sound | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Brief prep + rehearse; peer feedback | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~5-min spontaneous talk + field questions | 9 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-7 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — a couple of minutes' prep + notes. Stretch — zero prep, abstract topic.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 7):** a ~5-min spontaneous talk — structured, engaging, fluent, self-repaired (rubric: Fluency + Coherence at C1). **Queue:** directly feeds the C1 summative.
**Coach notes:** ~5 min spontaneous is demanding — reward sustained flow + composure + engagement over perfection. Strong C1 proof clip.
