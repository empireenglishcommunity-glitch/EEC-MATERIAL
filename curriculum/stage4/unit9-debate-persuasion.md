## S4-U9-L01 — Rhetorical & Persuasive Technique

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 9 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U5 emphasis + U8 argument.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use rhetorical devices to persuade (rule of three, rhetorical questions, contrast, appeals).
- The learner can structure a persuasive case (credibility + logic + resonance).
- The learner can move an audience while staying honest.

**Target language:** rhetorical devices (rule of three, rhetorical questions, antithesis) + appeal language (credibility/logic/emotion — lightly).
**Accent Lab focus:** persuasive prosody (build, emphasis, pause). **Mindset:** persuade with integrity.

**Materials:** a persuasion-technique card; a persuasive-speech clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: U5 emphasis (clefts/inversion) + U8 argument | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a persuasive speech using rhetorical devices | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Rhetorical devices + appeals + persuasive structure; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Persuasive prosody (build/emphasis/pause) | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Add rhetorical devices to a persuasive point | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Persuade on a topic (~2 min) using ≥2 devices; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check persuasive technique + honesty | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a persuasive short pitch | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — technique card. Stretch — a polished, layered persuasive pitch.
**Success check:** a persuasive turn using rhetorical technique. **Queue:** persuasion feeds L05.
**Coach notes:** honesty guardrail — persuasion, not manipulation. Reward technique + integrity; avoid over-emotional appeals.

## S4-U9-L02 — Advanced Diplomacy & Face-Saving

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 9 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can disagree/deliver hard messages while preserving the other's "face."
- The learner can use advanced diplomatic devices (acknowledging, reframing, softening).
- The learner can keep relationships intact under tension.

**Target language:** face-saving language (*I completely understand where you're coming from; that's a valid concern; perhaps we could look at it differently*).
**Accent Lab focus:** warm, measured diplomatic prosody. **Mindset:** grace under tension.

**Materials:** an advanced-diplomacy set; tension scenario cards; a clip; Google Form.

| 5 | Guided output | Deliver hard messages diplomatically (scenario cards) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Handle a tense scenario with face-saving diplomacy; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check diplomacy + relationship preservation | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a face-saving diplomatic message | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — diplomacy set. Stretch — a high-tension, high-stakes scenario.
**Success check:** hard message delivered with preserved face. **Queue:** feeds L04/L05.
**Coach notes:** face-saving is culturally universal but linguistically specific in English — model the acknowledge-reframe-soften pattern.

## S4-U9-L03 — Nuance in Disagreement (Steel-Manning)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 9 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can represent an opposing view fairly (steel-man) before disagreeing.
- The learner can express partial agreement and fine distinctions.
- The learner can disagree with nuance, not binary opposition.

**Target language:** steel-manning + nuance (*The strongest version of your point is…; I agree up to a point; the distinction I'd draw is…; it's more nuanced than…*).
**Accent Lab focus:** measured, fair-minded prosody. **Mindset:** intellectual honesty wins respect.

**Materials:** a steel-manning/nuance set; debate prompts; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: diplomacy (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a speaker steel-mans then disagrees with nuance | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Steel-manning + partial agreement + fine distinctions; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Fair-minded, measured prosody | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Steel-man an opposing view, then disagree with nuance | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Address a view you disagree with (steel-man + nuanced rebuttal); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check fairness + nuance | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a steel-man + nuanced disagreement | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — sentence frames. Stretch — a genuinely contested topic.
**Success check:** fair steel-man + nuanced disagreement. **Queue:** feeds L04/L05.
**Coach notes:** steel-manning is a sophisticated, respected move — reward fair representation of the other side before rebuttal.

## S4-U9-L04 — Handling Sensitive / Controversial Topics

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 9 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can discuss sensitive/controversial topics respectfully and diplomatically.
- The learner can navigate disagreement without giving or taking offense.
- The learner can gracefully decline or de-escalate when appropriate.

**Target language:** sensitive-topic navigation (*I think reasonable people can differ; I'd rather not go too deep, but…; let's keep it respectful*).
**Accent Lab focus:** calm, respectful prosody. **Mindset:** contribution — add value, avoid harm.

**Materials:** a sensitive-topics guidance note (respect, de-escalation, opting out); scenario cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: steel-manning + diplomacy | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a respectful discussion of a sensitive topic | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Respect + de-escalation + graceful opt-out; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Calm, respectful prosody | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Navigate a sensitive scenario respectfully | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Discuss a mildly sensitive topic respectfully; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check respect + de-escalation | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a respectful take on a sensitive topic | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — guidance note + frames. Stretch — de-escalate a heated exchange.
**Success check:** respectful, diplomatic handling of a sensitive topic. **Queue:** feeds L05.
**Coach notes:** keep chosen topics appropriate + non-inflammatory; the skill is *how* to discuss sensitively, not the topic itself. Model respect + the option to gracefully decline.

## S4-U9-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Persuade + Handle Pushback on a Sensitive Topic" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 9 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can persuade on a topic (~3 min) and handle pushback with diplomacy, steel-manning, and nuance.
- The learner can stay respectful and composed on sensitive ground.
- The learner records their **Unit-9 persuade-and-handle-pushback**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-9 skills. **Accent Lab focus:** persuasive + measured prosody; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** using your voice well — persuade with integrity, disagree with grace.

**Materials:** a model performance; a checklist card (persuasive technique / steel-man / nuanced disagreement / face-saving / composure);
recording prompt; Unit-9 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: rhetoric, diplomacy, steel-manning, sensitive-topic navigation | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean persuade-and-handle-pushback | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills persuasive/measured prosody + a residual sound | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse; peers prepare pushback | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Persuade (~3 min) + handle pushback; record | 8 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the performance; complete Unit-9 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — strong pushback, a more contested topic.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 9):** persuasive turn + diplomatic, nuanced handling of pushback (rubric: Interaction + Coherence + Range at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** the social/rhetorical peak of C1 — reward persuasion *with* diplomacy and composure. A compelling proof clip of mature, nuanced English.
