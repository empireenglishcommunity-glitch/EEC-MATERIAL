## S4-U3-L01 — Connotation & Word Choice

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 3 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 vocab range.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can distinguish connotation (positive/neutral/negative) among near-synonyms.
- The learner can choose words for precise tone/attitude.
- The learner can shade meaning deliberately.

**Target language:** connotation sets (*slim/thin/skinny; confident/assertive/arrogant; frugal/careful/stingy; determined/stubborn*).
**Accent Lab focus:** how word choice + stress convey attitude. **Mindset:** precision of word = precision of thought.

**Materials:** a connotation set; a word-choice-for-tone task; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 synonyms | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip/text where near-synonyms shift the attitude | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Connotation scales (+/0/–) within synonym groups; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress + word choice carry attitude | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Choose words to convey a target attitude; re-shade sentences | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Describe the same person 3 ways (+/0/– connotation); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check connotation accuracy | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 near-synonym choices + their connotations | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — connotation sets. Stretch — subtle near-synonym distinctions.
**Success check:** deliberate, accurate connotation choices. **Queue:** connotation recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** connotation errors ("stubborn" for "determined" about a boss) can offend — build sensitivity to the +/– loading of near-synonyms.

## S4-U3-L02 — Implying vs Stating (Indirectness)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 3 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can imply meaning rather than state it directly (implicature).
- The learner can use indirectness for politeness/diplomacy/effect.
- The learner can infer what an indirect speaker means.

**Target language:** indirectness devices (understated hints, "It's a bit warm in here" = please open a window;
"I'm not sure that's the best idea"). **Accent Lab focus:** intonation that signals the implied message.
**Mindset:** much real meaning is between the lines — say + hear it.

**Materials:** a direct↔implied set; an inference clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: connotation (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: indirect requests/opinions; infer the real meaning | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Implicature: meaning beyond the words; when/why we imply; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Intonation that carries the implication | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Convert direct → implied; interpret implied → intended | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Imply 3 requests/opinions indirectly; partner infers; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check clarity of implication + inference | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 2 indirect statements + their intended meaning | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — direct↔implied set. Stretch — layered/subtle implication.
**Success check:** effective implying + accurate inferring. **Queue:** implicature feeds U9 (sensitive topics).
**Coach notes:** cultural: English (esp. British/professional) leans indirect; over-directness can seem rude. Build awareness without over-generalizing cultures.

## S4-U3-L03 — Irony, Understatement & Humor

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 3 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can recognize and (carefully) use irony and understatement.
- The learner can catch humor/sarcasm signaled by tone.
- The learner can respond appropriately to non-literal speech.

**Target language:** irony/understatement patterns ("Not bad at all" = very good; "That went well" [sarcastic]);
humor signals. **Accent Lab focus:** the tone/intonation that flags irony vs literal. **Mindset:** getting the joke = belonging.

**Materials:** an irony/understatement clip bank; a literal-vs-ironic set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: implicature (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clips: ironic/understated/humorous lines; spot the real meaning | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | How tone flags irony/understatement; risks of misuse; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Ironic vs literal intonation | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Interpret ironic lines; produce mild understatement | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Respond to situations with understatement/light irony; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check interpretation + safe use | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an understatement/ironic response + its literal meaning | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — clip bank + glosses. Stretch — produce subtle irony safely.
**Success check:** recognizes + safely uses irony/understatement. **Queue:** feeds U6 (demanding input) & U7.
**Coach notes:** interpreting irony matters more than producing it (misfired sarcasm can offend). Prioritize comprehension; teach production cautiously.

## S4-U3-L04 — Interpreting Tone in Real Input

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 3 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can infer a speaker's attitude/tone from authentic input (word choice + intonation + context).
- The learner can identify implied stance in opinion pieces/interviews.
- The learner can summarize not just *what* was said but *how/why*.

**Target language:** attitude/tone vocabulary (skeptical, enthusiastic, cautious, dismissive, tongue-in-cheek).
**Accent Lab focus:** reading intonation for attitude. **Mindset:** deep listening — hear the stance, not just the words.

**Materials:** authentic opinion/interview clips; a tone-labeling task; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: connotation + irony | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Authentic clip; learners infer the speaker's attitude | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Cues for tone (words + intonation + context); brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Map intonation → attitude | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Label tone in excerpts; justify with evidence | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Summarize a clip's content + the speaker's attitude; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tone inference + evidence | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a "what they said + how they felt about it" summary | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — tone vocabulary + guided cues. Stretch — subtle/mixed attitudes.
**Success check:** accurate tone inference with evidence. **Queue:** feeds U6/U9.
**Coach notes:** this is receptive C1 nuance — a bridge to Unit 6 (demanding input). Reward evidence-based inference.

## S4-U3-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Say It Indirectly / Read the Subtext" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 3 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can handle a nuanced exchange (~3 min): imply meaning, choose connotation deliberately, and interpret a partner's subtext.
- The learner can navigate a delicate situation indirectly.
- The learner records their **Unit-3 nuance performance**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-3 nuance. **Accent Lab focus:** intonation carrying implied meaning; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** nuance = cultural + linguistic maturity.

**Materials:** delicate-situation cards; a model nuanced exchange; a checklist card (deliberate connotation / an implication / interpret subtext / appropriate tone);
recording prompt; Unit-3 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: connotation, implicature, irony, tone | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean nuanced exchange | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills implication intonation + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Handle a delicate situation indirectly in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record a nuanced exchange (imply + interpret) | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the performance; complete Unit-3 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — situation cards + checklist. Stretch — a subtle, high-stakes delicate exchange.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 3):** a nuanced ~3-min exchange with deliberate connotation + implication + subtext reading (rubric at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U9/U10.
**Coach notes:** nuance is the heart of C1 — reward subtlety, appropriateness, and accurate subtext reading over surface complexity.
