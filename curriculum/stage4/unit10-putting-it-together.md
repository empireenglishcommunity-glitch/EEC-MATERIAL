## S4-U10-L01 — Big Review 1: Cohesion + Idiom + Nuance + Register (Retrieval)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 10 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1–U4.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and integrate cohesion, idiom/natural phrasing, nuance/implicature, and register control.
- The learner can produce polished, natural extended speech.
- The learner can self-correct residual lexical/discourse issues.

**Target language:** integrated U1–U4 range. **Accent Lab focus:** naturalness across a long turn; record-and-compare.
**Mindset:** you command the discourse + lexical layer of advanced English.

**Materials:** retrieval decks (cohesion/idiom/nuance/register); mixed prompts; the C1 rubric card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid retrieval across U1–U4 | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A model that integrates cohesion + idiom + nuance + register | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-surface residual issues (over-idiom, register slips, weak cohesion) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Naturalness + prosody; record-and-compare | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Integrated prompt combining ≥3 of the four areas | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak ~2 min integrating the areas naturally; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self-correct against the residual list | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an integrated ~2-min talk | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — retrieval decks + frames. Stretch — spontaneous, abstract topic.
**Success check:** natural integration of ≥3 areas. **Queue:** feeds the L05 summative.
**Coach notes:** retrieval + integration — reward naturalness + appropriateness over showing off. Log residuals for L03.

## S4-U10-L03 — Near-Native Polish Clinic (Naturalness + Self-Monitoring)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 10 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: all units.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can target their final residual sounds/prosody features for near-native polish.
- The learner can self-monitor and self-correct naturalness in real time.
- The learner can sustain natural rhythm, thought groups, and emphasis.

**Target language:** each learner's residual features + prosody. **Accent Lab focus:** near-native prosody/naturalness;
self-monitoring. **GC-5 honesty guardrail:** near-native is an **aspirational ceiling for the most dedicated — never a promise**; celebrate clarity + naturalness gains, never shame a residual accent.
**Mindset:** self-monitoring is the lifelong engine — you keep improving after the course.

**Materials:** each learner's cumulative flags; prosody/naturalness drill sets; record-and-compare vs the full journey; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Learners state their final residual features | 4 min |
| 2 | Input (perceive) | Near-native models for each target | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-cue articulation/prosody + the self-monitoring routine | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab (produce) | Targeted drills → connected speech; record-and-compare | 10 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Speak + self-flag naturalness slips in real time | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | 2-min spontaneous speech; self-correct live | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to the full arc (Day-1 → now) — the transformation | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a record-and-compare of your top target vs an earlier clip | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — 1–2 features + naturalness. Stretch — near-native polish in spontaneous speech.
**Success check:** measurable naturalness gains + active self-monitoring. **Queue:** any residuals are ongoing/lifelong (aspirational).
**Coach notes:** honesty first (GC-5) — the win is **clear, confident, natural** English and a self-monitoring habit for life, not "native." Frame progress against the learner's own journey.

## S4-U10-L05 — C1 Summative + Milestone Speaking Sample

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 10 · ~50 min · recorded + live (assessment, English-only) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can demonstrate the **C1 exit criteria**: flexible, fluent, spontaneous, nuanced English across
  social/academic/professional purposes; understand demanding/implicit/idiomatic language; express with precision + near-native naturalness.
- The learner completes the **Stage-4 summative quiz** (range/nuance/accuracy).
- The learner records the **C1 milestone sample** on the standard (extended) prompt.

**Target language:** the full C1 range. **Grammar/lexis:** full range + nuance + idiom, assessed for accuracy + naturalness.
**Accent Lab focus:** clear + confident + natural (scored on Pronunciation — clarity/naturalness, **not** "native").
**Arabic support:** 0% (full immersion). **Mindset:** Mastery & Contribution — plan lifelong maintenance + mentoring; consider the Exam Track for certification.

**Materials:** the **Stage-4 summative quiz**; the **C1 assessment protocol** (extended wide-ranging performance);
the C1-level rubric; the **standard milestone prompt** (before/after §2, extended for C1); consent record.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up | Light retrieval + reassurance; a celebration of range, not a filter | 4 min |
| 2 | Summative quiz | Range/nuance/accuracy recognition + short production (full Stage-4) | 12 min |
| 3 | Assessment setup | Explain the performance format + rubric (English-only) | 3 min |
| 4 | Accent tune-up | 60–90s personal top-1 feature warm-up | 2 min |
| 5 | C1 performance | Extended wide-ranging performance (monologue + discussion + source response), recorded + rubric-scored | 22 min |
| 6 | Milestone sample | Record the **standard prompt** (extended) — the C1 point on the proof timeline | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback + result | C1-level rubric result; wins + next path (Exam Track / maintenance) | 5 min |
| 8 | Celebrate / next | Save the milestone clip (with consent); plan lifelong maintenance + optional exam track | 2 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — prep + a slightly shorter performance still covering the criteria. Stretch — fully spontaneous, wider/technical range.
**Success check (Stage-4 C1 summative):** meets the **C1 threshold** across the rubric (esp. Range, Fluency, Coherence, Accuracy, Pronunciation-naturalness); quiz passed at the set band. **Queue:** graduates to lifelong maintenance/mentoring; candidates proceed to the Exam Track for certification.
**Coach notes:** extend the proof timeline — Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → B1 → B2 → **C1** (with consent, an exceptional testimonial of the full arc). Honesty (GC-5): the win is **advanced, flexible, natural English** — not "native." Frame Stage 4 as the advanced ceiling beyond the flagship B2 graduation.
