# Stage 4 · Unit 2 — Idioms & Natural Phrasing (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 6.1 (roadmap Stage 4) · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage4/00-stage4-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** none (immersion). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can understand and use common
> idioms, advanced phrasal verbs, and fixed expressions appropriately — sounding natural, not "textbook" — recorded.
> **Lexis spine:** idioms + advanced phrasal verbs + fixed expressions/binomials · **Accent Lab:** idioms as
> stress chunks, natural rhythm · **Mindset:** collecting language for life.

**Unit lessons:** L01 idioms by theme · L02 advanced phrasal verbs · L03 fixed expressions & binomials ·

a fixed-expression + binomial set, a model natural-speech clip (American), a Google Form per lesson.

## S4-U2-L01 — Idioms by Theme

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 2 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 collocations.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can understand and use ~15 high-frequency idioms grouped by theme.
- The learner can infer idiom meaning from context.
- The learner can use idioms naturally in speech.

**Target language:** idioms by theme (work: *on the same page, the bottom line*; emotions: *over the moon, fed up*;
time: *in the nick of time, around the clock*; success: *pay off, a game changer*). **Accent Lab focus:** idioms as
single stress units; natural rhythm. **Mindset:** start an idiom notebook — collect from real input for life.

**Materials:** a themed idiom set; a context-inference set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 collocations | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip rich in themed idioms; infer meanings from context | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Idiom meaning + form (fixed); themes; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Idiom stress/rhythm as chunks | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Use themed idioms in sentences/mini-contexts | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk about work/emotions using ≥4 idioms; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check idiom form + appropriate use | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a short talk using 4 themed idioms | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — idiom set + glosses. Stretch — infer + use idioms from unglossed input.
**Success check:** ≥4 idioms used correctly + naturally. **Queue:** idioms recycle this unit + cumulatively.
**Coach notes:** idioms are fixed — a wrong word breaks them. Teach as whole chunks; prioritize high-frequency, register-neutral ones first.

## S4-U2-L02 — Advanced Phrasal Verbs

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 2 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use advanced/multi-word phrasal verbs (including three-part ones).
- The learner can handle separability and choose phrasal vs Latinate verbs by register.
- The learner can use them fluently in context.

**Target language:** advanced phrasal verbs (*put up with, come down to, get around to, run out of, look down on,
cut back on*); separable/inseparable; register (phrasal = informal). **Accent Lab focus:** stress on the particle;
linking. **Mindset:** phrasal verbs = the sound of natural English.

**Materials:** an advanced phrasal-verb set; a separability chart; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: themed idioms (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: natural speech with advanced phrasal verbs | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Meaning + separability + register (phrasal vs formal single verb); brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Particle stress ("put UP with"); linking | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Replace formal verbs with phrasal ones; handle separability | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk using ≥4 advanced phrasal verbs; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check form + separability + register | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a short text using 4 advanced phrasal verbs | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — phrasal-verb set + chart. Stretch — three-part phrasal verbs in context.
**Success check:** ≥4 advanced phrasal verbs used correctly. **Queue:** phrasal verbs recycle cumulatively.
**Coach notes:** separability + object placement (pronoun goes in the middle: "put it up with" → no; "put up with it" → yes) is tricky — drill the common patterns.

## S4-U2-L03 — Fixed Expressions & Binomials

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 2 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use fixed expressions and binomials (*back and forth, sooner or later, more or less*).
- The learner can deploy discourse-level fixed phrases (*to cut a long story short, at the end of the day*).
- The learner can sound idiomatic and fluent.

**Target language:** binomials + fixed expressions. **Accent Lab focus:** fixed rhythm/stress of the expressions.
**Mindset:** these phrases are pre-assembled fluency — memorize + deploy.

**Materials:** a fixed-expression + binomial set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: phrasal verbs (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip using binomials + discourse fixed phrases | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Fixed word order + meaning; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Fixed stress/rhythm | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Insert fixed expressions/binomials into speech | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk using ≥4 fixed expressions/binomials; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check fixed forms + naturalness | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a short talk using 4 fixed expressions | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — expression set. Stretch — weave several into a coherent turn.
**Success check:** ≥4 fixed expressions/binomials used correctly. **Queue:** recycle cumulatively.
**Coach notes:** binomials have fixed order ("back and forth", not "forth and back") — teach as immovable chunks.

## S4-U2-L04 — Using Idioms Appropriately (Register & Overuse)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 2 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can judge when idioms/phrasal verbs are appropriate (register, audience, frequency).
- The learner can avoid over-use and dated/awkward idioms.
- The learner can integrate idiom naturally, not forcefully.

**Target language:** register-appropriate idiom use; frequency judgment. **Accent Lab focus:** natural integration (not spotlighting the idiom).
**Mindset:** taste + judgment — advanced users know *when* to use language.

**Materials:** appropriate/inappropriate examples; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: idioms/phrasal/fixed (L01–L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Contrast: natural idiom use vs forced/over-use | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Register + frequency judgment; avoid dated idioms; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Integrate idiom smoothly (don't over-stress it) | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Edit over-idiomatic speech to natural levels | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Give a talk with tasteful, appropriate idiom use; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check appropriateness + naturalness | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a naturally-idiomatic (not forced) short talk | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — appropriate-use examples. Stretch — self-edit for over-use.
**Success check:** tasteful, appropriate idiom use. **Queue:** feeds L05/U4 (register).
**Coach notes:** the C1 skill is *judgment* — a few well-placed idioms beat a stream of them. Reward restraint + fit.

## S4-U2-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Tell a Story With Natural Idiom" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 2 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can tell an engaging ~3-min story using idioms, phrasal verbs, and fixed expressions naturally + appropriately.
- The learner can answer questions while maintaining natural phrasing.
- The learner records their **Unit-2 idiomatic story**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-2 lexis. **Accent Lab focus:** idiom/phrasal rhythm; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** natural phrasing = the sound of a real English user.

**Materials:** a model idiomatic story; a checklist card (idioms / phrasal verbs / fixed expressions / appropriate use / natural delivery);
recording prompt; Unit-2 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: idioms/phrasal/fixed expressions | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean idiomatic story | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills idiom rhythm + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse the story; peer Q&A | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~3-min idiomatic story + field a question | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the story; complete Unit-2 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — spontaneous, richly idiomatic.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 2):** a ~3-min story with natural, appropriate idiom (rubric: Range + Fluency at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** reward *natural, appropriate* idiom — not maximum idiom. Overloading is the classic mistake; taste is the target.
