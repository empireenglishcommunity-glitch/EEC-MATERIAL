## 0. About the test (orientation)

- **TOEFL iBT** = internet-based academic English test (US-oriented; universities, visas, some employers).
- **Four sections**, ~2 hours total, integrated-skills design: **Reading · Listening · Speaking · Writing.**
- **Scoring:** each section **0–30** → total **0–120**. Common targets: ~80 (many universities), ~100 (competitive).
- **Key traits:** academic content (lectures, campus situations, texts); **integrated tasks** (read/listen → speak/write);
  strict timing; note-taking allowed and essential; typed responses.

> These are stable format facts; **always confirm the current official format, timing, and question counts on
> the official ETS TOEFL site before a test date**, as the test is periodically updated.

## 1. Module goals & structure

- **Goal:** the candidate can navigate the TOEFL iBT format confidently, apply per-section strategies, manage
  time, take effective notes, and produce test-ready integrated + independent responses — reaching their target band.
- **Approach (shared with IELTS module):** **diagnostic → format familiarization → per-section strategy →
  timed skill drills → full timed mock(s) → scored feedback → targeted improvement loop.**
- **Sessions (≈ lessons):**
  - E-T01 Orientation + diagnostic (mini full test) → baseline score estimate + plan
  - E-T02 Reading — strategies + timed practice
  - E-T03 Listening — note-taking + timed practice
  - E-T04 Speaking — the 4 tasks (templates + timing)
  - E-T05 Writing — integrated + academic-discussion tasks
  - E-T06 Full timed mock #1 + scored feedback
  - E-T07 Targeted improvement (weak sections) + full timed mock #2 → final readiness

---

## E-T01 — Orientation + Diagnostic

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / Exam Track · ~90 min · prerequisites: strong B2/C1.
**Objectives:** understand the format + scoring; take a short diagnostic across all four sections; get a baseline
estimate + a personalized plan and target.
**Flow:** format walkthrough (sections/timing/scoring) → short diagnostic (1 reading passage, 1 lecture, 1 speaking task, 1 writing task, timed) → self/coach scoring vs official-style descriptors → identify strengths/weaknesses → set target score + study plan.
**Assets:** format one-pager; a mini diagnostic set; a score-tracking sheet.
**Coach notes:** the diagnostic sets honest expectations and focuses effort on the lowest-scoring sections.

---

## E-T02 — Reading (Strategies + Timed Practice)

**Metadata:** ~75 min. **Objectives:** apply reading strategies (skim for structure, scan for detail, question-type tactics) under time.
**Question types:** factual/negative-factual, inference, vocabulary-in-context, sentence simplification, insert-text, prose-summary/table.
**Strategy:** read for structure first; manage ~per-passage timing; eliminate distractors; don't over-read; use paragraph mapping.
**Flow:** type-by-type tactics → guided passage → timed passage → review errors by type → log accuracy per type.
**Assets:** question-type tactic card; timed passages; an error-by-type log.
**Coach notes:** time management + question-type recognition beat vocabulary alone — drill both.

---

## E-T03 — Listening (Note-Taking + Timed Practice)

**Metadata:** ~75 min. **Objectives:** take effective notes on lectures/conversations; answer detail, function, attitude, and organization questions.
**Content:** academic lectures + campus conversations; questions on main idea, detail, purpose, attitude, inference, organization.
**Strategy:** structured note-taking (main/support/signpost — reuse C1 Unit 6); listen for emphasis/attitude; predict question focus; don't panic on missed detail.
**Flow:** note-taking system → guided lecture + notes → timed set → review → refine notes.
**Assets:** a note-taking template; lecture/conversation clips; an answer key with rationale.
**Coach notes:** builds directly on C1 Unit 6 (demanding input) — the note system is the core skill.

---

## E-T04 — Speaking (The Tasks: Templates + Timing)

**Metadata:** ~75 min. **Objectives:** deliver clear, well-organized spoken responses within strict prep/speak times.
**Tasks:** an **independent** task (state + support an opinion) + **integrated** tasks (read/listen → summarize/synthesize + respond).
**Strategy:** light templates (frame → points → example → conclusion); ~15s prep habits; hit the time; clear delivery > perfect grammar; use notes.
**Accent Lab focus:** clarity, pacing, and emphasis under time pressure (reuse C1 prosody work).
**Flow:** task-by-task templates → guided attempts → timed attempts (record) → self/peer score vs descriptors → refine delivery + timing.
**Assets:** response templates; timers; the speaking rubric descriptors; recording setup.
**Coach notes:** delivery + organization + timing are the levers; reuse C1 spontaneity + prosody skills. Templates reduce panic — don't over-rely.

---

## E-T05 — Writing (Integrated + Academic-Discussion Tasks)

**Metadata:** ~75 min. **Objectives:** produce a well-structured **integrated** response (read + listen → synthesize) and an **academic-discussion** response (state + support a view in a discussion context) within time.
**Strategy:** integrated = accurately relate the lecture to the reading (often contrast); discussion = clear position + reasons + example, engaging with others' points; paragraphing, cohesion (reuse C1 Unit 1), academic register (reuse C1 Unit 4/8 hedging).
**Flow:** task structures + model responses → guided outline → timed writing → score vs descriptors → revise.
**Assets:** task templates; model responses; the writing rubric descriptors.
**Coach notes:** typed, timed — reuse academic register + cohesion + argument from C1. Accuracy + task fulfillment + organization drive the score.

---

## E-T06 — Full Timed Mock #1 + Scored Feedback

**Metadata:** ~140 min (full test) + review. **Objectives:** complete a full, timed mock under realistic conditions; get a scored estimate + a targeted improvement plan.
**Flow:** full mock (all four sections, official timing) → score each section vs descriptors → section-by-section debrief → prioritize the 1–2 weakest sections for E-T07.
**Assets:** a full mock set; per-section scoring sheets; a debrief template.
**Coach notes:** realistic conditions (timing, no interruptions) are essential — the mock reveals stamina + timing gaps, not just skill gaps.

---

## E-T07 — Targeted Improvement + Full Timed Mock #2 (Final Readiness)

**Metadata:** ~140 min + focused drills. **Objectives:** close the gaps on the weakest sections; complete a second full mock; confirm readiness vs the target score.
**Flow:** focused drills on weak sections (from E-T06) → full timed mock #2 → compare scores to mock #1 + target → final readiness call + test-day plan (logistics, timing, nerves).
**Assets:** targeted drill sets; a second full mock; a test-day checklist.
**Coach notes:** confirm the candidate consistently hits (or is close to) their target across two mocks before booking; give an honest readiness assessment.

---

## Assessment & reporting

- **Diagnostic + 2 full mocks** with **section scores (0–30) + total (0–120)** vs the candidate's target.
- Track accuracy by question type (Reading/Listening) and rubric scores (Speaking/Writing) across attempts.
- Feed results to the Cohort Tracker; produce a final **readiness report** (honest go/not-yet-ready call + gaps).
