## S4-U6-L01 — Fast / Connected Authentic Speech

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 6 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 connected speech.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can follow fast, connected native speech (reductions, linking, elision).
- The learner can catch key information despite gaps.
- The learner can re-tune the ear to natural speed.

**Target language:** connected-speech features in reception (gonna/wanna, linking, dropped sounds). **Accent Lab focus:**
recognizing reductions by ear (production ↔ perception loop). **Mindset:** you don't need every word — get the message.

**Materials:** a fast authentic clip; a "what did you catch?" task; a transcript for checking; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 reductions (production) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Fast authentic clip (first listen: gist; second: detail) | 8 min |
| 3 | Notice | Which features caused difficulty (reductions/linking); check vs transcript; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Perceive → produce the tricky reductions | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Predict + verify reduced forms; shadow a fast segment | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Summarize the clip's gist + one detail; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check comprehension accuracy | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Listen to a fast authentic clip; post the gist + 2 details | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — slower first, transcript support. Stretch — no transcript, faster clips.
**Success check:** gist + key details from fast speech. **Queue:** listening recycles this unit + cumulatively.
**Coach notes:** the production ↔ perception link is key — the reductions they've learned to say (B2) are the ones to now recognize. Build tolerance for gaps.

## S4-U6-L02 — Varied Accents & Registers

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 6 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can understand a range of English accents (American/British/Australian/global) and registers.
- The learner can adjust listening strategies to unfamiliar accents.
- The learner can follow formal and casual speech alike.

**Target language:** accent/register variation (reception). **Accent Lab focus:** noting accent differences without
losing meaning; anchoring on rhythm/content words. **Mindset:** the world speaks many Englishes — understand them all.

**Materials:** a multi-accent clip bank; an accent-features note; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: fast-speech strategies (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clips in several accents/registers; catch the message | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Key accent differences; strategies to adjust; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Anchor on rhythm/content words across accents | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Summarize speakers with different accents | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report what a non-American-accent speaker said; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check comprehension across accents | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a summary of a varied-accent clip | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — familiar accents first. Stretch — challenging/global accents.
**Success check:** comprehension across ≥2 accents. **Queue:** feeds exam listening.
**Coach notes:** EEC's model accent is American, but real life is multi-accent — receptive flexibility is a genuine C1 need (and exam requirement).

## S4-U6-L03 — Lectures / Podcasts + Note-Taking

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 6 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can follow an extended lecture/podcast and take structured notes.
- The learner can identify main ideas, supporting points, and signposts.
- The learner can reconstruct content from notes.

**Target language:** lecture signposting (reception) + note-taking conventions. **Accent Lab focus:** tracking
prosodic emphasis to spot key points. **Mindset:** structured listening = deep comprehension.

**Materials:** a lecture/podcast excerpt; a note-taking template; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: accent strategies (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | A ~3–4 min lecture/podcast excerpt; take notes | 8 min |
| 3 | Notice | Note-taking (main/support/signpost); using prosody to spot key points; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Emphasis signals importance — track it | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Reconstruct the content from notes in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Summarize the lecture from notes (~1.5 min); record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check note quality + summary accuracy | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Listen to a talk; post structured notes + a summary | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — note template + shorter excerpt. Stretch — dense/technical lecture.
**Success check:** structured notes + an accurate summary. **Queue:** feeds exam listening + U8.
**Coach notes:** note-taking is a transferable academic/professional + exam skill — teach a simple, repeatable system.

## S4-U6-L04 — Inferring Meaning From Demanding Input

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 6 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can infer implicit meaning, speaker stance, and unstated conclusions from demanding input.
- The learner can handle unfamiliar vocabulary via context.
- The learner can distinguish fact from opinion/implication.

**Target language:** inference from demanding audio/text (links to U3 nuance). **Accent Lab focus:** intonation → stance inference.
**Mindset:** understand beyond the literal — the C1 receptive edge.

**Materials:** demanding clips/texts with implicit content; an inference task; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: note-taking + U3 tone inference | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | A clip/text with implicit stance/conclusions | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Inferring the unsaid; guessing vocab from context; fact vs opinion; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Intonation → stance | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Answer inference questions with textual/audio evidence | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | State the speaker's implied stance + your inference; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check inference + evidence | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an inference (implied meaning + evidence) from a source | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — guided inference questions. Stretch — subtle/ambiguous sources.
**Success check:** accurate, evidence-based inference. **Queue:** feeds U9 + exam reading/listening.
**Coach notes:** inference is heavily tested in exams and central to C1 — reward evidence-based reasoning over guessing.

## S4-U6-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Summarize + Respond to a Demanding Source" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 6 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can summarize a demanding source (lecture/podcast/article) and respond with their own view (~3 min).
- The learner can integrate content + inference + evidence.
- The learner records their **Unit-6 summarize-and-respond**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-6 receptive + productive skills. **Accent Lab focus:** clear delivery of a summary; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** engaging with demanding input = real-world/academic readiness.

**Materials:** a demanding source; a note template; a checklist card (accurate summary / main + supporting points / an inference / your response + reasons);
recording prompt; Unit-6 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: listening/note-taking/inference strategies | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Engage with a demanding source (notes) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break the task into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills clear summary delivery + a residual sound | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse summary + response; peer Q&A | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the summarize-and-respond (~3 min) | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the summarize-and-respond; complete Unit-6 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — notes + checklist. Stretch — a dense source, spontaneous response.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 6):** accurate summary + inference + reasoned response to a demanding source (rubric at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U10 + exam prep.
**Coach notes:** summarize-and-respond is the core of TOEFL/IELTS integrated tasks — this unit doubles as exam foundation.
