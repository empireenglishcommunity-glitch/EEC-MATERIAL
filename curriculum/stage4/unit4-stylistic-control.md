## S4-U4-L01 — Academic Style (Nominalization, Hedging, Precision)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 4 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B2 register + U1 cohesion.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can produce academic-style speech (nominalization, cautious hedging, precise/impersonal phrasing).
- The learner can present information objectively.
- The learner can sound scholarly when needed.

**Target language:** nominalization (*decide → the decision*), academic hedging (*it appears that, arguably, to some extent*),
impersonal structures. **Accent Lab focus:** measured, even academic prosody. **Mindset:** register range = adaptability.

**Materials:** an academic-features card; an academic-talk clip; a spoken↔academic set; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B2 formal/informal | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: an academic-register explanation | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Nominalization, hedging, impersonal precision; brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Measured academic prosody | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Convert casual → academic; nominalize; hedge | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Explain a topic in academic style (~1.5 min); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check academic features | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post an academic-style explanation | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — features card. Stretch — a fully nominalized, hedged mini-lecture.
**Success check:** recognizable, accurate academic register. **Queue:** academic style feeds U8 + exam writing.
**Coach notes:** over-nominalization gets impenetrable — teach balance; academic ≠ maximally complex. Precision + appropriate hedging is the goal.

## S4-U4-L02 — Colloquial & Creative Style

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 4 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can produce natural colloquial speech (informal markers, idiom, contractions, vivid phrasing).
- The learner can add creative flair (imagery, emphasis, rhythm) for engagement.
- The learner can sound relaxed and vivid.

**Target language:** colloquial markers (*kind of, you know, like, sort of, totally*), vivid/creative devices (simile, vivid verbs).
**Accent Lab focus:** relaxed, lively prosody + reductions. **Mindset:** be expressive — personality in English.

**Materials:** a colloquial/creative features card; a lively clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |

| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: academic style (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: relaxed, vivid, colloquial storytelling | 6 min |

| 4 | Accent Lab | Relaxed prosody + reductions; expressive stress | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Retell something dryly → then colloquially/vividly | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Tell a vivid, colloquial anecdote; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check natural colloquial + creative flair | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a vivid, colloquial anecdote | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — features card. Stretch — heightened creative/vivid delivery.
**Success check:** natural colloquial + some creative flair. **Queue:** feeds U7 (fluency/flair).
**Coach notes:** this is where personality shows — reward expressiveness + naturalness; it balances the "stiff academic" tendency.

## S4-U4-L04 — Shifting Register for Effect

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 4 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can shift register deliberately mid-discourse for effect (e.g., academic point → colloquial aside).
- The learner can match register to audience/purpose precisely.
- The learner can use register shifts as a rhetorical tool.

**Target language:** integrated register control; register-shift signals. **Accent Lab focus:** prosody shifts with register.
**Mindset:** conscious control = mastery.

**Materials:** register-shift model clips; audience/purpose cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| 4 | Accent Lab | Prosody follows the register shift | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Deliver a point, then shift register for effect | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Give a talk with a deliberate register shift; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check control + effectiveness of the shift | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a talk with a purposeful register shift | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — audience/purpose cards. Stretch — multiple smooth shifts.
**Success check:** a deliberate, effective register shift. **Queue:** feeds U9/U10.
**Coach notes:** the C1 skill is *conscious, purposeful* control — reward shifts that serve a communicative purpose, not random switching.

## S4-U4-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "One Idea, Three Styles" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 4 / C1 · Unit 4 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can present the same idea in academic, professional, and colloquial styles (~3 min total).
- The learner can explain the register choices and their effects.
- The learner records their **Unit-4 three-style performance**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-4 register control. **Accent Lab focus:** prosody by style; each learner's residual polish.
**Mindset:** adaptability across contexts is a hallmark of an advanced user.

**Materials:** a model three-style performance; a checklist card (academic version / professional version / colloquial version / prosody matches each);
recording prompt; Unit-4 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: features of each register | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean three-style performance | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills register prosody + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse all three versions; peer feedback on fit | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the three-style performance + explain choices | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the performance; complete Unit-4 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — add a creative/diplomatic 4th version.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 4):** three clearly-distinct register versions of one idea (rubric: Range + Coherence at C1). **Queue:** recycles into U10.
**Coach notes:** reward *distinctness + appropriateness* of each register — this is the clearest demonstration of stylistic control.
