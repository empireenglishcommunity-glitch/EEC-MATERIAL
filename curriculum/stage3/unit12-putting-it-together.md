## S3-U12-L01 — Big Review 1: Tenses + Conditionals + Deduction (Retrieval)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 12 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U1–U5.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can retrieve and use the full tense range, 3rd/mixed conditionals, and modals of deduction accurately.
- The learner can combine them in extended, natural speech.
- The learner can self-correct the top remaining errors.

**Target language:** full Stage-3 tense/conditional/deduction range. **Grammar:** integrated retrieval + consolidation.
**Accent Lab focus:** connected speech + naturalness across long turns. **Mindset:** you command the whole system now.

**Materials:** retrieval decks (tenses/conditionals/deduction); mixed prompts; the rubric card; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Rapid retrieval across tenses/conditionals/deduction | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A model that ranges across the full grammar in natural speech | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-surface top residual errors (per group history) | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Naturalness + connected speech; record-and-compare | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Extended prompt combining ≥4 structures in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Speak ~2 min ranging across the grammar; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self-correct against the residual-error list | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a wide-ranging ~2 min talk | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — retrieval decks + frames. Stretch — spontaneous, less-supported range.
**Success check:** accurate, natural use across ≥4 structures. **Queue:** feeds the L05 benchmark.
**Coach notes:** retrieval + integration — reward natural range over isolated perfection; note residuals to target in L03.

## S3-U12-L03 — Accent & Fluency Clinic (Naturalness + Self-Monitoring)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 12 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: all units.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can target their top-3 residual sounds/prosody features for final refinement.
- The learner can self-monitor and self-correct in real time.
- The learner can sound clear *and natural* (prosody, thought groups, emphasis).

**Target language:** each learner's flagged residuals + prosody. **Grammar:** (accent/fluency clinic). **Accent Lab focus:**
personalized final refinement; naturalness; self-monitoring/self-correction. **Mindset:** self-monitoring is the lifelong skill that keeps you improving.

**Materials:** each learner's cumulative tracker flags; minimal-pair + prosody drill sets; record-and-compare vs. Day-1/earlier samples; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Learners state their top-3 residual features | 4 min |
| 2 | Input (perceive) | Model contrasts + natural prosody for each target | 5 min |
| 3 | Notice | Re-cue articulation + prosody; the self-monitoring routine | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab (produce) | Targeted drills → connected speech; record-and-compare | 10 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Read + speak, self-flagging slips in real time | 5 min |
| 6 | Free output | 2-minute spontaneous speech; self-correct live | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Compare to Day-1 + earlier milestone samples — the full arc | 2 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a record-and-compare of your top target vs. an earlier clip | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — focus 1–2 features + intelligibility. Stretch — self-correct fluently in spontaneous speech.
**Success check:** measurable naturalness/clarity gains + active self-monitoring. **Queue:** any residuals → Stage 4 (C1) polish (aspirational).
**Coach notes:** honesty guardrail (GC-5) — the goal is **clear, confident, neutral American**; native-like is a ceiling, never promised. Compare to the learner's own journey (Day-1 → now) — the arc is the proof and the motivation.

## S3-U12-L04 — Full B2 Mock Conversation (20–30 min)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 12 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can sustain a natural 20–30 min conversation across everyday + professional topics.
- The learner can narrate, opine, argue, speculate, report, and handle nuance — spontaneously, in English.
- The learner can self-assess against the B2 rubric and target final polish.

**Target language:** the full Stage-3 range in spontaneous interaction. **Grammar:** everything, in use.
**Accent Lab focus:** overall naturalness + self-monitoring across a long conversation. **Mindset:** this is the flagship — rehearse it as the celebration it is.

**Materials:** the wide-ranging conversation prompt bank (everyday + professional); the B2 rubric card; peer-feedback sheet; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up | Light small talk → warm up the voice + mind (English-only) | 4 min |
| 2 | Setup | Explain the benchmark format + the 5 rubric dimensions + the defined minimum | 4 min |
| 3 | Accent tune-up | 60–90s personal top-1 feature warm-up | 2 min |
| 4 | Mock conversation | Full 20–30 min conversation (everyday → professional → abstract), recorded | 25 min |
| 5 | Feedback | Rubric-based, forward-looking (final polish before L05) | 8 min |
| 6 | Assignment | Self-assess with the rubric; prep any weak areas for the benchmark | 2 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — prompt bank + topic prep. Stretch — fully spontaneous, more abstract/professional topics.
**Success check:** sustains a natural 20–30 min conversation at/near the B2 threshold across all dimensions. **Queue:** directly precedes the graduation benchmark.
**Coach notes:** this is the exact flagship format — keep it English-only, warm, and confidence-building; it removes anxiety and previews the graduation assessment precisely.

## S3-U12-L05 — B2 Flagship Benchmark + Graduation "After" Sample ⭐

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 12 · ~50 min · recorded + live (flagship assessment, English-only) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can demonstrate the **B2 graduation benchmark**: a natural **20–30 min conversation** on
  everyday + professional topics, **clearly and confidently with a neutral American accent**, meeting a
  **defined minimum across all five rubric dimensions**.
- The learner completes the **Stage-3 summative quiz** (grammar/vocab/range).
- The learner records the **graduation "after" sample** on the standard prompt — the headline transformation proof.

**Target language:** the full B2 range. **Grammar:** full range, assessed for accuracy + naturalness.
**Accent Lab focus:** clear + confident + neutral American (scored on Pronunciation — clarity/naturalness, **not** "native").
**Arabic support:** 0% (full immersion). **Mindset:** graduation — you are a **confident English user**. Celebrate + plan lifelong maintenance (and optional C1/exam track).

**Materials:** the **Stage-3 summative quiz**; the **B2 benchmark protocol** (20–30 min conversation, everyday +
professional topics); the 5-dimension rubric with the **defined minimum**; the **standard graduation prompt**
(before/after protocol §2); consent record (for the flagship testimonial per tier).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up | Light retrieval + reassurance; this is a celebration + proof, not a filter | 4 min |
| 2 | Summative quiz | Grammar/vocab/range recognition + short production (full Stage-3) | 12 min |
| 3 | Benchmark setup | Explain the conversation format + the rubric + defined minimum (English-only) | 3 min |
| 4 | Accent tune-up | 60–90s personal top-1 feature warm-up | 2 min |
| 5 | **B2 benchmark conversation** | The **20–30 min** everyday + professional conversation, recorded + rubric-scored | 25 min |
| 6 | Graduation "after" sample | Record the **standard prompt** (extended) — the headline before/after proof clip | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback + result | Rubric result vs the defined minimum; **B2 graduation determination**; wins + next path | 6 min |
| 8 | Celebrate / next | Save the graduation clip (with consent); plan maintenance + optional Stage 4 (C1/exam) | 2 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — topic prep + a slightly shorter conversation still covering the criteria. Stretch — fully spontaneous, wider professional/abstract range.
**Success check (Stage-3 flagship summative):** meets the **B2 threshold with a defined minimum across all five dimensions** (Fluency, Accuracy, Range/Coherence, Interaction, Pronunciation) in the 20–30 min conversation; quiz passed at the set band. **Queue:** any residuals → Stage 4 (C1) polish; graduate transitions to community/maintenance (Phase 5) or the Exam Track.
**Coach notes:** THIS is the transformation proof the entire program is engineered toward. Pair the graduation "after" sample with the full timeline — Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → B1 → **B2 graduation** — the flagship testimonial (with consent). Frame the win in the learner's own arc, honestly: **clear, confident, professional English** — not "native," but genuinely fluent and capable.
