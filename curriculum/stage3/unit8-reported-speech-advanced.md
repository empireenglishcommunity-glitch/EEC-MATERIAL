## S3-U8-L01 — Reporting-Verb Range (Overview)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 8 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B1 reported speech.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can choose a precise reporting verb (suggest, admit, deny, warn, remind, offer, promise, refuse, recommend, complain, insist).
- The learner can report *what kind* of speech act happened, not just "said".
- The learner can move beyond say/tell to convey meaning precisely.

**Target language:** reporting-verb set (functional groups: suggesting, admitting/denying, warning/reminding,
offering/promising/refusing, recommending/insisting/complaining). **Grammar:** reporting-verb choice.
**Accent Lab focus:** stress the reporting verb; phrasing. **Mindset:** precise retelling = professional credibility.

**Materials:** a reporting-verb reference card (grouped by function); a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B1 say/tell + backshift | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "He admitted he was wrong. She offered to help. They refused to pay." | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Reporting verbs capture the speech act; functional groups; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Stress the reporting verb; phrase the frame | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Match quotes → precise reporting verbs | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 exchanges with precise verbs; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check reporting-verb accuracy | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 reports using non-say/tell verbs | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — grouped verb card. Stretch — report a short exchange with 3 different verbs.
**Success check:** precise reporting-verb choice for the speech act. **Queue:** reporting verbs recycle this unit.
**Coach notes:** patterns come next (L02–L03); here the goal is *choosing the meaning-precise verb*. Keep the functional grouping to reduce overload.

## S3-U8-L02 — Patterns: verb + *-ing* / verb + *that*

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 8 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use verb + *-ing* patterns (*suggested doing, admitted doing, denied doing*).
- The learner can use verb + *that* patterns (*claimed/admitted/insisted that…*).
- The learner can report with the correct structure for each verb.

**Target language:** *suggest/recommend/admit/deny/mention + -ing* or *+ that*. **Grammar:** verb + -ing / + that clause.
**Accent Lab focus:** rhythm across the pattern; weak *that*. **Mindset:** structure follows the verb — learn verbs with their patterns.

**Materials:** a verb + pattern card; a transform set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: reporting-verb range (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "She suggested taking a break. He admitted that he'd forgotten." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | verb + -ing vs verb + that (which verbs take which); brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Pattern rhythm; weak *that* | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform quotes using -ing / that patterns | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 exchanges with these patterns; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check verb + correct pattern | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 reports (verb + -ing / + that) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — pattern card. Stretch — mix both patterns in one report.
**Success check:** correct verb + -ing / + that. **Queue:** recycles into L04.
**Coach notes:** teach high-frequency verbs with their pattern as chunks (suggest + -ing, admit + -ing/that) — pattern memory beats rule memory here.

## S3-U8-L03 — Patterns: verb + *to* / verb + object + *to*

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 8 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use verb + *to* (*offered/promised/refused to*) and verb + object + *to* (*advised/warned/reminded someone to*).
- The learner can report offers, promises, refusals, warnings, and advice.
- The learner can select the correct pattern per verb.

**Target language:** *offer/promise/refuse/agree + to*; *advise/warn/remind/tell/ask/encourage + object + to*.
**Grammar:** verb + to / + object + to. **Accent Lab focus:** weak *to*; linking. **Mindset:** report action-oriented speech accurately.

**Materials:** a verb + to pattern card; a transform set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: verb + -ing / + that (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "He promised to call. She warned me not to be late." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | verb + to vs verb + object + to; negative *not to*; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak *to*; link "warned-me-to" | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform quotes using to / object + to patterns | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 offers/promises/warnings/advice; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check pattern per verb | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 reports (verb + to / + object + to) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — pattern card. Stretch — combine several patterns in a mini-report.
**Success check:** correct verb + to / + object + to. **Queue:** recycles into L04/L05.
**Coach notes:** the object requirement (advise/warn/remind need an object; offer/promise don't) is the tricky bit — group and drill accordingly.

## S3-U8-L04 — Reporting a Meeting / Negotiation Accurately

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 8 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report the key points of a meeting/negotiation with varied reporting verbs + patterns.
- The learner can summarize who proposed/agreed/objected/promised what.
- The learner can produce an accurate spoken summary.

**Target language:** meeting/negotiation reporting (proposed, agreed, objected, offered, insisted, compromised).
**Grammar:** integrated reporting verbs + patterns + backshift. **Accent Lab focus:** phrasing a summary; emphasis on key points.
**Mindset:** accurate summaries build professional trust.

**Materials:** a meeting/negotiation source dialogue; a reporting-summary frame; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: all reporting patterns (L01–L03) | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | A short meeting dialogue → a model spoken report of it | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Selecting verbs to summarize key points accurately; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Phrase the summary; stress key outcomes | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Report the meeting dialogue in pairs | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Summarize a discussion/meeting (real or given); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check accuracy + verb/pattern range | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a spoken summary of a discussion | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — source dialogue + frame. Stretch — report a real meeting from your work.
**Success check:** an accurate meeting summary using varied reporting verbs. **Queue:** feeds U10 (professional) & U12.
**Coach notes:** direct professional transfer (status updates, minutes-to-speech) — connects to U10; reward accuracy + concision.

## S3-U8-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Report a Discussion/Negotiation" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 8 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report a discussion/negotiation (~2 min): ≥4 reporting verbs across ≥3 patterns, accurately.
- The learner can answer clarifying questions about the report.
- The learner records their **Unit-8 reported summary**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-8 language. **Grammar:** advanced reported speech consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** reporting-frame rhythm; each learner's residual sound.
**Mindset:** professional, accurate retelling — a workplace-ready skill.

**Materials:** a source discussion; a model report; a checklist card (4+ reporting verbs / -ing pattern / that pattern / to pattern / accuracy);
recording prompt; Unit-8 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: reporting verbs + the four patterns | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch/read a source discussion + its report | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break the report into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills reporting-frame rhythm + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Report the source discussion in pairs | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record a report of a real/given discussion | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the report; complete Unit-8 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — source + checklist + prep. Stretch — report a real negotiation with nuance.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 8):** ~2 min report using ≥4 reporting verbs across ≥3 patterns accurately (rubric at B2). **Queue:** recycles into U10/U12.
**Coach notes:** the pattern accuracy matters, but so does *meaning-precise verb choice* — reward both. Ties directly to professional communication.
