# Stage 3 · Unit 3 — Future Forms (Advanced) (full lessons, v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 6.1 · **Built on:** `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md` · **Map:** `stage3/00-stage3-unit-map.md`
> **Arabic support:** none (immersion). **Unit goal:** by the end, the learner can talk about the future
> with precision — ongoing future actions (future continuous), completed future actions (future perfect),
> arrangements, and hedged predictions with appropriate certainty — recorded.
> **Grammar spine:** future continuous + future perfect + arrangements + degrees of certainty · **Accent Lab:**
> intonation of certainty vs doubt · **Mindset:** planning your post-program English life.

**Unit lessons:** L01 future continuous · L02 future perfect · L03 arrangements vs plans vs predictions (review + extend) ·

## S3-U3-L01 — Future Continuous: "Will Be Doing"

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 3 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B1 future (going to/will).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use the future continuous (*will be + -ing*) for actions in progress at a future time.
- The learner can ask about future arrangements politely ("Will you be using…?").
- The learner can describe a future moment/routine.

**Target language:** future continuous; future time points (this time next week, at 8 tomorrow). **Grammar:**
*will be + -ing*. **Accent Lab focus:** phrasing; weak "will be" ("I'll be…"). **Mindset:** picture a future day living/working in English.

**Materials:** a future-moment timeline; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B1 will/going to | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "This time next week I'll be flying to Dubai." | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | *will be + -ing* = in progress at a future point; polite enquiry use; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | "I'll be" linking; intonation | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe what you'll be doing at future moments | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 future-continuous sentences about your week/year; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *will be + -ing* | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "This time next ___, I'll be ___." | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — timeline + frames. Stretch — use the polite-enquiry function in a request.
**Success check:** correct future continuous for future-in-progress. **Queue:** recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** highlight the "softer, less direct" feel of the future continuous for arrangements/enquiries — a natural, professional touch.

## S3-U3-L02 — Future Perfect: "Will Have Done"

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use the future perfect (*will have + participle*) for actions completed before a future point.
- The learner can talk about deadlines/milestones.
- The learner can combine it with *by + time*.

**Target language:** future perfect; *by + future time* (by 2030, by the end of the year). **Grammar:**
*will have + past participle*. **Accent Lab focus:** phrasing "will have + participle"; weak *have*. **Mindset:** set a future milestone for your English.

**Materials:** a milestone timeline; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: future continuous (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "By next year, I'll have finished my degree." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *will have + participle* = done before a future point; *by*; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak "will have"; phrase it smoothly | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | State milestones with future perfect + by | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 "by [future], I'll have ___" milestones; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *will have + participle* | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 future milestones (future perfect) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — timeline + participle reminder. Stretch — combine future perfect + continuous in one plan.
**Success check:** correct future perfect for pre-future completion. **Queue:** recycles into L05/U12.
**Coach notes:** keep it milestone-focused (goals, deadlines) — the meaning is clearest there. Reuse U1 participles.

## S3-U3-L03 — Arrangements vs Plans vs Predictions (Review + Extend)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can choose present continuous (arrangements), *going to* (plans/intentions), and *will* (predictions/decisions) accurately.
- The learner can combine future forms naturally in one turn.
- The learner can talk about a fixed schedule vs a flexible plan.

**Target language:** all future forms, contrasted. **Grammar:** present continuous (fixed arrangements) vs going to vs will.
**Accent Lab focus:** natural switching between forms. **Mindset:** you can express future nuance now — precision is fluency.

**Materials:** a future-forms comparison card; a diary/schedule prop; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: future continuous + perfect (L01–L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip mixing forms: "I'm meeting a client at 3 (arrangement), I'm going to prepare (plan), it'll be fine (prediction)." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Arrangement (pres cont) vs plan (going to) vs prediction/decision (will); brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Switch cleanly between forms | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe a schedule + plans + predictions from a diary prop | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Talk about tomorrow using ≥3 future forms; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check form choice | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a "my week ahead" using ≥3 future forms | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — comparison card. Stretch — justify each form choice.
**Success check:** appropriate mix of future forms in one turn. **Queue:** recycles into U12.
**Coach notes:** present continuous for fixed arrangements is the under-used one — highlight it (it's very natural/professional for scheduling).

## S3-U3-L04 — Degrees of Future Certainty (Hedged Predictions)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 3 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can express how certain a prediction is (from near-certain to unlikely).
- The learner can hedge predictions naturally (*bound to, likely to, may well, probably won't*).
- The learner can discuss the future with appropriate caution.

**Target language:** certainty scale (*definitely/bound to → likely to/may well → might/could → probably won't → unlikely to*).
**Grammar:** modal + adverb hedging on future forms. **Accent Lab focus:** intonation of certainty vs doubt; stress the hedge.
**Mindset:** confident uncertainty — sound thoughtful, not vague.

**Materials:** a certainty-scale card; prediction prompts; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: future forms (L03) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Prices are bound to rise. It'll probably slow down. It may well change." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | The certainty scale + where each phrase sits; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Intonation signals certainty/doubt; stress the hedge | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rate predictions on the scale; make hedged predictions | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Make 3 hedged predictions about a topic; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check certainty language + naturalness | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 predictions across the certainty scale | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — certainty-scale card. Stretch — justify each prediction's certainty level.
**Success check:** predictions with appropriate, natural certainty hedging. **Queue:** feeds U5 (deduction) & U11 (debate).
**Coach notes:** this connects to U5 modals of deduction and U11 debate — hedged, thoughtful prediction is a hallmark of educated B2 speech.

## S3-U3-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Where You'll Be in 5 Years" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 3 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can talk about their future (~2 min): plans + milestones (future perfect) + a future-in-progress picture + hedged certainty.
- The learner can respond to questions about their future.
- The learner records their **Unit-3 future talk**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-3 language. **Grammar:** advanced future forms consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** certainty/doubt intonation + phrasing; each learner's residual sound.
**Mindset:** plan your post-program English life — make the future concrete.

**Materials:** a model future talk; a checklist card (a plan / a future-perfect milestone / a future-continuous picture / hedged certainty);
recording prompt; Unit-3 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: future continuous, future perfect, form choice, certainty scale | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean ~2 min future talk | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills certainty intonation + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer Q&A about the future | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~2 min future talk + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the talk; complete Unit-3 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — a nuanced, hedged vision with follow-ups.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 3):** ~2 min future talk using ≥3 future forms + hedged certainty (rubric at B2). **Queue:** recycles into U12.
**Coach notes:** motivating + linguistically rich — ties the mindset (post-program English life) to the grammar. Encourage a concrete, believable vision.
