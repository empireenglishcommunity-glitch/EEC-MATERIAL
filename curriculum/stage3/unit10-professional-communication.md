## S3-U10-L01 — Meetings: Participate, Interrupt, Clarify

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 10 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: U9 (register/diplomacy).

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can contribute to a meeting (give views, agree/disagree, build on ideas).
- The learner can interrupt politely and ask for clarification.
- The learner can keep/hand over the floor.

**Target language:** meeting language (I'd like to add…, Could I just come in here?, Sorry to interrupt, but…,
Just to clarify…, Could you expand on that?). **Grammar:** functions + diplomacy (U9). **Accent Lab focus:**
polite-interruption intonation; emphasis on key points. **Mindset:** your voice belongs in the meeting.

**Materials:** a meeting-phrase card; a meeting clip; role cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: diplomacy/softening (U9) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a short meeting (contribute, interrupt politely, clarify) | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Meeting functions + polite interruption + clarifying; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Interruption intonation; emphasis on your point | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Role-play a mini-meeting (contribute + interrupt + clarify) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Contribute to a topic as if in a meeting; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check meeting phrases + politeness | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 meeting-contribution phrases in use | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — phrase card + roles. Stretch — chair a segment (invite, summarize).
**Success check:** polite contribution + interruption + clarification. **Queue:** meeting language recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** polite interruption is a real skill many avoid — practice it explicitly so learners can hold their own in fast meetings.

## S3-U10-L02 — Giving a Short Presentation (Structure + Signposting)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 10 · ~45 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can structure a short presentation (intro → points → conclusion).
- The learner can signpost (*First…, Moving on…, To sum up…*).
- The learner can present clearly with impactful emphasis.

**Target language:** presentation signposting + structure language. **Grammar:** connectors (B1) + register (U9).
**Accent Lab focus:** presentation prosody; pausing; emphasis for impact. **Mindset:** presenting in English = career leverage.

**Materials:** a presentation structure/signposting card; a model presentation clip; slide-free prompt topics; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: connectors + meeting phrases | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a 2-min model presentation (intro/points/conclusion + signposts) | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Presentation structure + signposting language; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Prosody + strategic pausing + emphasis | 6 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Outline + rehearse a 2-min presentation in pairs | 10 min |
| 6 | Free output | Deliver a 2-min presentation on a prepared topic; record | 6 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check structure + signposting + delivery | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a 2-min presentation recording | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — structure card + prep. Stretch — present with less prep + handle a curveball.
**Success check:** a structured, signposted 2-min presentation. **Queue:** feeds L05 + U12.
**Coach notes:** delivery (prosody, pausing, emphasis) is as assessed as content — this is prime Accent Lab refinement territory.

## S3-U10-L03 — Negotiating: Propose, Concede, Bargain

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 10 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can make proposals, concede, and bargain toward agreement.
- The learner can use conditional bargaining (*If you…, we could…*) and diplomatic language.
- The learner can reach a compromise politely.

**Target language:** negotiation language (How about…?, We could… if…, I see your point, but…, Would you be
willing to…?, Let's meet in the middle). **Grammar:** conditionals (B1/B2) + diplomacy (U9). **Accent Lab focus:**
persuasive but warm intonation. **Mindset:** win-win confidence — negotiate without anxiety.

**Materials:** a negotiation-language card; negotiation scenario cards; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: conditionals + diplomacy | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a short negotiation (propose → concede → compromise) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Negotiation moves + conditional bargaining; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Persuasive-yet-warm intonation | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Role-play a negotiation from scenario cards | 9 min |
| 6 | Free output | Negotiate a deal with a partner; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check negotiation language + diplomacy | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 negotiation phrases in use | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — negotiation card + scenario. Stretch — a tougher negotiation with a resistant partner.
**Success check:** a coherent negotiation reaching a compromise. **Queue:** feeds U11/U12.
**Coach notes:** ties conditionals (U4/B1) + diplomacy (U9) to a real professional skill — reward reaching agreement while staying polite.

## S3-U10-L04 — Professional Calls & Follow-Ups

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 10 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can handle a professional call (open, state purpose, discuss, agree next steps, close).
- The learner can leave a clear voicemail and confirm follow-ups verbally.
- The learner can turn an email's content into spoken communication.

**Target language:** call structure + follow-up language (*I'm calling about…, just to confirm…, I'll follow up
with…, let's touch base…*). **Grammar:** integrated functions. **Accent Lab focus:** clear phone prosody; enunciation.
**Mindset:** calls in English = professional independence.

**Materials:** a call/voicemail script set; a clip; role cards; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: meeting/negotiation phrases | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: a professional call (open → purpose → next steps → close) + a voicemail | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Call structure + follow-up language; brief note | 5 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Phone-clear prosody; enunciate | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Role-play a professional call; leave a voicemail | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Make a professional call (with next steps); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check call structure + clarity | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a voicemail + a follow-up confirmation | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — call script. Stretch — handle a difficult caller / a problem call.
**Success check:** a clear professional call with agreed next steps. **Queue:** feeds U12.
**Coach notes:** phone loses visual cues, so enunciation + structure matter more — great, practical Accent Lab application.

## S3-U10-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Present + Field Questions" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 10 · ~45 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can give a ~3 min presentation and field questions professionally (Q&A).
- The learner can use signposting, diplomacy, and clear delivery.
- The learner records their **Unit-10 presentation + Q&A**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-10 language. **Grammar:** professional functions consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** presentation prosody + emphasis; each learner's residual sound.
**Mindset:** English as a career multiplier — this is a real professional deliverable.

**Materials:** a model presentation + Q&A; a checklist card (structured intro/points/conclusion / signposting / handle 2 questions / diplomacy / delivery);
recording prompt; Unit-10 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: meeting/presentation/negotiation/call phrases | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean presentation + Q&A | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills presentation prosody + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse presentation; peers prepare questions | 10 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Deliver the ~3 min presentation + field 2–3 questions; record | 8 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the presentation + Q&A; complete Unit-10 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — less prep + harder questions.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 10):** a structured ~3 min presentation + handled Q&A (rubric at B2, esp. delivery + interaction). **Queue:** recycles into U12 (the flagship covers professional topics).
**Coach notes:** this is the professional half of the flagship promise — a strong proof clip. Assess delivery + handling questions as much as content.
