## S3-U6-L01 — Passive Across Tenses

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 6 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B1 simple passive.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the passive in present/past continuous, present perfect, and future.
- The learner can choose the right passive tense for the context.
- The learner can describe ongoing/completed/future processes impersonally.

**Target language:** passive tenses (is being done, was being done, has been done, will be done). **Grammar:**
*be* (in the tense) + past participle. **Accent Lab focus:** weak auxiliaries ("is being", "has been"); clear participle.
**Mindset:** precise, professional description — the passive is a workplace staple.

**Materials:** an all-tense passive table; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B1 present/past simple passive | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "The road is being repaired. The report has been sent. It will be reviewed." | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | Passive = *be* (in tense) + participle; the tense table; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak auxiliaries; clear participles | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Convert active→passive across tenses | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | Describe a project/process in ≥3 passive tenses; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check tense of *be* + participle | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 passive sentences in different tenses | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — passive table. Stretch — a status update using multiple passive tenses.
**Success check:** correct passive across ≥3 tenses. **Queue:** advanced passive recycles this unit.
**Coach notes:** the engine is still "be + participle" — only *be* changes tense. Anchor there; reuse participle work from U1.

## S3-U6-L02 — Modal + Perfect Passives

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 6 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form modal passives (*must be done, should be done, can be done*) and perfect passives (*has been done*).
- The learner can talk about rules, requirements, and completed actions impersonally.
- The learner can combine modality + passive for professional register.

**Target language:** modal passive (must/should/can/have to + be + participle); *by* optional. **Grammar:** modal +
*be* + participle; perfect passive. **Accent Lab focus:** weak "be/been" in strings; stress the participle. **Mindset:** professional precision + politeness.

**Materials:** rules/requirements set; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: passive tenses (L01) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "Forms must be submitted by Friday. It should be checked first." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | modal + *be* + participle (rules/requirements); brief note | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Weak be/been; participle clarity | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | State rules/requirements with modal passives | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 workplace/process rules in modal passive; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check modal + be + participle | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 modal-passive rules | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — rules set + frames. Stretch — mix modal + perfect passives.
**Success check:** correct modal/perfect passives. **Queue:** recycles into L05/U10.
**Coach notes:** modal passives are everywhere in professional writing/speech (policies, instructions) — high transfer value to U10.

## S3-U6-L03 — Reporting Passive (Impersonal)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 6 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can report beliefs/claims impersonally (*It is said that…, He is thought to…*).
- The learner can present information without attributing it to a specific source.
- The learner can sound objective/academic when needed.

**Target language:** *It is said/believed/thought/reported that…*; *He is said/thought to + base/have + participle*.
**Grammar:** reporting passive (two patterns). **Accent Lab focus:** phrasing the frame; weak forms. **Mindset:** objective register for news/reports.

**Materials:** reporting-passive frame card; a news-style clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: modal/perfect passives (L02) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "It is believed that prices will rise. The CEO is said to be leaving." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Two patterns: *It is said that…* / *He is said to…*; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Phrase the frame smoothly; weak forms | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Transform claims into impersonal reports (both patterns) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Report 3 "known/believed" facts impersonally; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check both reporting-passive patterns | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 impersonal reports | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — frame card. Stretch — convert between the two patterns.
**Success check:** correct impersonal reporting passive. **Queue:** feeds U8 (reported) & U11 (abstract topics).
**Coach notes:** high-register and useful for discussing news/research in the flagship conversation — model it clearly; it's often new even to strong B1s.

## S3-U6-L04 — Causative: *have / get something done*

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 6 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L03.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use the causative (*have/get + object + past participle*) for services arranged.
- The learner can distinguish doing it yourself vs having it done.
- The learner can talk about services (repairs, appointments, arrangements).

**Target language:** *have/get + something + done* (service context). **Grammar:** causative structure. **Accent Lab focus:**
phrasing "have/get + object + participle"; stress the participle. **Mindset:** describe real-life logistics precisely.

**Materials:** a services set (car, hair, house, documents); a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: passive forms | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I had my car repaired. I need to get my hair cut." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *have/get + object + participle* = someone else does it for you; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Phrase the structure; participle clarity | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Describe services you have/get done | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 things you've had/will get done; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check causative structure | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 causative sentences (services) | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — services set + frame. Stretch — combine with past/future ("I'm having it done next week").
**Success check:** correct causative for services. **Queue:** recycles into L05/U12.
**Coach notes:** learners confuse "I repaired my car" (self) vs "I had my car repaired" (service). Contrast directly with clear examples.

## S3-U6-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "Describe a System/Service" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 6 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can describe a system/service (~2 min): passive across tenses + a modal passive + a causative + (optionally) impersonal reporting.
- The learner can answer clarifying questions.
- The learner records their **Unit-6 system/service description**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-6 language. **Grammar:** advanced passive + causative consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** weak auxiliaries in complex strings; each learner's residual sound.
**Mindset:** precise professional description — a workplace-ready skill.

**Materials:** a model system/service description; a checklist card (passive tenses / a modal passive / a causative / clear participles);
recording prompt; Unit-6 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: passive tenses, modal passive, reporting passive, causative | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean system/service description | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills weak auxiliaries + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer clarifying questions | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~2 min description + answer one live question | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the description; complete Unit-6 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — add impersonal reporting + a modal passive rule.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 6):** ~2 min description using advanced passive + causative accurately (rubric at B2). **Queue:** recycles into U10/U12.
**Coach notes:** completes the "structures for precision" — high professional value. Reward accurate, natural passive use over quantity.
