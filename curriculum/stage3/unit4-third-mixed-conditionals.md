## S3-U4-L01 — Third Conditional: Form

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 4 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: B1 1st/2nd conditionals.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form the 3rd conditional (*If* + past perfect, + *would have* + participle).
- The learner can talk about imaginary past situations (things that didn't happen).
- The learner can use contractions (*'d have*) naturally.

**Target language:** 3rd conditional; past-hypothetical contexts. **Grammar:** *If* + past perfect, *would have +
participle*. **Accent Lab focus:** *'d have* → /dəv/ ("I'd've"); stress content words across clauses. **Mindset:** regret → data: what would you do differently?

**Materials:** the 3rd-conditional structure slide; a conditionals-overview card; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B1 2nd conditional (unreal present) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "If I'd studied harder, I would have passed." | 7 min |
| 3 | Notice | *If* + past perfect, *would have* + participle = unreal past; brief explanation | 8 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | *'d have* reduction ("I'd've"); multi-clause stress | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build 3rd conditionals from "what didn't happen" prompts | 7 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 third-conditional sentences about your past; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *would have + participle* | 1 min |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 3 "If I'd ___, I would have ___." | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — structure slide + prompts. Stretch — add *could/might have* for possibility.
**Success check:** 3 correct 3rd conditionals. **Queue:** 3rd conditional recycles every warm-up this unit.
**Coach notes:** the reduced pronunciation ("would've/I'd've") is essential for natural delivery *and* listening — drill it. Common error: "would of" in writing (it's "would have").

## S3-U4-L02 — Third Conditional in Use (Regret, Criticism, Hypothetical Past)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 4 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can use the 3rd conditional to express regret, criticism, and past hypotheticals.
- The learner can soften criticism with it (*you could have…*).
- The learner can discuss "what would have happened if…".

**Target language:** regret/criticism functions; *could/might/should have*. **Grammar:** 3rd conditional +
modal perfects. **Accent Lab focus:** intonation of regret vs neutral hypothetical. **Mindset:** reframe regret as a lesson, not a wound.

**Materials:** regret/criticism scenario cards; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|

| 3 | Notice | Functions: regret, gentle criticism, hypothetical past; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Regret intonation; would/could/might have | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | React to scenarios with 3rd conditionals (regret/criticism) | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | Discuss "what would have happened if…" for a past event; record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check function + form | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post a regret + a "what would have happened if" | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — scenario cards + frames. Stretch — soften a criticism diplomatically (link to U9).
**Success check:** 3rd conditionals used for regret/criticism/hypothesis. **Queue:** recycles into L05/U12.
**Coach notes:** the softening use ("you could have asked me") is professionally valuable — preview U9 diplomacy.

## S3-U4-L03 — Mixed Conditionals (Past ↔ Present)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 4 · ~40 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L01–L02.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can form mixed conditionals (past condition → present result, and present condition → past result).
- The learner can link a past event to a present consequence.
- The learner can express how the past shapes now.

**Target language:** mixed conditionals. **Grammar:** *If* + past perfect → *would* + base (present result); *If* +
past simple → *would have* + participle (past result from a general truth). **Accent Lab focus:** phrasing/stress across mixed clauses.
**Mindset:** your past choices shaped now — and today's choices shape tomorrow.

**Materials:** a past→present timeline; a mixed-conditional card; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: 2nd (present unreal) + 3rd (past unreal) | 5 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "If I'd taken that job, I'd be in Dubai now." (past condition → present result) | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | Mixing time frames; the two common patterns; brief explanation | 7 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | Phrase + stress across the mixed clauses | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Build mixed conditionals from past→present prompts | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | 2 mixed conditionals about your life; record | 4 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check the time-frame mix | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post: "If I'd ___, I'd be ___ now." | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — timeline + the two-pattern card. Stretch — both mixed patterns in one turn.
**Success check:** correct mixed conditional linking time frames. **Queue:** recycles into U12.
**Coach notes:** mixed conditionals are conceptually the hardest B2 grammar — keep to the most common pattern (past condition → present result); don't overload. Reward meaning over perfect form.

## S3-U4-L04 — *wish / if only* + Past Perfect (Past Regrets)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 4 · ~35 min · recorded + live · prerequisites: L03; B1 *wish* + past.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can express past regrets with *wish/if only* + past perfect.
- The learner can distinguish present wishes (+ past) from past regrets (+ past perfect).
- The learner can talk about things they regret.

**Target language:** *I wish/if only + past perfect*. **Grammar:** *wish/if only* + past perfect (past regret) vs
+ past (present wish, from B1). **Accent Lab focus:** phrasing "I wish I'd…"; emphasis on the regret. **Mindset:** name a regret, extract the lesson, let it go.

**Materials:** present-wish vs past-regret contrast card; a clip; Google Form.

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall | Retrieval: B1 "I wish + past" (present wish) | 4 min |
| 2 | Input | Clip: "I wish I'd learned English earlier. If only I hadn't quit." | 6 min |
| 3 | Notice | *wish + past perfect* = past regret; contrast with present wish; brief note | 6 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab | "I wish I'd" phrasing; regret emphasis | 4 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Turn past events into regrets; contrast present wish vs past regret | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output | 3 real past regrets (+ the lesson learned); record | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Check *wish + past perfect* | — |
| 8 | Assignment | Post 2 past regrets + what you learned | — |

**Differentiation:** Support — contrast card. Stretch — pair each regret with a 3rd conditional.
**Success check:** correct *wish/if only + past perfect* for past regrets. **Queue:** recycles into U12.
**Coach notes:** tie to the mindset — regrets reframed as lessons. Contrast clearly with the B1 present wish to avoid conflation.

## S3-U4-L05 — Unit Speaking Task: "A Decision You'd Change" (+ Review)

**Metadata:** Stage 3 / B2 · Unit 4 · ~40 min · recorded + live (assessment-style) · prerequisites: L01–L04.

**Objectives (can-do):**
- The learner can discuss a past decision (~2 min): 3rd conditional + a mixed conditional + a *wish* regret + the lesson.
- The learner can respond to reflective follow-up questions.
- The learner records their **Unit-4 reflection**.

**Target language:** integrated Unit-4 language. **Grammar:** 3rd/mixed conditionals + wish consolidated.
**Accent Lab focus:** multi-clause stress + reduced *would have*; each learner's residual sound.
**Mindset:** reframing — regret becomes insight and forward motion.

**Materials:** a model reflection; a checklist card (the decision / a 3rd conditional / a mixed conditional / a wish regret / the lesson);
recording prompt; Unit-4 quiz (Google Form).

**Lesson flow:**

| # | Phase | Content | ~Time |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | Warm-up / recall (retrieval-heavy) | Rapid: 3rd conditional, mixed, wish + past perfect | 5 min |
| 2 | Input/model | Watch a clean reflection on a past decision | 4 min |
| 3 | Notice | Break it into the checklist | 4 min |
| 4 | Accent Lab clinic | Each learner drills reduced *would have* + a residual sound | 5 min |
| 5 | Guided output | Rehearse with the checklist; peer reflective questions | 8 min |
| 6 | Free output (task) | Record the ~2 min reflection + answer follow-ups | 5 min |
| 7 | Feedback | Self + peer check vs checklist; coach group-level feedback | 3 min |
| 8 | Assignment/queue | Post the reflection; complete Unit-4 quiz | 1 min |

**Differentiation:** Support — checklist + prep. Stretch — weave 3rd + mixed + wish fluidly with a forward-looking conclusion.
**Success check (formative summary of Unit 4):** ~2 min reflection integrating 3rd/mixed conditionals + a regret (rubric at B2). **Queue:** recycles into U12.
**Coach notes:** conceptually demanding but personally meaningful — reward accurate meaning and natural reduced forms; don't expect flawless mixed conditionals.
