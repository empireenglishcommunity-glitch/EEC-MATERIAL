## 1. Stage 3 overview

- **CEFR:** B2 · **EEC label:** Confident ⭐ (flagship graduation)
- **Entry criterion:** B1 achieved (Stage 2 exit or placement).
- **Guided-hour budget:** ~150–200 hrs · **Cohort length:** ~16–24 weeks @ ~7 hrs/week.
- **The flagship promise (headline):** *"Hold a natural 20–30 minute conversation on everyday and
  professional topics — clearly and confidently, with a neutral American accent."*
- **Arabic support:** **none** — full immersion; all instruction, feedback, and interaction in English.
- **Vocabulary target:** ~3,500–4,000+ words cumulative (professional/workplace, topic-specific, collocations, idiom-lite).
- **Skill weighting:** speaking ●●● · listening ●●● · reading ●●● · writing ●●● (all near-balanced at B2).

### Structure
- **12 units** · **Unit 12 = consolidation + the flagship B2 benchmark assessment.**
- **~5 lessons per unit** (≈ 60 lessons total), each ~40–50 min, built on the Lesson Template.
- **Weekly rhythm:** ~1 unit/1–1.5 weeks; recorded lessons + 1–2 live discussion/professional-practice sessions (English-only).

### Sequencing logic
B2 is the **"accuracy + range + naturalness"** stage. First **complete and sharpen the tense system**
(perfect continuous, past perfect, advanced future — U1–U3) so learners narrate and speculate with full
precision. Then the **hypothetical + inference** grammar (3rd/mixed conditionals, modals of deduction —
U4–U5). Then the **"advanced structures for nuance"** block (advanced passive, advanced relatives, advanced
reported speech — U6–U8) that lets learners sound educated and precise. Then the **naturalness + real-world
layer**: register & nuance (U9), professional communication (U10), abstract topics & debate (U11) — the skills
the flagship conversation demands. Unit 12 consolidates and runs the **20–30 min B2 benchmark** + the final
graduation "after" sample. Every unit recycles B1/A2 language and adds to the review queue.

### What's new vs B1
- **Full tense range + precision**: perfect continuous, past perfect, advanced future forms used accurately.
- **Nuance & register**: formal/informal control, diplomacy/softening, collocations, natural phrasing.
- **Professional & abstract range**: meetings, presentations, negotiating, defending opinions on abstract topics.
- **Accent = refinement**: from "clear" to "clear + natural" — prosody, thought groups, emphasis, self-monitoring.
- **Length & spontaneity**: from B1's 8–12 min discussion to a **20–30 min** wide-ranging conversation.

## 2. Cross-stage threads (Stage-3 slice)

- **Accent Lab focus (from `curriculum/03` §6 — Stage 3 = refinement):** fine-tuning residual segmental
  interference **per learner**; **prosody** (thought groups, phrasing), **sentence & contrastive stress** for
  meaning, **intonation for attitude** (certainty, doubt, politeness, emphasis), connected-speech mastery, and
  **self-monitoring / self-correction** — sounding clear *and confident* in professional contexts. Honesty
  guardrail (GC-5): the target is **clear, confident, neutral American**; native-like is an aspirational ceiling, never promised.
- **Mindset focus ("Real-World Fluency & Identity Lock-in" — `curriculum/04`):** using English in real life
  (work, calls, content, relationships); owning an English-speaking identity; from **"learner" to "user"**;
  strategies to keep improving independently after the program; confidence in high-stakes/professional settings.
- **Assessment:** end-of-unit formative checks (quiz + speaking task); **Unit 12 flagship B2 benchmark** — a
  **20–30 min conversation** covering everyday + professional topics, scored on the 5-dimension rubric with a
  **defined minimum across all five dimensions**; record the **graduation "after" sample** — the headline
  transformation proof (Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → B1 → **B2 graduation**), and, with consent, the flagship testimonial asset.

## 4. Unit detail + lesson lists

> Each lesson is authored with `curriculum/02-lesson-template.md`. Lesson IDs: `S3-U{n}-L{nn}`.
> Objectives are the "By the end, the learner can…" for that lesson. **All instruction English-only (immersion).**

### Unit 1 — Perfect Aspect Mastery
- **Can-do:** talk about duration/ongoing recent activity precisely; choose perfect vs simple accurately.
- **Grammar/function:** present perfect continuous (*have been doing*); continuous vs simple perfect; advanced *for/since/how long*.
- **Accent Lab:** thought groups; perfect contractions ("I've been…"). **Mindset:** identity — "I'm an English speaker now."
- **Lessons:** L01 present perfect continuous (form + use) · L02 perfect continuous vs simple (result vs activity) ·
  L03 *how long* + duration precision · L04 talking about ongoing projects/efforts · L05 speaking task ("what you've been working on") + review

### Unit 2 — Past Perfect & Narrative Mastery
- **Can-do:** narrate complex past events with correct sequence; explain what had happened before.
- **Grammar/function:** past perfect (*had done*) + past perfect continuous (*had been doing*); sequencing with past simple/continuous.
- **Accent Lab:** narrative prosody; phrasing long turns. **Mindset:** owning your progress story (retell your learning journey).
- **Lessons:** L01 past perfect (had done) · L02 past perfect continuous · L03 sequencing complex narratives (all past tenses) ·
  L04 explaining causes/backstory · L05 speaking task ("a turning point in your life") + review

### Unit 3 — Future Forms (Advanced)
- **Can-do:** talk about the future with precision and appropriate certainty.
- **Grammar/function:** future continuous (*will be doing*); future perfect (*will have done*); present continuous for arrangements; degrees of certainty (*likely/bound to/may well*).
- **Accent Lab:** intonation of certainty vs doubt. **Mindset:** planning your post-program English life.
- **Lessons:** L01 future continuous · L02 future perfect · L03 arrangements vs plans vs predictions (review + extend) ·
  L04 degrees of future certainty (hedged predictions) · L05 speaking task ("where you'll be in 5 years") + review

### Unit 4 — Third & Mixed Conditionals
- **Can-do:** talk about unreal past situations and their results; mix time frames; express regret.
- **Grammar/function:** 3rd conditional (*if* + past perfect, *would have*); mixed conditionals; *wish/if only* + past perfect.
- **Accent Lab:** stress across multi-clause conditional sentences. **Mindset:** reframing regret as learning.
- **Lessons:** L01 3rd conditional form · L02 3rd conditional in use (regret/criticism/hypothetical past) ·
  L03 mixed conditionals (past↔present) · L04 *wish/if only* + past perfect (past regrets) · L05 speaking task ("a decision you'd change") + review

### Unit 5 — Modals of Deduction & Speculation
- **Can-do:** make logical deductions and speculate about present and past with appropriate certainty.
- **Grammar/function:** *must/might/may/could/can't* (present deduction); *must/might/could/can't + have + participle* (past deduction).
- **Accent Lab:** intonation for certainty vs doubt; stress on the modal. **Mindset:** confident uncertainty — hedge naturally.
- **Lessons:** L01 present deduction (must/might/can't be) · L02 past deduction (must/might/can't have been) ·
  L03 speculating about situations/people · L04 explaining evidence + conclusions · L05 speaking task ("solve a mystery / speculate") + review

### Unit 6 — The Passive (Advanced)
- **Can-do:** use the passive across all tenses; report impersonally; describe services/arrangements (causative).
- **Grammar/function:** passive across tenses (perfect/continuous/modal passive); reporting passive (*It is said that…, He is thought to…*); causative *have/get something done*.
- **Accent Lab:** weak auxiliaries in complex verb strings. **Mindset:** sounding precise & professional.
- **Lessons:** L01 passive across tenses · L02 modal + perfect passives · L03 reporting passive (impersonal) ·
  L04 causative *have/get something done* · L05 speaking task ("describe a system/service") + review

### Unit 7 — Relative Clauses (Advanced)
- **Can-do:** add non-essential information smoothly; reduce clauses; use prepositions + relatives naturally.
- **Grammar/function:** non-defining relative clauses (commas, intonation); reduced relative clauses; *preposition + which/whom*; *which* referring to a whole clause.
- **Accent Lab:** pausing/intonation of non-defining clauses. **Mindset:** adding detail without losing the listener.
- **Lessons:** L01 non-defining relative clauses · L02 defining vs non-defining (meaning + punctuation) ·
  L03 reduced relative clauses · L04 prepositions + which/whom; *which* for a whole idea · L05 speaking task ("describe someone/something with rich detail") + review

### Unit 8 — Reported Speech (Advanced)
- **Can-do:** report a range of speech acts accurately with varied reporting verbs and patterns.
- **Grammar/function:** reporting-verb range (*suggest, admit, deny, warn, remind, offer, promise, refuse, recommend*) + their patterns (+ *-ing* / + *that* / + *to* / + object + *to*).
- **Accent Lab:** rhythm across reporting frames. **Mindset:** retelling professionally & accurately.
- **Lessons:** L01 reporting-verb range (overview) · L02 verb + *-ing* / verb + *that* patterns · L03 verb + *to* / verb + object + *to* patterns ·
  L04 reporting a meeting/negotiation accurately · L05 speaking task ("report a discussion/negotiation") + review

### Unit 9 — Register & Nuance
- **Can-do:** shift between formal and informal English; soften/be diplomatic; use natural collocations.
- **Grammar/function:** formal vs informal choices; diplomatic/softening language (*I was wondering if…, would it be possible…, to be honest…*); high-frequency collocations & natural phrasing.
- **Accent Lab:** register-appropriate prosody; politeness intonation. **Mindset:** code-switching confidently.
- **Lessons:** L01 formal vs informal (vocab, grammar, tone) · L02 diplomacy & softening (bad news, disagreement, requests) ·
  L03 collocations & natural phrasing (sound less "textbook") · L04 adapting register to the situation · L05 speaking task ("same message, two registers") + review

### Unit 10 — Professional Communication
- **Can-do:** participate in meetings, give a short presentation, negotiate, and handle professional calls.
- **Grammar/function:** meeting language (chairing, interrupting politely, clarifying); presentation structure & signposting; negotiating (proposing, conceding, bargaining); phone/video-call & email-to-speech norms.
- **Accent Lab:** presentation prosody; emphasis for impact. **Mindset:** English as a career multiplier.
- **Lessons:** L01 meetings (participate, interrupt, clarify) · L02 giving a short presentation (structure + signposting) ·
  L03 negotiating (propose/concede/bargain) · L04 professional calls & follow-ups · L05 speaking task ("present + field questions") + review

### Unit 11 — Abstract Topics & Debate
- **Can-do:** discuss abstract/professional topics, build and defend a structured argument, handle nuance.
- **Grammar/function:** structured argument (claim → evidence → example → conclusion); defending/conceding; handling counter-arguments; moving from small talk to deep talk.
- **Accent Lab:** contrastive stress; persuasive intonation. **Mindset:** thinking + arguing in English.
- **Lessons:** L01 handling abstract topics (frameworks + vocab) · L02 building a structured argument · L03 defending & conceding under challenge ·
  L04 small talk → deep talk (transitions) · L05 speaking task ("debate a professional/abstract topic") + review

### Unit 12 — Putting It Together (B2 Flagship Consolidation & Graduation) ⭐
- **Can-do:** demonstrate the **B2 exit criteria** — a natural **20–30 min conversation** on everyday +
  professional topics, clearly and confidently, with a neutral American accent, and a defined minimum across all rubric dimensions.
- **Grammar/function:** integrated review of the full Stage-3 range.
- **Accent Lab:** overall naturalness + self-monitoring; final refinement of each learner's residuals. **Mindset:** graduation — "learner → confident user."
- **Lessons:** L01 big review 1 (tenses + conditionals + deduction) · L02 big review 2 (advanced passive/relatives/reported + register) ·
  L03 accent & fluency clinic (naturalness + self-monitoring) · L04 full B2 mock conversation (20–30 min) · L05 **B2 flagship benchmark + graduation "after" sample**

## 5. Assessment placement (links to Task 1.6 + before/after protocol)

- **Before Unit 1:** confirm B1 exit / B2 readiness (Stage-2 summative or placement).
- **End of each unit:** short formative check (quiz + speaking task) — feedback, not gatekeeping.
- **Unit 12, L05 — the flagship benchmark:** the **20–30 min B2 conversation** (everyday + professional topics),
  scored on the 5-dimension rubric with a **defined minimum in every dimension** → B2 graduation determination;
  record the **graduation "after" sample** — the headline proof (Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → B1 → **B2**), and, with consent, the flagship testimonial.

## 6. Spaced-review threading

- Every unit's warm-ups retrieve prior Stage-3 units + **B1/A2** (e.g., U4 3rd conditional contrasts with the
  B1 1st/2nd; U8 advanced reported extends B1 reported; U6 advanced passive extends B1 passive).
- New target language enters the review queue (~next lesson → ~3 days → ~1 week → later units).
- **The full tense system, conditionals, and connected-speech/prosody** are deliberately re-drilled across
  units. Unit 12 is retrieval-heavy and integrative (the whole program consolidates into the flagship conversation).

## 7. Build order (recommendation)

Build in teaching order **Unit 1 → Unit 12**, so the cohort continues seamlessly from B1, with later units
completed just ahead of the cohort (design §3.4). Author every lesson with the Lesson Template and obey the
design checklist. The **naturalness + real-world block (U9–U11)** and **Unit 12** are the priority for the
flagship graduation outcome — they most directly build the 20–30 min professional conversation.
