# Accent Lab — Free Series Scripts (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 2.2 · **Requirements:** R6 (content), R4 (Accent Lab)
> **Reads with:** `content/01-content-strategy-and-calendar.md`, `curriculum/03-accent-lab-syllabus.md`,
> `brand/01-brand-foundations.md`.
> **Purpose:** Ready-to-film scripts for EEC's signature free series — the content that proves the method
> and feeds the funnel. Short-form (TikTok/Reels/FB/Shorts) + two long-form YouTube outlines. Narration in
> **Egyptian Arabic**, English models on screen; every script ends with a next step (CTA).

---

## 0. How to use

- Batch-film these (founder on camera). Editor cuts + adds **bilingual subtitles** (Arabic + English) and
  brand templates (brand §8).
- Keep shorts **20–45s**. One sound = one video. Model each sound **slow → natural**.
- Rotate CTAs (below). Never promise a guaranteed native accent (GC-5) — promise **clarity + a quick win**.

**CTA rotation (pick one per video):**
- Follow: *"تابعني علشان سلسلة Accent Lab كاملة."*
- Comment: *"اكتبلي كلمة إنجليزي بتتلخبط فيها وأنا أصلّحهالك."*
- Waitlist/challenge: *"عايز نظام كامل من الصفر؟ اللينك في البايو — سجّل في الـ waitlist."*

**Series naming:** **"Accent Lab — الأصوات اللي العربي بيغلط فيها"** (episodes numbered).

## 1. Short-form scripts

> Format: **# · Title/Hook · target (syllabus card) · script (AR narration + EN models) · on-screen · demo · CTA.**

### AL-01 — "school مش iskool" ⭐ (signature)
- **Target:** consonant clusters / no vowel insertion (Card C9).
- **Hook (0–2s):** *"لو بتقول 'iskool' بدل 'school'، الفيديو ده هيغيّر نطقك."*
- **Value:** *"العربي بيحط حرف علة بين السواكن. الإنجليزي لأ — بنعدّي على السواكن على طول."*
  Model: **s-k-school** (slow) → **school** (natural). Same with **street** (مش istreet) و **spring**.
- **On-screen:** ❌ is-kool → ✅ school · ❌ is-treet → ✅ street
- **Demo:** hand gesture "glide through," no stop between sounds.
- **Proof/so-what:** *"ده مش تفصيلة — ده اللي بيخلّي كلامك يبان native أكتر."*
- **CTA:** Follow the series.

### AL-02 — "job مش gob" ⭐ (Egyptian-specific killer)
- **Target:** /dʒ/ vs /g/ (Card C4) — Egyptians have /g/, struggle with /dʒ/.
- **Hook:** *"إحنا كمصريين بنقول الجيم /g/ — علشان كده بنغلط في صوت الـ /dʒ/."*
- **Value:** *"job مش gob. جرّب: d + j... 'job'. كمان: 'orange', 'engineer', 'manage'."*
  Model each slow → natural. Contrast: **get** (/g/) vs **jet** (/dʒ/).
- **On-screen:** ❌ gob → ✅ job · get vs jet
- **Demo:** tongue to the ridge, soft release.
- **CTA:** Comment a word with "j".

### AL-03 — "people مش beople"
- **Target:** /p/ vs /b/ (Card C1).
- **Hook:** *"العربي مفيهاش حرف P — علشان كده بنقول beople."*
- **Value:** the **paper-strip test**: hold paper near your mouth — **/p/** puffs it (people, happy, stop),
  **/b/** doesn't. Model pairs: **pen/Ben, pig/big**.
- **On-screen:** ❌ beople → ✅ people (puff icon)
- **Demo:** paper moving on /p/.
- **CTA:** Follow / try the paper test and comment.

### AL-04 — "very مش fery"
- **Target:** /v/ vs /f/ (Card C2).
- **Hook:** *"في فرق بين very و fery — وأنت غالباً بتقول fery."*
- **Value:** *"سنانك الفوق على شفتك التحت + صوت من الحلق = /v/."* Model: **very, five, love, video**.
  Pairs: **van/fan, save/safe**.
- **On-screen:** ❌ fery → ✅ very (teeth-lip icon)
- **CTA:** Comment.

### AL-05 — "think مش sink/tink"
- **Target:** /θ/ and /ð/ (Card C3).
- **Hook:** *"لو بتقول 'sink' وأنت قصدك 'think'، إنت مش لوحدك."*
- **Value:** *"طرف لسانك بين سنانك: think, three, mother, this."* /θ/ (no voice) vs /ð/ (voice).
- **On-screen:** ❌ sink/tink → ✅ think
- **Demo:** tongue slightly out, exaggerate then relax.
- **CTA:** Follow the series.

### AL-06 — "ship مش sheep" (vowels)
- **Target:** /ɪ/ vs /iː/ (Card V1).
- **Hook:** *"'ship' و 'sheep' مش نفس الكلمة — والفرق ممكن يكسّفك."*
- **Value:** short & relaxed **ship** vs long & smiley **sheep**. Pairs: **bit/beat, live/leave**.
- **On-screen:** 🚢 ship (short) vs 🐑 sheep (long)
- **CTA:** Comment which one is harder.

### AL-07 — "readin vs reading" (the -ing sound)
- **Target:** /ŋ/ (Card C5).
- **Hook:** *"'readin' ولا 'reading'? في صوت ناقص."*
- **Value:** the /ŋ/ — back of tongue up, nasal, **من غير g قوية في الآخر**. Model: **doing, morning, going**.
- **On-screen:** ❌ readin / readingg → ✅ reading
- **CTA:** Follow.

### AL-08 — "13 مش 30" (a mistake that costs money)
- **Target:** -teen vs -ty word stress (Card P1) + /θ/.
- **Hook:** *"الغلطة دي ممكن تخليك تدفع غلط: thirteen ولا thirty؟"*
- **Value:** stress: thir**TEEN** (على الآخر) vs **THIR**ty (على الأول). Same: fif**teen**/**fif**ty.
- **On-screen:** 13 = thir-TEEN · 30 = THIR-ty
- **Proof:** *"في المحل ده بيفرق بجد — نطق الـ stress صح."*
- **CTA:** Waitlist (system).

### AL-09 — "left مش right" (American /r/ + /l/)
- **Target:** /l/–/r/ quality (Card C6).
- **Hook:** *"الـ R الأمريكاني مش راء مكررة — بلاش ترررر."*
- **Value:** bunch the tongue back, no trill: **red, right, car, sorry**. Contrast **light/right**.
- **On-screen:** ❌ (rolled r) → ✅ American r
- **CTA:** Comment a word with R.

### AL-10 — "ليه بتفهم إنجليزي بس مش بتعرف تتكلم؟" (system crossover)
- **Target:** method framing via speaking (pillar: System) — still an Accent Lab-adjacent hook.
- **Hook:** *"بتفهم الأفلام بس أول ما تتكلم بتقفل؟ المشكلة مش في معلوماتك."*
- **Value:** *"الطلاقة مهارة مش معلومة — محتاج input + كلام فعلي + مراجعة. التِكّات مش هتوصّلك."*
  Tease the roadmap (zero → confident) + Accent Lab as one piece.
- **On-screen:** tips ≠ system · understand ≠ speak
- **CTA:** Waitlist — "نظام كامل من الصفر."

### AL-11 — "gonna / wanna" (natural speech teaser)
- **Target:** reductions (Card P3) — a "sound natural" quick win.
- **Hook:** *"الـ natives مبيقولوش 'going to' — بيقولوا gonna."*
- **Value:** **going to → gonna, want to → wanna, got to → gotta** (informal speech). Model in a sentence.
- **On-screen:** going to → gonna
- **CTA:** Follow for connected speech.

### AL-12 — "Day 1 vs Now" (proof format template)
- **Target:** proof pillar (uses consented before/after — protocol §6).
- **Hook:** *"من الصفر لحد محادثة حقيقية — بصّ الفرق."*
- **Value:** split screen: learner's **Day 1** clip → **Now** clip (same prompt). Label dates/level.
- **On-screen:** "Day 1 — beginner" → "10 weeks — A1"
- **Proof:** *"ده اللي بيحصل لما تمشي على نظام."*
- **CTA:** Join the challenge / waitlist.
- **Note:** only with Tier-2 consent; honest framing (timeframe + effort).

## 2. Long-form YouTube outlines

### YT-01 — "5 أصوات كل مصري بيغلط فيها في الإنجليزي (وإزاي تصلّحها)"
- **Length:** 8–12 min. **Goal:** authority + evergreen search + funnel.
- **Structure:** hook + founder credibility (10s) → why Arabic causes predictable errors (the system angle)
  → the 5 sounds (/dʒ/ "job", clusters "school", /p/, /v/, /θ/) each with cue + model + drill → a mini
  practice paragraph → CTA (waitlist + "this is one module of a full system").
- **Repurpose into:** AL-01–05 shorts.

### YT-02 — "ليه معظم الناس بتفشل في تعلّم الإنجليزي (والنظام الصح)"
- **Length:** 8–12 min. **Goal:** position EEC vs tips culture; sell the *system* idea.
- **Structure:** the dropout problem (honest data) → why tips/hacks fail → the EEC method in plain terms
  (input + speaking + feedback + review + accent + mindset) → the CEFR roadmap idea (zero → B2) → proof →
  CTA (waitlist/challenge).
- **Repurpose into:** AL-10 + mindset shorts.

## 3. Repurposing & production notes

- **Film once, cut many:** one batch session → 4–6 shorts; one YouTube → 2–3 shorts + a Short.
- **Every short needs:** clear audio, bilingual subtitles, the ❌→✅ visual, and a single CTA.
- **Model audio:** American-English models from the Accent Lab audio bank; founder demonstrates.
- **Consistency:** same intro sting, subtitle style, and end-card (brand §8).
- **Engagement loop:** answer "comment a word" replies with quick fix videos (endless content + leads).
