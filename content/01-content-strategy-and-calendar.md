# EEC Content Strategy & Calendar (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 2.1 · **Requirements:** R6 (Free Content Engine), supports R10 (GTM)
> **Reads with:** `brand/01-brand-foundations.md`, `curriculum/03-accent-lab-syllabus.md` (§9 spin-off),
> `operations/01-tool-stack.md`, `strategy/01-foundational-strategy.md`.
> **Purpose:** The plan for EEC's free content — the top of the funnel. It builds an audience, proves the
> method, and routes viewers to the waitlist → entry challenge → flagship cohort. Content is **marketing,
> not the product**; its job is trust and attention that convert.

---

## 1. Positioning & principles

**The wedge:** the market (esp. Egyptian English content, led by free giants like ZAmerican) is full of
*isolated tips and accent tricks*. EEC's angle is the opposite:

> **"A real system, not viral tricks."** Every piece of content is a visible piece of a larger,
> results-driven method.

**Principles:**
1. **System over hacks** — even a 30-second clip hints at the bigger roadmap.
2. **Egyptian Arabic, adult, respectful** — relatable and high-reach; speak to ambition, not with hype.
3. **Prove, don't claim** — student results and honest before/afters over bold promises (GC-5).
4. **Every post has a job** — attract, teach a quick win, or convert. Always a next step.
5. **Consistency > virality** — a reliable cadence beats chasing one viral hit (we practice what we preach).
6. **Repurpose relentlessly** — film once, cut into many (protects the solo founder's time).

---

## 2. Objectives (what content must achieve)

- Grow a **followed audience** across platforms (attention + trust).
- Demonstrate the **method + the founder's credibility** (authority).
- Drive viewers to **lead capture (waitlist)** → **entry challenge** → **flagship cohort**.
- Feed the **proof flywheel** (each cohort's results become content that lowers CAC).

---

## 3. Platforms & their roles

| Platform | Primary role | Format focus |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Reach / discovery (top of funnel) | Short vertical video (15–60s), hooks, quick wins |
| **Instagram (Reels + carousels)** | Reach + nurture | Reels (repurposed TikToks) + carousels + Stories (community/CTAs) |
| **Facebook** | Reach in Egypt (older/broader) | Reels + posts + groups (very strong in Egypt) |
| **YouTube (long + Shorts)** | Authority + depth + evergreen search | 5–12 min lessons/explainers; Shorts repurposed |
| **WhatsApp/Telegram broadcast** | Owned nurture (warm audience) | Tips + CTAs to waitlist/challenge |
| **Email (later)** | Owned list, highest conversion | Sequences to challenge/cohort |

**Priority for launch:** TikTok + Reels + Facebook for reach (cheap, fast in Egypt); one weekly YouTube
for depth/authority. Build the **owned list** (waitlist + WhatsApp/Telegram) from day one — algorithms
rent attention; a list is owned.

## 4. Content pillars (the recurring themes)

Rotate these so the feed is varied but coherent. Suggested mix per week in parentheses.

1. **The Accent Lab** *(≈30%)* — signature. "The sounds Arabic speakers always get wrong," *"job vs gob"*,
   *"why we say 'iskool' (and how to stop)"*. High recognition + quick win + demonstrates the method.
2. **System & Method** *(≈15%)* — why tips fail, how real fluency is built, the roadmap idea, "input +
   speaking + review," honest expectations. Positions EEC vs the hacks crowd.
3. **Quick Wins / Micro-lessons** *(≈20%)* — one useful, immediately usable bit (a phrase, a fix, a
   grammar point in 30s) — always tied to the bigger system.
4. **Proof / Student Results** *(≈15%)* — before/after clips (consented), testimonials, milestones. The
   most persuasive content; grows as cohorts complete.
5. **Mindset & Motivation** *(≈10%)* — consistency, fear of speaking, habits, "the one who shows up wins."
   Mirrors the Mindset track; builds the culture.
6. **Founder story / Behind the scenes** *(≈10%)* — the Egyptian→near-native journey, real life in Dubai
   with 200+ nationalities, why EEC exists. Builds trust and relatability.

## 5. The funnel (how content converts)

```
   CONTENT (TikTok/Reels/FB/YouTube)
        │  every piece has a CTA
        ▼
   LEAD CAPTURE — the Waitlist (owned: form + WhatsApp/Telegram)
        │  nurture with tips + proof
        ▼
   ENTRY CHALLENGE (7–14 days, low price) — first quick win + filter
        │  upsell to serious learners
        ▼
   FLAGSHIP COHORT (core product)
        │  on graduation
        ▼
   COMMUNITY MEMBERSHIP (retention) → produces PROOF → feeds content (flywheel)
```

**CTA rules:** free content rarely "sells the cohort" directly. It sends people to a **low-friction next
step** — join the waitlist / free challenge / "comment a word and I'll fix your pronunciation." Warm them,
then the challenge and cohort convert.

## 6. Short-form structure (the reusable template)

Every short video follows: **Hook → Value → Proof/So-what → CTA.**
- **Hook (first 1–2s):** the scroll-stopper. e.g., *"لو بتقول 'iskool' بدل 'school'، الفيديو ده ليك."*
- **Value (10–40s):** ONE clear idea/fix, shown and modeled (say it slow → natural).
- **Proof / so-what:** why it matters (intelligibility, sounding natural) — the system angle.
- **CTA:** one next step (follow for the series / comment a word / link in bio → waitlist/challenge).

**Hook bank (starter ideas):** "3 sounds Arabic speakers always get wrong"; "job vs gob"; "stop saying
'iskool'"; "you don't need more grammar — you need this"; "why you understand English but can't speak it";
"I went from zero to near-native — here's the system, not tricks."

## 7. Cadence & the weekly calendar

**Launch cadence (sustainable for a solo founder + 1 editor):**
- **Short-form:** 4–6 per week (TikTok → repurposed to Reels + FB + Shorts).
- **Long-form YouTube:** 1 per week (a fuller Accent Lab or method lesson).
- **Owned nurture:** 2–3 WhatsApp/Telegram broadcasts per week (tip + soft CTA).
- **Live (once audience warms):** a weekly free live Q&A / mini-lesson (great for conversion).

**Sample weekly plan:**

| Day | Piece | Pillar | Platform(s) | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Accent Lab short | Accent Lab | TikTok/Reels/FB | Follow the series |
| Tue | Quick win micro-lesson | Quick Wins | TikTok/Reels/FB | Comment a word |
| Wed | YouTube lesson (long) | Method / Accent Lab | YouTube (+ Short cut) | Waitlist link |
| Thu | Proof / student clip | Proof | Reels/FB/TikTok | Join the challenge |
| Fri | Mindset/motivation | Mindset | TikTok/Reels | Follow / waitlist |
| Sat | Founder story / BTS | Founder | Reels/FB | Trust (soft CTA) |
| Sun | Recap carousel / broadcast | Mixed | IG carousel + WhatsApp | Waitlist/challenge |

## 8. Production & repurposing workflow (lean)

1. **Batch film** (founder, 1–2 sessions/week on camera) — record several shorts + the YouTube lesson at once.
2. **Editor cuts** (Egyptian editor, EGP): the founder's raw footage → 4–6 shorts + 1 long video +
   Shorts, with **bilingual subtitles** (Arabic + English) for reach and accessibility.
3. **Repurpose:** one YouTube lesson → 2–3 shorts; one idea → post + carousel + broadcast.
4. **Schedule/publish** via a simple scheduler (or native); log in the content calendar.
5. **Engage:** reply to comments (pronunciation fixes in comments = great engagement + lead magnet).

This maps to the tool stack (§7, tool-stack doc) and the lean team (Handbook/ops): founder = face, editor
+ community manager = EGP part-time.

## 9. Brand & compliance

- Voice = brand foundations §7 (encouraging, honest, disciplined; Egyptian Arabic; no hacks/hype).
- **Never** promise guaranteed native accent or "fluent in X days."
- All proof is **consented + honestly framed** (before/after protocol §4, §9).
- Consistent visual templates (brand §8): thumbnails, subtitle style, quote cards, the EEC mark.

---

## 10. Metrics (feeds R13 funnel KPIs)

Track the funnel, not just vanity:
- **Reach/attention:** views, watch-time, follower growth (per platform).
- **Engagement:** saves, shares, comments (saves/shares > likes for intent).
- **Conversion funnel:** content → **waitlist signups** → **challenge buyers** → **cohort enrollments**;
  conversion rate at each step.
- **CAC:** ad spend + attributable effort ÷ enrollments — target *falling* cohort over cohort (flywheel).
- Review at cohort cadence; double down on pillars/formats that convert (not just those that go viral).

## 11. Starter plan — first 30 days (seed the audience before launch)

Goal: build initial audience + waitlist ahead of the founding cohort.
- **Week 1:** publish the founder-story piece + 3 Accent Lab shorts; open the **waitlist**; set up channels.
- **Week 2:** 4–5 shorts (Accent Lab + quick wins) + first YouTube lesson; start WhatsApp/Telegram broadcasts.
- **Week 3:** add a "system vs hacks" piece + first mindset piece; begin **warm-starting** the founder's
  Dubai/Egyptian network toward the waitlist.
- **Week 4:** run a free **live** mini-lesson/Q&A; tease the **entry challenge**; convert waitlist → challenge.
- Throughout: reply to every comment; capture emails/numbers; note which hooks/pillars land.
