> **Purpose:** The action plan to actually **go live** — start publishing consistently and **warm-start**
> the founder's existing Dubai/Egyptian network — so there's an audience and a waitlist ready when the
> founding cohort opens (Phase 3). This is an execution playbook, not new theory.

---

## 1. Goals of the launch window (first ~4–6 weeks)

1. **Publish consistently** on the chosen platforms (build reach + trust).
2. **Grow the waitlist** (the owned audience) toward a target big enough to fill a founding cohort.
3. **Warm-start** the founder's real network (Dubai + Egypt) — the highest-intent, lowest-cost leads.
4. **Run the first entry challenge** to filter and convert → **fill the founding cohort** (hands to Phase 3).

**North-star for the window:** enough waitlist + challenge sign-ups to enrol a **~15–30 person founding
cohort** (strategy §5 / Task 3.1).

## 3. Publishing launch (weeks 1–4)

Follow the content-strategy starter plan (§11 there), operationalized:

| Week | Publishing focus | Owned-audience action | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder-story piece + 3 Accent Lab shorts (AL-01,02,03); open waitlist | Broadcast: welcome + lead magnet | First followers + first waitlist signups |
| 2 | 4–5 shorts (AL-04–08) + first YouTube (YT-01); start "comment a word" replies | 2–3 nurture broadcasts | Momentum + engagement leads |
| 3 | "System vs hacks" + mindset short + more Accent Lab; begin **warm-start outreach** (§4) | Nurture + reply videos | Waitlist growth + warm leads |
| 4 | Free **live** mini-lesson/Q&A; tease the entry challenge | Invite waitlist → challenge | Convert waitlist → challenge |

**Daily posting workflow (lean):** editor delivers cuts → founder approves → schedule/post → **founder
replies to comments daily** (esp. "comment a word" → quick-fix reply videos = engagement + leads). Batch
film weekly so a busy week never breaks the cadence (consistency = the brand promise).

**Engagement routine (15–20 min/day):** reply to comments/DMs, welcome new waitlist members, post one
community prompt. Relationships convert; drive-by views don't.

## 4. Warm-start the network (the unfair advantage)

The founder lives in Dubai among Egyptians/Arabs who **need better English for work** and already trust
him — the warmest, cheapest, fastest leads. Work this deliberately, not passively.

**Steps:**
1. **List contacts** who want/need English (colleagues, friends, community, family, referrals) — aim for
   a first list of 30–100 names.
2. **Personal outreach** (WhatsApp/DM, one-to-one, not a mass blast): a warm, honest note —
   > *"بشتغل على نظام لتعليم الإنجليزي من الصفر لحد محادثة بثقة، مخصوص للعرب. عامل مجموعة أولى صغيرة
   > بسعر خاص. تحب أبعتلك التفاصيل؟"*
3. **Invite to the waitlist** (or straight to the challenge when open) — give the lead magnet first.
4. **Ask for intros:** *"تعرف حد نفسه يتعلم إنجليزي؟"* — referrals from a trusted person convert well.
5. **Offer founding-cohort priority + discount** in exchange for feedback + (consented) testimonials.
6. **Use them as content:** early wins from the network become your first proof clips (with consent).

**Why this matters:** founding cohorts filled from a warm network complete better, give better feedback,
and produce the testimonials that power the proof flywheel for future paid acquisition.

## 5. Timeline to the founding cohort

```
Weeks 1–4:  publish + grow waitlist + warm-start outreach
Week 4–5:   run the ENTRY CHALLENGE (wave 1) from waitlist + network
Week 5–6:   Day-7 challenge pitch → open FOUNDING COHORT enrollment (Phase 3)
Week 6+:    onboarding + kickoff → deliver Stage 0 (Phase 4)
```
*(Timeline flexes to the founder's 24–30 hrs/week; the sequence is what matters, not exact dates.)*

## 6. Roles during launch (lean)

- **Founder:** on-camera content, live sessions, warm-start outreach, comment replies, the Day-7 pitch.
- **Video editor (Egypt, EGP):** cuts, subtitles, templates, Shorts/repurposing.
- **Community manager / VA (Egypt, EGP):** schedule posts, welcome/nurture waitlist, moderate community,
  run check-ins/nudges, admin the challenge.

## 7. Metrics & targets for the window (feeds R13)

- **Publishing consistency:** hit the weekly cadence (leading indicator — control it).
- **Waitlist growth:** signups/week; source attribution (which content/warm-start converts).
- **Engagement:** saves/shares/comments/replies; DM conversations.
- **Warm-start:** # contacts reached, # to waitlist, # referrals.
- **Challenge:** signups, completion, and **challenge → cohort** conversion.
- **Outcome target:** waitlist + challenge large enough to enrol the founding cohort (~15–30).

## 9. Definition of done for Phase 2

Phase 2 is complete when: **channels are live and publishing consistently, the waitlist is capturing and
nurturing leads, the warm-start outreach is underway, and the entry challenge is ready to run** — i.e.,
there is a real audience + pipeline to fill the founding cohort. From here, **Phase 3** designs and opens
the founding-cohort offer to that pipeline.
