# Entry Challenge — Daily Lessons (Day 0–7) (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 3B.5 · **Requirements:** R6, R9 · **Reads with:** `content/04-entry-challenge.md` (design),
> `curriculum/03-accent-lab-syllabus.md`, `curriculum/stage0/unit0…unit1`, `curriculum/04-mindset-and-accountability-track.md`.
> **Purpose:** The ready-to-deliver content for the **"7 Days to Your First English Conversation"** challenge.
> Each day = a short recorded lesson (Egyptian Arabic) + one action + one community post. Reuses existing
> Stage-0 / Accent Lab / Mindset material, so almost no new build. Deliver in the challenge group + our site.

**Daily shape:** 🎬 short video (2–5 min) · ✅ action (record/do) · 💬 community post · ⏱️ ~10–20 min total.

---

## Day 0 — Welcome & your "before" clip
- **Goal:** get set up, set a goal, record the baseline.
- 🎬 Welcome (Egyptian Arabic): *"أهلاً بيك في تحدي ٧ أيام. في آخر التحدي هتقدر تعمل تعريف بنفسك ومحادثة بسيطة — وهتشوف الفرق بنفسك."* How the 7 days work.
- ✅ **Record your "Day-0" clip** (30s) on the standard prompt: *"What's your name? Where are you from? What do you do?"* (attempt in English, even if broken — that's the point).
- ✅ Set your goal + your daily 15-min slot (Mindset M0).
- 💬 Post: your intro clip + your goal. Say hi to 2 people.
- **Coach note:** reassure — it's a baseline, not a test. Reply to every post.

## Day 1 — Two quick sound wins: /p/–/b/ and /v/–/f/
- **Goal:** first pronunciation win.
- 🎬 Accent Lab cards C1 + C2: **/p/** = lips + puff (paper test); **/v/** = top teeth on lower lip + voice.
- ✅ Record yourself saying: **pen, book, van, very, five** (slow → natural).
- 💬 Post your voice note. Notice the puff on /p/.
- **Coach note:** expect "beople/fery"; celebrate audible puffs + teeth-on-lip.

## Day 2 — Stop saying "iskool": consonant clusters
- **Goal:** the signature Arabic fix.
- 🎬 Accent Lab card C9: glide through clusters, **no added vowel**. Build: s…k…**school**.
- ✅ Record: **school, street, spring, desk** — clean, no "is-" in front.
- 💬 Post your voice note.
- **Coach note:** this is the highest-value fix — make it feel like a superpower unlock.

## Day 3 — "job مش gob" + your first frame
- **Goal:** the Egyptian-specific /dʒ/ + start speaking.
- 🎬 Accent Lab card C4: **/dʒ/** (job) vs our /g/. Contrast **get / jet**. Then the frame *"I'm a/an ___."*
- ✅ Record: **job, orange, engineer** + *"I'm a/an [your job]."*
- 💬 Post: say your job in English.
- **Coach note:** "gob→job" is memorable; tie it to their real job.

## Day 4 — Introduce yourself (name + country + job)  *(optional live)*
- **Goal:** first connected self-introduction.
- 🎬 Stage-0 U1 frames: *"Hello, I'm… I'm from… I'm a…"*
- ✅ **Record a 3-sentence self-introduction** (real info).
- 🔴 **Optional live session:** everyone introduces themselves in pairs (breakout rooms) — the community high point.
- 💬 Post your 3-sentence intro.
- **Coach note:** watch dropped "am/is" ("I teacher") — model the fix warmly.

## Day 5 — A simple conversation
- **Goal:** first back-and-forth.
- 🎬 Greetings + *"How are you?"* + a 4-line mini-dialogue (Stage-0 U1-L01).
- ✅ Record/roleplay a 4-line exchange with a challenge buddy (or solo, both roles): greet → how are you → intro → reply.
- 💬 Post your exchange; tag a buddy.
- **Coach note:** celebrate the first real dialogue.

## Day 6 — Consistency: why you'll actually make it
- **Goal:** lock the mindset.
- 🎬 Mindset M1–M2: consistency > intensity; mistakes are data; never miss twice.
- ✅ Commit to your practice slot; **re-listen to your Day-0 clip** and note 2 things already better.
- 💬 Post one "brave mistake" + your commitment.
- **Coach note:** this is the retention lever — make them feel the progress.

## Day 7 — Your "after" clip + your next step
- **Goal:** see the transformation + the invitation.
- 🎬 Recap the week's wins.
- ✅ **Record the SAME prompt as Day 0** (*"Tell me about yourself, your family, your day"* — attempt fuller). Compare Day-0 ↔ Day-7 = your mini before/after.
- 💬 Post your before/after (with consent) — celebrate!
- 🎯 **The invitation:** here's the full journey (zero → confident B2) + the **Founding Cohort** — challenge-graduate discount + your challenge fee credited toward tuition. (Link to the offer.)
- **Coach note:** this is the emotional peak → make the cohort offer right here (see runbook §4).
