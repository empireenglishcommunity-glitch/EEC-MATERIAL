# The Entry Challenge — "7 Days to Your First English Conversation" (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 2.4 · **Requirements:** R9 (business/funnel), R10 (GTM), supports R6
> **Reads with:** `content/03-waitlist-funnel.md`, `curriculum/stage0/` (Units 0–1),
> `curriculum/03-accent-lab-syllabus.md`, `curriculum/04-mindset-and-accountability-track.md`,
> `curriculum/06-before-after-protocol.md`, `brand/01-brand-foundations.md`.
> **Purpose:** The paid on-ramp between free content and the flagship cohort. A short, cheap, high-win
> challenge that (1) gives a real quick win, (2) filters *serious* learners (money + action = intent),
> (3) lets them experience the EEC method, and (4) converts them into the flagship cohort. It also
> produces a mini before/after — proof from day one.

---

## 1. Concept & promise

**Name:** **"تحدي ٧ أيام: أول محادثة إنجليزي"** — *"7 Days to Your First English Conversation."*

**Promise (honest, achievable):**
> في ٧ أيام، هتصلّح أصعب أصوات بتغلط فيها، وتقدر تعمل **تعريف بنفسك ومحادثة بسيطة بالإنجليزي** —
> وهتسجّلها فيديو تشوف الفرق بنفسك. مش هتبقى fluent في أسبوع — بس هتاخد أول win حقيقي وتعرف إنت في نظام بيشتغل.

**Why this challenge:** it samples the real product (Stage 0 Units 0–1 + Accent Lab + Mindset), delivers
an emotional "I can speak!" moment, and creates a natural bridge to the full journey (zero → B2).

---

## 2. Format & delivery

- **Length:** 7 days (kept short = high completion; can test a 14-day variant later).
- **Daily load:** ~10–20 min — one short recorded lesson + one action + one community post.
- **Delivery (lean, tool stack):** recorded daily lessons (Skool/Circle or even a private channel) +
  **WhatsApp/Telegram** cohort chat for accountability + **one optional live** session (Day 4 or 7).
- **Community:** a dedicated challenge group; daily check-ins and voice-note posts.
- **Capacity:** runs in **cohorts/waves** (shared start date) for momentum — not evergreen self-paced.

---

## 3. The 7-day plan

> Each day = short lesson (Egyptian Arabic) + action + community post. Built from Stage 0 Units 0–1 +
> Accent Lab cards + Mindset M0–M2. Day 0 = onboarding.

**Day 0 — Onboarding & your "Day 1" clip**
- Welcome + how the challenge works; join the group.
- **Record your baseline** 30-sec clip on the standard prompt (*"What's your name? Where are you from?
  What do you do?"*) — the *before* (before/after protocol).
- Set your goal + daily time (Mindset M0). Post your intro + goal.

**Day 1 — Your first sound wins: /p/ vs /b/ and /v/ vs /f/**
- Accent Lab cards C1 + C2 (paper-strip test; teeth-on-lip). Action: record *pen, book, van, very, five*.

**Day 2 — Stop saying "iskool": consonant clusters**
- Accent Lab card C9 (signature). Action: say *school, street, spring, desk* cleanly; post a voice note.

**Day 3 — "job مش gob" + your first frame**
- Accent Lab card C4 (/dʒ/, Egyptian-specific) + introduce *"I'm a/an ___."* Action: say your job clearly.

**Day 4 — Introduce yourself (name + country + job)** *(optional live)*
- Stage 0 U1 frames: *"Hello, I'm… I'm from… I'm a…"*. Action: record a 3-sentence self-intro.
- **Optional live session:** everyone introduces themselves in breakout pairs — the community high point.

**Day 5 — A simple conversation (greetings + "How are you?" + intro)**
- Stage 0 U1-L01 + a mini back-and-forth. Action: record/roleplay a 4-line exchange with a challenge buddy.

**Day 6 — Mindset: consistency > intensity (why you'll actually make it)**
- Mindset M1–M2: the daily habit + beating the fear of speaking. Action: commit to your practice slot;
  re-listen to your Day-0 clip and note what's already better.

**Day 7 — Your "after" clip + the next step**
- **Record the same prompt again** (the *after*). Compare to Day 0 → the mini before/after win.
- Celebrate in the community; share (with consent) — social proof.
- **The invitation:** here's the full journey (zero → confident B2) and the **Founding Cohort** — with a
  challenge-graduate discount + priority. (Conversion pitch — §6.)

## 4. What the learner gets

- 7 short daily lessons (recorded) + daily actions.
- The challenge community + accountability + (optional) live session.
- Their **Day-0 vs Day-7 clip** (a real, personal win).
- A taste of the method (input + speaking + Accent Lab + mindset) and the founder's teaching.
- A clear next step + a graduate-only offer for the cohort.

---

## 5. Pricing (tiered, low-friction)

- **Purpose:** low enough to be impulse + filter (skin in the game), not a profit center.
- **Egypt tier (EGP):** a small, accessible price (impulse level).
- **Gulf/diaspora tier (USD):** a modestly higher equivalent.
- **Options to test:** paid entry with a **"applies-to-cohort" credit** (challenge fee deducted from cohort
  tuition if they enroll) — a strong conversion lever; or a symbolic price + refund-on-completion.
- Payments individual-first: InstaPay/wallet/bank (EGP) + personal PayPal (USD, fee-on-top) — tool stack §2.

*(Exact numbers validated in the founding round; anchor on value + the credit-toward-cohort mechanic.)*

---

## 6. Conversion to the flagship cohort (the point)

The challenge exists to fill cohorts. Mechanics:
1. **Emotional peak → offer:** pitch the cohort on Day 7, right after the before/after win.
2. **Graduate-only incentive:** challenge fee credited toward tuition + priority + a small bonus + deadline.
3. **Continuity of community:** they already have momentum, buddies, and trust — the cohort is "keep going."
4. **Follow-up sequence:** a short post-challenge WhatsApp/email sequence (3–4 messages) for non-buyers:
   recap their win → address objections → last-chance for the cohort intake → invite to next wave/community.
- **Target:** define a baseline **challenge → cohort conversion rate** in the founding round; improve it.

## 7. Success metrics (feeds R13)

- **Challenge completion rate** (target high — it's short; aim 70%+).
- **# who record both Day-0 and Day-7 clips** (proof assets).
- **Challenge → cohort conversion rate** (the key business metric).
- **Testimonials/before-after pairs generated** (with consent).
- **CAC via challenge** vs direct-to-cohort (which path is cheaper?).
