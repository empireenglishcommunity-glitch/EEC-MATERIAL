# Waitlist & Lead-Capture Funnel (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 2.3 · **Requirements:** R6 (content), R10 (GTM)
> **Reads with:** `content/01-content-strategy-and-calendar.md`, `brand/01-brand-foundations.md`,
> `operations/01-tool-stack.md`.
> **Purpose:** Turn free-content attention into an **owned audience** (the waitlist), then nurture it into
> the entry challenge and flagship cohort. Includes the waitlist landing-page copy, capture mechanics,
> segmentation, and the nurture sequence. Algorithms rent attention; a waitlist is owned — this is how we
> stop renting.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Own the audience.** Move viewers off rented platforms into a list we control (form + WhatsApp/Telegram, email later).
2. **Low friction.** Ask for the minimum; every extra field costs signups.
3. **Give to get.** Offer a real quick win for joining (lead magnet), not just "join the waitlist."
4. **Honest + on-brand.** Same voice as everywhere (brand §7): system, not hacks; no false promises (GC-5).
5. **Segment early.** Capture tier (Egypt vs Gulf/diaspora) + goal + level to personalize and price right.

---

## 2. The funnel map

```
  FREE CONTENT (TikTok/Reels/FB/YouTube)  →  "link in bio" / "comment a word"
        │
        ▼
  WAITLIST LANDING PAGE  →  capture (name, WhatsApp/email, tier, goal, level)
        │  instant: deliver lead magnet + welcome
        ▼
  NURTURE (WhatsApp/Telegram broadcast + email)  →  tips + proof + story
        │  when a cohort opens:
        ▼
  ENTRY CHALLENGE (7–14 days, low price)  →  first quick win + filter
        │
        ▼
  FLAGSHIP COHORT (core)  →  COMMUNITY (retention) → proof → back into content
```

---

## 3. Lead-capture mechanics

**Entry points (all roads → the landing page or a direct opt-in):**
- **"Link in bio"** on every profile → landing page.
- **Content CTAs** (content strategy §5) → landing page / challenge.
- **"Comment a word"** replies → DM → invite to the waitlist.
- **WhatsApp/Telegram click-to-chat** link (very high intent in Egypt) → auto-welcome + capture.

**What we collect (minimal, R3-privacy-aware):**
- Name · WhatsApp number **or** email (at least one) · **Tier** (Egypt / Gulf-diaspora — from country) ·
  **Goal** (job / travel / study / migration / confidence) · **Self-rated level** (beginner / some / intermediate).
- Optional: how they found us (attribution).

**Tools (tool stack):** a simple form (Google Form / platform form / a landing-page tool) + WhatsApp/Telegram
for delivery + a spreadsheet or the community platform to hold the list. Automate later (Zapier/Make).

---

## 4. The lead magnet (give to get)

Offer **one** clear quick win in exchange for joining (rotate/test):
- **"دليل الـ 5 أصوات اللي المصريين بيغلطوا فيها"** — a short PDF/video (from the Accent Lab series) + a
  1-page practice sheet. High relevance, instant value, proves the method.
- Alternatives to test: a free "speak your first 5 sentences" mini-lesson; early/discounted access to the
  next entry challenge; a placement mini-quiz + "what level am I?" result.

Deliver **instantly** on signup (link + welcome message).

## 6. Nurture sequence (waitlist → challenge)

Delivered via WhatsApp/Telegram broadcast (+ email when set up). Warm, valuable, ~2–3/week; each ends with
a soft next step. Goal: trust + readiness so the challenge/cohort converts.

| # | Timing | Message (theme) | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instant | Welcome + deliver the lead magnet + "here's how EEC is different (system, not tricks)" | Watch the guide |
| 2 | Day 1 | Founder story (short) — why EEC exists; relatable + credible | Reply with your goal |
| 3 | Day 3 | Quick win #1 (an Accent Lab fix, e.g., "school مش iskool") | Try it + reply |
| 4 | Day 5 | Mindset: consistency > intensity; the real reason people fail | — |
| 5 | Day 8 | Proof: a before/after or testimonial (consented) | — |
| 6 | Day 10 | Quick win #2 (e.g., "job مش gob") + tease the roadmap (zero → B2) | — |
| 7 | Day 12 | Address the #1 objection ("I've tried before / no time") honestly | Reply with worries |
| 8 | Launch | **Entry challenge opens** — early access + waitlist discount, limited spots | Join the challenge |
| — | Ongoing | If not converted: keep delivering weekly value; re-invite next cohort | — |

*(For those who join the challenge, a separate short sequence drives challenge → flagship cohort.)*

## 7. Segmentation & personalization

- **Tier (Egypt vs Gulf/diaspora):** determines pricing shown and currency (EGP vs USD) — strategy §12.
- **Goal:** tailor messaging (job seekers ≠ migrants ≠ confidence-seekers).
- **Level:** beginners → reassure "from zero"; intermediates → "break your plateau."
- Tag each lead in the list; use tags to send relevant offers.

## 8. Metrics (feeds R13 funnel KPIs)

- **Signup rate:** landing-page visitors → waitlist signups (test headline/magnet to improve).
- **Source/attribution:** which content/platform drives the best leads.
- **Nurture engagement:** open/reply rates on broadcasts.
- **Waitlist → challenge** and **challenge → cohort** conversion rates.
- **Cost per lead / CAC** — target falling over time (proof flywheel).
