# Founding-Cohort Success Criteria (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 3.4 · **Requirements:** R8B (Founding Cohort), R13 (KPIs)
> **Reads with:** `program/01-founding-cohort-offer.md`, `operations/02-cohort-tracker.md`,
> `curriculum/05-assessment-suite.md`, `curriculum/06-before-after-protocol.md`, `strategy/01-foundational-strategy.md`.
> **Purpose:** Define — *in advance* — what makes the founding cohort a success, how each target is
> measured, and the go/scale decision it drives (including the "go full-time" call). Setting criteria
> before the cohort keeps the evaluation honest and evidence-based, not vibes-based.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Decide the bar before you run.** Pre-committed criteria prevent post-hoc rationalization.
2. **Education first, then business.** Results are the product; if learners transform, the business follows.
3. **Proof is a deliverable.** Testimonials + before/after clips are as important an output as revenue.
4. **Measure what the tracker already captures** (R13) — no new machinery.
5. **The founding cohort is a learning experiment**, not just a sale — capture what to improve for cohort #2.

---

## 2. Success criteria (with thresholds)

Each metric has **Minimum viable** (acceptable — proceed with fixes), **Target** (a clear win), and
**Stretch** (excellent). Sources = the Cohort Tracker (Task 0.5) + assessment suite.

### A. Educational (the ones that matter most)
| Metric | Minimum | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Completion rate** | ≥ 60% | **≥ 70%** | ≥ 85% |
| **Avg CEFR level gain** (completers) | ≥ 0.8 | **≥ 1.0 (reach A1)** | > 1 (into A2) |
| **% of completers hitting the A1 conversation benchmark** | ≥ 55% | **≥ 70%** | ≥ 85% |
| **Documented before/after transformations (consented)** | ≥ 6 | **≥ 10** | ≥ 15 |
| **Measured speaking-score gain** (Day-1 → graduation, rubric) | clear positive | **strong across dimensions** | large + accent gains |

### B. Business
| Metric | Minimum | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enrollment** | ~12 | **15–25** | ~30 (full) |
| **Contribution margin** (rev − ad+team+tools, EGP base) | break-even+ | **clearly profitable at small scale** | strong margin |
| **Willingness to pay** | proven (people paid) | **paid at/near founding price** | minimal discounting needed |
| **Challenge → cohort conversion** | baseline set | **healthy baseline (define)** | above expectation |
| **CAC** | baseline set | **within a sustainable range** | low + falling |

### C. Proof & brand
| Metric | Minimum | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Testimonials collected (consented)** | ≥ 6 | **≥ 10** | ≥ 15 |
| **Learner satisfaction** (simple survey/NPS-style) | mostly positive | **strongly positive** | enthusiastic + referrals |
| **Referrals / "bring a friend"** | a few | **several** | many (organic demand) |

### D. Operational
| Metric | Minimum | Target | Stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly cadence held** | mostly | **consistently** | flawless |
| **Live-session attendance** | ~50% | **~65%+** | ~80%+ |
| **Founder time** | ≤ ~30 hrs/wk (sustainable) | **within 24–30 hrs, repeatable** | efficient / delegable |
| **SOPs workable** | usable with fixes | **repeatable for cohort #2** | smooth + delegable |

## 3. Measurement plan (when & how)

- **Baseline (onboarding):** placement level + Day-1 speaking sample + goal logged (per learner).
- **Weekly:** activity flags (`weekN_active`), attendance, task completion → early at-risk detection.
- **Mid-cohort:** completion trend + progress-vs-Day-1 check (also re-motivates — Mindset M7).
- **Close:** summative + graduation samples scored on the rubric; compute all KPIs in the tracker; run
  the satisfaction survey; collect testimonials/consented clips.
- **Owner:** community manager maintains the tracker; founder reviews at mid-cohort and close.

---

## 4. The go/scale decision framework

Evaluate the whole picture at close-out (not any single metric in isolation):

- 🟢 **GREEN — Scale.** Educational **Target** met (completion ≥70%, ~1 level gain, benchmark hit) **and**
  business at least break-even+ **and** ≥10 proof assets.
  → **Action:** run cohort #2 **bigger + at higher/standard price**; launch the **community membership**;
  begin **building Stage 1 (A2)**; make the **go-full-time** call if economics support it; consider first
  paid ads at scale (proof lowers CAC).

- 🟡 **YELLOW — Iterate, then rerun.** Mixed: e.g., strong education but weak conversion, or good sales but
  completion < target.
  → **Action:** fix the *specific* weak lever (offer, funnel, pacing, onboarding, or accountability) and
  run cohort #2 at similar scale/price. **Do not** scale spend yet. Hold the full-time decision.

- 🔴 **RED — Rethink.** Below minimums on education *and* business (low completion + weak willingness to pay).
  → **Action:** diagnose root cause — segment fit? price? promise? method delivery? Revisit strategy
  assumptions before spending more. Possibly re-pilot with a tighter segment/offer.

**Guiding rule:** *education gates the decision.* Great sales with poor learning outcomes is a 🟡/🔴, not a
🟢 — because EEC's entire thesis is that results are the moat.

---

## 5. Post-cohort actions (regardless of color)

- [ ] Compute + record all KPIs in the tracker; write a one-page **cohort report**.
- [ ] Collect + edit **consented before/after pairs + testimonials** → proof library (flywheel).
- [ ] Run a **retro**: what worked, what didn't, top 3 improvements for cohort #2 (curriculum, ops, funnel).
- [ ] **Transition** graduates → Stage 1 (A2) and/or community membership.
- [ ] Update pricing/offer/funnel based on evidence (challenge conversion, CAC, willingness to pay).

---

## 6. The "go full-time" call (founder decision)

Green-light going full-time when the founding cohort shows **all** of:
- Educational **Target** results (proof the system works), **and**
- **Profitable** unit economics with the EGP cost base, **and**
- **Repeatable** operations within ~24–30 hrs/week (so scaling won't break you), **and**
- **Demand signals** (waitlist growth, referrals, challenge conversion) suggesting cohort #2 fills.

If some-but-not-all: stay part-time, run cohort #2 to confirm, then decide. (Strategy: results + proof
first; full-time is a *consequence* of a working system, not a leap of faith.)
