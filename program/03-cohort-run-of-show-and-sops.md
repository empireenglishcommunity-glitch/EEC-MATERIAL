# Cohort Run-of-Show & SOPs (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 3.3 · **Requirements:** R7 (flagship cohort), R5 (accountability), R11 (operations)
> **Reads with:** `methodology/01-eec-methodology-handbook.md` (§13 live sessions, §11 feedback),
> `curriculum/stage0/`, `curriculum/04-mindset-and-accountability-track.md`, `curriculum/05-assessment-suite.md`,
> `curriculum/06-before-after-protocol.md`, `operations/01-tool-stack.md`, `operations/02-cohort-tracker.md`.
> **Purpose:** The week-to-week operating manual for delivering a cohort — the standard weekly rhythm, the
> live-session run-of-show, and the SOPs for feedback, accountability, community, assessment, and cohort
> close-out. This is what makes delivery repeatable (and eventually delegable) rather than improvised.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Rhythm beats intensity.** A predictable weekly cadence carries learners further than sporadic effort.
2. **Live time = interaction, not lecture.** Explanations live in recordings; live is for speaking + feedback (Handbook §13).
3. **Feedback protects confidence.** Delayed, group-level, rubric-based; never a wall of corrections (Handbook §11).
4. **No one disappears quietly.** The accountability/nudge SOP catches drift early (the completion engine).
5. **Everything is logged.** The Cohort Tracker is the single source of truth for progress + KPIs.

---

## 2. The standard week (template)

One **unit per week** (roadmap pacing). A typical week:

| Day | Activity | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Release the week's unit** (recorded lessons) + week overview post + mindset micro-lesson (Mx) | Community mgr | Learners begin self-paced |
| Mon–Wed | Self-paced lessons + Accent Lab drills + daily practice posts | Learners | Daily check-ins |
| Wed | **Mid-week nudge** + "post your voice note" prompt | Community mgr | Catch early drift |
| Thu | **Weekly LIVE session** (speaking + feedback + accent + Q&A) | Founder | The core event (§3) |
| Fri | Delayed feedback recap + peer-pod task | Founder/pods | From the live session |
| Sat | **Unit speaking task** (L05) recorded + community share | Learners | Proof + practice |
| Sun | **Weekly review** (spaced retrieval) + goal check-in + celebrate wins | Community mgr | Prep next unit |

*(Adjust days to the cohort's schedule; keep the *shape*: release → self-paced → live → task → review.)*

---

## 3. The weekly LIVE session — run-of-show (~60 min)

Reusable script every week (Handbook §13). Maximize learner talk-time; the coach facilitates, doesn't lecture.

| # | Segment | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warm welcome + recall warm-up** | 5 min | Everyone speaks in the first 2 min (retrieval of prior language) |
| 2 | **Micro-recap** of the week's target | 5 min | Brief; the teaching was in the recordings |
| 3 | **Interaction core** (pods/pairs) | 25 min | Role-plays, opinion exchanges, the unit's speaking task; coach circulates breakout rooms |
| 4 | **Spotlight + delayed group feedback** | 10 min | A few learners share; coach gives *group-level* correction of common errors (no shaming) |
| 5 | **Accent moment** | 8 min | One focused Accent Lab drill (the week's target sound) |
| 6 | **Close: wins + commitment + preview** | 7 min | Celebrate; each sets next week's commitment; preview next unit |

**Facilitation rules:** learner talk-time > coach talk-time; use breakout pods for shy learners; correct
common errors at the group level; protect psychological safety; differentiate prompts for mixed levels.

## 4. SOP — Feedback loop

- **During fluency tasks:** do **not** interrupt; note errors.
- **After tasks (delayed):** address the **top 2–3 common errors** at the group level + the lesson target.
- **Individual feedback:** 2 strengths + 1 focus (never a long list); gentle techniques first
  (elicitation → recast → clarification → brief explicit) — Handbook §11.
- **Pronunciation:** compare to the model; name the specific fix; flag weak sounds in the tracker → they
  resurface via the review engine + Accent Clinic.
- **Self-record:** learners keep recording milestone samples (proof + self-assessment).

## 5. SOP — Accountability & nudges (the completion engine)

- **Daily:** community manager scans check-ins/activity; likes/replies to practice posts (belonging).
- **Inactivity trigger:** WHEN a learner has no activity for **3–4 days** → **automated warm nudge**
  (WhatsApp/Telegram) → if still silent in ~2 days → **personal human check-in** from coach/community mgr.
- **Tone:** care, not guilt. Reference their goal (M0). Offer one small, easy next step ("just do the
  5-min Accent Lab today").
- **Restart ritual:** frictionless "welcome back," no catch-up shame ("never miss twice").
- **Peer pods:** pods check on their own members; pod leaders (optional) prompt each other.
- Log activity in the Cohort Tracker (`weekN_active`, `last_active_date`).

## 6. SOP — Community management

- **Daily prompt** (a question, a "post your voice note," a win to celebrate).
- **Reply to everything** early on (founder + community mgr) — response speed = retention.
- **Moderate** for safety + positivity (celebrate attempts; no mockery of mistakes).
- **Weekly rituals:** "win of the week," "brave mistake" thread, mindset micro-lesson discussion.
- **Celebrate milestones** (unit completions, before/after clips with consent) — fuels motivation + proof.

## 7. SOP — Assessment during the cohort

- **Per unit:** run the end-of-unit formative (quiz + L05 speaking task); light rubric scoring; log notes
  + flag weak sounds (Assessment Suite §6; tracker).
- **Mid-cohort:** a progress check + share each learner's growth vs Day-1 (motivation — Mindset M7).
- **End of stage:** the **summative** (quiz + full speaking task) + the **graduation "after" sample** on
  the standard prompt → CEFR determination, certificate, tracker update (Assessment Suite §7).

## 8. Role task lists

**Founder (coach) — weekly:**
- [ ] Prep + run the weekly live session (run-of-show §3).
- [ ] Give group-level + spotlight feedback; review flagged samples.
- [ ] Post/record any needed clarifications; appear in the community a few times.
- [ ] Review the tracker at mid-week; personally reach any at-risk learner.

**Community manager / VA (EGP) — daily/weekly:**
- [ ] Release the weekly unit + overview + mindset micro-lesson.
- [ ] Daily community prompts + replies; scan activity; fire nudges per the SOP.
- [ ] Chase incomplete tasks/assessments; run the restart ritual.
- [ ] Keep the Cohort Tracker updated (activity, scores, sample links, consent).

## 9. SOP — Mid-cohort review & close-out

**Mid-cohort review:** check completion trend + at-risk list (from `weekN_active`); intervene; surface
progress evidence to re-motivate; gather quick feedback.

**Close-out (end of the stage/cohort):**
- [ ] Run summative + capture **graduation samples**; score on the rubric.
- [ ] Compute cohort **KPIs** (completion %, avg CEFR gain, % hitting benchmark, # transformations) — R13/tracker.
- [ ] Collect **testimonials + consented before/after pairs** (before/after protocol §6) → proof flywheel.
- [ ] **Transition:** invite graduates to **Stage 1 (A2)** and/or the **community membership**.
- [ ] **Retro:** capture lessons learned → improve cohort #2 (curriculum, pacing, ops).

## 10. Edge cases & escalation

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Struggling / falling behind learner | Personal check-in; adjust pace; extra pod support; reassure (mindset) |
| Persistent inactivity | Nudge → human check-in → restart ritual; if truly disengaged, offer next-cohort deferral |
| Tech issues | Community mgr resolves (login/Zoom); keep a simple troubleshooting FAQ |
| Community negativity/conflict | Moderate quickly; reinforce the "celebrate attempts" culture; private resolution |
| Guarantee/refund claim | Check the "Do the Work" criteria (attendance + assignments + assessments); apply stated terms fairly |

## 11. Tools reference (tool stack §)
Course/community platform (Skool/Circle) · Zoom (breakout pods) · WhatsApp/Telegram (nudges/community) ·

end-of-cohort: completion %, avg CEFR gain, % at benchmark, # transformations, membership conversion.
