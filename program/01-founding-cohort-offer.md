> the offer the waitlist and entry-challenge graduates are invited into.

---

## 1. Principles for the founding cohort

1. **Small, high-touch, over-deliver.** A tight first group we can genuinely transform → testimonials.
2. **Honest promise, real proof.** Sell a provable outcome (A1 conversation + before/after clip), not "fluent fast" (GC-5).
3. **Founding = a deal + a duty.** Discounted price in exchange for feedback + (consented) testimonials.
4. **Completion is the product.** Cohort + community + accountability engineered for ≥70% completion.
5. **Lean & tiered.** EGP cost base; Egypt + Gulf/diaspora pricing tiers (strategy §12).

## 3. Scope — what's included

- **Full Stage-0 (Foundations) curriculum** — 11 units, ~55 lessons (recorded, Egyptian-Arabic scaffolded).
- **Weekly live speaking sessions** (Zoom; breakout pods) — the coaching + practice core.
- **The Accent Lab** — targeted American-pronunciation training for Arabic speakers, threaded throughout.
- **The Mindset & Accountability track** — M0–M10, plus check-ins, nudges, and the re-engagement safety net.
- **Assessment + progress** — placement, Day-1 sample, per-unit checks, and the A1 summative + graduation clip.
- **Private community** — peer pods, daily practice, belonging (WhatsApp/Telegram + platform).
- **Certificate of completion** (brand-compliant) + a documented CEFR gain.

**Clear next step:** on completion, learners are invited to **Stage 1 (Elementary / A2)** — built just
ahead — and/or the ongoing **community membership** (retention).

- 📈 **Before/after proof** — see and hear your own transformation.

## 5. The promise & the honest guarantee

**Promise (what we sell):**
> في ~١٢ أسبوع، لو مشيت على النظام، هتقدر تعمل **محادثة إنجليزي بسيطة بثقة** (تعرّف بنفسك، عيلتك، يومك،
> تطلب حاجة، تدّي اتجاهات) بنطق أوضح — وهيبقى معاك فيديو **قبل/بعد** يثبت الفرق.

**Honest, effort-based guarantee (risk reversal that stays credible):**
> **The "Do the Work" Guarantee:** attend the live sessions, complete the lessons and assignments, and
> take the assessments — if you do all that and **don't reach the A1 conversation benchmark**, you get
> **free enrollment in the next cohort** (or a refund, per the stated terms) to get you there.

Why effort-based: it signals real confidence, protects against non-doers, and is **honest** — we can't
promise results to someone who doesn't show up. (Finalize refund vs free-continuation + exact conditions
before launch; keep terms simple and written.)

## 6. Pricing (tiered, founding discount)

**Structure (validate exact numbers in this round):**
- **Founding discount:** a significant one-time discount off the future standard price (e.g., ~40–50% off),
  framed clearly ("founding price, this cohort only").
- **Two tiers** (strategy §12):
  - **Egypt tier (EGP):** accessible; **payment plan** option (2–3 scheduled InstaPay/bank transfers).
  - **Gulf/diaspora tier (USD):** ~3–5× the Egypt tier for the same program (personal PayPal, fee-on-top).
- **Challenge credit:** entry-challenge fee **credited** toward tuition (Task 2.4) — rewards the on-ramp.
- **Anchor on value, not "cheap":** compare to the cost/outcome of years of failed self-study, random
  tutors, or apps that don't make you speak — EEC sells a *result*, not lessons.

**Illustrative unit economics (placeholders — replace with real numbers):**
> If ~20 learners enrol at a blended tuition, with the **cost base in EGP** (Egyptian ads + editor +
> community manager + platform), a founding cohort should be **profitable at small scale** — and each
> cohort's testimonials lower the ad cost of the next (the flywheel). Track actuals in the Cohort Tracker
> (revenue, ad spend, team, tools → margin + CAC).

## 7. The founding-member deal (the exchange)

Founding members get a better price + more attention; in return they agree to:
- **Show up + do the work** (the guarantee assumes this).
- Give **feedback** (helps us improve the program for cohort #2).
- Provide a **testimonial** and allow use of their **before/after clip** *if they consent* (Tier 2,
  before/after protocol §4) — never required, always opt-in.

**Bonuses (stack, optional — test):** a free month of the community membership after graduation; a
"bring a friend" perk; priority + discount into Stage 1 (A2).

## 8. Positioning — why this vs the alternatives

| Alternative | Why EEC Foundations wins |
|---|---|
| Free content (ZAmerican etc.) | Free ≠ finished. We give structure, accountability, live practice, and **proof of results** |
| Apps (Duolingo/Babbel) | They don't make you **speak**; we're speaking-first, Arabic-specific, human-coached |
| Random online tutors | One coherent, proven method + community — not a teacher lottery |
| Doing nothing / "someday" | A fixed start date, a small group, and a guarantee — the push to actually begin |

---

## 9. Enrollment terms (for the offer)

- **Limited spots** (15–30) — real scarcity (small = high-touch), not fake urgency.
- **Fixed start date + enrollment deadline** (cohort momentum).
- **Founding price ends** when the cohort fills or the deadline hits.
- **Placement** happens at onboarding (Task 3.2) — everyone records a Day-1 sample.
- Clear, written **refund/guarantee terms** + payment-plan terms.

## 11. Success criteria (preview of Task 3.4)

A successful founding cohort:
- **Enrols ~15–30** learners.
- **≥70% completion.**
- **Average ≥1 CEFR level gain**; most reach the **A1 conversation benchmark**.
- **≥10 documented before/after transformations + testimonials** (consented).
- **Proves willingness to pay** (revenue + a defined challenge→cohort conversion).
