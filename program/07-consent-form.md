# EEC Media & Testimonial Consent Form (v1.0)

> **Spec task:** 3B.6 · **Requirements:** R3 · **Reads with:** `curriculum/06-before-after-protocol.md` (§4),
> `brand/01-brand-foundations.md` (§10).
> **Purpose:** The layered, opt-in consent learners sign (digitally) at onboarding, governing how their
> recordings/quotes may be used. Bilingual (Arabic primary + English). Tiered so a learner can allow
> measurement while declining marketing. **Nothing is used beyond the tier granted.**

---

## Plain-language explainer (say this first)

**AR:** *"إحنا بنسجّل صوتك/فيديوهاتك علشان نقيس تقدمك (قبل/بعد) ونديك feedback. مش هنستخدم أي حاجة بره ده
إلا لو إنت وافقت. إنت بتختار المستوى اللي يريّحك، وتقدر تسحب موافقتك في أي وقت."*

**EN:** *"We record your audio/video to measure your progress (before/after) and give you feedback. We won't
use anything beyond that unless you agree. You choose the level you're comfortable with, and you can
withdraw at any time."*

---

## Choose ONE tier (opt-in)

**◻ Tier 0 — Internal only** *(الافتراضي / default — always allowed)*
- **AR:** تسجيلاتي تُستخدم فقط لتقييمي وتقدّمي والـ feedback ليّا. مش هتتنشر ولا تتشارك بره الإدارة.
- **EN:** My recordings are used only for my own assessment, progress, and feedback. Not shared or published.

**◻ Tier 1 — Community**
- **AR:** ممكن تتشارك تسجيلاتي **جوّه مجتمع EEC الخاص** (مثلاً احتفال بتقدّمي)، من غير نشر عام.
- **EN:** My recordings may be shared **inside the private EEC community** (e.g., celebrating progress), not publicly.

**◻ Tier 2 — Marketing (public)**
- **AR:** ممكن تُستخدم تسجيلاتي/اقتباساتي **بشكل عام** (سوشيال ميديا، صفحات البيع، إعلانات) للترويج لـ EEC.
- **EN:** My recordings/quotes may be used **publicly** (social media, sales pages, ads) to promote EEC.
  - Name may be shown: **◻ Yes ◻ No** · Face/video may be shown: **◻ Yes ◻ No** (audio-only if No)
  - Channels allowed: **◻ All ◻ Only: ____________**

---

## Your rights (apply to every tier)

- **AR:** تقدر **تسحب موافقتك في أي وقت** بإبلاغنا؛ ساعتها نوقف أي استخدام مستقبلي ونشيل المحتوى من الأصول
  النشطة قدر الإمكان. الانسحاب مش بيأثر على مكانك في البرنامج.
- **EN:** You may **withdraw consent at any time** by telling us; we then stop future use and remove the
  content from active assets where feasible. Withdrawing does **not** affect your place in the program.
- We use only what you granted; we never imply a result is typical if it isn't (honest framing).

## Minors
The beachhead is adults (18+). If a learner is under 18 (future kids/teens phase), a **parent/guardian
must consent**, with stricter handling — out of scope for v1.

## Operational notes
- Capture at onboarding (`program/02` form has the tier selector); store the signed record in the
  learner's folder; set `consent_to_use` (+ tier) in the Cohort Tracker.
- Before any public use, re-check the learner's tier + the final-edit approval step (before/after protocol §6).
