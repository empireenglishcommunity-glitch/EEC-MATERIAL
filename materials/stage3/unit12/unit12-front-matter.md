## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** hold a natural **20–30 minute conversation** in English — everyday, professional and abstract —
> clearly and confidently, meeting a **defined minimum in all five rubric dimensions**. Then record the graduation
> sample that closes the proof timeline.

**This is the flagship.** Every lesson in this programme was engineered backwards from the benchmark in L05. Nothing new
gets taught in this unit; everything gets made **available under pressure**.

**Two things get settled here.** That knowing a structure and reaching for it while you are thinking about something else
are **different skills** — the first four lessons close that gap. And that the goal was always **clear, confident, neutral
American**, never "native" — an honest standard you can actually meet, and meet today.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- A recorded **20–30 minute B2 conversation**, scored on five dimensions — the promise of this programme, evidenced.
- A **self-monitoring routine** that works with no coach in the room: choose one · cue it · repair, never restart · log one.
- The **five-point timeline** — Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 → B1 → **B2** — same prompt, same person, four months apart.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (L01–L03 ~40 min, L04 ~45, L05 ~50) plus this wrapper. **L04 and L05 each contain a
> 25-minute unbroken recorded conversation — protect that block above everything else in the plan.**
> **L01 and L02 are retrieval, not re-teaching, and the temptation to re-explain is strong.** Re-explaining the mixed
> conditional for eight minutes feels productive and changes almost nothing; struggling to produce it under mild time
> pressure is what makes it available in speech. Read the residual-error tables as a **checklist** and make learners
> self-identify. The output that matters from both lessons is **three specific written residuals each** — *I say "should
> have checked" when I mean "should have been checked"*, not *my passive is weak*. Those six are the entire input to L03,
> and the clinic is only as personalised as the lists are specific. L02 additionally carries the error learners are least
> prepared for: **one register for the whole conversation**, plus its mirror image, **over-formality as a safety
> behaviour** — which is the version that catches strong students.
> **L03 deliberately has no fixed content, and that is not a planning failure.** Build it from the six targets and the
> tracker flags in the room. Two non-negotiables: state the **honesty guardrail** out loud — clear, confident, neutral
> American, with native-likeness an aspirational ceiling never promised and never a pass criterion — and compare each
> learner **only to their own earlier recordings**, never to each other and never to a native model as a standard. Drive
> every feature to **rung 4** (ninety seconds of unscripted speech); rungs 1–3 are where a clinic feels productive and
> rung 4 is where it becomes true. The deliverable is the **routine**, not any single sound, and the metric worth tracking
> is the **caught-versus-heard gap**, because monitoring accuracy is what predicts improvement after the course ends.
> **L04 exists so that on benchmark day nothing but the English is unfamiliar.** Do not shorten it — the length **is** the
> rehearsal. Name the three-phase arc explicitly (most learners did not know it existed), read the **actual rubric card**
> rather than summarising it, and state the **defined minimum**: their weakest dimension is their result. Make **no
> determination** and say so, because some learners arrive believing this is the assessment. Push back genuinely at least
> twice, once in phase 2. Watch for the two silent failures — the learner who never asks a question (Interaction cannot be
> inferred) and the learner whose register never moves — and name both today, when they are still cheap to fix.
> **L05 is the flagship and it is run to the governing documents, not to this file.** Rubric bands, defined minimum and
> determination procedure come from `curriculum/05-assessment-suite.md`; the standard prompt, capture conditions, storage
> and consent come from `curriculum/06-before-after-protocol.md`. **The benchmark and the graduation sample are two
> separate recordings in two separate files** — the conversation determines the result, the standard prompt exists so the
> five-point comparison is fair, and nothing in it is scored. **Confirm the consent tier before any capture**, treat it as
> granular and revocable, and honour a later withdrawal without argument. Do not round up for motivation or down for
> strictness; if a dimension is below the minimum, name which one, why, and the date it is being redone. Give the two wins
> as **quotes** — a graduate remembers the sentence you read back to them far longer than the band.
