## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> Go **along** this street, go **past** the bank, and turn **into** King Street. The station is **across** the road.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
| Preposition | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| along | على طول | *go along the street* |
| past | تعدّي | *go past the bank* |
| into | تدخل | *turn into King Street* |
| across | تعدّي عرض | *across the road* |
| up / down | فوق / تحت | *go up the street* |

- Combine with imperatives: *Go along…, go past…, turn into…*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Movement prepositions + combine with imperatives.
