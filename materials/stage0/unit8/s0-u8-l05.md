## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> — Excuse me, is there a pharmacy near here? — Yes. Go straight, then turn left. Go past the bank, and it's on the right. — Turn left, then past the bank? — Yes! — Thank you!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean directions exchange (with a clarification).

---

## 🧩 The Stages — مراحل المحادثة
1. ✅ **Ask** — *Excuse me, is there a ___ near here?*
2. ✅ **Give the route** — *Go straight, turn left, go past…*
3. ✅ **Confirm** — *So, turn left then past the bank?*
4. ✅ **Thank** — *Thank you!*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the exchange into stages; give the card.
