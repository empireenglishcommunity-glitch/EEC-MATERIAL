## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Ask *Is there a pharmacy near here?*
- Answer *Yes, there is / No, there isn't* + say roughly where.
- Start politely with *Excuse me*.

> هتسأل عن مكان قريب، وترد، وتقول هو فين تقريبًا.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> — **Excuse me**, is there a **bank** near here? — Yes, there is. It's **next to** the pharmacy. — Thank you!

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **Excuse me, is there a + place + near here?**
- **Short answers:** *Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.*
- **Location:** *It's next to / near the ___.* (prepositions من U3)

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Is-there question + short answers + *Excuse me* + location answers.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Excuse me | لو سمحت | *Excuse me, is there…?* |
| Is there a…near here? | فيه … قريب؟ | *Is there a pharmacy near here?* |
| It's near / next to… | جنب / قريب من… | *It's next to the bank.* |
