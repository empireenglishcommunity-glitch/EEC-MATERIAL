## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن
اسأل *"Is there a ___ near here?"* (L02).

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> **Go straight**, then **turn left**. **Take the first** street on the right. It's **on the right**, next to the bank.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (imperatives)
- **Imperative** = فعل من غير فاعل (أمر/توجيه): *Go. Turn. Take. Stop.*
- **left / right / straight** · **take the first/second** street.
- **It's on the left / right.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Imperatives (base verb, no subject) + direction words.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| go straight | امشي دغري | *Go straight.* |
| turn left / right | لف شمال / يمين | *Turn left.* |
| take the first/second | خد أول/تاني | *Take the first street.* |
| on the left / right | على الشمال / اليمين | *It's on the right.* |

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: /l/ (left) vs /r/ (right).**
- **left**: اللسان يلمس ورا السنان (L).
- **right**: اللسان مقوّس، من غير تكرار (R).
+ نبرة التوجيه **نازلة** وحازمة ↘.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *Turn left. Turn right.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** /l/–/r/ clarity (real stakes) + firm falling instruction tone.

---

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — Left or right? (اسمع/اقرا):** لف شمال → turn ___ · لف يمين → turn ___

**Exercise 2 — Give a 2-step route (على الخريطة):** *Go ___, then turn ___.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** left · right.
