## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Name **places in town** (bank, supermarket, hospital, school, restaurant, station, pharmacy).
- Say what's near you: *There is a bank near my house.*
- Say where you go: *I go to the supermarket.*

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> There is a **bank** near my house. There is a **supermarket** and a **pharmacy** on my street. I go to the **gym** on Friday.

**Places (gloss):** bank بنك · supermarket سوبرماركت · hospital مستشفى · school مدرسة · restaurant مطعم · station محطة · pharmacy صيدلية

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **There is a + place** (near…): *There is a bank near my house.*
- **go to the + place:** *I go to the supermarket.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Places vocab + recycle *there is a* / *go to the*.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |

| supermarket / restaurant / station | سوبرماركت / مطعم / محطة | *I go to the supermarket.* |
