## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** name **places in town**, ask if something's **near here**, and **give/follow directions** —
> then run a full directions exchange from memory.
> مهمتك: تسمّي أماكن المدينة، تسأل عن مكان قريب، وتدّي وتفهم اتجاهات.

**Why now:** navigation is a real-world skill — and it recycles a lot of earlier grammar in a fresh context.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Places in Town | places vocab · *There is a ___ near ___* · *go to the ___* |
| **L02** | "Is There a…Near Here?" | ask about a place · *Excuse me* · short answers + location |
| **L03** | Giving Directions ⭐ | imperatives · left/right/straight · *on the left/right* |
| **L04** | Movement Prepositions | along/past/into/across · fuller routes · clarify |
| **L05** | Give Directions (milestone) 🎙️ | a full directions exchange + recording |

**Grammar spine:** places · Is there…? (recycled there is/are) · imperatives · prepositions of movement.
**Accent Lab:** /l/–/r/ (left/right) · instruction intonation · linking.
**Mindset:** confidence in real interactions — ask strangers; mistakes cost nothing.
**Arabic support:** ~30%, easing to ~25% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **directions-exchange recording** (a practical navigation conversation).

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** heavily integrative (recycles U0 survival phrases, U3 prepositions, U4 frequency). The
> **/l/–/r/** clarity has real stakes — drill it. Imperatives (no subject) build confidence. Encourage
> clarifying with survival phrases. Log the clip (with consent) at ~9 weeks.
