## The roadmap — خريطة المرحلة

**11 units · 55 lessons · about 10–14 weeks at roughly 7 hours a week.**

| Unit | Theme | What you take from it |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Welcome & Sounds | the alphabet, the hard sounds, survival phrases, numbers 0–20 |
| **1** | Introducing Yourself | greet, and say your name, country and job |
| **2** | Family & People | talk about your family; ages and numbers to 100 |
| **3** | My Things & Places | name objects; say what there is in a room, and where |
| **4** | Daily Routine | describe your day; tell the time; say how often |
| **5** | Free Time & Ability | likes and dislikes; say what you can and cannot do |
| **6** | Food & Shopping | order politely; ask a price |
| **7** | Describing People & Things | colours, adjectives, appearance |
| **8** | Places & Directions | ask for and give simple directions |
| **9** | Happening Now | say what is happening right now |
| **10** | Putting It Together | review, the accent clinic, and your graduation |

**The grammar spine:** *to be* → present simple → *there is/are* → *can* → present continuous.
Everything stays in the present. The past tense belongs to Stage 1.

**Vocabulary target:** roughly **500–800** high-frequency words.

**Accent Lab** runs through every unit, starting with the two sounds Arabic does not have: **/p/** and
**/v/**.

> الأصوات مش رفاهية بنسيبها للآخر — بنبدأ بيها من الوحدة صفر، لأنها الفرق بين إن حد يفهمك ولا لأ.

## How to use this book — إزاي تستخدم الكتاب

Every lesson runs in the same order, so you always know where you are. Read the section label, do the
section, then move on.

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **Your Conquest** | what you will be able to do by the end |
| 👑 **Why this matters** | why it is worth your 30 minutes |
| 🔁 **Warm-up** | recall the last lesson before adding to it |
| 👂 **Watch & Listen** | hear the language before you produce it |
| 🧩 **Decode it** | the rule, explained plainly |
| 🗡️ **Your Arsenal** | the words, with their meaning and an example |
| 🎙️ **Accent Lab** | the sound, the drill, and record-and-compare |
| 🏋️ **Train** | written practice |
| 🎤 **Your Turn** | speak, and record yourself |
| 📜 **Your Orders** | what to do before the next lesson |
| 🧠 **Remember** | what to carry forward |
| ✅ **Self-check** | the answer key, so you can study alone |

**Three rules that decide whether this works:**

1. **Say everything out loud.** Reading silently builds a reader, not a speaker.
2. **Record when the book asks you to.** The recordings are your proof and your feedback.
3. **Twenty minutes every day beats three hours once a week.** Consistency beats intensity.

> ⭐ **القاعدة الذهبية:** الاستمرار أهم من الشدّة. لو فوّت يوم، ماتحاسب نفسك — كمّل تاني من بكرة.

## How you are assessed — التقييم

- **Before Unit 0:** a placement check and your **Day-1 recording** — the baseline.
- **End of every unit:** a short quiz and a speaking task. This is feedback, not a gate; nothing stops
  you from continuing.
- **Unit 10:** the **summative assessment** and your **graduation recording**, on the same question as
  Day 1. Passing means a recorded determination of **CEFR A1**.

> التقييم هنا مش عشان يمنعك — هو عشان تشوف تقدمك بعينك، وتعرف إنت فين بالظبط.

## An honest promise — وعد صادق

We promise **clear, confident, neutral American pronunciation**. We do not promise a native accent, and
we do not promise fluency in a number of days. Anyone who promises you that is selling something.

> إحنا مانوعدكش إنك تتكلم زي الأمريكاني الأصلي، ولا إنك هتبقى fluent في أسبوعين. اللي نوعد بيه: نطق
> واضح وواثق، وإنك تقدر تتكلم فعلاً — وده بياخد وقت والتزام.

**What Stage 0 actually delivers,** stated as plainly as we can: you will be able to introduce yourself
and others, ask and answer simple questions about familiar things, handle basic everyday needs, and
speak in simple connected sentences in the present — with support, and with mistakes. That is a real A1,
and it is a genuine achievement from zero.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Stage overview for the coach.** 11 units, 55 lessons, ~80–100 guided hours, ~1 unit per week.
> Arabic support runs at roughly **70%** and eases slightly by Units 9–10; the fade schedule is in
> Handbook §6. Skill weighting is speaking ●●● · listening ●●● · reading ● · writing ●.
> **Non-negotiables:** the Accent Lab and the record-yourself task never get dropped — they carry the
> method and the proof engine. Capture the **Day-1 sample before Unit 0** and the **graduation sample in
> U10-L05 on the identical prompt**; that pair is the strongest marketing asset the programme has, and it
> cannot be reconstructed later. Log both in the Cohort Tracker with consent.
> **The two errors to expect all stage:** dropped *to be* ("I teacher") and the missing article
> ("I engineer"). Correct them from Unit 1 and keep recycling.
> **Where learners quit:** Units 0–2, from embarrassment rather than difficulty. Reply personally to
> every early recording; celebrate loudly and specifically.
