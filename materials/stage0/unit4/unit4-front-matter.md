## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** describe your **daily routine**, **tell the time**, and say **how often** you do things —
> then record a "my typical day."
> مهمتك: توصف روتينك، تقول الساعة كام، وتقول بتعمل الحاجات قد إيه.

**Why now:** your routine is the easiest topic to practice — you live it every day.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | My Day (present simple I/you) | daily activities · present simple (base verb) |
| **L02** | He/She + Verb-s ⭐ | 3rd-person -s (*He works*) · -s sounds |
| **L03** | Telling the Time | o'clock/half/quarter/digital · *at + time* |
| **L04** | Frequency Adverbs | always/usually/sometimes/never + word order · days |
| **L05** | Your Typical Day (milestone) 🎙️ | a 6–8 sentence routine + recording |

**Grammar spine:** present simple (I + he/she -s) · telling time · frequency adverbs.
**Accent Lab:** 3rd-person -s (/s/,/z/,/ɪz/) · sentence rhythm/stress.
**Mindset:** track your practice · recover after a missed day (no guilt spiral) · habit-stacking.
**Arabic support:** ~50%, easing to ~45% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **"my typical day" recording** (a full paragraph of connected speech).
- The ability to describe routines — yours and others'.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** the persistent **"he work" (dropped -s)** error is the top target (L02) — kind but
> relentless. Keep I/you fluent (L01) before the -s. Word order is the frequency-adverb challenge (L04).
> Recycle prior units. Log the typical-day clip (with consent) — strong proof at ~5 weeks.
