# He/She + Verb-s ("She Works…")

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 4 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit4-daily-routine.md` · **S0-U4-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 5 · Guided 6 · Record 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** 3rd-person -s (*He works. She gets up early*) · -s sounds (/s/,/z/,/ɪz/) · *What does he/she do?*
> **"He work" (dropped -s) is one of the most persistent Arabic-transfer errors — target it relentlessly + kindly.**
> **Support:** 2 verbs, clear -s. **Stretch:** mix I + he/she. **Arabic ~50%.**

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Describe someone else's routine: *He works. She gets up early.*
- Pronounce **-s** endings (/s/, /z/, /ɪz/).
- Ask *What does he/she do?*

> هتوصف روتين حد تاني بـ **he/she + verb-s**.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (he/she + -s)
- مع **he / she / it** → زوّد **-s** على الفعل: *work → works · get → gets · watch → watches.*
- نفس أصوات جمع الـ -s من U3: **/s/ · /z/ · /ɪz/.**

> ⚠️ مش *"he work"* → الصح *"he **works**"*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** The -s rule; connect to plural -s sounds from U3.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: 3rd-person -s sounds** (same three as plurals):
- **/s/** works · **/z/** goes, plays · **/ɪz/** watches, finishes.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *works · goes · watches.*

> [!TEACHER]
