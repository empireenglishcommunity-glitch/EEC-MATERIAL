## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I **get up** at seven. I **have** breakfast, then I **go** to work. In the evening, I **come** home and **watch** TV.

**Activities (gloss):** get up أصحى · have breakfast أفطر · go to work أروح الشغل · come home أرجع البيت · sleep أنام

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (present simple I/you)
- **Present simple** = العادات، كل يوم.
- مع **I / you / we / they** → الفعل زي ما هو (base): *I get up. You work.*
- **Sequence:** first… then… after that…

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Routine verbs in order; present simple = habits. Keep I/you only (he/she -s is L02).

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: linking + content-word stress.**
اربط الأفعال: *get‿up · go‿to‿work.* واضغط على الكلمة المهمة: *I **go** to **work**.*

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *I get up, then I go to work.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Natural linking (get-up, go-to-work) + content-word stress.
