## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> — What time is it? — It's **seven o'clock**. — And now? — It's **half past eight**. I get up **at** seven.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
| الشكل | مثال |
|---|---|
| o'clock | 7:00 = seven o'clock |
| half past | 7:30 = half past seven |
| quarter past | 7:15 = quarter past seven |
| quarter to | 7:45 = quarter to eight |
| digital (الأسهل) | 7:15 = seven fifteen |

- **at + time:** *I go to work **at** eight.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Digital form first (easiest), then half/quarter. *at + time*.
