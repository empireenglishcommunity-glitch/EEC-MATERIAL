## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> I usually get up at seven. I have breakfast, then I go to work at eight. I always have lunch at one. In the evening, I come home and watch TV. I never sleep late. I sometimes read before bed.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean 7-sentence model with times + frequency.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست اليوم الكويس
1. ✅ **Morning** — get up + a time.
2. ✅ **Work/study** — go to work/study + a time.
3. ✅ **Frequency** — always/usually/sometimes/never.
4. ✅ **Evening** — come home / watch / sleep.
5. ⭐ **Extra** — a family member's day (he/she + -s).

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these boxes; give the card.
