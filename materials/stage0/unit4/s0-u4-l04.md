## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I **always** get up early. I **usually** have breakfast. I **sometimes** work on Friday. I **never** drink coffee.

**Days:** Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday · Saturday · Sunday

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
**Frequency scale:** always (100%) → usually → often → sometimes → never (0%).

**Word order (مهم):**
- قبل الفعل الأساسي: *I **always** get up early.*
- بعد **to be**: *I am **always** late.*
- ❌ *"I get up always"* → ✅ *"I always get up."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** The scale + word order (before main verb; after *to be*) + days. Word order is the target.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| always / usually | دايمًا / عادةً | *I always get up at 6.* |
| sometimes / never | أحيانًا / أبدًا | *I never drink coffee.* |
| How often…? | كل قد إيه…؟ | *How often do you exercise?* |
