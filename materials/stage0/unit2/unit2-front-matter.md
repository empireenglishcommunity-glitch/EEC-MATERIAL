## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** talk about your **family** — who they are, how many, their names and ages — and record a
> short "my family" piece.
> مهمتك: تتكلم عن عيلتك (مين، كام، الأسامي، الأعمار) وتسجّل وصف قصير.

**Why now:** family is the first real topic in any conversation — and it builds directly on your Unit-1 self-intro.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | My Family | name family members · *my/your* · *I have a big/small family* |
| **L02** | "This is my…" (his/her) | introduce a person · *his/her* · *this/that* |
| **L03** | have / has | *I have / She has* · plural -s · *Do you have…?* |
| **L04** | Ages & Numbers 20–100 | *How old are you?* · 20–100 · **thirteen ≠ thirty** |
| **L05** | Describe Your Family (milestone) 🎙️ | a 4–6 sentence family description + recording |

**Grammar spine:** possessives (my/your/his/her) + this/that + have/has.
**Accent Lab:** /θ/–/ð/ (mother/father/three) · plural -s (/s/,/z/) · -teen vs -ty.
**Mindset:** overcoming the fear/embarrassment of speaking — speak anyway.
**Arabic support:** ~60%, easing to ~50% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **"my family" recording** (another before/after proof clip).
- The ability to talk about the people in your life.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** builds on U1. Grammar = possessives + have/has + ages. **With Units 0–2 finished, the
> founding cohort can begin** (≈ first 3 weeks). Two habitual errors to hunt: **"he have"** and **his/her**
> mix-ups (gender follows the owner). Recycle U1 self-intro in warm-ups. Log the family clip (with consent).
