## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> This is my family. This is my **mother** and my **father**. I have one **brother** and two **sisters**.

**Family (gloss):**

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| mother / father | أم / أب |
| brother / sister | أخ / أخت |
| son / daughter | ابن / بنت |
| husband / wife | زوج / زوجة |

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (my / your)
- **my** = بتاعي: *my mother, my brother.*
- **your** = بتاعك: *your sister, your family.*
- **a big / small family** = عيلة كبيرة/صغيرة.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min, Arabic.** Family words on the tree + my/your. Keep it concrete with a real family photo/tree.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| my / your | بتاعي / بتاعك | *my mother · your brother* |
| big / small family | عيلة كبيرة/صغيرة | *I have a big family.* |
| I have… | عندي… | *I have two sisters.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: /ð/ in *mother · father · brother*.**
طرف لسانك **خفيف بين سنانك** + بصوت. مش "moder" ولا "broder".

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *mother · father · brother.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Drill /ð/ (tongue tip lightly between teeth + voice). Expect /d/ or /z/ substitution — model, don't over-correct yet.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — my or your?**
1. This is ______ mother. (بتاعي)

**Exercise 3 — Say it:** *This is my mother. This is my brother.*

> **~5 min.** **Ex1:** my · your.
