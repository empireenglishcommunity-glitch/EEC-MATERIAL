## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (have / has)
- **I / you / we / they → have** · **he / she / it → has**
  - *I have two sisters.* · *She has a son.*
- **Plurals:** brother → brother**s** · sister → sister**s**.
- **Question:** *Do you have…?* → *Yes, I do. / No, I don't.*

> ⚠️ مش *"he have"* → الصح *"he has"*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min, Arabic.** have vs has (he/she → has). Plural -s. The two classic errors: "he have" + a vowel before -s.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: plural -s endings.**
- **/z/**: brother**s** · sister**s** (بعد صوت مجهور)
- **/s/**: aunt**s** (بعد صوت مهموس)
- ❌ ماتزودش حرف علة: مش "brother-es".

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *brothers · sisters · aunts.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** /z/ vs /s/ on plurals; no extra vowel. This -s work seeds U4 (3rd-person -s) and beyond.
