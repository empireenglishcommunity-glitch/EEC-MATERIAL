## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> I have a big family. This is my father — his name is Hassan, he's fifty-five. This is my mother, Amira. I have two brothers and one sister. My sister is thirteen.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean 5–6 sentence model.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الوصف الكويس
1. ✅ **How big** — *I have a big/small family.*
2. ✅ **Who** — *This is my ___ (his/her name is ___).*
3. ✅ **How many** — *I have ___ brother(s)/sister(s).*
4. ✅ **Ages** — *He's/She's ___.*
5. ⭐ **Extra** — job / where they live.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these boxes; give the card.

---

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 2 mini-quiz
1. *She ___ a big family.* (have/has) → **has**
2. *This is my brother. ___ name is Ali.* → **His**
3. 13 = **thirteen** · 30 = **thirty** (وين الضغط؟)
4. *How ___ are you? — I'm 25.* → **old**
