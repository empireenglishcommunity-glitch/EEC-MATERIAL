# "This Is My…" — Introducing People (his / her)

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 2 · **Lesson 2**

*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit2-family-and-people.md` · **S0-U2-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 4 · Guided 6 · Record 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** introduce a person (*This is my friend, Ali*) · use *his/her* · use *this/that*.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Introduce another person: *This is my friend, Ali.*
- Use **his** and **her** correctly.
- Use **this** (قريب) and **that** (بعيد).

> هتقدّم شخص تاني، وتستخدم **his/her** صح، و**this/that**.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن
سمِّ فردين من عيلتك بـ *my* (من L01).

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> This is my friend. **His** name is Omar. And this is my sister — **her** name is Mona.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **this** = ده/دي (قريب) · **that** = ده/دي (بعيد).
- **his** = بتاعه (لو صاحب الحاجة **ولد**) · **her** = بتاعها (لو **بنت**).
  - ⚠️ الجنس بيتبع **صاحب الحاجة**: *her brother* (لو المتكلَّم عنه بنت)، *his sister* (لو ولد).

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min, Arabic.** this/that + his/her. The key rule: gender follows the **owner**. Drill it — this is the classic error.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| this / that | ده قريب / بعيد | *This is my brother.* |
| his / her | بتاعه / بتاعها | *His name is Ali. Her name is Sara.* |
| This is… | ده/دي… | *This is my friend.* |

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
- **/ð/ in *this / that*** (لسانك بين سنانك).
- **/h/ in *his / her*** — ماتبلعهاش.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *This is my brother. His name is Ali.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** /ð/ in this/that + clear /h/ in his/her.

---

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — his or her?**
1. This is my brother. ______ name is Ali.
2. This is my sister. ______ name is Nour.
3. This is my father. ______ job is teacher.

**Exercise 2 — this or that? (قريب/بعيد):** picture-based — قريب → ______ · بعيد → ______

**Exercise 3 — Introduce (قدّم شخص):** *This is my ___. His/Her name is ___.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** His · Her · His. **Ex2:** this · that.
