# Ages & Numbers 20–100

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 2 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded · Prerequisite: L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit2-family-and-people.md` · **S0-U2-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 5 · Guided 5 · Record 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** numbers 20–100 · ask/give ages · distinguish **-teen vs -ty** (thirteen/thirty).
> **The -teen/-ty confusion causes real misunderstandings (13 vs 30) — over-practice it. Stress is the cue.**
> **Support:** own age only. **Stretch:** 3 people + *How old is your…?* **Arabic ~55%.**

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Say **numbers 20–100**.
- Ask and give **ages**: *How old are you? → I'm ___.*
- Hear the difference: **thirteen ≠ thirty**.

> هتنطق 20–100، وتسأل وتقول السن، وتفرّق بين **-teen** و**-ty**.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> — How old are you? — I'm **twenty-five**. My father is **sixty**, and my sister is **thirteen**.

**Tens:** twenty · thirty · forty · fifty · sixty · seventy · eighty · ninety · one hundred

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **Ages:** *How old are you?* → *I'm ___ (years old).* · *He's/She's ___.*
- **الفخ -teen vs -ty:**
  - **thir-TEEN** (13) — الضغط في الآخر ↗
  - **THIR-ty** (30) — الضغط في الأول
  - نفس الحكاية: four**teen**/**for**ty · fif**teen**/**fif**ty

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Tens 20–100 + age frames. Drill the -teen (stressed end) vs -ty (stressed start) contrast.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| How old are you? | عندك كام سنة؟ | *— How old are you? — I'm 25.* |
| I'm … (years old) | عندي … سنة | *I'm twenty-five.* |
| twenty … hundred | عشرين … مية | *My father is sixty.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: -teen vs -ty (stress) + /θ/.**
- thir**TEEN** ↗ vs **THIR**ty · fif**TEEN** vs **FIF**ty
- **/θ/** in *thirteen / thirty* (لسانك بين سنانك).

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *thirteen · thirty · fifteen · fifty.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** The stress contrast is the cue — drill minimal pairs; keep /θ/ clean.
