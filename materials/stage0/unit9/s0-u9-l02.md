# What Are They Doing? (Describing Scenes)

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 9 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit9-happening-now.md` · **S0-U9-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 4 · Guided 6 · Record 4 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** describe others' actions now · *What is/are ___ doing?* · describe a busy scene.
> **Great "thinking in English" practice — narrate real surroundings.** Watch dropped *be* again. **Arabic ~25%.**
> **Support:** 3 sentences. **Stretch:** 5 sentences + question exchange.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Describe what others are doing now: *He's reading. They're playing.*
- Ask *What are you/they doing?*
- Describe a busy scene.

> هتوصف الناس بتعمل إيه دلوقتي، وتسأل *What are they doing?*

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **What is he/she doing? → He's/She's + -ing.**
- **What are they doing? → They're + -ing.**
- مشهد فيه كذا فعل في نفس الوقت.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Continuous questions + he/she/they; multiple actions in a scene.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What is he doing? | هو بيعمل إيه؟ | *He's cooking.* |
| What are they doing? | هما بيعملوا إيه؟ | *They're playing.* |
| cooking / talking / watching | بيطبخ / بيتكلم / بيتفرج | *She's talking.* |
