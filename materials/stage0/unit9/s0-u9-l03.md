## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I **usually drink** tea, but **right now** I**'m drinking** coffee. She **usually works** in an office, but today she**'s working** at home.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
| Meaning | Tense | Time markers |
|---|---|---|
| habit / كل يوم | present **simple** (I drink) | usually · every day · always |
| now / دلوقتي | present **continuous** (I'm drinking) | now · right now · at the moment |

- **Pattern:** *I usually ___, but right now I'm ___-ing.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** The core A1 contrast. Time markers signal the choice. Don't over-explain.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| usually + simple | عادةً | *I usually walk to work.* |
| right now + continuous | دلوقتي | *Right now, I'm taking the bus.* |
| but | بس | *…but right now I'm…* |
