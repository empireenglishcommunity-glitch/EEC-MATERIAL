## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> Right now, I**'m sitting** at my desk. I**'m drinking** coffee and I**'m studying** English.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (present continuous)
- **am/is/are + verb-ing** = بيحصل **دلوقتي**: *I'm sitting. She's working. They're playing.*
- **-ing spelling:** run → run**ning** (double) · write → wri**ting** (drop -e) · study → study**ing**.
- ⚠️ ماتنساش الـ **be**: *I'm studying* ✅ (مش "I studying").

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** be + -ing for NOW; spelling; contractions. Target the dropped *be*.
