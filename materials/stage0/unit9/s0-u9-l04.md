## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Name **clothes** (shirt, trousers, dress, shoes, jacket, hat).
- Say what you're wearing: *I'm wearing a blue shirt.*
- Combine **colour + item** (adjective + noun order from U7).

> هتقول بتلبس إيه دلوقتي (colour + clothes).

بتجمع القديم (colour + noun) مع الجديد (continuous) بحرية — دي الطلاقة بتتكوّن.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I**'m wearing** a **black jacket** and **blue jeans**. She**'s wearing** a **red dress**.

**Clothes:** shirt قميص · trousers/jeans بنطلون/جينز · dress فستان · shoes جزمة · jacket جاكيت · hat قبعة

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **be + wearing + (colour) + item:** *I'm wearing a blue shirt.*
- adjective **before** noun (من U7): *a blue shirt* (مش "a shirt blue").

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Clothes + *wearing* + colour + item; reinforce adjective-before-noun.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| dress / jacket / hat | فستان / جاكيت / قبعة | *a red dress* |
