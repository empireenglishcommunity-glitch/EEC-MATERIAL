## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** describe **what's happening now** (present continuous), contrast it with what you
> **usually** do, say what people are **wearing** — then narrate a live scene.
> مهمتك: تحكي اللي بيحصل دلوقتي، وتقارنه بعادتك، وتقول الناس لابسة إيه.

**Why now:** this is the last big grammar unit — juggling two tenses is near-A1-exit control.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | "I'm …-ing" | present continuous (now) · -ing spelling · contractions |
| **L02** | What Are They Doing? | describe others' actions · *What is/are ___ doing?* · scenes |
| **L03** | now vs usually ⭐ | continuous vs simple · *I usually…, but right now I'm…* |
| **L04** | What Are You Wearing? | clothes + colours + continuous |
| **L05** | Describe What's Happening (milestone) 🎙️ | a 6–8 sentence scene narration + recording |

**Grammar spine:** present continuous (now) · continuous vs simple (now/usually).
**Accent Lab:** -ing endings (/ɪŋ/) · stress the time marker (now vs usually) · /ʃ/ (shirt/shoes).
**Mindset:** thinking in English — reduce in-head translation.
**Arabic support:** ~25%, easing to ~20% by L05 (near immersion).

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **"what's happening now" narration** (live narration + two tenses = near-A1-exit fluency).

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** the last big grammar unit. Target the **dropped *be*** ("I studying") throughout. L03
> (now vs usually) is the highest-order Stage-0 grammar — let time markers do the work. Integrate U7
> adjective+noun (clothes). ~10 weeks in — prep learners for Unit 10 (summative + graduation "after" clip).
