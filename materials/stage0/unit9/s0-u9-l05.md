## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> It's the evening. My family is at home. My father is watching TV. My mother is cooking — she's wearing a blue dress. My brother is studying. I usually watch TV too, but right now I'm speaking English!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean 6–7 sentence scene narration.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الحكي الكويس
1. ✅ **Set the scene** — *It's the evening / We're at home.*
2. ✅ **Actions now** — *He's ___-ing, she's ___-ing.*
3. ✅ **Clothes** — *She's wearing ___.*
4. ✅ **Contrast** — *I usually ___, but right now I'm ___.*
5. ⭐ **A question** — *What are you doing?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these boxes; give the card.
