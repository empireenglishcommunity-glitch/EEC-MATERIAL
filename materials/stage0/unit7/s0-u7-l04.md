## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Use **have got / has got**: *I've got brown eyes. She's got a car.*
- Use contractions: *I've got · she's got.*
- Ask *Have you got…?* → *Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.*

> هتستخدم **have got** للصفات والملكية، مع الاختصارات.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **I/you/we/they → 've got** · **he/she/it → 's got**
  - *I've got brown eyes. She's got a car.*
- **= have/has** (للملكية) — نفس المعنى.
- **Question:** *Have you got…?* → *Yes, I have / No, I haven't.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** have got = have; contractions; questions. Link to U2.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I've got / she's got | عندي / عندها | *I've got green eyes.* |
| Have you got…? | عندك…؟ | *Have you got a car?* |
| a pet / a car / brothers | حيوان أليف / عربية / إخوات | *He's got a dog.* |
