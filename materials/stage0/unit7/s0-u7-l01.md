## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> It's a **red** car. It's **big** and **new**. My bag is **black**.

**Colours:** red · blue · green · yellow · white · black · brown · orange
**Adjectives:** big/small · old/new · good/bad · nice · beautiful · tall/short

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **It's + adjective:** *It's red. It's big.*
- **What colour is it? → It's ___.**
- Opposites: big↔small, old↔new, good↔bad.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Colours + opposite adjectives; *It's + adjective*; *What colour…?*

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: /r/ vs /w/.**
- **/r/** (red, brown): اللسان مقوّس، **من غير تكرار**.
- **/w/** (white, yellow): **دوّر شفايفك** (زي ما بتنفخ شمعة).

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *red · white · brown · yellow.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** /r/ (no roll) vs /w/ (lip rounding). Light + visual.
