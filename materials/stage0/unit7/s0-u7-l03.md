## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> She **is tall**. She has **long black hair** and **brown eyes**. He **is** young and friendly.

**Appearance:** tall/short · young/old · long/short hair · black/blonde/brown hair · blue/brown/green eyes

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **He/She is + adjective:** *She is tall. He is young.*
- **hair/eyes:** *long black hair · brown eyes* (adjective + noun order من L02!).
- **Question:** *What does he/she look like?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Appearance adjectives; *He/She is + adjective*; preview *has got hair* (→ L04).
