## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** describe **things** and **people** with adjectives and **have got** — then record a
> description of someone you know.
> مهمتك: توصف الحاجات والناس بالصفات وبـ have got، وتسجّل وصف شخص تعرفه.

**Why now:** description makes your English vivid — and fixes the big Arabic→English word-order difference.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Colours & Adjectives | colours + basic adjectives · *It's + adjective* |
| **L02** | Adjective + Noun ⭐ | adjective **before** noun (*a big house*) — the word-order fix |
| **L03** | Appearance | tall/short · hair/eyes · *He/She is ___* |
| **L04** | have got | *I've got brown eyes* · contractions · *Have you got…?* |
| **L05** | Describe a Person (milestone) 🎙️ | a 6–8 sentence person description + recording |

**Grammar spine:** adjective + noun order · colours · have got.
**Accent Lab:** adjective stress · /w/ and /r/ · contractions (I've/she's got).
**Mindset:** progress mindset — notice + celebrate small wins.
**Arabic support:** ~35%, easing to ~30% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **"describe someone" recording** (descriptive fluency — a clear, visible gain vs Day-1).

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** the **adjective–noun order** fix (L02) is the signature word-order win — Arabic reverses
> it ("a house big"). Drill it warmly; it recurs at every level. /r/ vs /w/ rounding cue. have got = have
> (link to U2). Keep person descriptions respectful. Log the clip (with consent) at ~8 weeks.
