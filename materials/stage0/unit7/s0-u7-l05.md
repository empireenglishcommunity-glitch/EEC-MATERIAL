## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> This is my friend Karim. He's tall and friendly. He's got short black hair and brown eyes. He's got a nice car. He likes football. I like him a lot!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean 6–7 sentence description.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الوصف الكويس
1. ✅ **Who** — *This is my ___.*
2. ✅ **Appearance** — *He/She is tall/short…*
3. ✅ **Features** — *He/She's got ___ hair / eyes.*
4. ✅ **A possession/like** — *He's got a car. She likes ___.*
5. ⭐ **adjective + noun order** clean.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these boxes; give the card.

---
