# Describing Things (Adjective + Noun)

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 7 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit7-describing-people-and-things.md` · **S0-U7-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 7 · Accent 4 · Guided 6 · Record 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** adjective **before** noun (*a big house*) · 2 adjectives · simple comparison (optional).
> **Signature word-order fix:** Arabic puts the adjective AFTER the noun → expect "a house big". Drill explicitly + warmly; recurs at every level.
> **Support:** one adjective + noun. **Stretch:** 2 adjectives + comparison. **Arabic ~35%.**

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Put the **adjective BEFORE the noun**: *a big house, a beautiful car.*
- Use **2 adjectives**: *a small old phone.*

> هتحط الصفة **قبل** الاسم (عكس العربي) — *a big house* مش "a house big".

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
دي أهم قاعدة ترتيب في الإنجليزي، وبتفرق في كل جملة وصف. لبنة كل يوم = جملة صح كل يوم.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> It's a **beautiful old** house. I have a **new black** bag. She has a **small red** car.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- في الإنجليزي: **adjective + noun** — الصفة **قبل** الاسم.
  - ✅ *a big house* · ❌ *a house big*
- ترتيب صفتين (تقريبي): opinion → size → age → colour: *a beautiful small old car.*

> ⚠️ العربي بيقول "بيت كبير" (الاسم الأول). الإنجليزي بالعكس: **big house**.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min, Arabic.** The core word-order fix. Contrast with Arabic order explicitly. Expect "a house big".
