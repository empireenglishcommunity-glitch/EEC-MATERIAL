## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
سلسلة: likes (+ -ing) → hobbies (play/go/do) → can/can't → أسئلة *Do you like…*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Chain recall.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> In my free time, I love reading and I play football. I can swim, but I can't play the guitar — yet! — Do you like football? — Yes, I do! Me too.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a model description + a short exchange.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الكلام الكويس
1. ✅ **Likes** — *I love/like ___ (-ing).*
2. ✅ **Hobbies** — *I play/go/do ___.*
3. ✅ **Ability** — *I can ___, I can't ___ (yet).*
4. ✅ **A question** — *Do you like ___?*
5. ⭐ **Follow-up** — *Me too / Really?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these boxes; give the card.

---
