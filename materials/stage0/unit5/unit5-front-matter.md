## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** talk about **hobbies** and **likes/dislikes**, say what you **can and can't** do, and have
> your first real **mini-conversation** — then record a "my free time" piece.
> مهمتك: تتكلم عن هواياتك واللي بتحبه، وتقول تقدر تعمل إيه، وتعمل أول محادثة حقيقية.

**Why now:** free-time chat is where real conversation begins — questions + answers + follow-ups.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | "I Like…" (+ -ing) | like/love/hate + noun/-ing · don't like |
| **L02** | Hobbies | play/go/do · *In my free time, I…* |
| **L03** | "Can You…?" (ability) ⭐ | can/can't (no -s) · weak can vs strong can't |
| **L04** | "Do You Like…?" 🗣️ | Do-you-like Qs + short answers + follow-ups (first real convo) |
| **L05** | Talk About Your Free Time (milestone) 🎙️ | a 6–8 sentence free-time talk + a short conversation |

**Grammar spine:** like/love/hate + -ing · can/can't · *Do you like…?* questions.
**Accent Lab:** -ing endings (/ŋ/) · weak/strong can/can't · rising question intonation · content-word stress.
**Mindset:** build a personal English **input diet** (one show/podcast you enjoy) · "I can't… **yet**."
**Arabic support:** ~45%, easing to ~40% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **"my free time" recording** + your **first mini-conversation** (Interaction emerges).

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** L04 is the first genuine back-and-forth — celebrate it. Target errors: like + to/-ing,
> /ɪn/ for /ɪŋ/, no -s on *can* ("she cans"), "You like…?" (missing *do*), "Yes, I like" (→ "Yes, I do").
> The **Interaction** rubric dimension now applies. Log the clip (with consent). Push the input-diet habit.
