## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I **love reading**. I **like** football. I **don't like cooking**. I **hate** waking up early!

**Scale:** love ❤️ > like 🙂 > don't like 🙁 > hate ✖️

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (like + -ing)
- **like / love / hate + noun:** *I like football.*
- **like / love / hate + verb-ing:** *I love read**ing**. I hate cook**ing**.*
- **negative:** *I **don't** like ___.*

> ⚠️ مش *"I like to cooking"* — بعد like بنقول **-ing**: *I like cooking.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** like/love/hate + noun and + -ing; negative don't like.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

|---|---|---|
| like / love / hate | بحب / بعشق / بكره | *I love reading.* |
| don't like | ماباحبش | *I don't like cooking.* |
| reading / cooking / swimming | القراية / الطبخ / العوم | *I like swimming.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: -ing endings /ɪŋ/.**
readi**ng** · swimmi**ng** · cooki**ng** — مش "readin" ولا "readingg". + اربط *"I‿like"*.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *I love reading. I like swimming.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Clean /ŋ/ (not /ɪn/ or /ɪŋg/); link "I-like".

---

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — Add -ing:** I like (read) ___ · I love (swim) ___ · I hate (cook) ___

**Exercise 2 — Fix it:** ❌ *I like to swimming.* → ✅ __________

**Exercise 3 — True for you:** *I love ___. I don't like ___.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** reading · swimming · cooking. **Ex2:** *I like swimming.*
