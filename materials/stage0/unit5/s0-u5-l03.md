# "Can You…?" — Ability

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 5 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L02
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit5-free-time-and-ability.md` · **S0-U5-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 5 · Guided 6 · Record 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** *can/can't* for ability · *Can you…?* + short answers · others' abilities.
> **Two wins:** no -s on modals ("she can cook", not "cans"); hearing weak *can* /kən/ vs strong *can't* /kænt/.
> **Support:** I can / I can't only. **Stretch:** he/she can + follow-up. **Arabic ~40%.**

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Say what you **can** and **can't** do: *I can swim. I can't drive.*
- Ask *Can you…?* → *Yes, I can. / No, I can't.*
- Talk about others: *She can cook well.*

> هتقول تقدر تعمل إيه ومش تقدر، وتسأل *Can you…?*

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
الـ mindset: *"I can't… **yet**."* ضيف "yet" وافضل بتتعلم — القدرة بتيجي بالتمرين.

قول هوايتين من L02.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I **can** swim, but I **can't** drive. — **Can you** cook? — Yes, I **can**! My sister **can** cook very well.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (can / can't)
- **can / can't + base verb** (نفس الشكل للكل — **مفيش -s**!): *She **can** cook.* (مش "cans")
- **Question:** *Can you…?* → *Yes, I can. / No, I can't.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** can/can't + base verb; NO 3rd-person -s; short answers.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I can / can't | أقدر / ماقدرش | *I can swim. I can't drive.* |
| Can you…? | تقدر…؟ | *Can you cook?* |
| swim / drive / cook / sing | أعوم / أسوق / أطبخ / أغني | *She can sing.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: weak *can* /kən/ vs strong *can't* /kænt/.**
- *I can swim* → الـ can خفيفة /kən/.
- *I can't swim* → الـ can't واضحة + /t/.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *I can swim. I can't drive.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Hear + produce weak can vs strong can't (the final /t/ is the cue).
