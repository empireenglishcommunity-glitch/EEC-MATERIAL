## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> — **Do you like** football? — Yes, I **do**! Do you? — No, I **don't**. But I like basketball. — Me too!

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **Do you like + noun / -ing?** → *Do you like cooking?*
- **Short answers:** *Yes, I do. / No, I don't.* (مش "Yes, I like")
- **Keep it going:** *Me too! · Really? · Why?*

> ⚠️ مش *"You like football?"* → لازم **Do**: *Do you like football?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Do-you-like Qs + short answers + follow-ups. Hunt "You like…?" and "Yes, I like".

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
- **Rising intonation** على سؤال yes/no ↗.
- Link *"do you"* → /djə/ ("d'you like…?").

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *Do you like football?* ↗

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Rising yes/no intonation + natural "d'you" linking.
