## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Ask and answer **"Where are you from?"**
- Name several **countries** (and say you're **Egyptian**).
- Talk about someone else: **"He's from Egypt."**

> هتسأل وترد على *"Where are you from?"*، وتسمّي كذا بلد، وتتكلم عن حد تاني: *He's from Egypt.*

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
سؤال *"Where are you from?"* بييجي في أول أي محادثة مع أجنبي. لبنة تانية في التعريف بنفسك.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> **A:** Hi! Where are you from?
> **B:** I'm from Egypt. And you?
> **A:** I'm from Saudi Arabia.
> **B:** This is Layla. **She's** from the UAE.

**Countries (gloss):**

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Egypt | مصر |
| Saudi Arabia | السعودية |
| the UAE | الإمارات |
| England / America | إنجلترا / أمريكا |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice; learners catch *Where are you from?* + *I'm from…* and the *she's from…* report.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **Question:** *Where are you from?* = إنت منين؟
- **Answer:** *I'm from + (country).* → *I'm from Egypt.*
- **Talking about others (to be extended):**
  - *He's from ___* (هو من…) · *She's from ___* (هي من…)
- **Nationality (خفيف):** country **Egypt** → nationality **Egyptian**. *I'm Egyptian.*

> ملاحظة: مع الدول زي **the UAE** بنقول **the**. مع أغلب الدول (Egypt, Saudi Arabia) من غير *the*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Teach Q→A + *he's/she's from*. Introduce **only one** nationality (*Egyptian*) — don't overload; nationalities are irregular (A1 = the pattern, not the list).

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Where are you from? | إنت منين؟ | *— Where are you from? — I'm from Egypt.* |
| I'm from… | أنا من… | *I'm from Cairo.* |
| He's / She's from… | هو/هي من… | *She's from the UAE.* |
| Egyptian | مصري | *I'm Egyptian.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: the American /r/ in *from* and *are*.**
- الـ /r/ الأمريكاني: اللسان **مقوّس لورا**، **من غير تكرار** (مش راء عربية مكررة).
- **Stress the country:** *"I'm from **E**gypt."* (الضغط على اسم البلد).

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** قول *"Where are you from? — I'm from Egypt."* → قارن الـ /r/ والضغط على البلد.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Drill American /r/ (from, are, Saudi Arabia). Watch for a trilled Arabic ر. Stress the country name.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — Complete:**
1. — Where ______ you from? — I'm ______ Egypt.
2. This is Omar. ______ from Alexandria.
3. This is Nour. ______ from the UAE.

**Exercise 2 — True for you:**
- Where are you from? → __________________

**Exercise 3 — Match the country to *the*:**
| Country | the? (نعم/لا) |
|---|---|
| Egypt | ______ |
| UAE | ______ |
| Saudi Arabia | ______ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Pair-check.
> **Answers — Ex1:** *are / from* · *He's* · *She's*. **Ex3:** Egypt → لا · UAE → نعم (the UAE) · Saudi Arabia → لا.
