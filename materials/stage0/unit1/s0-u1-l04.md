## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **Question:** *What do you do?* = بتشتغل إيه؟
- **Answer:** *I'm **a** / **an** + (job).*
- **قاعدة a / an:**
  - **a** قبل صوت ساكن → *a teacher, a doctor, a student.*
  - **an** قبل صوت متحرك (a, e, i, o, u) → *an engineer, an accountant.*
- **Others:** *He's a driver.* / *She's a doctor.*

> ⚠️ ماتنساش الـ **a/an**! *"I engineer"* ❌ → *"I'm an engineer"* ✅.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min, in Arabic.** Teach *What do you do?* + a/an rule (consonant vs vowel **sound**). **Watch for:** omitting the article (*"I engineer"*) — the key error to fix, gently + repeatedly.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What do you do? | بتشتغل إيه؟ | *— What do you do? — I'm a nurse.* |
| I'm a / an … | أنا (وظيفة) | *I'm an engineer.* |
| He's / She's a … | هو/هي (وظيفة) | *She's a doctor.* |
| a teacher / an engineer | مدرّس / مهندس | *a* + ساكن · *an* + متحرك |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: linking *a/an* + the job, and stressing the job.**
- *"a teacher"* → اربطهم: **a‿teacher**. *"an engineer"* → **an‿engineer**.
- **Stress the job word:** *"I'm an en**gi**neer."*

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** قول *"a teacher"* و *"an engineer"* → قارن الربط والضغط.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Drill *a teacher / an engineer* linking; stress the job. Keep the /aɪ/ + /m/ from L02 clean.
