## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** by the end of Unit 1 you'll **introduce yourself fully in English** — greet, give your
> name, country, and job, ask someone else the same, and record it on video.
> مهمتك في الوحدة دي: تعرّف نفسك كامل بالإنجليزي، وتسأل حد تاني، وتسجّل الفيديو بتاعك.

**Why it's your first campaign:** every conversation on earth starts with *"Hi, I'm…"*. Win this, and you
walk into any conversation with confidence. **لبنة كل يوم، والإمبراطورية بتتبني.**

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Greetings & "How are you?" | greet by time of day · ask/answer *How are you?* · say goodbye |
| **L02** | "Hello, I'm…" | introduce yourself (name · country · job) · ask *What's your name?* |
| **L03** | "Where are you from?" | ask/answer *Where are you from?* · name countries · *He's/She's from…* |
| **L04** | "What do you do?" | ask/answer *What do you do?* · use **a/an** with jobs |
| **L05** | Self-Introduction (milestone) 🎙️ | give a connected 4–6 sentence self-intro + **record your milestone** |

**Grammar spine:** the verb **to be** (I'm / you're / he's / she's) + **a/an**.
**Accent Lab:** /h/ · /aɪ/ + /m/ · American /r/ · a/an linking · word stress.
**Mindset:** the daily habit — pick your 20-minute slot; consistency > intensity.
**Arabic support:** heavy (~70%), easing slightly toward L05.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Unit-1 self-introduction recording** (your first "before" proof clip — we compare it later).
- The confidence to **start any conversation** in English.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (~30 min each) + this wrapper. Grammar spine = *to be* + a/an. The Unit-1
> speaking task (L05) is a **key proof asset** — capture every learner's recording (with consent) for the
> before/after timeline. Watch the two habitual A1 errors all unit: **dropped *to be*** ("I teacher") and
> **missing article** ("I engineer"). Recycle greetings + self-intro in every warm-up.
