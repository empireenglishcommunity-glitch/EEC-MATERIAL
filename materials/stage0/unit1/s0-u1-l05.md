## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة سريعة)
افتكر بسرعة كسلسلة: **greeting → name → from → job.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Rapid retrieval chain around the room: greeting, name, country, job — one each, fast.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج (Model)
اسمع النموذج ده (5 جمل):
> Hello! My name is Nour. I'm from Egypt — I'm from Cairo. I'm a pharmacist. Nice to meet you!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean 5-sentence model. Learners just listen for the shape.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست التعريف الكويس
تعريف كويس فيه:
1. ✅ **Greeting** (Hello / Hi + time of day)
2. ✅ **Name** (My name is… / I'm…)
3. ✅ **Country** (I'm from…)
4. ✅ **Job** (I'm a/an…)
5. ⭐ **One extra** (Nice to meet you / I live in…)

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these 5 boxes; give each learner the checklist card.

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق (clinic)
كل واحد يدرّب **أضعف صوت** عنده من الوحدة:
- /h/ (hello, how) · /aɪ/ + /m/ (I'm) · American /r/ (from) · a/an linking · stress on the key word.

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one before the milestone.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Personalized: each learner drills their **one** flagged weak sound from U0–U1.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (the milestone)
سجّل تعريف بنفسك كامل ومتصل (4–6 جمل): **greeting + name + country + job + one extra.**

> 🎙️ **Record your Unit-1 self-introduction and save it.** ده الـ milestone بتاعك — أول دليل على تقدمك. احتفل بيه! 👑

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 1 mini-quiz (صحّح لنفسك)
1. Choose: *I ___ a teacher.* (am / is / are) → **am**
2. a or an? *I'm ___ engineer.* → **an**
3. Complete: *Where ___ you from?* → **are**
4. Match: *How are you?* → **I'm fine, thanks.**
