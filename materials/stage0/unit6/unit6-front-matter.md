## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** talk about **food**, ask **prices**, and **order/request politely** — then perform a
> café/shop **role-play** from memory.
> مهمتك: تتكلم عن الأكل، تسأل عن الأسعار، وتطلب بأدب، وتعمل role-play في كافيه.

**Why now:** this is English you'll actually *use* — the first fully functional real-world skill.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Food & Drink | name foods/drinks · *I eat/drink/like* · meals |
| **L02** | some / any | some (+) / any (−,?) · countable vs uncountable |
| **L03** | "I'd Like…" | order politely · *Can I have…? · Would you like…?* |
| **L04** | Prices | *How much/How many?* · say prices · 13 vs 30 |
| **L05** | Café/Shop Role-Play (milestone) 🎙️ | a full greet→order→pay→thank exchange + recording |

**Grammar spine:** some/any · would like · countable/uncountable · how much/how many.
**Accent Lab:** clear numbers/prices (13 vs 30) · polite rising intonation for requests.
**Mindset:** real-world speaking reps — order in English; use it outside class.
**Arabic support:** ~40%, easing to ~35% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **café/shop role-play recording** (a functional, real-world skill — very motivating).

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** high-motivation, functional unit. Reframe "I want" → "I'd like" (politeness). Keep
> count/uncount light. The 13/30 price confusion has real stakes — reinforce the stress cue. Encourage
> learners to use it in a real café. Log the role-play clip (with consent) at ~7 weeks.
