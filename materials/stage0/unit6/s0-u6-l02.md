## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> I have **some** rice, but I don't have **any** eggs. Is there **any** milk?

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **some** في الإثبات: *I have some bread.*
- **any** في النفي والسؤال: *I don't have any milk. Is there any juice?*
- **Countable** (تقدر تعدّه): an apple, two eggs, three bananas.
- **Uncountable** (ماتعدّهوش): rice, water, milk, bread — **مفيش جمع**.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min, Arabic.** some/any + count/uncount. Common error: pluralizing uncountables ("rices"). Keep examples concrete.
