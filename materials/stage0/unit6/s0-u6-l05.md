## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> — Hello! Can I help you? — Yes, I'd like a coffee and a sandwich, please. — Sure. Anything else? — No, thanks. How much is it? — That's twenty-five pounds. — Here you are. — Thank you!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean café role-play.

---

## 🧩 The Stages — مراحل التعامل
1. ✅ **Greet** — *Hello! Can I help you?*
2. ✅ **Order / ask** — *I'd like ___, please. · How much is it?*
3. ✅ **Respond** — *Anything else? · That's ___ pounds.*
4. ✅ **Pay + thank** — *Here you are. · Thank you!*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the role-play into these stages; give the card.
