# "I'd Like…" — Ordering & Requests

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 6 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L02
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit6-food-and-shopping.md` · **S0-U6-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 4 · Guided 6 · Record 4 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** order politely with *I'd like* · *Can I have…?* · *Would you like…?* (offer).
> **Learners default to "I want" (can sound rude) — reframe "I'd like" as the polite, natural choice.** Model warmth.
> **Support:** one item + "I'd like…, please." **Stretch:** mini-order + follow-up. **Arabic ~35%.**

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Order politely: *I'd like a coffee, please.*
- Make requests: *Can I have…?* and respond (*Here you are. Thank you.*).
- Offer: *Would you like…?*

> هتطلب بأدب بـ **I'd like** بدل "I want".

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
الأدب + ابتسامة = تعاملات حقيقية أسهل بكتير. *"I'd like"* أحلى بكتير من *"I want"*.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **I'd like** (= I would like) = عايز، بأدب: *I'd like a coffee, please.*
- **Can I have…?** = ممكن آخد…؟
- **Would you like…?** = تحب…؟ (عرض).
- ماتقولش "I want" في المطعم — مش مؤدبة.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** I'd like vs I want; Can I have…?; Would you like…?. Emphasize politeness.
