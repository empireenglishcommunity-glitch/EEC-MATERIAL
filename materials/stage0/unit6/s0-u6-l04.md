## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> — **How much** is this? — It's **fifteen** pounds. — **How many** do you want? — Two, please. — That's **thirty**.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **How much** + سعر / غير معدود: *How much is the rice?*
- **How many** + معدود: *How many apples?*
- **Prices:** *It's ___ (pounds).* · *That's ___, please.*

> ⚠️ **thirteen (13)** مش **thirty (30)** — الضغط بيفرق (من U2)!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** how much vs how many; saying prices. Reinforce -teen/-ty.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| How many…? | كام واحدة؟ | *How many apples?* |
| That's … , please | يبقى… لو سمحت | *That's twenty, please.* |
