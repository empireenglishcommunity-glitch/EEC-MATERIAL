# Food & Drink Vocabulary (I like / I eat…)

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 6 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Unit 5
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit6-food-and-shopping.md` · **S0-U6-L01***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 4 · Guided 6 · Record 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** name foods/drinks · *I eat/drink/like ___* · meals.
> **Keep it personal + appetising** — high-motivation topic; learners talk easily. **Arabic ~40%.**
> **Support:** 4 foods + "I like…". **Stretch:** a 3-meal description.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Name common **foods and drinks**.
- Say what you **eat / drink / like**.
- Name the **meals** (breakfast, lunch, dinner).

> هتسمّي الأكل والمشروبات، وتقول بتاكل وتشرب إيه.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> For **breakfast** I eat **bread** and drink **tea**. For **lunch** I eat **rice** and **chicken**. I like **fruit**.

**Food/drink (gloss):** bread عيش · rice رز · chicken فراخ · fruit فاكهة · water مية · tea شاي · coffee قهوة · juice عصير

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **I eat + food · I drink + drink · I like + food.**
- **Meals:** breakfast (فطار) · lunch (غدا) · dinner (عشا).
- **"For breakfast, I eat ___."**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Food/drink + meals; recycle eat/drink/like from U5.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| bread / rice / chicken | عيش / رز / فراخ | *I eat rice.* |
| tea / coffee / water / juice | شاي / قهوة / مية / عصير | *I drink tea.* |
| breakfast / lunch / dinner | فطار / غدا / عشا | *For lunch, I eat…* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *For breakfast I eat bread and drink tea.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Clear food-word vowels; link "I eat".
