## 🧩 The "Keep It Going" Toolkit — عدّة استمرار الكلام
- **Ask back:** *Do you like…? What about you? What are you doing?*
- **Follow-ups:** *Really? Me too! Why?*
- **Survival:** *Sorry? Can you repeat that, please?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Questions + follow-ups toolkit.
