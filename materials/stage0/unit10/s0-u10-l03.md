## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع (minimal pairs)
Common targets: **/p/–/b/** (pen/Ben) · **/v/–/f/** (van/fan) · **/θ/–/ð/** (think/this) · **/l/–/r/** (light/right) · **-ing** · clusters (street).

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Model each target sound with minimal pairs.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — الطريقة (articulation cues)
For each of **your 3 sounds**, re-learn the **mouth position**:
- **/p/–/b/:** both lips; /p/ = puff of air, /b/ = voice on.
- **/θ/–/ð/:** tongue tip between teeth.
- **/v/–/f/:** top teeth on bottom lip; /v/ = voice on.
- **/l/–/r/:** /l/ tongue touches ridge; /r/ tongue pulls back, no touch.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Re-teach the cue for each of the learner's 3 sounds.

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق (core, ~9 min)
For **each** of your 3 sounds, cycle:
**perceive → say alone → in words → in a phrase → record-and-compare.**

> 🎧 **Record & compare** each cycle. Listen for the gain.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~9 min core.** Cycle each sound; record-and-compare is essential.

---

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب
Put each of your 3 sounds into **one sentence** and refine.
> Example (/θ/ + /v/): *I think of five things.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** One sentence per sound; refine.
