## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج (full A1 conversation)
> **A:** Hi! How are you? **B:** I'm fine, thanks. I'm Nour. **A:** Nice to meet you. Where are you from?
> **B:** I'm from Alexandria. I'm a teacher. **A:** Do you have a big family? **B:** Yes, I have two sisters.
> I usually work in the morning, and I like reading in the evening. **A:** Can I have your number?
> **B:** Sure! Right now, I'm waiting for a friend — see you soon!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Watch the full model; point out rubric dimensions in action.

---

## 🧩 The Rubric — معايير التقييم (5 dimensions)
| Dimension | بالعربي | Means… |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluency** | الطلاقة | keep going, not too many long pauses |
| **Accuracy** | الصح | correct basic grammar (to be, -s, tenses) |
| **Pronunciation** | النطق | clear + intelligible (your 3 sounds) |
| **Range** | التنوّع | variety of Stage-0 language |
| **Interaction** | التفاعل | ask back, respond, keep it going |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Walk through the 5 dimensions in plain terms so learners know the criteria.
