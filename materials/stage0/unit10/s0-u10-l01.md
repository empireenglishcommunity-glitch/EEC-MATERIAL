## 🧩 The Self-Check Card — كارت التصحيح الذاتي
Watch these **4 errors**:
1. **to be** — *I **am** an engineer* (مش "I engineer").
2. **a/an** — *an engineer · a teacher.*
3. **he/she + -s** — *He work**s**.*
4. **there is/are** — *There **is** a park · There **are** two shops.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The 4 recurring Stage-0 errors; hand out the card.
