## 1️⃣ Settle — اهدا
> This is a conversation, not a test. You've done all of this before. Take a breath. 👑

> [!TEACHER]
> **~2 min.** Brief, warm reassurance (near-English).

---

## 2️⃣ The Quiz — الاختبار (recognition)
Mixed Stage-0 check (Google Form): **to be · a/an · plurals · present simple (incl. he/she -s) · there is/are · some/any · can · would like · adjective order · present continuous · now-vs-usually · prices · directions vocab.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min.** Learner completes the recognition quiz.

---

## 3️⃣ The Speaking Task — المحادثة (the A1 exchange)
**greet → self-introduce → family + routine + free time → one functional exchange (order / directions) → describe what's happening now → close.** ~5–8 minutes. Scored on the rubric.

**Your rubric (0–5 each):** Fluency · Accuracy · Pronunciation/Accent · Range · Interaction.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8–12 min.** A1 exchange with coach (or paired + coach-observed); score on the 5-dim rubric. A1 pass threshold per the assessment suite (Task 1.6).

---

## 4️⃣ The Graduation Sample — تسجيل التخرّج 🎬
Record the **standard prompt** (identical to Day 1):
> *"Tell me about yourself, your family, and your typical day."*

This becomes the **"after"** half of your before/after pair.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~3 min.** Same prompt as Day-1 for a fair before/after. This is the flagship testimonial asset (with consent).
