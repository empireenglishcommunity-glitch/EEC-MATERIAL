## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> The keys are **on** the table. The bag is **under** the chair. The lamp is **next to** the bed.

**Prepositions (gloss):** in جوّه · on على · under تحت · next to جنب · behind ورا · in front of قدّام · between بين

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **Where is** + حاجة واحدة → *It's + preposition + the ___.*
- **Where are** + جمع → *They're + preposition.*
- استخدم الصورة (كورة + علبة) عشان تحس بالفرق: in / on / under.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Prepositions with a box+ball visual. *Where is…? → It's ___.* Contrast in/on/under.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| on / in / under | على / جوّه / تحت | *It's on the table.* |
| next to / behind / in front of | جنب / ورا / قدّام | *It's next to the bed.* |
| Where is…? | فين…؟ | *— Where is the key? — It's under the book.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
