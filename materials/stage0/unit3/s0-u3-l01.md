## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Name **everyday objects** (book, pen, phone, table, chair, bag, key, cup).
- Use **a / an** with objects.
- Ask *What's this?* and answer *It's a/an ___.*

> هتسمّي الحاجات اللي حواليك، وتستخدم **a/an**، وتسأل وترد *What's this? → It's a ___.*

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (a/an + It's)
- **a** قبل صوت ساكن: *a book, a pen, a phone.*
- **an** قبل صوت متحرك: *an apple, an umbrella, an egg.*
- **What's this? → It's a/an ___.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Objects + revisit a/an (vowel **sound**, not letter). *It's* for objects.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a / an | (نكرة) | *a pen · an apple* |
| It's a/an… | ده… | *It's a phone.* |
| What's this? | ده إيه؟ | *— What's this? — It's a key.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Target: clear final consonants — no added vowel.**
انطق آخر صوت واضح: **book · bag · cup · key** — مش "booku / baga".

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *book · bag · cup · phone.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Clean final consonants (seeds the L02 cluster work). Expect "book-uh" — cue "say the last sound, then stop."
