# Plurals (and Their Sounds)

**Empire English** · Stage 0 — **Recruit** (A1) · Unit 3 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage0/unit3-my-things-and-places.md` · **S0-U3-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Notice 6 · Accent 6 · Guided 5 · Record 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** regular plurals (+ key irregulars) · plural -s sounds (/s/,/z/,/ɪz/) · count objects.
> **Core Accent Lab win:** consonant clusters WITHOUT vowel insertion ("deskuh" → desks). Cue: "say the cluster, don't break it."
> **Support:** regular +s only. **Stretch:** boxes (/ɪz/) + one irregular. **Arabic ~55%.**

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Make **plurals** (book→books, box→boxes; man→men).
- Pronounce plural **-s** as /s/, /z/, /ɪz/.
- Count objects: *two books, three chairs.*

> هتعمل الجمع وتنطق الـ -s صح، وتعدّ الحاجات.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
الجمع في كل جملة تقريبًا. ونطق التجمعات الساكنة (desks) من غير حرف علة زيادة = فرق كبير في وضوح كلامك.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة (الجمع)
- **+s** عادي: book → book**s** · pen → pen**s**
- **+es** بعد s/x/ch/sh: box → box**es** · watch → watch**es**
- **Irregulars:** man → men · woman → women · child → children

**التلات أصوات:**
- **/s/** بعد صوت مهموس: book**s**
- **/z/** بعد صوت مجهور/علة: pen**s**, chair**s**
- **/ɪz/** بعد s/x/ch/sh: box**es**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** +s/+es rule + the 3 sounds + a couple irregulars.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق ⭐
**Target: consonant clusters — no inserted vowel.**
انطق التجمع كامل: **desks · books · chairs · next** — مش "deskuh / bookuh".
- Build it: s…k…s → *desks.*

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *desks · books · chairs.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** The signature Arabic-transfer fix. Sort words by -s sound, then drill clusters. Cue: "say the cluster, don't break it."
