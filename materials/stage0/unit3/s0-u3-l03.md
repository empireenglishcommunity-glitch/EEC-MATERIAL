## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
*there is/are* بتوصف بيها أي مكان — بيتك، شغلك، المدينة. أساسية جدًا.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
> This is my room. **There is** a bed and a desk. **There are** two windows and three books.

**Furniture (gloss):** bed سرير · table ترابيزة · chair كرسي · window شباك · door باب · sofa كنبة

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
- **There is** + حاجة واحدة: *There is a bed.*
- **There are** + جمع: *There are two windows.*
- **Questions:** *Is there a…?* / *Are there any…?* → *Yes, there is / No, there isn't.*

> ⚠️ مش *"there is two chairs"* → الصح *"there **are** two chairs"*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** is (one) vs are (many) + questions. Hunt the agreement error.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| There is… | فيه (واحد) | *There is a bed.* |
| There are… | فيه (كذا) | *There are two chairs.* |
| Is there…? | فيه…؟ | *Is there a window?* |

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
- **/ð/ in *there*** (لسانك بين سنانك + صوت).
- Link **"there are"** → "there-are" بسلاسة.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *There is a bed. There are two windows.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** /ð/ in *there* + linking *there are*.
