## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** name everyday **objects**, say what **there is/are** in a place, locate things with
> **prepositions**, and describe your own room.
> مهمتك: تسمّي الحاجات، تقول إيه اللي موجود في مكان، وتحدد أماكن الأشياء، وتوصف أوضتك.

**Why now:** describing your space is the first time you paint a whole scene in English.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Everyday Objects & a/an | name objects · a/an · *What's this? → It's a/an ___* |
| **L02** | Plurals & Their Sounds ⭐ | +s/+es + irregulars · /s/,/z/,/ɪz/ · clusters (no extra vowel) |
| **L03** | There is / There are | *there is/are* + questions · describe contents |
| **L04** | Prepositions of Place | in/on/under/next to… · *Where is…? → It's ___* |
| **L05** | Describe Your Room (milestone) 🎙️ | a 5–7 sentence room description + recording |

**Grammar spine:** a/an/the · plurals · there is/are · prepositions of place.
**Accent Lab:** consonant clusters (desks/books) without vowel insertion · plural -s · /ð/ *there*.
**Mindset:** design a practice-friendly environment (your "English corner").
**Arabic support:** ~55%, easing to ~50% by L05.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **"my room" recording** (visible growth vs Day-1).
- The ability to describe any space in English.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** the **cluster / vowel-insertion** fix (L02) is the signature Accent Lab win — "say the
> cluster, don't break it." Hunt the agreement error ("there is two chairs") and "in" overuse. Recycle
> prior units in warm-ups. Log the room clip (with consent). ~4 weeks in — celebrate the growth.
