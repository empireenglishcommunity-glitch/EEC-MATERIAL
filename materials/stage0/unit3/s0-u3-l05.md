## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
> This is my room. There is a bed and a desk. On the desk, there are three books and a lamp. There are two windows. My bag is under the chair. I like my room!

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play a clean 6-sentence model.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الوصف الكويس
1. ✅ **What's in it** — *There is / There are ___.*
2. ✅ **How many** — *two windows, three books.*
3. ✅ **Where** — *on the desk · under the chair.*
4. ✅ **a/an + clear plurals.**
5. ⭐ **Extra** — a colour / *I like my room.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Break the model into these boxes; give the card.
