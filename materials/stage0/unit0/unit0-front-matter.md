## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** step into the Empire — understand how the journey works, produce your **first English
> sounds** (including the ones Arabic doesn't have), learn **survival phrases**, and count **0–20**. Confidence from minute one.
> مهمتك: تدخل الرحلة، تنطق أول أصوات إنجليزي (حتى اللي مش في العربي)، وتتعلم جمل النجاة والأرقام.

**Why it's the gateway:** before we build sentences, we set your **identity**, your **daily habit**, and your **mouth's new skills**. لبنة كل يوم.

---

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Welcome & How It Works 🎙️ | understand the journey · set your goal + daily time · record your Day-1 clip |
| **L02** | The Alphabet & Letter Sounds | say A–Z · spell your name · name vs sound |
| **L03** | Hard Sounds (1): /p/–/b/, /v/–/f/ ⭐ | hear + produce /p/ and /v/ (the Accent Lab begins) |
| **L04** | Survival Phrases | keep a conversation alive · ask to repeat · be polite |
| **L05** | Numbers 0–20 | say 0–20 · give your age + phone number · /θ/ preview |

**Accent Lab:** awareness → /p/–/b/, /v/–/f/ → /θ/ preview (three/thirteen).
**Mindset:** identity ("I'm an English speaker in training") + the daily 20-minute habit.
**Arabic support:** heavy (~70–90%) — this is the gentlest on-ramp.

---

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Day-1 recording** (the very start of your before/after proof).
- Your **first English sounds** + the confidence to stay in any conversation.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** the on-ramp. L01 = emotional safety + commitment (not language). L03 = the first Accent
> Lab (/p/, /v/) — frame it as a superpower unlock. Reply personally to Day-1 posts (retention). Capture the
> Day-1 clip (with consent) for the before/after timeline. Recycle survival phrases in every future warm-up.

- **Feeds:** Cohort Tracker `week1_active` + early pronunciation notes.
