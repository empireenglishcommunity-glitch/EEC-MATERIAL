# Reporting Advice & Requests — *asked me to…*

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 7 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01–L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit7-reported-speech.md` · **S2-U7-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 5 · Train 8 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** report requests, orders and advice with *verb + person + to* · use *not to* for negatives · choose the reporting verb that carries the right force.
> **This pattern is easier than backshift and just as useful — say so.** There is no tense to shift: *"Close the door"* becomes *"He asked me **to** close the door."* Learners who found L02 hard get a confidence win here, so schedule it as a lift.
> **The negative is *not to*, and it goes before *to*:** *"He told me **not to** worry."* ❌ *"He told me to not worry"* is heard but non-standard; ❌ *"He didn't tell me to worry"* means something else entirely.
> **The reporting verb carries the force.** *Asked* is polite, *told* is firm, *advised* is a recommendation, *warned* is a danger. Same structure, very different social meaning — that is the real content of the lesson.
> **Support:** the reporting-verb strength card. **Stretch:** report four instructions with four different verbs.
> **Arabic ~12%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Most of what people tell you is not information — it is instructions, requests and advice. This one pattern
reports all of it, and it is more reliable than backshift because nothing has to be transformed.

> والفعل بيوصل القوة: **asked** مؤدب، و**told** أقوى.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Reporting a day of instructions (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** How was the first day?
> **B:** Fine, but everyone told me something different. My manager asked me **to** shadow Layla for a week.
> **A:** That's reasonable.
> **B:** Then IT told me **not to** install anything myself. And Layla advised me **to** write everything down,
> which was actually the best advice I got.
> **A:** Anything else?
> **B:** Someone warned me **not to** use the third-floor kitchen. Nobody explained why.
> **A:** That's ominous.
> **B:** And my manager reminded me **to** finish the online training by Friday.

**Notice:** five different reporting verbs, one structure — **verb + person + to + base verb**. The negative simply
adds *not* before *to*.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| He asked me to wait. | طلب مني أستنى |
| They told me not to install it. | قالولي ماأنصبهوش |
| She advised me to write it down. | نصحتني أكتبها |
| Someone warned me not to… | حد حذرني ما… |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners list the five verbs and rank them by force. That ranking is the
> lesson's social content.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**One structure: *verb + person + to + base verb*.**

> *He **asked me to** wait.* · *She **told him to** stop.* · *They **advised us to** apply.*

**1) The negative puts *not* before *to*:**

> *He told me **not to** worry.* ✅
> *He told me to not worry.* ❌ non-standard
> *He didn't tell me to worry.* ❌ different meaning — he said nothing

> ⚠️ **خد بالك:** النفي **not to** بييجي قبل **to**.

**2) The verb chooses the force.**

| Verb | Force | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **asked** | polite request | *She asked me to help.* |
| **told** | firm, or an order | *He told me to wait.* |
| **advised** | a recommendation | *She advised me to rest.* |
| **warned** | danger | *He warned me not to go.* |
| **reminded** | you already knew | *She reminded me to call.* |
| **invited** | an offer | *They invited me to join.* |

**3) All of them need a person.** Like *tell*, never *say*:

> *He asked **me** to wait.* ✅ · *He asked to wait.* — means **he** wanted to wait

**4) Compare with the frames you already have:**

| | |
|---|---|
| *He said he was tired.* | a statement |
| *He asked me if I was tired.* | a question |
| *He asked me **to** rest.* | a request |

**5) Direct commands report naturally here.** *"Sit down!"* → *She told me **to** sit down.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Two minutes on the structure, two on *not to*, two on the verb-force table. Drill by giving a
> direct instruction and asking for it reported with a specified verb.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *to* is weak: /tə/.**

> *asked me to wait* → *askt-mi-**tə**-wait*

Said as /tuː/ it sounds careful and slow. It is a grammar word, so it reduces.

**Part 2 — the whole frame is one run**, and the stress lands on the action:

> *He asked me to **WAIT**.* · *She told me not to **WORRY**.*

**Part 3 — but *not* takes a beat**, because it reverses the instruction:

> *She told me **NOT** to worry.*

Learners who swallow *not* report the opposite of what they were told, which matters more here than in most
structures.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"She asked me to wait, and she told me not to worry."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on weak /tə/, two on the stressed *not*, one on the whole frame as a single run. The
> stressed *not* is a meaning-protecting habit.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — report it.**
1. *"Please wait here."* → He asked me __________________________
2. *"Don't touch that."* → She told me __________________________
3. *"You should rest."* → The doctor advised me __________________________
4. *"Don't forget to call."* → She reminded me __________________________
5. *"Come to dinner."* → They invited me __________________________

**Exercise 2 — choose the verb.** *asked · told · warned · advised · reminded*
1. My mother ______ me not to walk home alone at night.
2. My boss ______ me to finish by five. (firm)
3. My friend ______ me to try that restaurant. (recommendation)
4. She ______ me to bring my passport. (I already knew)

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *He asked me wait.* → __________________________
2. *She told me to not worry.* → __________________________
3. *He asked to me to help.* → __________________________
4. *She said me to sit down.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — report your day.** Four things people asked or told you to do recently — use four different verbs,
and make one negative.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **verb + person + to + base verb.** No tense shift at all.
- Negative: ***not to*** — *He told me **not to** worry.*
- The verb carries the force: *asked · told · advised · warned · reminded · invited*.
- They all need a **person**: *asked **me** to…*
- *to* is weak /tə/, but ***not*** takes a beat.
