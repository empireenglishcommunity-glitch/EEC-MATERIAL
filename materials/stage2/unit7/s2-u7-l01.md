# *say* vs *tell*

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 7 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Unit 6
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit7-reported-speech.md` · **S2-U7-L01***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 7 · Accent Lab 5 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** choose between *say* and *tell* · use *tell* with a person and *say* without one · build reporting frames fluently.
> **Two classic errors, and they are mirror images:** ❌ *"She said me"* and ❌ *"She told that…"*. One rule fixes both — ***tell* needs a person; *say* does not.** Drill them as a pair, never separately.
> **This is the foundation of the whole unit.** Every lesson from here builds on a reporting frame, so a shaky *say/tell* choice will contaminate L02–L05. Spend the time now.
> **Teach *that* as optional.** *"She said (that) she was tired"* — both are correct, and dropping it is more natural in speech. Learners who think it is required sound stilted.
> **Support:** the two-column frame card. **Stretch:** report three things using a different reporting verb each time.
> **Arabic ~12%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Half of ordinary conversation is repeating what somebody else said — a colleague, a doctor, a message from your
mother. Without reporting verbs you can only quote directly, which sounds childish and quickly becomes impossible
in a long story.

> والغلطتين المشهورتين: حد يقول *she said me*، وحد يقول *she told that*.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Passing on messages (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Did you speak to Mona?
> **B:** Yes. She **said** she'd be late.
> **A:** Did she **say** why?
> **B:** She **told me** the metro was closed. And she **told** everyone the meeting is moving to three.
> **A:** She didn't **tell me** that.
> **B:** She **said** she'd message the group. Actually — she **told** me to remind you.
> **A:** Well, now you have.

**Notice the pattern.** *Said* appears alone. *Told* always has a person straight after it — *told **me**, told
**everyone***. Not once is it the other way round.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| She said she'd be late. | قالت إنها هتتأخر |
| She told me the metro was closed. | قالتلي المترو مقفول |
| Did she say why? | قالت السبب؟ |
| She didn't tell me. | ماقالتليش |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners circle the word right after every *told*. It is always a person —
> let them find the rule.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**One rule.**

| Verb | Needs a person? | Frame |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | no | *She **said** (that) she was tired.* |
| **tell** | **yes** | *She **told me** (that) she was tired.* |

> ⚠️ **الغلطتين:** *She said me* غلط لأن **say** مش بتاخد شخص. و*She told that* غلط لأن **tell** لازم شخص قبل الجملة.

**1) The correct four:**

> *She **said** she was busy.* ✅
> *She **told me** she was busy.* ✅
> *She said me she was busy.* ❌
> *She told she was busy.* ❌

**2) *that* is optional** — and usually dropped in speech:

> *He said **(that)** he'd call.* · *He told me **(that)** he'd call.*

**3) Fixed expressions with *tell*** that do not follow the pattern — learn them whole:

> *tell a story · tell the truth · tell a lie · tell the time · tell the difference*

**4) Fixed expressions with *say*:**

> *say sorry · say hello · say goodbye · say a word · say nothing*

**5) The useful negative and question forms:**

> *She **didn't tell** me.* · ***Did** she **say** why?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** Three minutes on the rule and the correct-four contrast, two on optional *that*, two on the fixed
> expressions. Drill orally: give a sentence with one verb, ask for the same meaning with the other.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| She said (that)… | قالت إن… | *She said she was tired.* |
| He told me (that)… | قالّي إن… | *He told me he was busy.* |
| Did she say why? | قالت ليه؟ | *Did she say why?* |
| She didn't tell me. | ماقالتليش | *She didn't tell me anything.* |
| tell the truth | يقول الحق | *Tell me the truth.* |
| tell a story | يحكي حكاية | *He told us a story.* |
| say sorry | يعتذر | *He didn't say sorry.* |
| according to… | على حسب كلام… | *According to Mona, it's closed.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *that* is weak, when you use it at all.**

> *that* → /ðət/ — quick, unstressed, the vowel almost gone

> *She said thət she was tired.*

**Part 2 — *told me* links into one shape:**

> *told me* → *tol-**dmee*** — the /d/ runs straight into the /m/
> *told him* → *tol-**dim*** — the /h/ usually disappears entirely

That /h/ dropping is worth hearing: *"told him"* really does sound like *"toldim"* in natural speech.

**Part 3 — the reporting frame is unstressed; the message carries the beat.**

> *She told me the metro was **CLOSED**.*

If learners stress *told me*, the sentence sounds like an accusation rather than information.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"She told me she'd be late, but she didn't say why."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on weak *that*, two on the *told me/told him* linking, one on frame stress. The
> /h/-dropping is a listening gain as much as a speaking one.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — *say* or *tell*?**
1. She ______ me she was tired.
2. He ______ he would call later.
3. Did they ______ you the price?
4. Nobody ______ anything.
5. Please ______ me the truth.
6. He didn't ______ sorry.

**Exercise 2 — rewrite with the other verb.**
1. *She said she was leaving.* → (tell + me) __________________________
2. *He told me he'd finished.* → (say) __________________________

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *She said me she was busy.* → __________________________
2. *He told that he was late.* → __________________________
3. *She told to me the answer.* → __________________________
4. *Can you say me the time?* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — pass it on.** Report three real things people said to you this week — use *say* twice and *tell*
once.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** told · said · tell · said · tell · say. **Ex3:** *told me* · *told me that* · drop *to* ·
> *tell me the time*. Ex3.4 is a good one — *tell the time* is a fixed expression, so *say* is impossible.
