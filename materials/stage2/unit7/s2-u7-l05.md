# Unit Speaking Task: "Report a Conversation" (+ Review)

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 7 · **Lesson 5** — *UNIT MILESTONE*
⏱️ ~40 min · 📹 Recorded + live (assessment-style) · Prerequisite: L01–L04
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit7-reported-speech.md` · **S2-U7-L05***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~40 min):** Warm-up 5 · Model 4 · Checklist 4 · Accent clinic 5 · Rehearse 8 · Record 5 · Feedback 3 · Assign 1.
> **Objectives:** report a whole conversation — statements, questions and requests — in 8–10 sentences · keep the reporting frames varied · record the Unit-7 talk.
> **Backshift will not be flawless, and that is acceptable at B1.** Score whether the conversation is **followable**: who said what, in what order, with the right force. A retained present tense where the fact is still true is not an error.
> **The pass/fail question is word order in reported questions.** That one is genuinely wrong when it is wrong, and it is the item to note for the queue.
> **Run the passing-a-message game if time allows.** Keep it light — a message passed along three people and then compared with the original makes the whole unit feel purposeful in ninety seconds.
> **Support:** 8 sentences with the checklist. **Stretch:** report a disagreement, with both sides' words and the right reporting verbs.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: a *said* sentence → a *told me* sentence → a reported yes/no question → a reported *wh-* question →
an *asked me to* → a *not to*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One each, fast, no repeats. This sweeps all four lessons before the load rises.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model reported conversation (📹 in the portal).

> I had a strange call from my landlord yesterday. He **said** the building **was** being inspected next month and
> he **told me** the inspectors **would** need access to every flat.
>
> Then he **asked me if I was** planning to renew the contract, which surprised me, because I'd only moved in in
> March. I **asked him whether** there **was** a problem and he **said** there **wasn't** — he just **wanted** to
> know early.
>
> He **asked me to** send him a copy of my ID again, and **told me not to** worry about the inspection. He also
> **reminded me to** pay the water bill separately, which I had completely forgotten.
>
> I **asked him when** the inspection **would** be, and he **said** he **didn't** know yet. So the whole call was
> mostly him asking me things.

**Notice the variety.** Statements with *said* and *told me*, two reported questions in **statement order**, three
*to*-patterns including a *not to*, and appropriate verbs throughout — *reminded* for the bill, not *told*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for content, once for structures. Ask learners to count the reported questions and check
> the word order in each.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست المحادثة
1. ✅ **Two or more** reported statements — *said* / *told me*
2. ✅ One reported **yes/no** question — *if* or *whether*
3. ✅ One reported ***wh-*** question, in **statement order**
4. ✅ One ***asked/told me to***…
5. ✅ One ***not to***
6. ✅ **Tenses shifted** where the fact has changed
7. ⭐ A reporting verb chosen for its **force** — *advised · warned · reminded*
8. ⭐ Answer one question about the conversation

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Item 3 is the accuracy discriminator; item 7 is the range one.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **weak *that*** → /ðət/, or dropped entirely
- ***told me / told him*** linking — and the disappearing /h/
- **/æskt/** — *asked*, one syllable, no inserted vowel
- **falling tune** on a reported question
- **weak *to*** → /tə/, with a **stressed *not***
- **unstressed frames** — the message carries the beat

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One personal target each. /æskt/ and the falling tune are the two that most affect
> intelligibility here.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
Rehearse against the checklist with a partner, then record.

Report **a real conversation** you had recently, in **8–10 sentences**. Include statements, at least two questions,
one request or instruction, and one negative instruction. Then answer one question about it.

> 🎙️ **Record it and keep it.** 👑

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min recording + 3 feedback.** Ask your follow-up unscripted — *"What did you say back?"* — which pushes one
> more reporting frame out of them.
> **Success check (Unit-7):** a followable 8–10 sentence report with correct *say/tell* choice, **statement order
> in every reported question**, at least one accurate *to*-pattern with a *not to*, and the main tense shifts
> applied (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Range at B1). Do not penalise a retained present where the fact is still
> true.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 7 wrap)
- ***tell* needs a person; *say* does not.** ❌ *said me* · ❌ *told that*
- Reporting moves the tense **one step back**: *is→was · will→would · can→could*.
- **Pronouns** and **time words** shift too.
- **A reported question is a statement** — no inversion, no *do*, no question mark.
- Yes/no → *if / whether*; *wh-* → keep the word, then statement order.
- Requests: ***verb + person + to + base***, negative ***not to***.
- The reporting verb carries the **force**.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 7 mini-quiz
1. *She ___ me she was tired.* → **told**
2. *He ___ he was busy.* → **said**
3. *"I'm ready"* → *He said he ___ ready.* → **was**
4. *"I'll call"* → *She said she ___ call.* → **would**
5. *"Can you swim?"* → *He asked me ___ I could swim.* → **if / whether**
6. *"Where do you live?"* → *She asked me where I ___.* → **lived**
7. *"Please wait."* → *He asked me ___ wait.* → **to**
8. *"Don't worry."* → *She told me ___ worry.* → **not to**
9. *She asked me where do I live.* → wrong; say **where I lived**
10. Verb for a warning about danger → **warned**
