## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: a *who* clause → a *which* clause → a *where* clause → a *whose* clause → one clause with the pronoun
dropped.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One each, fast, no repeats. The fifth item checks L03, which learners often avoid under pressure.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model description (📹 in the portal).

> The person I want to describe is my aunt Hoda, **who** raised four children on a teacher's salary and still
> found time to argue with everyone about politics. She's the kind of person **who** notices when you've gone
> quiet. She lives in the flat **where** my father grew up, in a building **whose** lift has been broken since
> roughly 2009.
>
> The place I'd choose is a café **that** used to be a bookshop, about ten minutes from her flat. It's the place
> **where** I did most of my studying, mainly because it's the only one nearby **that** has decent light and
> doesn't play music. The owner is a man **whose** memory is unbelievable — he still remembers the order I used to
> have three years ago.
>
> The thing I like most about both of them is the same thing: they pay attention.

**Notice:** six relative clauses, one reduced clause (*the order I used to have*), and every sentence still easy
to follow — because each clause is grouped rather than crammed.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for content, once to count clauses. Ask why it does not feel complicated: the answer is
> grouping and a maximum of two clauses per sentence.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الوصف
1. ✅ Describe **one person** and **one place**
2. ✅ Use **who** and **which** or **that**
3. ✅ Use **where**
4. ✅ Use **whose**
5. ✅ One clause with the **pronoun dropped** — *the thing I like most*
6. ✅ Clauses placed **right after** their noun, with no doubled subject
7. ⭐ One sentence with **two** clauses — grouped, not crammed
8. ⭐ Answer one question about your description

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Item 5 is the fluency marker; item 6 is the accuracy one.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **clause grouping** — one group per clause, each kept fast
- **unstressed pronouns** — *who · that · which* are signposts, not content
- **/huːz/** — *whose*, with a voiced ending
- **/wer/** — *where*, with a real /w/
- **no phantom pause** where a dropped pronoun used to be

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One personal target each. Grouping is the priority: it is what makes the added complexity sound
> effortless instead of laboured.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 6 wrap)
- **who** people · **which** things · **that** both · **where** places · **whose** possession.
- The pronoun **replaces** the subject — never add another one.
- Clause goes **right after** its noun; defining clauses take **no commas**.
- Drop the pronoun when **another subject follows** it — and keeping it is never wrong.
- **where** already means *in which*; **whose** takes a bare noun.
- Group long sentences instead of slowing them.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 6 mini-quiz
1. *the woman ___ called me* → **who / that**
2. *the book ___ I read* → **which / that** (or nothing)
3. *the café ___ we met* → **where**
4. *the man ___ car broke down* → **whose**
5. *A vet is a person who ___ treats animals.* → wrong; **drop *he***
6. *the town where I live ___* → wrong; **drop *in***
7. Can you drop the pronoun in *the film that I saw*? → **yes**
8. Can you drop it in *the film that won*? → **no**
9. *the place which I was born* → wrong; say **where I was born**
10. Maximum comfortable clauses in one sentence → **two**
