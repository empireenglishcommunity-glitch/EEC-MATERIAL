# Future Time Clauses: *when · as soon as · before · after · until*

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 4 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit4-first-conditional.md` · **S2-U4-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 7 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 8 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** use *when · as soon as · before · after · until* + present to refer to the future · sequence future events · distinguish *if* from *when*.
> **This is L01's rule with a different word in front.** Teach them together explicitly: after these conjunctions the verb is **present**, and *will* lives in the main clause. Presenting it as a second, unrelated rule wastes the transfer.
> **The genuinely new content is *if* against *when*.** *If* = it may happen. *When* = it definitely will, we just do not know the moment. ❌ *"If I graduate"* said by a final-year student changes the meaning to doubt, which is not what they intend.
> ***Until* needs care:** it means *up to that point*, and it pairs naturally with a negative main clause — *"I won't leave until she arrives."*
> **Support:** the conjunction card + the same two-clause frame as L01. **Stretch:** chain three future events in sequence.
> **Arabic ~15%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Plans are rarely a single step. They are ordered: *after I finish, before I leave, as soon as they reply*. These
words let you lay out a sequence in the future — and the tense rule you already learned yesterday carries all of
them.

> والفرق: **if** يمكن تحصل، و**when** هتحصل بالتأكيد.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Planning an evening (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** **As soon as** I **finish** work, I**'ll come** straight over.
> **B:** Good. **When** you **get** here, we**'ll order** food.
> **A:** **Before** I **leave** the office, I**'ll send** you the address.
> **B:** And **after** we **eat**, we can watch that film you mentioned.
> **A:** I **won't** be able to stay late — I have an early start.
> **B:** That's fine. We **won't** start the film **until** you**'re** ready to go, then we'll skip it.

**Notice:** *finish · get · leave · eat · are* — every verb after the time word is **present**, and every future
is in the other half. Exactly like *if*.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| As soon as I finish… | أول ما أخلّص… |
| When you get here… | لما توصل… |
| Before I leave… | قبل ما أمشي… |
| I won't start until… | مش هبدأ لحد ما… |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners list the five time words and the verb after each. The pattern
> should now feel familiar rather than new.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Same rule as L01: after the time word, use the *present*.**

| Time word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **when** | at that time (it will happen) | *When I **get** home, I**'ll call** you.* |
| **as soon as** | immediately after | *As soon as it **stops**, we**'ll go**.* |
| **before** | earlier than | *I**'ll finish** before he **arrives**.* |
| **after** | later than | *After I **eat**, I**'ll study**.* |
| **until** | up to that point | *I **won't** go until she **calls**.* |

> ⚠️ **نفس القاعدة:** بعد كلمة الوقت مضارع، والمستقبل في النص التاني. ماتقولش *when I will finish*.

**1) *if* or *when*? The difference is certainty.**

| | Means |
|---|---|
| ***If** I pass the exam…* | I might pass — it is uncertain |
| ***When** I pass the exam…* | I will pass — only the timing is open |

A final-year student says *"**when** I graduate"*. Saying *"if I graduate"* tells the listener you have doubts.

**2) *Until* likes a negative main clause.**

> *I **won't** leave **until** he arrives.* — I stay up to that point
> *Wait here **until** I **get** back.*

**3) Clause order flips, and the comma follows the same rule as L01:**

> ***When** I get home,* *I'll call you.* = *I'll call you ***when** I get home*.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** Three minutes confirming the shared rule, three on *if* against *when* — the real new content —
> and one on *until*. The certainty contrast is worth a quick round of situations.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *as soon as* is one squeezed chunk.**

> **as soon as** → /əˈsuːnəz/ — *"uh-**SOON**-uz"*

The stress sits on *soon*; the two *as* words almost vanish.

**Part 2 — the time clause runs on, then a break, then the future half.**

> *As soon as I **FINISH*** ⟋ *I**'ll COME** over.*

**Part 3 — protect the *'ll* and the *won't*.** In a negative, *won't* takes a clear beat — it is the whole
meaning:

> *I **WON'T** go until she calls.*

> ولاحظ إن **won't** بتاخد نبرة واضحة، لأنها هي اللي شايلة المعنى.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"As soon as I finish, I'll call you — but I won't leave until he arrives."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Two minutes on */əˈsuːnəz/*, two on the clause break and the stressed *won't*.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — put the verbs in the right form.**
1. I ______ (call) you when I ______ (arrive).
2. As soon as she ______ (finish), we ______ (start).
3. We ______ (not / leave) until it ______ (stop) raining.
4. After I ______ (get) paid, I ______ (buy) it.

**Exercise 2 — *if* or *when*?**
1. ______ I finish university next year, I'll look for a job. (certain)
2. ______ it snows tomorrow, school will close. (uncertain)
3. ______ the sun sets, it gets cold. (certain, every day)
4. ______ he calls, tell him I'm out. (uncertain)

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *When I will finish, I'll call you.* → __________________________
2. *I'll wait until you will come.* → __________________________
3. *As soon as I will arrive, I'll text.* → __________________________
4. *I'll leave until he arrives.* (meaning: stay till then) → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — chain it.** Three steps of one real plan, using *before*, *after* and *as soon as*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min.** **Ex1:** 'll call/arrive · finishes/'ll start · won't leave/stops · get/'ll buy. **Ex2:** When ·
> If · When · If. **Ex3:** drop each *will* in the time clause; 4) *I **won't** leave until he arrives.*

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- After **when · as soon as · before · after · until** → **present**, never *will*.
- *will* belongs in the **other** half.
- **if** = it may happen · **when** = it will, timing unknown.
- **until** = up to that point, and it likes *won't*.
- **as soon as** → /əˈsuːnəz/, one chunk.
