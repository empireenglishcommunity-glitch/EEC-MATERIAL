# Past Participles: the Third Column

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 1 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01 · Stage 1 irregular pasts
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit1-present-perfect-experiences.md` · **S2-U1-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 8 · Accent Lab 5 · Train 7 · Your Turn 3 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** form regular participles (*-ed*) and the high-frequency irregulars · separate past simple from participle where they differ · use participles accurately inside the present perfect.
> **The top error in the whole unit is ❌ *"I've saw" / "I've went"*** — the past form used where the participle belongs. It is not carelessness: at A2 the second column *was* the past, and nothing needed a third.
> **The three-column table is the anchor of two units.** It powers the present perfect now and **the passive in U8**. Time spent drilling column three here is paid back twice.
> **Do not teach the whole irregular list.** Eleven high-frequency participles carry almost every sentence a B1 learner needs. A long list produces recognition, not production.
> **Support:** the three-column card stays on the desk. **Stretch:** a short paragraph using five participles.
> **Arabic ~15%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
The present perfect you learned in L01 is only as good as the participle you put in it. Get the auxiliary right
and the participle wrong, and the sentence still fails. This is also the form the **passive** will need later,
so it is worth over-learning now.

> الزمن الجديد محتاج عمود تالت.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A short experience text (📹 in the portal).

> I**'ve worked** in three countries and I**'ve lived** in two. I**'ve seen** things I never expected — I**'ve
> eaten** street food in Bangkok, I**'ve driven** through the desert at night, and I**'ve met** people who
> changed how I think. I**'ve never written** about any of it, and I**'ve never taken** a single photograph.

**Notice two groups.** *Worked · lived* end in **-ed** — the same as their past. But *seen · eaten · driven ·
written · taken* are **not** *saw · ate · drove · wrote · took*. Those verbs have a genuinely different third
form.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I've worked there. | اشتغلت هناك |
| I've seen it. | شفته |
| I've eaten it. | أكلته |
| I've never taken one. | عمري ما خدت واحدة |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Learners sort the participles into "same as the past" and "different from the past". The sort is
> the lesson; the table then confirms what they found.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**1) Regular verbs: the participle is just *-ed*.** Identical to the past simple, so nothing new to learn.

| Base | Past | Participle |
|---|---|---|
| work | worked | **worked** |
| visit | visited | **visited** |
| study | studied | **studied** |

**2) Irregular verbs have three columns, and column three is the new one.**

| Base | Past | **Participle** |
|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | **been** |
| go | went | **gone** |
| do | did | **done** |
| see | saw | **seen** |
| eat | ate | **eaten** |
| have | had | **had** |
| make | made | **made** |
| take | took | **taken** |
| meet | met | **met** |
| write | wrote | **written** |
| give | gave | **given** |

**3) Notice the patterns — they reduce the memory load.**

| Pattern | Verbs |
|---|---|
| all three the same | *cut · put · let* |
| past = participle | *had · made · met · bought · found* |
| participle ends **-en / -n** | *eaten · taken · written · given · seen · gone* |

> ⚠️ **أشهر غلطة في الوحدة:** إنك تحط العمود التاني مكان التالت.

**Where each column lives:**

> **Column 2** stands alone: *I **saw** it yesterday.*
> **Column 3** needs an auxiliary: *I**'ve seen** it.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min.** Drill column three orally, fast, in isolation — base → past → participle, chorally. Then put the
> participles straight into present perfect frames. The pattern table matters: learners who see *-en* stop
> guessing.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — the three *-ed* sounds return.** They behave on participles exactly as they did on the past simple:

| /t/ | /d/ | /ɪd/ |
|---|---|---|
| work**ed** · watch**ed** | liv**ed** · stay**ed** | visit**ed** · want**ed** |

**Part 2 — the *-en* participles need a clear final syllable.** It is unstressed and easy to swallow, but it is
the only thing marking the form:

> *eat-**en*** /ˈiːtən/ · *tak-**en*** /ˈteɪkən/ · *writ-**ten*** /ˈrɪtən/ · *giv-**en*** /ˈɡɪvən/

Say *taken* and count two syllables. If you hear one, the participle has collapsed into *take*.

**Part 3 — put it together with the contraction from L01:**

> *I've taken* → *I-**vtaken*** · *She's written* → *She-**zwritten***

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I've seen it, I've eaten there, and I've written about it."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on *-ed* recall, three on the *-en* endings, which are the genuinely new articulation
> problem in this lesson.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — give the participle.**
1. see → ______ 2. go → ______ 3. eat → ______ 4. take → ______
5. write → ______ 6. work → ______ 7. do → ______ 8. give → ______

**Exercise 2 — past simple or participle?**
1. I ______ (see) him yesterday.
2. I've ______ (see) that film twice.
3. She ______ (write) to me last week.
4. She's ______ (write) three books.

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *I've saw that film.* → __________________________
2. *He's went home.* → __________________________
3. *We've eat already.* → __________________________
4. *They've took my seat.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — true for you.** Three experiences, each using an **irregular** participle.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** seen · gone · eaten · taken · written · worked · done · given. **Ex2:** saw · seen ·
> wrote · written. **Ex3:** *seen* · *gone* · *eaten* · *taken*. Ex2 is the diagnostic — it isolates exactly
> which column the learner reaches for.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Regular participle = **-ed**, same as the past.
- Irregular verbs have **three** forms; the third one follows the auxiliary.
- **Column 2 alone · Column 3 with have/has.**
- Many participles end **-en**: *eaten · taken · written · given · seen*.
- ❌ *I've saw* → ✅ *I've **seen***.
