> **Objectives:** talk about experiences in 7–9 sentences combining present perfect, *ever/never* and *been/gone*, with one or two past-simple details · interview a partner and react · record the Unit-1 talk.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: an auxiliary + participle → an irregular participle → a *Have you ever…?* question → a *never*
sentence → a *been/gone* choice.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One item each, fast, no repeats. This retrieves all four lessons before the load rises.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model experiences talk (📹 in the portal).

> I**'ve been** lucky with travel. I**'ve visited** six countries, and I**'ve been** to Turkey three times
> because my cousin lives there. I**'ve tried** a lot of food I couldn't name — I**'ve eaten** octopus, and I
> **'ve never eaten** anything worse. The best trip was Georgia; I **went** there **in 2022** with two friends
> and we **drove** through the mountains for a week. I**'ve never been** anywhere so green. I**'ve also worked**
> abroad, in Dubai, for eight months. My brother **has gone** to Canada now, so I think I**'ll visit** him next.

**Notice the shape:** experiences in the present perfect · one *never* for contrast · **one** stretch into the
past simple, triggered by *in 2022* · a *gone* for somebody currently away · and a closing plan.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for the story, once for the structures. Ask why *went* and *drove* appear — the answer
> is *in 2022*, and that is the pivot U2 will formalise.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الحكاية
1. ✅ **Three or more experiences** in the present perfect
2. ✅ One **never** sentence
3. ✅ One ***been*** (a place you returned from)
4. ✅ One ***gone*** (somebody still away) — or a *Have you ever…?* to your partner
5. ✅ One **past-simple detail** with a time — *I went in 2022*
6. ✅ **Contractions** audible throughout — *I've · she's*
7. ⭐ Handle one **follow-up question** you did not prepare

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Item 5 is the discriminator between a solid A2 talk and a B1 one.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **contractions** — *I've · you've · he's · she's* (present, but never dropped)
- **linking** — *have-you-ever* → /həvjuˈevər/ · *I've eaten* → *I-veaten*
- ***-en* participles** — *eaten · taken · written · given*
- ***-ed* participles** — /t/, /d/, /ɪd/
- **been /ɪ/ against gone /ɒ/**

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One personal target each, taken from your L01–L04 notes. The dropped auxiliary is the priority if
> it is still happening — it changes the tense, and nothing else on this list does.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 1 wrap)
- **have / has + past participle** — and *has* only for *he · she · it*.
- Column three is the participle: *seen · gone · eaten · taken · written*.
- ***ever*** in questions, ***never*** in negatives, both between auxiliary and participle.
- **been** = came back · **gone** = still away.
- **No specific time.** Name a time and switch to the past simple.

---

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 1 mini-quiz
1. *She ___ visited Rome.* → **has**
2. Participle of *see* → **seen**
3. Participle of *take* → **taken**
4. *Have you ever ___ to Japan?* → **been**
5. *I ___ never tried it.* → **have**
6. *He's ___ to the shop — he'll be back.* → **gone**
7. *I've been to Paris ___ 2019.* → wrong; say **I went to Paris in 2019**
8. Short answer to *Have you eaten?* (no) → **No, I haven't.**
