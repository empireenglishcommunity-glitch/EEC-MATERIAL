# Unit Speaking Task: "Discuss a Topic With a Partner" (+ Review)

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 10 · **Lesson 5** — *UNIT MILESTONE*
⏱️ ~40 min · 📹 Recorded + live (assessment-style) · Prerequisite: L01–L04
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit10-discussion-skills.md` · **S2-U10-L05***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~40 min):** Warm-up 5 · Model 4 · Checklist 4 · Accent clinic 4 · Rehearse 7 · Record 8 · Feedback 6 · Assign 2.
> **This is the closest task to the B1 summative, so prioritise interaction over accuracy.** Turn-taking, responding and hedging are the scored behaviours here. A grammatically cleaner speaker who does not respond to their partner is performing worse on this rubric, not better.
> **It is a flagship confidence moment — treat it as one.** Two learners sustaining three or four minutes of unscripted English is the clearest evidence the programme works. Name it.
> **Record the pair, not the individual.** The evidence is the exchange. Score each learner on their contribution to it.
> **Both failure modes need managing:** the dominant speaker and the silent one. If one partner is much stronger, give the quieter learner the summariser role — it guarantees them a substantial turn.
> **Support:** all three phrase cards plus the checklist. **Stretch:** sustain beyond four minutes and reach a genuine conclusion rather than a stalemate.
> **Arabic ~5%** — English-only run.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: an agreement + addition → a polite disagreement + reason → a hedge → a turn-taking phrase → a
summarising phrase.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One each, fast, no repeats. This sweeps all four lessons before the unscripted run.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model pair discussion (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Shall I start? So, should employers be allowed to monitor staff who work from home? **Personally**, I think
> no — **because** it destroys trust, and **in my experience** trust is what actually makes people work.
> **B:** **I see your point**, **but** I'd say some monitoring is reasonable. **If** you're paying someone for eight
> hours, **you'd** expect eight hours.
> **A:** **That's a fair point.** **Although** I'd argue you should measure the work, not the hours.
> **B:** **Hmm — I suppose it depends on** the job. **For** creative work, **probably** output. **For** a support
> desk, **maybe** hours matter more.
> **A:** **Exactly**, **and what's more**, the monitoring software I've seen is genuinely awful — it takes
> screenshots.
> **B:** **Can I just add something?** That **was** the part that surprised me too. **As far as I know**, most of it
> isn't even legal here.
> **A:** **Really?** I didn't know that.
> **B:** **I could be wrong**, but I think there are rules about it.
> **A:** **So, to sum up** — we agree monitoring hours is the wrong measure, **although** we disagree about whether
> any monitoring is fair.
> **B:** **More or less**, yes. Good place to stop.

**Notice everything from the unit working at once:** agreement with addition, polite disagreement with a reason,
hedges buying time, a scope limit, an interruption, a correction accepted gracefully, and a summary at the end. Also
notice the **conditionals from U4–U5** and the **passive** appearing naturally — that is B1 range.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for the argument, once for the machinery. Ask which speaker they would rather work with —
> the answer is usually B, who hedges and brings things in.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست المناقشة
1. ✅ Open with an **opinion + reason**
2. ✅ **Agree** with your partner and **add** something new
3. ✅ **Disagree** politely, with a reason
4. ✅ **Hedge** at least twice
5. ✅ **Limit the scope** once — *in my experience · as far as I know*
6. ✅ Use one **turn-taking** phrase
7. ✅ **Summarise** at the end
8. ⭐ Stay in English for the whole discussion
9. ⭐ Reach an actual **conclusion**, not a stalemate

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Items 2 and 8 are the discriminators: adding proves listening, and
> staying in English proves independence.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **warm agreement** — *Exactly* high-then-falling, never flat
- **the softening rise** on disagreement — *I'm not sure I agree* ↗️
- **unstressed *but*** — heavy *but* sounds combative
- **unstressed hedges** — *I spose · kinda · sorta*
- **interrupt higher, not louder**
- **the falling turn-end** ↘️ — it is how you hand over the floor

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** One personal target each. Flat agreement and a harsh *but* are the two that most damage how a learner
> is perceived, so prioritise those.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
Rehearse briefly, then record with your partner.

Discuss **one question for three to four minutes**. Hit every item on the checklist. Stay in English — if a word is
missing, describe it or hedge around it. Finish with a summary.

**Choose one:**
> *Should employers monitor remote staff? · Is social media worse for teenagers than for adults? · Should everyone
> learn a second language? · Is it better to rent or to buy? · Does working less make you more productive?*

> 🎙️ **Record the discussion and keep it.** 👑

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min recording (both pairs) + 6 feedback.** Record the **pair**. Time it — three minutes is the floor.
> **Success check (Unit-10):** a sustained three-to-four-minute discussion with opinions and reasons, genuine
> agreement **and** disagreement, at least two hedges, one turn-taking move, a summary, and no Arabic (rubric:
> **Interaction** + Fluency + Coherence at B1).
> **Say what it proves:** they have just held an unscripted conversation in a second language. That is the "cope
> independently" outcome B1 is defined by.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 10 wrap)
- **Listen → react → add.** Agreement alone closes a topic.
- Vary the strength: *Exactly · I agree · That's a good point, although…*
- Disagree in order: **acknowledge → object → reason**. Never *You're wrong*.
- Disagree with **the idea**, not the person.
- Hedges **soften · limit scope · buy time** — one or two per claim.
- Manage the turn: *Can I just add something? · Can I come back to…? · So, to sum up…*
- Interrupt **higher, not louder**; end your turn on a **fall**.
- **Never switch to Arabic.** Describe the word or hedge around it.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 10 mini-quiz
1. Strong agreement, one word → **Exactly / Absolutely**
2. Agreement that concedes → **That's a good point**
3. Add a new point → **And what's more…**
4. Softest disagreement → **I'm not sure I agree**
5. The move before every disagreement → **acknowledge (*I see your point*)**
6. *I don't agree with ___.* (idea, not person) → **that**
7. A hedge that limits scope → **in my experience / as far as I know**
8. A phrase to get into the conversation → **Can I just add something?**
9. A phrase to close it → **So, to sum up…**
10. Interrupt by getting → **higher, not louder**
