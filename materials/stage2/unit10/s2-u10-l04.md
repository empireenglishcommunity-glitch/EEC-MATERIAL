# A Short Structured Discussion / Debate

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 10 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01–L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit10-discussion-skills.md` · **S2-U10-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Model 5 · Decode 5 · Accent Lab 4 · Discussion 12 · Feedback 3 · Assign 2.
> **This is the summative format in miniature. Keep it English-only, low-stakes, and reward participation and reactions over perfect grammar.** A learner who contributes six imperfect turns has done better than one who contributes two accurate ones.
> **Run it as a real discussion, not a performance.** Your job is to set the topic, enforce English, and then get out of the way. Resist correcting mid-flow; take notes and feed back at the end.
> **The two failure modes are silence and monologue.** Assign roles if needed — a timekeeper and someone responsible for bringing in quiet members — because turn-taking is an assessed B1 behaviour, not an optional social nicety.
> **Turn-taking has language of its own**, and learners rarely have it: interrupting politely, holding the floor, coming back to a point. That is the new content of this lesson.
> **Support:** all three phrase cards on the desk. **Stretch:** chair the discussion — bring people in, summarise at the end.
> **Arabic ~5%** — English-only run.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
Four people, one question, four minutes (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** So — should social media have a minimum age of sixteen? I think it should, mainly because of the effect on
> sleep and attention.
> **B:** **I'd agree with that**, **and what's more**, the platforms know exactly what they're doing.
> **C:** **Can I just add something?** **I see your point**, both of you, **but** I'm not convinced a ban works. Kids
> **tend to** find a way round it.
> **A:** **That's a fair point.**
> **C:** **What I mean is**, education might do more than prohibition.
> **D:** **Sorry, can I come back to** something B said? About the platforms knowing. **In my experience**, the
> design is the real problem, not the age.
> **B:** **Exactly** — **that's** what I was trying to say.
> **A:** **So, to sum up**, we mostly agree the design is the issue, but we disagree about whether a ban helps.
> **C:** **More or less**, yes.

**Notice the machinery.** Nobody waits for permission, but nobody talks over anybody either. There are phrases for
**getting in** (*Can I just add something?*), for **going back** (*Can I come back to…?*), and for **closing**
(*So, to sum up…*).

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners list every phrase used to **manage the conversation** rather than
> to state an opinion. That list is the lesson.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Discussion needs three kinds of language. You have two already.**

| Kind | From | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| stating & justifying | U9 | *In my opinion… because… for example…* |
| reacting | L01–L03 | *Exactly · I see your point, but… · I suppose…* |
| **managing the turn** | **new today** | *Can I just add something?* |

**1) Getting in politely:**

> *Can I just add something? · Can I say something here? · Sorry, just quickly — · Could I jump in?*

**2) Holding your turn** when you are not finished:

> *Just to finish the point… · If I could just finish… · One more thing —*

**3) Going back to something:**

> *Can I come back to what you said about…? · Going back to your point…*

**4) Bringing somebody in** — this is what a good participant does, and it is easy to score:

> *What do you think? · You've been quiet — any view? · Do you agree with that?*

**5) Summarising** — whoever does this sounds like the most competent person in the room:

> *So, to sum up… · So we mostly agree that… · It sounds like the real issue is…*

**6) The rule for this lesson: stay in English.** If a word is missing, describe it (U6-L04) or hedge around it
(L03). Switching to Arabic ends the discussion for everyone.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on getting in and holding, one on going back, two on bringing in and summarising. Assign
> the summariser role explicitly for the discussion.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — interrupting is done with **pitch**, not volume.** Start slightly **higher** than the speaker, not
louder:

> *Can I just add something?* ↗️ — high, quick, light

Getting louder reads as aggression. Getting higher reads as eagerness, which is welcome.

**Part 2 — the falling tune says *I have finished*.** This is the practical payoff of U9-L03:

> *…and that's why I think it matters.* ↘️

If your turn ends on a rise, nobody will take the floor, and you will keep talking past the point you meant to
stop.

**Part 3 — fillers keep the floor while you think:**

> *Well… · I mean… · You know… · The thing is…*

Said quickly and quietly, they hold your place. Said slowly, they sound like you are lost.

**Part 4 — yielding gracefully** is short and warm: *Sorry, go ahead.* ↘️

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"Can I just add something? I see your point, but in my experience it's the design."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Two minutes on higher-not-louder, two on the falling turn-end. Both are behavioural and transfer
> immediately.

## 🏋️ Train — البروفة الكاملة

**The discussion — 12 minutes, English only.**

Groups of three or four. One question. Each person must:

- give an opinion with a **reason**
- **agree** with someone and **add** a point
- **disagree** with someone politely, with a reason
- **hedge** at least once
- **bring in** another person at least once

One person is the **summariser** and closes with *"So, to sum up…"*

**Choose one question:**
> *Should social media have a minimum age? · Is remote work better for everyone? · Should university be free? ·
> Is it better to be a specialist or a generalist? · Does technology make us less patient?*

**The one rule:** if you cannot find a word, **describe it or hedge around it**. No Arabic.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~12 min.** Set the topic, enforce English, then step back. Note patterns; do not correct in flow. If somebody
> dominates, quietly hand the summariser role to the quietest person for round two.

## 🎤 Your Turn — دورك
In the discussion above, hit all five requirements. Then record a **30-second summary** of what the group decided.

> 🎙️ **Record the summary.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~3 min feedback.** **Success check:** active participation with agreement, disagreement and reasons; at least
> one hedge; one person brought in; no Arabic; and a summary that accurately reflects the discussion.

## 📜 Your Orders — مهمتك
- Post your 30-second summary.
- **Use hedges and fillers to keep going.** Freezing is the only real failure in a discussion. *"Hmm — the thing
  is…"* costs nothing and keeps you in the room.

---

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Three kinds of language: **stating · reacting · managing the turn**.
- Get in: *Can I just add something?* · Hold: *Just to finish the point…*
- Go back: *Can I come back to…?* · Bring in: *What do you think?*
- Summarise — it makes you sound like the most competent person present.
- Interrupt **higher, not louder**. End your turn on a **fall**.
- If a word is missing, **describe it or hedge**. Never switch languages.
