**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 9 · **Lesson 5** — *UNIT MILESTONE*
⏱️ ~40 min · 📹 Recorded + live (assessment-style) · Prerequisite: L01–L04

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~40 min):** Warm-up 5 · Model 4 · Checklist 4 · Accent clinic 5 · Rehearse 8 · Record 5 · Feedback 3 · Assign 1.
> **Objectives:** give and justify an opinion in a sustained turn · connect ideas with all four relations · acknowledge another view · record the Unit-9 talk.
> **This directly rehearses the B1 summative**, which is opinion-plus-discussion. Treat it as a dress rehearsal and say so — the format is the same, only shorter.
> **It is also a strong proof clip and a confidence marker.** Put it beside the learner's Stage-0 recording: from describing a daily routine to arguing a position. That is the clearest single demonstration of the whole programme.
> **Score organisation and justification above accuracy.** A well-structured, well-signposted turn with three tense slips is stronger B1 evidence than two flawless sentences.
> **Do not accept an unsupported opinion.** If the reason or the example is missing, prompt for it — that habit is the point of the unit.
> **Support:** 8 sentences with the checklist and the connector card. **Stretch:** handle a genuine counter-argument from the assessor.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: a *because* → a *so* → an *although* → a *however* → an *in addition* → one opinion frame.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One each, fast, no repeats. This sweeps the whole connector toolkit before the sustained turn.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model opinion turn (📹 in the portal).

> **In my opinion**, learning grammar rules explicitly is still worth the time, and there are **two** main reasons.
>
> **Firstly**, rules give you something to check against. **For example**, when I write an email now, I can look at
> a sentence and know why it's wrong, **so** I can fix it myself. Before, I only knew it *felt* wrong, **which**
> wasn't much use.
>
> **Secondly**, grammar is faster than waiting for exposure. **Although** children learn without rules, they get
> thousands of hours that adults simply don't have. **In addition**, most of us are learning while working full
> time.
>
> **However**, I'd admit that rules alone don't produce fluency. **I see the argument** that too much grammar makes
> people afraid to speak, and **because** I was that person for years, I take it seriously.
>
> **Overall**, I'd say grammar is necessary **but** not sufficient. You need both.

**Notice everything working together.** An opinion frame, an announced number, two signposted reasons, an example,
a concession with *however*, a relative clause from U6, and a closing summary. This is what B1 range sounds like.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for the argument, once for the machinery. Ask learners to count the connectors — there are
> more than they expect, and none of them is difficult.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الرأي
1. ✅ An **opinion frame** — *In my opinion · Personally · I'd say*
2. ✅ **Announce the number** of reasons
3. ✅ **Two signposted reasons** — *firstly · secondly*
4. ✅ At least one **example** — *for example · for instance*
5. ✅ One **contrast** — *although · however*
6. ✅ One **addition** — *also · in addition · as well as*
7. ✅ A **closing** signal — *overall · all in all*
8. ⭐ **Acknowledge** the other side — *I see your point, however…*
9. ⭐ Handle one **counter-argument** from the assessor

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Items 4 and 8 are the discriminators between a list of assertions and
> an argument.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **list intonation** — items rise ↗️, the last one falls ↘️
- **double stress on contrast** — *cheap but **slow***
- **the double pause around *however*** — full stop, *However*, comma
- **unstressed frames** — *in my opinion* is scaffolding, not news
- **protecting the reason** — it must not fade after a confident opinion
- **/bɪkəz/** and **/fərɪɡˈzɑːmpl̩/** as single chunks

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One personal target each. The falling final item is the priority — without it, learners cannot signal
> that their turn has ended, which causes real problems in U10's discussions.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
Rehearse against the checklist with a partner, then record.

Give your opinion on **one topic** in **8–10 sentences**. Announce your reasons, signpost them, include an example,
concede something with *however*, and close with a summary. Then respond to one counter-argument.

**Choose one:** *Is social media good for society? · Should university be free? · Is working from home better? · Is
it too late to learn a language as an adult?*

> 🎙️ **Record it and keep it.** 👑

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min recording + 3 feedback.** Give a **real** counter-argument after the recording — not a prompt to
> continue, an actual objection. Handling it is the interaction evidence.
> **Success check (Unit-9):** a connected 8–10 sentence argued opinion with a frame, two signposted reasons, at
> least one example, one correct contrast, one addition, and a closing (rubric: Fluency + **Interaction** + Range at
> B1). Score organisation and justification above form accuracy.
> **Pair it with the Stage-0 recording where consent allows.** Routine description to argued position is the single
> most persuasive before/after in the programme.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 9 wrap)
- **because** → cause ⬅️ · **so** → result ➡️ · **so that** → purpose.
- **but** mid-sentence · **although** starts a clause · **however** = full stop, comma.
- **also** before the verb · **as well as** + noun or *-ing* · **in addition** starts a sentence.
- **Announce the number**, signpost, then close: *overall*.
- **Opinion → reason → example** — and the example is the part people skip.
- **Hedge** to stay credible; **concede** to sound mature.
- Items rise, the last one **falls**.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 9 mini-quiz
1. *I stayed in ___ it was raining.* (cause) → **because**
2. *It was raining, ___ I stayed in.* (result) → **so**
3. *I left early ___ I could catch the train.* (purpose) → **so that**
4. *It was expensive. ___ , I bought it.* → **However**
5. *___ it was late, I stayed.* → **Although**
6. *Although it was late, but I stayed.* → wrong; **drop *but***
7. *As well as ___ (be) cheap, it's fast.* → **being**
8. *I ___ think it's better.* (also) → **also think**
9. The three parts of an argued opinion → **opinion, reason, example**
10. Intonation on the last item of a list → **falls**
