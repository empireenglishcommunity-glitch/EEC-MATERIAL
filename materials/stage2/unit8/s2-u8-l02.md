# Past Simple Passive

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 8 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit8-passive.md` · **S2-U8-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 5 · Train 8 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** form the past simple passive · describe historical events, results and finished processes · keep the participle distinct from the base verb.
> **The error to hunt is the base verb after *was/were*:** ❌ *"It was build in 1902."* Learners hear the auxiliary, know the sentence is past, and stop transforming. Anchor it: **the auxiliary carries the tense, the participle never changes.**
> **Keep contexts concrete and factual** — famous buildings, inventions, results. Abstract passives are harder and unnecessary here; the structure is best learned on things learners can picture.
> **This is the same structure as L01 with one word changed.** Say that plainly: *is* → *was*, *are* → *were*. Nothing else moves. Framing it as new grammar wastes the transfer.
> **Support:** the participle card + a timeline of dated facts. **Stretch:** a three-sentence history of something local.
> **Arabic ~12%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Most of what you know about the past came to you in the passive: buildings *were built*, laws *were passed*, things
*were invented*, *destroyed*, *discovered*. In all of it the doer is either unknown or beside the point.

> والفعل المساعد هو اللي بيشيل الزمن.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A short history (📹 in the portal).

> The bridge **was built** in 1902 and **was destroyed** twice — once by a flood and once during the war. It
> **wasn't repaired** properly until the sixties.
>
> The old station next to it **was designed** by an Italian architect whose name **was forgotten** for decades.
> Then, in 2018, the original drawings **were found** in a cupboard in the town hall. They **were sent** to a
> museum in Cairo, where they **were exhibited** last year.
>
> The station itself **was closed** in 1994. Nothing **was done** with the building for twenty years.

**Notice:** every verb is *was* or *were* plus a **participle** — *built · destroyed · repaired · designed ·
forgotten · found · sent · exhibited · closed · done*. Not one is a base verb.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| It was built in 1902. | اتبنى سنة ١٩٠٢ |
| It was destroyed twice. | اتهد مرتين |
| The drawings were found. | الرسومات اتلاقت |
| Nothing was done. | مفيش حاجة اتعملت |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners list the participles and mark any that differ from the past simple
> — *found/found*, *forgotten/forgot*, *done/did*, *sent/sent*. That mixed set is the lesson's real difficulty.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The form: *was / were* + past participle.**

| Subject | *be* | participle |
|---|---|---|
| The bridge | **was** | built |
| The drawings | **were** | found |

> *It **was built** in 1902.* · *They **were sent** to Cairo.*

**1) One word changes from L01.** Nothing else:

| | |
|---|---|
| present | *It **is** made here.* |
| past | *It **was** made here.* |

> ⚠️ **خد بالك:** التصريف التالت ثابت مايتغيرش.

**2) The error to avoid — the base verb:**

> *It **was built** in 1902.* ✅
> *It was build in 1902.* ❌
> *It was builded in 1902.* ❌

**3) Negative and question:**

> *It **wasn't finished** on time.* · ***Were** they **found** together?*

**4) Watch the participles that differ from the past simple:**

| Base | Past | **Participle** |
|---|---|---|
| do | did | **done** |
| forget | forgot | **forgotten** |
| write | wrote | **written** |
| take | took | **taken** |
| break | broke | **broken** |

*Was ___* always takes the **third** column, never the second.

**5) The reason is the same as L01** — the doer is unknown, obvious, or unimportant. Dates and results matter; the
builder usually does not.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Two minutes on the one-word change, two on the base-verb error, two on the third-column table. That
> table is where the actual work is.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *was* and *were* are weak here too:**

> *was* → /wəz/ · *were* → /wə/
> *"It wəz built in 1902."*

This is the same weak form you learned in Unit 3 for the past continuous — and the same rule applies: weak, but
never deleted.

**Part 2 — the participle takes the beat, and its ending must be audible:**

> *It was **BUILT***  · *They were **FOUND*** · *It wasn't **FINISHED***

**Part 3 — the two-consonant endings are the hard part.** *Built · found · closed · sent* all end in a cluster that
learners simplify:

> *buil**t*** /lt/ · *foun**d*** /nd/ · *close**d*** /zd/ · *sen**t*** /nt/

Drop that final consonant and the passive becomes unrecognisable.

**Part 4 — link the date on:**

> *was built in* → *wəz-buil-**tin***

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"It was built in 1902 and it wasn't repaired until the sixties."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on weak auxiliaries, three on the final clusters. The clusters matter more here than
> anywhere else in the unit, because they *are* the participle.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- ***was / were* + past participle.**
- Only the **auxiliary** changes for tense; the participle never does.
- ❌ *was build* · ❌ *was builded* → ✅ *was **built***
- *was ___* takes the **third** column: *done · forgotten · written · taken · broken*.
- Keep the final clusters: *buil**t** · foun**d** · close**d***.
