## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model process description (📹 in the portal).

> I'll explain how the bread at my uncle's bakery **is made**, because I worked there for two summers.
>
> **First**, the flour **is weighed** — always by hand, because the machine **was broken** in 2019 and it **has
> never been replaced**. **Then** the dough **is mixed** with water, salt and yeast, and it **is left** to rise for
> about two hours. In winter it **is covered** with a cloth, because the room **isn't heated**.
>
> **After that**, the dough **is divided** and **shaped**. This part **is done by** my uncle himself — he doesn't
> trust anybody else with it, and honestly the shapes **are** noticeably better when he does it.
>
> **Next**, the loaves **are baked** for twenty minutes in a stone oven **that was built** by my grandfather.
> **Finally**, they **are stacked** on wooden shelves and **sold** within about four hours. Nothing **is kept**
> overnight.

**Notice:** eleven passives, five sequencers, one past passive, and exactly **two** uses of *by* — his uncle and
his grandfather — both people who genuinely matter to the story.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for content, once for structures. Ask why *by my uncle* belongs but *by hand* is not an
> agent — a useful distinction between doer and manner.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الشرح
1. ✅ **Six or more** passive verbs
2. ✅ **Four sequencers** — *first · then · after that · next · finally*
3. ✅ *be* present in **every** passive sentence
4. ✅ Correct **participles**, including at least one irregular
5. ✅ At most **one or two** uses of *by*, each justified
6. ✅ Consistent **tense** across the description
7. ⭐ One **past** passive — something that was built, broken or replaced
8. ⭐ Answer one question about the process

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Item 3 is the accuracy discriminator; item 5 is the judgement one.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **weak auxiliaries** — *it's · they're · was · were* — present but never dropped
- **participle endings** at speed — *picked · dried · roasted · packed*
- **final clusters** — *buil**t** · foun**d** · close**d** · sen**t***
- **unstressed *by*** with a stressed agent
- **signpost · pause · step** rhythm across the whole process

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One personal target each. The participle chain is the priority: it is where a fluent-sounding process
> description is won or lost.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 8 wrap)
- ***am/is/are* + participle** (present) · ***was/were* + participle** (past).
- Never drop *be*: ❌ *Coffee grown in Brazil.*
- The auxiliary carries the tense; the **participle never changes**.
- Use the passive when the doer is **unknown, obvious, or unimportant**.
- Add ***by*** only when the doer is **surprising, specific, or important** — never *by someone*.
- ***by*** = the doer · ***with*** = the tool.
- A process = **sequencers + passives**, with a consistent tense.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 8 mini-quiz
1. *Rice ___ (grow) here.* → **is grown**
2. *These cars ___ (make) in Japan.* → **are made**
3. *The bridge ___ (build) in 1902.* → **was built**
4. *My bike ___ (steal).* → **was stolen**
5. *Coffee grown in Brazil.* → wrong; say **is grown**
6. *It was build in 1902.* → wrong; say **was built**
7. *My phone was stolen by somebody.* → wrong; **drop *by somebody***
8. *It was cut ___ a knife.* → **with**
9. *The accident was happened.* → wrong; say **happened**
10. Sequencer for the last step → **Finally**
