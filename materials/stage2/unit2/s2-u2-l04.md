# News & Recent Events — "Guess What!"

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 2 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit2-present-perfect-recent.md` · **S2-U2-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 5 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 8 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 1.
> **Objectives:** share and react to recent news · ask *What's new? / Have you heard…?* · sustain a short catching-up conversation.
> **This lesson makes L03 communicative.** The announce-then-detail chain stops being an exercise and becomes the actual shape of every piece of news anybody tells you. Score the **pattern**, not isolated sentences.
> **Reward reactions as much as accuracy.** *"Really? That's great — when did you find out?"* is B1 interaction. A learner who delivers perfect tenses to a silent partner has not done this lesson.
> **Insist on real news.** Invented news produces invented language. Anything counts: a delivery arrived, an exam finished, a phone broke.
> **The tense pattern is fixed and worth stating plainly:** the **headline** is present perfect, the **details** are past simple. It is the same hinge as L03 wearing ordinary clothes.
> **Support:** news openers + reaction cards. **Stretch:** two follow-up questions and a reaction that keeps the conversation going.
> **Arabic ~15%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Catching up is the most common conversation there is — with a colleague, a friend, a family member you have not
seen in a month. It has a fixed shape, and once you can hear that shape you can join in without preparing
anything.

> والحلو إن الشكل ثابت، فمش محتاج تجهز كلام.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two friends catching up (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Hey! **Guess what** — I**'ve got** a new job!
> **B:** **Really?** That's brilliant! **When did** you **find out**?
> **A:** They **called** me **on Tuesday**. I **start** next month.
> **B:** Congratulations. **Have you told** your family **yet**?
> **A:** I**'ve told** my mum, but I **haven't told** my dad **yet**. What about you — **what's new**?
> **B:** Not much, honestly. Oh — **have you heard** about Omar? He**'s moved** to Canada.
> **A:** No! **When did** he **leave**?
> **B:** **Two weeks ago**. It all **happened** very fast.

**Notice the rhythm.** Headline in the **present perfect** → reaction → *when?* → details in the **past
simple** → hand the turn back. Every single time.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Guess what! | تعرف إيه! |
| I've got a new job. | جالي شغل جديد |
| Have you heard about…? | سمعت عن…؟ |
| What's new? | إيه الجديد؟ |
| When did you find out? | عرفت إمتى؟ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners label each line *headline · reaction · question · detail ·
> handover*. The labels are the lesson plan for their own conversation.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**News has four moves, and the tense changes at move four.**

| Move | Language | Tense |
|---|---|---|
| 1 **Headline** | *Guess what — I've…!* · *Have you heard…?* | **present perfect** |
| 2 **React** | *Really? · No way! · That's great! · Oh no.* | — |
| 3 **Ask** | *When did…? · How did…? · What happened?* | past simple |
| 4 **Details** | *They called me on Tuesday.* | **past simple** |

**1) Openers that hand you the floor:**

> *Guess what! · You'll never believe this. · Have you heard about…? · Did you know…?*

**2) Asking for news:**

> *What's new? · How have you been? · Anything happened since I saw you?*

**3) Reactions, which are not optional.** A missing reaction reads as indifference:

| Good news | Bad news | Surprise |
|---|---|---|
| *That's great! · Congratulations!* | *Oh no. · I'm sorry to hear that.* | *Really? · No way! · Seriously?* |

> ⚠️ **خد بالك:** ردّ الفعل مش رفاهية. لو سمعت خبر وسكت، اللي قصادك يفهم إنك مش مهتم.

**4) Then the hinge from L03 does its job.** The headline has no time — so it is present perfect. The details
answer *when* — so they are past simple.

> *I**'ve passed** my exam!* → *I **got** the result **this morning**.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Keep this short — the content is a discourse pattern, not new grammar. Board the four moves and
> the reaction table, then move to practice.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — news lives on pitch.** A headline said flat is not news:

> *I've got a **NEW JOB**!* — high, and stretch the stressed word
> *Guess **what**!* ↗️ — rising, to pull the listener in

**Part 2 — reactions are almost pure intonation.** The same two words work for delight or disaster; only the
melody differs:

> *Really?* ↗️ — surprise, interested
> *Really.* ➡️ — bored, or sceptical

Say *"Really?"* three ways: thrilled, sympathetic, unimpressed. If a partner cannot tell them apart, the
reaction is not doing its job.

**Part 3 — link the announcement:**

> *I've got* → *I-**vgot*** · *He's moved* → *He-**zmoved***

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"Guess what — I've passed my exam!" — "Really? That's amazing!"*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The three-way *Really?* drill is the one to keep; it transfers to every reaction in the language.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — headline or detail?** Choose the tense.
1. *I ______ (buy) a car!* (headline)
2. *I ______ (buy) it last Thursday.* (detail)
3. *She ______ (have) a baby!* (headline)
4. *The baby ______ (arrive) on Sunday.* (detail)

**Exercise 2 — react.** Give a natural reaction plus a follow-up question.
1. *I've broken my phone.* → __________________________
2. *I've finished my thesis!* → __________________________
3. *We've sold the house.* → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *Guess what — I got a new job last week!* (as a headline) → __________________________
2. *I've told him yesterday.* → __________________________
3. *When have you found out?* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — catch up.** Tell your partner **two** pieces of **real** recent news. They must react and ask a
follow-up for each. Then swap.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min.** **Ex1:** have bought · bought · has had · arrived. **Ex3:** *I've got a new job!* · *I told him
> yesterday* · *When did you find out?* Ex4 is the assessment — listen for the four moves and for genuine
> reactions.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **Headline = present perfect. Details = past simple.**
- Four moves: **headline → react → ask → details.**
- Openers: *Guess what! · Have you heard…? · What's new?*
- A reaction is part of the conversation, not decoration.
- *When did…?* — always past simple.
