## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** by the end of Unit 2 you'll **choose the right past tense on purpose** — talk about what
> you have just done, how long things have been true, and share news the way people really share it.
> مهمتك في الوحدة دي: تعرف تختار بين الزمنين بسؤال واحد، وتتكلم عن الفترة الأخيرة في حياتك.

**Why this unit is the hinge of the stage:** Unit 1 gave you the present perfect on its own, with the time
question switched off. This unit switches it back on. The **present perfect against past simple** contrast is
the single most important tense skill at B1 — and the one that separates a fluent-sounding intermediate from a
confident A2.

> ودي الوحدة اللي بتحدد شكل كلامك عن الماضي لباقي المرحلة.

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | *just · already · yet* | mark recency — and put each word in its own slot |
| **L02** | *for · since* | say how long, and stop saying *"I live here since…"* |
| **L03** | **Perfect vs past simple** ⭐ | ask one question and pick the right tense |
| **L04** | News & recent events | headline in the perfect, details in the past |
| **L05** | "Lately" talk (milestone) 🎙️ | an 8–10 sentence catch-up + **record your milestone** |

**Grammar spine:** *just / already / yet* · *for* and *since* for unfinished time · **present perfect against
past simple** · the announce-then-detail pattern in real discourse.
**Accent Lab:** weak ***for*** /fə/ · squeezed *just / already* against final, clear *yet* · *'ve been* linking ·
news intonation · switching tense cleanly mid-turn.
**Mindset:** trust the process on the flat stretch — measure duration, not perfection.
**Arabic support:** ~15% through L01–L04, easing to ~10% at L05.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Unit-2 "lately" talk** — the most reusable conversation in the language.
- One question — *is there a specific finished time?* — that fixes the commonest intermediate error.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (L03 and L05 ~40 min, the rest ~35) plus this wrapper.
> **L01's slip is *yet* in a positive sentence.** Teach *already* and *yet* as a positive/negative pair from the
> start; taught separately, learners cross them. And position is the real grammar: *just/already* between
> auxiliary and participle, *yet* at the end. Note too that *already* carries mild surprise — used as a neutral
> past marker it makes learners sound astonished by everything.
> **L02 contains the highest-value correction in the unit.** Arabic marks an ongoing situation with the present,
> so ❌ *"I live here since 2019"* is near-universal — and being perfectly understandable, it survives for years
> uncorrected. Anchor it: **unfinished time reaching now → present perfect.** The *for/since* choice itself is
> mechanical; do not spend the lesson there.
> **L03 is the most important lesson in Stage 2, and it will not be mastered in one sitting.** Plan for that.
> The realistic outcome is one reliable self-question — *is there a specific finished time?* — plus many
> contrastive reps. Teach the **trigger, not the event**: the time expression decides. Then drill the
> announce-then-detail chain, because that is what transfers to speech. Re-drill this contrast in every
> remaining unit of the stage.
> **L04 makes L03 communicative** and should be scored as a pattern, not as sentences. Insist on real news —
> invented news produces invented language — and reward reactions as much as accuracy.
> **L05 assesses tense choice and interaction together.** Expect the contrast to be imperfect; record each
> learner's pattern for the queue instead of correcting at length in the feedback minute.
