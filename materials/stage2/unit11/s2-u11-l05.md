## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)

> **~5 min.** One each, fast. This sweeps all four lessons and both halves of the task.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model two-part performance (📹 in the portal).

> **Part one — the story.**
> **I've never** been so embarrassed at work. **About two years ago** I **was giving** a presentation to a client —
> a big one, the kind you rehearse. **I'd prepared** for a week. **While** I **was setting up**, I **realised** the
> file on my laptop **was** the old version. The one **I'd been told** to delete.
>
> **So** I **presented** the wrong numbers for about ten minutes. Nobody **said** anything. **Then** the client's
> finance director **asked**, very politely, **whether** those figures **were** current. **And I said** — **and I
> still don't know why** — *"more or less."*
>
> **In the end** we **got** the contract. **Apparently** the honesty **helped**. But **I've checked** the file name
> before every presentation since.
>
> **Part two — the topic.**
> **On presentations generally** — **in my opinion** they're mostly theatre. **Firstly**, the decision **is usually
> made** before the meeting. **As far as I know**, in my company at least, the slides are for the record.
> **Secondly**, the format rewards confidence over accuracy, **which** isn't the same thing. **Although** — **and
> this is fair** — you do have to explain your thinking to somebody eventually. **So overall**, I'd say they're
> necessary and badly done. **Not** useless. **Just** badly done.

**Notice the range.** Story: continuous scene, past events, two *had* forms, a reported question, a reflection.
Topic: opinion frame, signposts, passive, relative clause, hedges, scope limit, concession, close. That is the whole
stage, used rather than displayed.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for effect, once for inventory. Ask learners to name the structures they recognise — they
> will find most of Stage 2, which is exactly the point.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الأداء
**Part one — the story (about two minutes):**
1. ✅ A **scene** in the past continuous
2. ✅ **Events** in the past simple
3. ✅ One ***had*** + participle — what happened earlier
4. ✅ One **reflection** in the present perfect
5. ✅ One **concrete detail** — a number, a colour, a quote

**Part two — the topic (about two minutes):**
6. ✅ An **opinion frame** + two signposted reasons
7. ✅ One **anchor** — a number or personal fact
8. ✅ One **hedge** and one **scope limit**
9. ✅ One **concession** with *although* or *however*
10. ✅ A **closing** summary

**Throughout:**
11. ⭐ No Arabic, no silence — use L04's strategies
12. ⭐ Handle one unprepared question

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Items 3, 4 and 7 are the B1 discriminators; item 11 is the
> independence one.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- **the American beat** — stress content, squeeze function words
- **rhythm groups** across a long turn — group, do not slow
- **topic-word stress** — *the **COST** of living · **COMM**ute · af**FORD**able*
- ***had*** reducing to *'d* and linking
- **unhurried time-buyers**, said low
- **numbers surviving** inside a fast sentence

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** One personal target each, taken from your L01–L04 notes. The beat is the Stage-2 thread and the
> priority for anyone still speaking word by word.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 11 wrap)
- Story layers: **scene · events · earlier (*had*) · reflection**.
- **Concrete detail** makes a story visible.
- Work story: **what you do · how long · how you got here · where you're going**.
- **Anchor** every opinion claim with a number or a personal fact.
- Four anti-freeze moves: **buy time · admit and narrow · work around · land somewhere**.
- ❌ Silence. ❌ Arabic. **Group** long turns; keep the beat.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 11 mini-quiz
1. Tense for the scene of a story → **past continuous**
2. Tense for what happened **earlier** → ***had* + participle**
3. Tense for a reflection now → **present perfect**
4. *I've worked there ___ three years.* → **for**
5. *I've worked there ___ 2019.* → **since**
6. *I'm responsible ___ the schedule.* → **for**
7. *My rent ___ (go up) since 2022.* → **has gone up**
8. Stress: *commute* (noun) → **first syllable**
9. Two forbidden moves when stuck → **silence and Arabic**
10. What makes an opinion claim real → **an anchor (number or personal fact)**
