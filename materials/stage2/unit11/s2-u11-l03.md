## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
An opinion on city life (📹 in the portal).

> **In my opinion**, the biggest problem with this city isn't the traffic — it's the **cost of living**, and
> specifically **rent**.
>
> **Firstly**, the numbers. **My rent has gone up** about forty percent **since** 2022, **although** my salary
> **has gone up** maybe ten. **As far as I know**, that's fairly normal here. **So** people my age are either
> **still living** with their parents or **sharing** with three others.
>
> **Secondly**, it changes the whole city. The area I grew up in **has been** completely rebuilt — the old
> buildings **were knocked down** and **replaced** with towers nobody I know can afford.
>
> **The traffic** is bad too, obviously. **Public transport has improved** a lot **since** the new lines
> **opened** — **although** it still doesn't reach where most people actually live.
>
> **So overall**, I'd say the city **is getting** better to look at and harder to live in. **That's** the trade
> nobody agreed to.

**Notice the anchoring.** Every claim has a number, a place or a personal fact attached. And notice the grammar
working invisibly: present perfect for change, passive for the demolition, comparatives, connectors, hedges.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners find the personal anchor in each paragraph. Then note how many
> Stage-2 structures appear without being announced — that is what integration sounds like.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**No new grammar. A structure you have, plus vocabulary you need.**

**1) The shape is Unit 9's, extended:**

> *Opinion → firstly + reason + **personal anchor** → secondly + reason + anchor → concession → overall*

**2) Topic vocabulary — technology and media:**

| English | Use |
|---|---|
| *screen time · notifications · algorithm* | phones and apps |
| *scrolling · posting · following* | behaviour |
| *misinformation · clickbait* | media quality |
| *addictive · distracting · time-consuming* | judgement |

**3) City life and cost:**

| English | Use |
|---|---|
| *the cost of living · rent · bills* | money |
| *public transport · the metro · a commute* | movement |
| *traffic · congestion · pollution* | problems |
| *crowded · noisy · convenient · affordable* | judgement |

**4) The structures these topics naturally need** — all of which you already have:

> **change** → present perfect: *rent **has gone up***
> **cause** → *because · so*
> **contrast** → *although · however*
> **what was done** → passive: *the buildings **were knocked down***
> **comparison** → *more expensive than · the worst*

**5) Always anchor.** One number, one place, or one personal fact per claim:

> *Prices are high.* — a headline
> *My rent has gone up forty percent since 2022.* ✅ — evidence

> ⚠️ خد بالك: مثال شخصي أو رقم بيخلي رأيك حقيقي.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Two minutes on the extended shape, three on vocabulary, one on anchoring. Do not teach new grammar —
> point at the structures already in use.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — topic vocabulary has predictable stress. Get these right:**

> *the **COST** of living · pub-lic **TRANS**-port · **COMM**-ute (noun) · af-**FORD**-able · ad-**DIC**-tive ·
> con-**GES**-tion · po-**LLU**-tion*

Two to note: ***commute*** is stressed on the **first** syllable as a noun, and ***affordable*** on the **second** —
both commonly wrong.

**Part 2 — the beat across a long opinion turn.** Content words only:

> *My **RENT** həz gone **UP** ə-bout **FOR**ty per-**CENT** since twenty twen-ty-**TWO**.*

**Part 3 — numbers need to survive.** They are the anchor, so they cannot be mumbled:

> *forty percent · since 2022 · ninety minutes*

**Part 4 — the concession gets its double pause**, as in U9-L02:

> *…**although** my salary has gone up maybe ten.* ⟋

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"The cost of living has gone up, although salaries haven't."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Three minutes on word stress — these are the words the topic requires — and two on protecting the
> numbers.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Same shape as U9: **opinion → reasons → concession → close**.
- **Anchor** every claim: a number, a place, or a personal fact.
- Change is **present perfect**: *rent has gone up*.
- What was done to the city is **passive**: *were knocked down*.
- Stress: *the **COST** of living · public **TRANS**port · **COMM**ute · af**FORD**able*.
- Protect your **numbers** — they are the evidence.
