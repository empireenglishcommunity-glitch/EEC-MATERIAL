# Unit Speaking Task: "If I Could… / My Dreams" (+ Review)

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 5 · **Lesson 5** — *UNIT MILESTONE*
⏱️ ~40 min · 📹 Recorded + live (assessment-style) · Prerequisite: L01–L04
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit5-second-conditional.md` · **S2-U5-L05***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~40 min):** Warm-up 5 · Model 4 · Checklist 4 · Accent clinic 5 · Rehearse 8 · Record 5 · Feedback 3 · Assign 1.
> **Objectives:** talk about imaginary situations, hypothetical advice and wishes in 8–10 sentences · switch between real and unreal · record the Unit-5 talk.
> **This closes the conditionals block, and it is a genuine range milestone.** Learners can now reason about what **will** and what **would** happen. Say that out loud — it is the most concrete evidence of progress available during the plateau.
> **Score the switching, not only the second conditional.** A talk that stays entirely imaginary is easier than one that moves between a real plan and a daydream. The rubric line is Range.
> **Listen for *'ll* against *'d*.** If the auxiliary is inaudible the whole distinction is unverifiable, so treat it as an accuracy issue rather than a pronunciation nicety.
> **Support:** 8 sentences with the checklist. **Stretch:** end with a real first-conditional plan that follows from the dream.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: a second conditional → a first conditional → an *If I were you* → an *I wish* → one *'ll* against
*'d* minimal pair.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One each, fast. The final item is a pronunciation check disguised as retrieval.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A model dreams-and-plans talk (📹 in the portal).

> **If** I **could** change one thing about my life, I**'d** work somewhere with a view. I know that sounds small.
> But I sit in a room with no windows for nine hours a day, and **if** I **had** natural light, I think I**'d** be
> a different person by six o'clock.
>
> **If** I **won** a serious amount of money, I **wouldn't** stop working — I**'d** just choose differently. I**'d**
> probably open a small place that sells books and coffee and loses money slowly.
>
> I **wish** I **had** started learning English earlier. **If** I **had** more confidence at twenty, I**'d** have
> applied for jobs abroad. **If I were** my younger self, I**'d** tell him to speak badly and often instead of
> waiting to be ready.
>
> Anyway — that's the dream. Realistically, **if** I **keep** studying at this pace, I**'ll** be able to interview
> in English by the summer. **If** that **works**, everything else gets easier.

**Notice the movement.** Three imagined paragraphs, then the last one switches to the **real** — *if I keep
studying, I'll be able to*. The dream is framed, and then a plan follows from it.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for content, once for structures. Ask where the tenses change to the real world and why —
> the answer is that the speaker moves from wishing to planning.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الكلام
1. ✅ **Three or more second conditionals** — *If I …, I'd …*
2. ✅ One ***I wish*** sentence
3. ✅ One ***If I were you*** or *If I were my younger self*
4. ✅ One **negative** — *I wouldn't…*
5. ✅ One switch to the **real** — a first conditional plan
6. ✅ **No *will* or *would* inside any *if*-clause**
7. ⭐ Audible ***'d*** throughout — and a reason for at least one imagined result

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Item 5 is the Range discriminator; item 6 is the accuracy one.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- ***'d*** — /aɪd/, present in every imagined result
- ***'ll*** against ***'d*** — the minimal pair that carries real against unreal
- **stressed *wouldn't*** — the negative carries the meaning
- ***I wish I*** → /aɪˈwɪʃaɪ/ as one shape
- **content-word stress** — *If I **HAD** more **TIME***

> 🎧 **Record & compare** the *'ll*/*'d* pair, then your weakest.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One personal target each, but run the *'ll*/*'d* pair with everyone — it is the sound this whole
> unit depends on.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 5 wrap)
- **if + past, would + plain verb** — the past form means **unreal**, not past.
- **Likely → *'ll*. Imaginary → *'d*.** One sound, whole meaning.
- No *will* and no *would* inside an *if*-clause.
- ***If I were you, I'd***… — advice as imagination, not instruction.
- ***wish* + past** = not true now; *I wish I could · had · were*.
- A wish names the gap; a **first conditional** crosses it.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 5 mini-quiz
1. *If I ___ (have) time, I'd help.* → **had**
2. *If I had money, I ___ travel.* → **would / 'd**
3. *If I ___ you, I'd wait.* → **were**
4. *I wish I ___ (can) drive.* → **could**
5. *I wish I ___ (have) more time.* → **had**
6. *If I would have money, I'd travel.* → wrong; say **If I had money**
7. *If I have a car, I would drive.* → wrong; say **If I had a car**
8. Real or unreal: *If it rains, I'll stay in.* → **real**
9. Real or unreal: *If it rained, I'd stay in.* → **unreal**
10. Turn *"I wish I spoke French"* into a plan → **If I study French daily, I'll improve**
