# Second Conditional: *if* + past, *would*

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 5 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Unit 4
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit5-second-conditional.md` · **S2-U5-L01***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 7 · Accent Lab 5 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** build the second conditional · talk about imaginary situations and their imagined results · understand that the past form here signals *unreal*, not *past*.
> **The conceptual hurdle is that the past tense is not about the past.** *If I had a car* is about **now** — a now that is not true. Gloss that once in Arabic, then stay inside clearly imaginary contexts so the meaning cannot be misread as history.
> **Keep every example unmistakably unreal today** — money you do not have, places you have not been, superpowers. If an example could be real, learners cannot tell which conditional they are practising, and the U4/U5 contrast (L02) collapses before you teach it.
> **Form errors to expect:** *would* inside the *if*-clause (❌ *"If I would have…"*) and a present tense there (❌ *"If I have a car, I would…"*). Both come from U4 interference, which is normal and worth naming.
> **Support:** the two-clause frame beside U4's, so the difference is visible. **Stretch:** add a reason for the imagined result.
> **Arabic ~15%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Everything in Unit 4 was possible. This structure covers the other half of thinking: the imagined, the wished-for,
the hypothetical. It is how people discuss choices they have not made, advice, dreams, and any question that
starts *"what would you do if…?"*

> والفعل الماضي هنا معناه إن الحاجة مش حقيقية.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two friends imagining (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** **If** I **had** more time, I**'d learn** the guitar properly.
> **B:** **If** I **won** the lottery tomorrow, I **wouldn't** work another day.
> **A:** Really? I think I**'d get** bored. **If** I **didn't** have a job, I**'d** probably invent one.
> **B:** **What would** you **do if** you **could** live anywhere?
> **A:** Somewhere by the sea. **If** I **lived** near water, I**'d be** calmer — I'm sure of it.
> **B:** **If** I **were** you, I**'d** just move.
> **A:** With what money?

**Notice:** *had · won · didn't have · could · lived · were* — all past forms. But nobody is talking about the
past. Every sentence is about an imagined **now**.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| If I had more time… | لو كان عندي وقت أكتر… |
| I'd learn the guitar. | كنت هتعلم جيتار |
| If I won the lottery… | لو كسبت اللوتري… |
| I wouldn't work. | مكنتش هشتغل |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: ask whether any sentence describes something that actually happened. None
> does — and that realisation is the lesson.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Two halves, both moved backwards.**

| Half | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| the **imagined situation** — *if* … | **past simple** | *If I **had** a car,* |
| the **imagined result** | ***would* / *'d*** + base verb | *I**'d drive** to work.* |

> ***If*** *I **spoke** French, I**'d live** in Paris.*

> ⚠️ **الفكرة الأساسية:** الفعل الماضي هنا مش بيتكلم عن الماضي — بيقول إن الحاجة **مش حقيقية**.

**1) *would* never goes in the *if*-clause.**

> *If I **had** money, I**'d** buy it.* ✅
> *If I **would have** money…* ❌
> *If I **have** money, I**'d** buy it.* ❌ — that is U4's tense in U5's frame

**2) *would* is followed by the plain verb** — no *to*, no *-s*, ever:

> *She**'d go**.* · *He**'d be** happy.* · *They**'d help**.*

**3) Negative in either half:**

> *If I **didn't** work here, I**'d** travel.* · *If I had time, I **wouldn't** stay home.*

**4) *could* and *might* can replace *would*** when the result is an ability or a weaker guess:

| | |
|---|---|
| *I**'d** go* | I would, definitely |
| *I **could** go* | I would be able to |
| *I **might** go* | perhaps |

**5) The question form is extremely useful:**

> ***What would** you **do if*** *you lost your phone?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** Four minutes on the two halves and the unreal meaning, three on the *would*-placement errors. Keep
> contexts impossible or improbable throughout.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *would* almost always reduces to *'d*.**

> *I would* → *I**'d*** /aɪd/ · *he would* → *he**'d*** /hiːd/ · *they would* → *they**'d*** /ðeɪd/

That /d/ is the entire signal that a sentence is hypothetical. Lose it and *"I'd go"* becomes *"I go"* — a
completely different meaning.

**Part 2 — *'d* links straight into the verb:**

> *I'd **get*** → *I-**dget*** · *I'd **be*** → *I-**dbe***

**Part 3 — the stress sits on the content words, not on *would*:**

> *If I **HAD** more time, I'd **LEARN** the guitar.*

**Part 4 — *wouldn't* is different: it takes a clear beat**, because the negative carries the meaning:

> *I **WOULDN'T** work another day.*

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"If I had more time, I'd learn the guitar — but I wouldn't stop working."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on the *'d* reduction and its survival, one on linking, two on the stressed *wouldn't*.
> A dropped *'d* is the priority error.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — put the verbs in the right form.**
1. If I ______ (have) a car, I ______ (drive) to work.
2. If she ______ (speak) English, she ______ (get) the job.
3. What ______ you ______ (do) if you ______ (lose) your passport?
4. If they ______ (not / live) so far, we ______ (visit) more often.

**Exercise 2 — answer for yourself.**
1. *What would you do if you had a free year?* → __________________________
2. *Where would you live if money didn't matter?* → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *If I would have time, I would call you.* → __________________________
2. *If I have a car, I would drive.* → __________________________
3. *If I had money, I would to buy it.* → __________________________
4. *If she had time, she would goes.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — three imaginary situations.** Complete each with your own result: *If I could live anywhere… / If
I had another career… / If I spoke five languages…*

> time* · *If I **had** a car* · *would **buy*** · *would **go***. Ex3.2 is the U4 interference error — name it as
