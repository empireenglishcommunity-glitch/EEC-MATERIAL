# *I Wish…* (Intro)

**Empire English** · Stage 2 — **Legionary** (B1) · Unit 5 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01–L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage2/unit5-second-conditional.md` · **S2-U5-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 7 · Accent Lab 5 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Assign 2.
> **Objectives:** express present wishes with *wish* + past · say what you wish you **could** or **had** · turn a wish into a plan.
> **Keep this to present wishes only.** *Wish* + past perfect (*"I wish I had studied"*) is B2 — if a learner produces it, accept it and say it is correct but later material. Opening it here doubles the difficulty for no gain.
> **The past form does the same job it did in L01: it marks *not true*.** That is why this lesson sits after the second conditional and not before — the mechanism is already familiar.
> **The meaning learners must feel is regret or dissatisfaction about *now*.** *"I wish I spoke French"* = I do not speak French, and I mind. It is not about the past.
> **Finish by converting a wish into a first conditional.** That move — from *"I wish I spoke fluently"* to *"If I practise every day, I'll get there"* — is the mindset content of the whole unit, and it takes ninety seconds.
> **Support:** the wish-frame card. **Stretch:** three wishes, each with the plan that would address it.
> **Arabic ~15%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
*Wish* is how English expresses dissatisfaction with the present without complaining about it. It is softer than
*"I don't have"* and more honest than pretending everything is fine — and it is everywhere in ordinary
conversation.

> ولاحظ إن الجملة بتتكلم عن دلوقتي، مش عن الماضي. *I wish I spoke French* معناها أنا مش بتكلم فرنساوي.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two colleagues at the end of a long week (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** I **wish** this week **was** over.
> **B:** Same. I **wish** I **had** more holiday left — I've used almost all of it.
> **A:** I **wish** I **could** work from home on Fridays. The commute is two hours.
> **B:** Have you asked?
> **A:** Not yet. I **wish** I **were** braver about that kind of thing, honestly.
> **B:** You should ask. I **wish** someone **had** told me that years ago — I never asked for anything.
> **A:** I **wish** I **spoke** better English too. Then I'd apply abroad.
> **B:** Your English is fine. Ask for the Fridays.

**Notice:** every wish uses a **past form** — *was · had · could · were · spoke* — and every one is about **right
now**. Nobody is describing history.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I wish this week was over. | نفسي الأسبوع ده يخلص |
| I wish I had more holiday. | نفسي كان عندي أجازة أكتر |
| I wish I could work from home. | نفسي أقدر أشتغل من البيت |
| I wish I spoke better English. | نفسي أتكلم إنجليزي أحسن |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: after each wish, learners state the true present situation. That inversion
> is the meaning.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The frame: *wish* + a past form.**

> *I **wish** I **had** a car.* → I do not have a car.
> *I **wish** I **spoke** Japanese.* → I do not speak Japanese.
> *I **wish** she **wasn't** angry.* → She is angry.

**1) The past form means *not true now*** — the same mechanism as the second conditional.

> ⚠️ **خد بالك:** الجملة دي عن الحاضر، ومش بنتكلم عن الماضي خالص. الفعل الماضي بس علامة إن الحاجة مش حقيقية.

**2) Three very high-frequency versions:**

| Frame | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *I wish I **had**…* | something you lack | *I wish I had more time.* |
| *I wish I **could**…* | an ability or permission | *I wish I could drive.* |
| *I wish I **were**…* | a state or identity | *I wish I were taller.* |

**3) *was* or *were*?** Same honest note as L03: *were* is the careful form, *was* is common in speech.

> *I wish I **were** there.* — standard · *I wish I **was** there.* — informal, very common

**4) Wishing about other people works too:**

> *I wish he **would** call.* — a complaint about somebody's behaviour
> *I wish they **didn't** live so far away.*

**5) Not for the past — yet.** *"I wish I had studied medicine"* is a **past** wish and belongs to B2. Today,
everything is about now.

**6) The move that matters — wish → plan.** A wish names the gap; a first conditional crosses it:

> *I wish I spoke fluent English.* → ***If** I speak for ten minutes every day, I**'ll** get much closer.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** Three minutes on the frame and the three versions, one on *was/were*, one on wishing about others,
> two on the wish→plan conversion. That last move is the unit's mindset content — do not skip it.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *I wish I* is three small words that run together.**

> *I wish I* → /aɪˈwɪʃaɪ/ — the /ʃ/ links straight into the second *I*

Said as three separate words it sounds recited. Said as one shape it sounds like feeling.

**Part 2 — the stress lands on the wished-for thing**, not on *wish*:

> *I wish I had **MORE TIME**.* · *I wish I could **DRIVE**.*

**Part 3 — wishes carry a falling, slightly slower melody.** That is what makes them read as regret rather than
as a request:

> *I wish I spoke better English.* ↘️

Say it twice — once as a complaint, once as a quiet regret. The words do not change.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I wish I had more time, and I wish I could work from home."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on the linked frame, one on stress placement, two on the regret melody. The
> complaint-versus-regret contrast is the memorable one.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — complete the wish.**
1. I don't have a car. → *I wish I ______ a car.*
2. I can't cook. → *I wish I ______ cook.*
3. It's raining. → *I wish it ______ raining.*
4. She isn't here. → *I wish she ______ here.*

**Exercise 2 — say the true situation.**
1. *I wish I lived closer.* → __________________________
2. *I wish I could play the piano.* → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *I wish I have more time.* → __________________________
2. *I wish I can swim.* → __________________________
3. *I wish I would be taller.* → __________________________
4. *I wish I will pass.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — wish, then plan.** Three real wishes about your life now. For each, add a first conditional that
would move you toward it.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** had · could · wasn't/weren't · were/was. **Ex3:** *had* · *could* · *were* · *If I study,
> I'll pass* — number 4 is not a wish at all but a condition, which is the useful correction.
