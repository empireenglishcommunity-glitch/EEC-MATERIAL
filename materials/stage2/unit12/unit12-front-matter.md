## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** prove the B1 exit criteria and record the milestone that extends your proof timeline to
> **four** points — Day 1 → Stage 0 → A2 → **B1**.

**This unit is written in English only.** Not as a test of endurance, and not to make the ending harder — but
because you no longer need the support, and the last week of a stage is the wrong moment to discover that you
still do. Everything here you have already done. Unit 12 only asks you to do it all at once.

**Nothing new is taught.** Four of the five lessons are retrieval, diagnosis and rehearsal; the fifth is the
assessment. If a structure has gone quiet since Unit 4, this is where you find out — while there is still time.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- A **B1 determination** built on quoted evidence across five rubric dimensions.
- Your **B1 milestone recording**, comparable with three earlier samples.
- Two written Stage-3 targets, and a residual-feature list that does not pretend to be empty.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (L01–L03 ~35 min, L04 ~40, L05 ~45) plus this wrapper.
> **The immersion is the point, and it needs framing, not enforcing.** Tell learners on L01 why the Arabic is
> gone. If someone stalls, feed an English phrase rather than switching language — that is the strategy they
> need in the assessment room and in every conversation after it.
> **L01 and L02 are retrieval, not re-teaching.** The temptation is to re-explain the perfect for eight minutes
> because it is comfortable. Resist it: retrieval under mild pressure is what makes a structure available in
> speech, and a lecture is what makes it available only in a worksheet. Make them produce.
> **L03 must be personalised from real evidence.** Use the two written priorities handed over from U11-L05. If
> they are missing, diagnose from the seeded passage before choosing targets — handing everyone the same three
> features is the failure mode here. Stage-2 targets are **rhythmic**, not segmental: the beat, weak forms,
> linking, list and contrast contours. The honesty guardrail is absolute — compare each learner only with their
> own earlier recordings, and never demand "native."
> **L04 must match L05 exactly.** Same length, same three parts, same rubric. Push back once, genuinely, for
> every learner: a discussion that is never challenged cannot evidence Interaction. Do not raise the difficulty
> to prepare them or lower it to encourage them — either change makes the rehearsal worthless.
> **L05 is governed by the committed documents, not by this unit.** Rubric bands and determination come from
> `05-assessment-suite.md`; prompt, conditions, storage and consent come from `06-before-after-protocol.md`.
> The assessment recording and the milestone sample are **two separate files** — the first determines
> performance, the second enables fair comparison. Consent is granular and revocable; enrolling is not
> permission to publish. Never round a determination up for motivation or down for strictness.
> **Carry the residuals forward.** A stage ending is not a claim that every feature is finished. Write the
> unresolved ones down and hand them to Stage 3.

## End-of-Unit Check — تقييم آخر الوحدة (summative)
- **Quiz:** mixed Stage-2 grammar and vocabulary — perfect and past, conditionals, relatives, reported speech,
  the passive, connectors — plus short production items.
- **Speaking task:** the L05 extended opinion-and-discussion task (8–12 min) **and** the standard-prompt
  milestone sample, scored on **Fluency · Coherence · Accuracy · Range · Interaction** at the B1 threshold.
- **Feeds:** the Cohort Tracker — B1 determination, milestone-clip link and consent tier, residual weak-feature
  flags for Stage 3, and the four-point proof timeline.
