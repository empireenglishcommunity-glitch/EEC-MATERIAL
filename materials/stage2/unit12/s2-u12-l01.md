## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
At A2 you could describe a finished day. You can now handle the whole of time: what happened, what was happening,
what had happened before that, and what has happened up to now. This lesson checks that the system is available when
you are not thinking about it.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. Knowing a tense means you can complete an exercise about it. Having a
tense means it arrives on its own, in the middle of a story, while your attention is on what you are saying rather
than on how you are saying it. The summative measures the second thing, which is why this unit tests retrieval under
time pressure instead of teaching anything new.

Expect the perfect-against-past choice to still wobble. It is the hardest single decision at this level, and even
confident speakers occasionally repair it mid-sentence. Noticing and fixing your own slip is not a failure — it is
precisely the self-monitoring that keeps you improving after the course ends.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A short account using the whole system (📹 in the portal).

> **I've worked** in three cities, but the year **I spent** in Amman **was** the strangest.
>
> **I'd applied** for something completely different — a research job — and **I hadn't expected** to get anything.
> **While** I **was waiting** to hear back, someone **offered** me a teaching post instead. **I used to** think I
> would hate teaching. **I'd never done** it.
>
> **I took** it because the rent **had already gone up** at home and I **needed** the money. **In the end I stayed**
> fourteen months. **I've been** back twice **since** then, and **I've never** managed to explain to anybody why
> that year mattered so much.

**Notice the choices.** *I've worked* (experience, no date) against *I spent* (a defined year). *I'd applied · hadn't
expected · had already gone up* for what came earlier. *Used to* for a former belief. *I've been back twice since*
for unfinished time. Nothing is decorative — each tense is doing a job the others cannot.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners justify three tense choices. Justification is the skill; recognition
> alone is not.

## 🧩 The Error Card — كارت الأخطاء

**The four patterned errors of this block.**

**1) A finished time with the present perfect:**

> ✅ *I **went** to Amman in 2019.* · ✅ *I**'ve been** to Amman.*
> ❌ *I've been to Amman in 2019.*

**2) The present simple for unfinished time:**

> ✅ *I**'ve lived** here for five years.* · ❌ *I live here for five years.*

**3) *yet* in a positive sentence, or *already* in a negative:**

> ✅ *I haven't finished **yet**.* · ✅ *I've **already** finished.*

**4) The past form where the participle belongs:**

> ✅ *I've **seen** it. · It was **built**.* · ❌ *I've saw it. · It was build.*

**The one question that resolves most of it:** *is there a specific finished time?* Yes → past simple. No → perfect.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Learners self-correct all eight wrong examples before you confirm. Then re-state the single question
> from U2-L03 — it is the most portable thing in the stage.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Contractions across tenses.** The auxiliary is the tense, and it is always weak — but never absent:

> *I**'ve** worked · I**'d** worked · I **wəz** working · I**'ve been** working*

**The two that sound identical:** *'d* is both *would* and *had*. Context decides — *'d* + base is *would*, *'d* +
participle is *had*:

> *I**'d** go* (would) · *I**'d gone*** (had)

**Participle endings under speed.** The whole system collapses without them:

> *work**ed** /t/ · liv**ed** /d/ · want**ed** /ɪd/ · se**en** · tak**en** · bui**lt** · fou**nd***

**Then the beat.** Content words carry it; auxiliaries compress:

> *I**'d** ap-**PLIED** fər **SOME**thing **COM**pletely **DIFF**erent.*

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I'd applied for something else, and I hadn't expected to get anything."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Two minutes on weak-but-present auxiliaries, two on the *'d* ambiguity, one on the beat. The *'d*
> distinction is a listening gain as much as a speaking one.

## 🏋️ Train — البروفة الكاملة

**Exercise 1 — choose the tense.**
1. I ______ (live) here since 2019.
2. I ______ (live) in Tanta when I was a child.
3. ______ you ever ______ (be) to Jordan?
4. I ______ (not / finish) yet.
5. When I arrived, the meeting ______ (already / start).
6. While I ______ (wait), my phone ______ (ring).

**Exercise 2 — justify.** For each answer above, say **why** in one phrase — *"unfinished time"*, *"a date"*,
*"earlier than that"*.

**Exercise 3 — fix and explain.**
1. *I've seen him yesterday.* → __________________________
2. *I work here for three years.* → __________________________
3. *I've already finished yet.* → __________________________
4. *It was build in 1902.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — two minutes.** Narrate a real period of your life using at least five different past forms. Your
partner counts them.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min.** **Ex1:** have lived · lived · Have … been · haven't finished · had already started · was waiting /
> rang. Ex2 is the important one — the justification is what transfers to the summative.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- One question: **is there a specific finished time?** Yes → past simple. No → perfect.
- Unfinished time to now → **present perfect** with *for* / *since*.
- **yet** in negatives and questions; **already** in positives.
- After an auxiliary, use the **participle**: *seen · built · found*.
- ***'d*** is *would* **or** *had* — the next word tells you which.
