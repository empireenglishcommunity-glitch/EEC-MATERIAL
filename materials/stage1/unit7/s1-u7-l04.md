## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
At a pharmacy (📹 in the portal).

> **Pharmacist:** Good morning. How can I help you?
> **Customer:** Good morning. I **have** a bad **sore throat** and a **temperature**.
> **Pharmacist:** How long **have you had** it?
> **Customer:** Since Sunday. I **feel** very **tired** too.
> **Pharmacist:** You **should** take these twice a day, after food. And you **have to** finish all of them
> — **don't** stop when you feel better.
> **Customer:** **Do I have to** stay at home?
> **Pharmacist:** You **don't have to**, but you **should** rest. If the temperature **doesn't** go down in
> two days, you **should** see a doctor.
> **Customer:** Thank you very much.

**Notice** how the pharmacist used all three: **should** for advice, **have to** for the necessary part, and
**don't have to** for what is optional.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| How can I help you? | أقدر أساعدك؟ |
| twice a day, after food | مرتين في اليوم، بعد الأكل |
| You have to finish all of them. | لازم تكملهم كلهم |
| Do I have to stay at home? | لازم أقعد في البيت؟ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners label each modal by function. Point out *How long have you
> had it?* as receptive only — the present perfect belongs to Stage 2.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The exchange has a fixed shape. Learn the shape and you can improvise inside it.**

| Step | What you say |
|---|---|
| 1 | *I have a … / I feel …* — your symptom |
| 2 | *Since Sunday. / For two days.* — how long |
| 3 | *What should I do? / Do I have to …?* — your question |
| 4 | *Thank you very much.* — close it |

> والترتيب ده بيوفر عليك التفكير، فتركز في الكلام بس.

**Health-habit language, for talking about prevention:**

- *I **exercise** twice a week.* · *I try to **sleep** eight hours.*
- *I **don't eat** much sugar.* · *I should **drink** more water.*

**Frequency phrases you will need:** *once · twice · three times* + *a day / a week*

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** بنقول **once** و**twice**، مش *one time* و*two times*. ومن تلاتة وطالع بنقول
> *three times*.

**Two useful questions:** *How many times a day?* · *Before or after food?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** The four-step shape is the deliverable. *once/twice* is the one genuinely new item — learners
> reliably produce *one time*.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Connected clarity — the whole exchange, not single words.**

Three things to hold together under pressure:

1. **/θ/–/ð/** in the health words — *throat · teeth · health*
2. **/hæftə/** for *have to*
3. **rising intonation** on your questions — *Do I have to stay at home?* ↗

> اللي بيحصل إن المتعلم يظبط الكلمة لوحدها، وأول ما يدخل في محادثة حقيقية يفقد الاتنين. فالتمرين هنا على
> الجملة الكاملة، مش على الكلمة.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I have a sore throat. Do I have to take anything?"*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Drill the full sentence rather than isolated words. Accuracy under connected-speech pressure is
> the actual target of this lesson.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — once, twice or three times?**
1. I go to the gym on Monday and Thursday. → ______ a week
2. I take this tablet in the morning only. → ______ a day
3. Morning, afternoon and night. → ______ a day

**Exercise 2 — complete the pharmacy exchange.**
> **Pharmacist:** How can I help you?
> **You:** ______________________________________
> **Pharmacist:** How long have you had it?
> **You:** ______________________________________
> **Pharmacist:** Take these twice a day.
> **You:** ______________________________________ *(ask if you have to stay home)*

**Exercise 3 — *should*, *have to*, or *don't have to*?**
1. You ______ finish all the tablets — it's important.
2. You ______ stay at home, but it's better if you rest.
3. I think you ______ drink more water.

**Exercise 4 — your habits.** Two things you do for your health, and one you should do more.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** twice · once · three times. **Ex2:** check the four-step shape.
> **Ex3:** have to · don't have to · should.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- The shape: **symptom → how long → your question → thank you**.
- **once · twice · three times** + *a day / a week*.
- **should** advice · **have to** necessary · **don't have to** optional.
- *have to* sounds like **/hæftə/**.
- Hold the /θ/ and the rising tone **inside** the conversation, not just in drills.
