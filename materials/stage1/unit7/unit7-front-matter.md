## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** by the end of Unit 7 you'll **say what's wrong and help someone else** — describe
> symptoms, give advice, separate what's necessary from what isn't, and handle a pharmacy.
> مهمتك في الوحدة دي: تقول إيه اللي وجعك، وتنصح غيرك، وتفرّق بين اللازم واللي مش لازم.

**Why this unit is different in kind:** everything before it was about **you** — your past, your plans, your
opinions. This unit is the first time you use English to **help another person**. That is a social skill, and
learners feel the difference.

> ودي أول وحدة نتيجتها إنك تنفع حد تاني، مش إنك تعبّر عن نفسك.

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Body & symptoms | *have* + a symptom · *feel* + an adjective · *My back hurts* |
| **L02** | *should / shouldn't* | give advice, and **soften** it so it helps |
| **L03** | *have to · must* | necessity — and **don't have to ≠ shouldn't** |
| **L04** | Health habits & the doctor | assemble all three in a real pharmacy exchange |
| **L05** | Advise a friend (milestone) 🎙️ | react, ask, advise with a reason + **record your milestone** |

**Grammar spine:** *have/feel* for symptoms · *should / shouldn't* · *have to / don't have to / must*.
**Accent Lab:** the **/θ/–/ð/** consolidation — health words are unusually thick with them — plus stress on
the advice verb, *have to* → /hæftə/, and the silent *l* in *should · could · would*.
**Mindset:** never miss twice · health is the base of consistency.
**Arabic support:** ~30% at L01, fading to ~25% by L05.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Unit-7 advice recording** — the first time you use English to look after someone.
- A pharmacy exchange you could genuinely handle abroad.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (L05 ~35 min, the rest ~30) plus this wrapper.
> **L01's split is the grammar:** English uses **have** for a symptom you possess and **feel** for a state.
> Blur them and you get *"I have tired"* and *"I feel headache"*. It is also the **/θ/–/ð/** consolidation
> point, and that is not arbitrary — *throat, teeth, mouth, health, breathe* are all in this lexical field.
> **L02's form takes two minutes; spend the rest on softeners.** Bare *should* sounds like an instruction and
> learners cannot hear that they are being blunt. *I think* and *maybe* are the whole social lesson.
> **L03 contains the single most valuable correction in the unit:** ***don't have to* does not mean
> *shouldn't***. It means there is no need. A learner who confuses them turns *"You don't have to come"* into
> a rejection. Give it four examples of its own.
> **L04 is integration, not new grammar.** Correct the assembly under conversational pressure, not the parts —
> learners routinely get each piece right in isolation and lose two of them inside a real exchange.
> **L05 is scored with Interaction, and warmth counts.** Give them a specific problem to respond to so the
> advice cannot be generic.
