# Giving Advice: *should / shouldn't*

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 7 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit7-health-and-advice.md` · **S1-U7-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 4 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** give advice with *should* · advise against with *shouldn't* · ask for advice.
> **This is a social skill, not only a structure.** Being able to advise a friend is one of the first genuinely *warm* things a learner can do in English, and they usually feel that. Say so.
> **The form is the easy part** — no *to*, no change for person. Spend the time on softeners, because bare *should* can sound bossy and learners do not hear that.
> **Support:** the advice-verb bank. **Stretch:** two pieces of advice with a reason each.
> **Arabic ~30%.**

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Tell someone what to do: *You should rest.*
- Advise against something: *You shouldn't work today.*
- **Ask** for advice: *What should I do?* 🎙️

> عرفت تقول إيه اللي وجعك. النهاردة تنصح غيرك — ودي مهارة اجتماعية حقيقية.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Advice is one of the warmest things you can offer in another language. It says *I am paying attention to you*
— and it takes one small word.

> والصعوبة مش في التركيب — ده أسهل جزء. الصعوبة إنك تقولها نصيحة مش أوامر.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A friend giving advice (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** I have a terrible headache and I feel dizzy.
> **B:** You **should** drink more water — **maybe** you're dehydrated. And you **shouldn't** look at your
> phone so much.
> **A:** **What should I do** about the headache now?
> **B:** I **think** you **should** rest for an hour. If it doesn't stop, you **should** see a doctor.
> **A:** OK, thanks.

**Notice:** the advice is softened. *maybe*, *I think* — they make a suggestion instead of an order.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| You should drink water. | لازم تشرب ماية |
| You shouldn't work today. | ماينفعش تشتغل النهاردة |
| What should I do? | أعمل إيه؟ |
| I think you should rest. | أنا شايف إنك ترتاح |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners mark every softener. Then say a bare version — *"Drink
> water. Rest."* — so they hear how different it lands.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Form: *should* + the simple verb. Nothing else changes, ever.**

| | |
|---|---|
| I / you / he / she / we / they | **should** rest |
| negative | **shouldn't** work |

> مفيش **s** مع *he* و*she*، ومفيش **to** بعد **should**. الفعل زي ما هو.

**Three uses:**

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| advice | *You should see a doctor.* |
| advice against | *You shouldn't drink coffee at night.* |
| asking for advice | *What should I do?* |

**Now the part that matters socially — soften it.** Bare *should* can sound like an instruction:

| Softer | |
|---|---|
| *I think you should…* | the most useful one |
| *Maybe you should…* | gentle |
| *You could…* | a suggestion, not advice |

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** *"You should sleep"* لوحدها ممكن توصل كأمر. حط **I think** أو **maybe** قبلها،
> وهتوصل نصيحة مش تعليمات.

**Watch the two form errors:** ❌ *He shoulds rest* · ❌ *You should to rest*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Form in two minutes — it is genuinely trivial. Spend the remaining four on softeners and have
> them re-say bare advice with *I think* in front, aloud.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| You should… | لازم / الأفضل إنك | *You should rest.* |
| You shouldn't… | ماينفعش | *You shouldn't smoke.* |
| What should I do? | أعمل إيه؟ | asking |
| I think you should… | أنا شايف إنك | softener |
| Maybe you should… | يمكن الأفضل | softener |
| see a doctor | تروح لدكتور | *You should see a doctor.* |
| take medicine | تاخد دوا | *take some medicine* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the stress goes on the ADVICE VERB, not on *should*.**

> You should **REST**. · You shouldn't **WORK** today.

*should* is weak and short. Stress it instead and you sound as if you are arguing.

**Target 2 — *should* has a silent *l*.**

> *should* = /ʃʊd/ — like *good* with a "sh". Not "shold".
> Same family as *could* and *would*, all with the silent *l*.

**Target 3 — *shouldn't* keeps its final /t/**, exactly like *didn't* and *wasn't*.

> النبرة على الفعل مش على **should**. ولاحظ إن الـ **l** ساكتة في *should* و*could* و*would*.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I think you should rest, and you shouldn't go to work."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The silent *l* generalises across three high-frequency modals, so it is efficient to teach
> here.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — advise them.**
1. *"I have a headache."* → __________________________
2. *"I can't sleep at night."* → __________________________
3. *"I feel tired all the time."* → __________________________

**Exercise 2 — *should* or *shouldn't*?**
1. You ______ drink water when you have a temperature.
2. You ______ eat heavy food when your stomach hurts.
3. You ______ see a doctor if it doesn't stop.

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *He shoulds rest.* → __________________________
2. *You should to see a doctor.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — soften it.** *"Sleep more."* → __________________________

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** accept any sensible advice with correct form. **Ex2:** should · shouldn't · should.
> **Ex3:** *He should rest* · *You should see a doctor*. **Ex4:** *I think you should sleep more.*

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **should / shouldn't + simple verb.** No *to*, no *-s*.
- *What should I do?* asks for advice.
- **I think · maybe** turn an order into a suggestion.
- Stress the **verb**, not *should*.
- The **l** is silent: *should · could · would*.
