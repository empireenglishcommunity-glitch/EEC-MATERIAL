> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Fast retrieval across the four lessons.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A friend responding to a problem (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** I feel terrible. I have a headache every day this week, and I can't sleep.
> **B:** That doesn't sound good. **How long have** you felt like this?
> **A:** About five days.
> **B:** **I think you should** see a doctor — five days is a long time. And **maybe you should** stop
> drinking coffee after six, **because** it stays in your body for hours.
> **A:** Do you think I **have to** take a day off?
> **B:** You **don't have to**, but honestly you **should** rest. You **shouldn't** work twelve hours when
> you feel like this. And you **should** drink much more water.
> **A:** Thanks. That helps.

**Notice:** every piece of advice is **softened** and one carries a **reason**. That is what makes it help
rather than instruct.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for warmth, once for structure. Ask which line felt most helpful — it is always the
> one with *because*.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست النصيحة الكويسة
1. ✅ **React first** — *That doesn't sound good · I'm sorry to hear that*
2. ✅ **Ask one question** — *How long? · Have you taken anything?*
3. ✅ **Two pieces of advice** — one *should*, one *shouldn't*
4. ✅ **Soften at least one** — *I think · maybe*
5. ✅ **Give a reason** — *because …*
6. ⭐ **Separate necessity from advice** — *You don't have to …, but you should …*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board it and hand out the card. Item 1 is the one learners skip, and skipping it is what makes
> advice sound cold.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
Rehearse with a partner, swap roles, then record.

A friend tells you a health problem. **React, ask one question, then give two pieces of advice** — softened,

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 7 wrap)
- **have** + a noun · **feel** + an adjective · *My back **hurts***.
- **should / shouldn't** + simple verb — advice. No *to*, no *-s*.
- **I think · maybe** stop advice sounding like an order.
- **have to** = necessary · **don't have to** = **not necessary**, not forbidden.
- /θ/ and /ð/ share a tongue position; *have to* sounds like /hæftə/.

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 7 mini-quiz
1. *I ___ a headache.* → **have**
2. *I ___ tired.* → **feel**
3. *You ___ see a doctor.* (advice) → **should**
4. *He ___ work on Saturday.* (necessary) → **has to**
5. *You ___ pay — it's free.* → **don't have to**
6. Softer version of *"Rest more."* → **I think you should rest more.**
