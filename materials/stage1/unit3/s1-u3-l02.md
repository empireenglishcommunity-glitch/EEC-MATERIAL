## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
These ten verbs cover most of what you actually do in a day — go, buy, take, make, come. A very small
amount of memorising opens a large amount of speech.

What matters is **using** them often, not studying them often. Repetition in speech is what fixes them.

> استخدمهم كتير، مش تحفظهم كتير.

Ten verbs, and you can describe most of an ordinary day.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A trip to the market (📹 in the portal).

> On Friday I **went** to the market and **bought** vegetables and fruit.
> I **got** a good price because I **came** early. Then I **took** a taxi home
> and **made** lunch for my family. They **ate** everything.

**Notice:** six irregular verbs in four sentences. This is normal — irregulars are everywhere.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I bought vegetables. | اشتريت خضار |
| I got a good price. | خدت سعر كويس |
| I came early. | جيت بدري |
| I took a taxi. | خدت تاكسي |
| I made lunch. | عملت الغدا |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice; learners count the irregulars. The density is the point — it justifies the drilling.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Set 2 — five more forms to know by heart.**

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| buy | **bought** |
| get | **got** |
| come | **came** |
| make | **made** |
| take | **took** |

> زي المرة اللي فاتت: مفيش قاعدة، دي قائمة. بس خد بالك إن بعضهم بيتشابه في الشكل، وده بيساعد في الحفظ
> جدًا — لما تلاقي مجموعة بتتغير بنفس الطريقة، احفظهم مع بعض مش واحد واحد.

**A pattern that helps** — some irregulars change in similar ways:

- *buy → bought* · *think → thought* · *bring → brought* — the **-ought** family
- *come → came* · *become → became* — vowel swap only

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك من *got*:** الفعل ده معناه واسع جدًا في الإنجليزي — *got a price*، *got home*،
> *got a message*. ماتترجمهاش حرفيًا كل مرة، خده كوحدة مع الكلمة اللي بعده.

**Your ten so far:** *went · had · did · saw · ate · bought · got · came · made · took*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Present set 2, then show the *-ought* family — grouping is what turns a list into memory.
> Warn about *got* being semantically wide; learners try to translate it one-to-one and get stuck.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the /ɔː/ in *bought*.** Long, open, and **no /g/ sound at the end**. The *gh* is silent.

> b**ough**t = "bort" · not "bowght", not "bogt"

**Target 2 — keep the clusters clean in *made* and *took*.**
One syllable each. Do not add a vowel: *took*, not "to-ok".

**Target 3 — the verb still carries the beat:**

> I **BOUGHT** vegetables and **MADE** lunch.

> النقطة الأهم: الـ **gh** في *bought* ماتنطقش خالص.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I bought it, I took it home, and I made dinner."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The silent *gh* is the single highest-value fix here; learners routinely voice it.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — past form.**
1. buy → ______ 2. take → ______ 3. come → ______ 4. make → ______ 5. get → ______

**Exercise 2 — complete with a set-1 or set-2 verb.**
1. I ______ a new phone last week.
2. She ______ to my house at eight.
3. We ______ a taxi because we were late.
4. He ______ a cake for her birthday.

**Exercise 3 — chain the story.** Add one sentence each, using a different irregular verb:
> *On Saturday I went to the mall…*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** bought · took · came · made · got. **Ex2:** bought · came · took · made.
> **Ex3:** run it round the group; each learner must use a verb nobody has used yet.
