# Telling a Story: a Small Event From Your Week

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 3 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit3-past-irregular-and-questions.md` · **S1-U3-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 5 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** tell a 5–7 sentence true story · use story language · ask one past question about a peer's story.
> **Stories are the A2 fluency engine.** Prioritise communication and flow. Correct only the two patterned errors — *did* + past form, and dropped endings — and do it as delayed feedback at the end, never mid-story.

> **Arabic ~30%.**

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Tell a **true story** in 5–7 sentences.
- Use story language: *One day · Suddenly · In the end*.
- Listen to someone's story and ask a past question about it. 🎙️

> عندك الأفعال والسؤال والنفي. النهاردة تحكي.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Storytelling is the most useful skill in real conversation. People do not speak in separate sentences — they
tell you things that happened. Once you can do that, you can join any gathering and say something that
belongs to you.

A story is not a grammar exercise. It is what conversation is actually made of, and it has a shape you can
rely on.

> والشكل ده هو اللي بتستند عليه لما تحكي.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A small, ordinary story (📹 in the portal).

> **One day** last week I **went** to work by bus. The bus **was** very late,
> so I **waited** for thirty minutes. **Suddenly** I **saw** an old friend at the bus stop —
> we **didn't** meet for five years! We **talked** and **took** the bus together.
> **In the end** I **arrived** late, but I **was** happy.

**The shape:** a beginning (*when*), a middle (*what happened*), and an end (*how it finished*).

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| One day… | مرة… |
| The bus was late. | الأتوبيس اتأخر |
| Suddenly… | وفجأة… |
| In the end… | وفي الآخر… |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play once for enjoyment, once for shape. Have them name the three parts. The shape is what
> they will lean on when they tell their own.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**A story has three parts, and each part has its own words.**

| Part | Words you use | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginning** | *One day · Last week · Yesterday* | set the scene |
| **Middle** | *Then · After that · Suddenly · Because* | what happened |
| **End** | *In the end · Finally · So* | how it finished |

> **Suddenly** هي أهم كلمة في الحكاية، لأنها بتقول للسامع إن فيه حاجة غير متوقعة جاية. حطها قبل
> المفاجأة، وهتلاقي الناس بتركز معاك.

**Two things a good story always has:**

1. **Past verbs throughout** — do not drift into the present halfway.
2. **A reason somewhere** — *because* tells us **why** it mattered.

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** لما تتحمس في الحكي، بيحصل إنك ترجع للمضارع في النص. خليك في الماضي من الأول للآخر.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Keep it short — the words are transparent. Spend your words on *Suddenly* as a listener
> signal, and on tense consistency, which is where longer speech breaks down.

| Suddenly | فجأة | *Suddenly it started to rain.* |

| lose → lost | ضيّعت | *I lost my phone.* |
| find → found | لقيت | *I found it.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Narrative rhythm: beat on the verbs, pause at the connectors.**

> One day, ‖ I **WENT** to work ‖ and the bus **WAS** late.
> **Suddenly,** ‖ I **SAW** my friend.

The small pause after a connector does two jobs: it gives the listener a moment to follow, and it gives
**you** a moment to plan the next sentence. Use it deliberately.

> الوقفة الصغيرة دي مش ضعف — دي وقت بتاخده لنفسك تفكر في الجملة اللي جاية.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** the first two sentences of the model, with the pauses.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Frame the pause as a *fluency tool*, not hesitation. Learners who accept it stop filling
> silence with "ummm".

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — put the story in order.** Number 1–5.
- ______ Suddenly, my phone rang.
- ______ In the end, I got there on time.
- ______ One day I woke up late.
- ______ It was my boss.
- ______ So I took a taxi.

**Exercise 2 — add the missing story words.**
> *______ last month I travelled to Alexandria. The train was full, ______ I stood for an hour.
> ______ a man offered me his seat. ______ it was a good trip.*

**Exercise 3 — fix the tense.**
> *One day I went to the market. I buy some fruit and I see my neighbour.*

**Exercise 4 — plan yours.** Write only three notes: beginning, what happened, end.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** 3 · 5 · 1 · 4 · 2. **Ex2:** One day · so · Suddenly · In the end.
> **Ex3:** *bought* and *saw* — the classic mid-story drift. **Ex4:** notes only; stop anyone writing a
> full script, or they will read it aloud instead of telling it.

## 🎤 Your Turn — دورك
Tell **your own true story** in 5–7 sentences, from your notes — not a script.

Then listen to a partner's story and **ask one past question** about it.

> 🎙️ **Record your story.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** **Success check:** a coherent 5–7 sentence story, connectors present, past tense held
> throughout. Give feedback on the two patterned errors only, and only after everyone has finished.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Three parts: **beginning · middle · end**.
- **One day · Suddenly · In the end** — cheap words, big effect.
- Stay in the past the whole way through.
- The pause after a connector is **your** thinking time.
- *(Storytelling comes back in Unit 8 for trips, and in Unit 10.)*
