> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback 1.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Irregular verbs are few in number but they are the **most used verbs in the language**. *go*, *have*, *see*
and *eat* appear in almost everything anyone says, so you cannot tell a story without them.

The good news is that A2 needs only about **ten** of them, and they stick through hearing and repetition
rather than through memorising a table.

> بتتحفظ بالسمع والتكرار، مش بالحفظ من ورقة.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Someone describes a good day (📹 in the portal).

> Yesterday I **went** to work early. At one o'clock I **had** lunch with a friend.
> After work I **saw** a film with my wife, and we **ate** dinner at a small restaurant.
> I **did** nothing else — it **was** enough.

**Notice:** not one of those verbs ends in **-ed**. They change shape completely.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I went to work. | رحت الشغل |
| I had lunch. | اتغديت |
| I saw a film. | شفت فيلم |
| We ate dinner. | اتعشينا |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners try to guess each present form. The guessing exposes the
> irregularity far better than a table does.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**These five verbs have their own past form. There is no rule — there is a list.**

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| go | **went** |
| have | **had** |
| do | **did** |
| see | **saw** |
| eat | **ate** |

> ماتحاولش تلاقي قاعدة، لأن مفيش. الأفعال دي شكلها بيتغير تمامًا، ولازم تتحفظ زي ما هي. وأسرع طريقة
> إنك تقولها في جمل حقيقية عن نفسك، مش تكررها لوحدها — العقل بيمسك الكلمة أحسن لما تكون في سياق.

**And they never take *-ed*:**

- ✅ *I **went** to work.* → ❌ *I goed to work.*
- ✅ *We **ate** dinner.* → ❌ *We eated dinner.*

> لو قلت *"goed"* أو *"eated"*، ده معناه إنك فهمت قاعدة الـ **-ed** صح وبتطبقها. المشكلة بس إن الأفعال
> دي استثناء. الحل التكرار، مش المراجعة.

**Careful with *did*.** It is the past of *do*, and it is also the question word you will meet in L03:

- *I **did** my homework.* → the verb
- *Where **did** you go?* → the question helper *(L03)*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Present the list, drill it orally, then contrast against a regular verb so the difference is
> felt. Flag *did* as doing two jobs but do not teach the question use yet — that is L03.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| go → went | رحت | *I went home.* |
| have → had | كان عندي / اتغديت | *I had lunch.* |
| do → did | عملت | *I did my work.* |
| see → saw | شفت | *I saw my friend.* |
| eat → ate | اكلت | *We ate together.* |
| early / late | بدري / متأخر | *I went early.* |
| enough | كفاية | *It was enough.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — stress the main verb.** In a past sentence the verb carries the beat:

> I **WENT** to work. · We **ATE** dinner. · I **SAW** a film.

**Target 2 — keep these three vowels clean and distinct:**

| Verb | Sound | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| w**e**nt | /e/ — short, like *bed* | not "wint" |
| s**aw** | /ɔː/ — long and open | not "so" |
| **a**te | /eɪ/ — like *eight* | not "at" |

> الفروق الصغيرة في حروف العلة دي هي اللي بتخلي الكلمة تتفهم من أول مرة.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I went out, I saw a friend, and we ate together."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Verb stress first, then the three vowels as minimal contrasts. *saw* is usually the weakest.

- There is no rule for these. There is a list, and repetition.

- Stress the **verb**; keep *went · saw · ate* distinct.
