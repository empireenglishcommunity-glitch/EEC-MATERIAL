# Past Questions & Negatives: *did · didn't*

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 3 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L02
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit3-past-irregular-and-questions.md` · **S1-U3-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 7 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** ask past questions with *did* + base verb · make negatives with *didn't* · hold a past mini-interview.

> **Support:** question frames and short-answer cards to read from. **Stretch:** add *wh-* follow-ups.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Ask about the past: *Did you go? · What did you do?*
- Say what did **not** happen: *I didn't go.*
- Interview someone about their day and answer back. 🎙️

> عندك الأفعال. ناقص إنك تسأل بيها.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Until now you have only been **telling**. The moment you can ask, you are having a conversation.

And there is a gift buried in this lesson: because the verb returns to its simple form after *did*, one rule
covers **every** verb, regular and irregular alike. You do not need to recall a single past form to ask a
past question.

> يعني القاعدة دي بتلغي صعوبة الأفعال الشاذة كلها في السؤال.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two friends catching up (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** **What did you do** yesterday?
> **B:** I **went** out with my cousin. We **had** dinner in Zamalek.
> **A:** **Did you see** Ali there?
> **B:** No, I **didn't**. He **didn't come** — he was working.
> **A:** **Where did you go** after that?
> **B:** Nowhere. I **got** home early.

**Notice:** after **did** and **didn't**, the verb goes back to its **simple** form — *see*, not *saw*.
*come*, not *came*.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| What did you do? | عملت إيه؟ |
| Did you see Ali? | شفت علي؟ |
| No, I didn't. | لأ |
| He didn't come. | هو ماجاش |
| Where did you go? | رحت فين؟ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time, learners mark every verb that follows *did/didn't* and notice none of
> them is a past form. Let them find it.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The one rule: *did* carries the past, so the verb does not.**

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong |
|---|---|
| Did you **go**? | Did you ~~went~~? |
| Did you **see** him? | Did you ~~saw~~ him? |
| I didn't **eat**. | I didn't ~~ate~~. |
| She didn't **take** it. | She didn't ~~took~~ it. |

> ⚠️ **دي أشهر غلطة في المستوى ده كله.** فكّر فيها كده: كلمة **did** هي اللي شايلة الماضي، فالفعل
> بعدها مش محتاج يشيله تاني. الماضي بيتقال **مرة واحدة** في الجملة.

**Questions — put *did* first:**

- ***Did** you go?* → **Yes, I did.** / **No, I didn't.**
- ***What* did** you do?* · ***Where* did** you go?* · ***Who* did** you see?*

**Negatives — *didn't* + simple verb:**

- *I **didn't go** to work.* · *We **didn't have** time.*

**And the bonus:** because the verb loses its past form, you do **not** need to remember whether it was
regular or irregular. *Did you go?* and *Did you work?* follow the identical pattern.

> يعني القاعدة دي بتلغي كل صعوبة الأفعال الشاذة في السؤال والنفي، وده مكسب كبير بجهد صغير.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** Teach the "past is said once" idea — it is the mental model that stops the error. Then drill
> conversion: you say an affirmative, they turn it into a question and a negative. Twenty reps beats an
> explanation.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — *did* is weak inside a *wh-* question.**
It shrinks almost to /dəd/ and runs into the next word:

> *What did you* → **"whaddya"** · *Where did you* → **"wheredya"**

That is not lazy speech — it is normal speech. Saying each word fully makes you sound like a textbook.

**Target 2 — *didn't* is the opposite: keep the final /t/ clear.**
It is what makes the sentence negative.

> I **didn't** go. — drop the /t/ and your listener hears the opposite.

> النقطة المهمة: **did** بتخف في السؤال، لكن **didn't** لازم تتنطق واضحة.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"What did you do yesterday? I didn't go out."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Model the reduced *whaddya* — learners are often surprised it is correct. Then contrast with
> a crisp *didn't*.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — fix the mistake.**
1. *Did you went to the party?* → __________________________
2. *I didn't saw him.* → __________________________
3. *Where did she took the bus?* → __________________________

**Exercise 2 — make it a question.**
1. You saw the film. → __________________________
2. He came late. → __________________________
3. They ate together. → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — make it negative.**
1. I bought the tickets. → __________________________
2. She had breakfast. → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — short answers.**
1. *Did you sleep well?* → Yes, ________ / No, ________

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** Did you go · I didn't see · Where did she take.
> **Ex2:** Did you see the film? · Did he come late? · Did they eat together?
> **Ex3:** I didn't buy the tickets · She didn't have breakfast. **Ex4:** Yes, I did / No, I didn't.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **did / didn't + the simple verb.** The past is said **once**.
- *Did you go?* — never *Did you went?*
- Short answers: **Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.**
- *did* goes weak in questions; **didn't** stays crisp.
- This rule works for regular and irregular verbs alike.
