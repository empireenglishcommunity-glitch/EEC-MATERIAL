## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: an irregular verb → a *did* question → a *didn't* negative → a connector.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Fast retrieval across all three past units. Keep the pace high.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A clean holiday narration (📹 in the portal).

> Last summer I **went** to the North Coast with my family. We **took** the car and **left** very early,
> **so** we **didn't** sit in traffic. **One day** we **went** to the beach and **ate** fish at a small
> restaurant — it **was** delicious. **Suddenly** the weather **changed** and it **rained**, so we
> **came** back to the hotel. I **didn't** swim much, **but** I **had** a really good week.
> **In the end** it **was** the best holiday of the year.

**Nine sentences.** Count them: how many irregular verbs? How many negatives?

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for flow, once for the count. The counting keeps accuracy visible inside a fluency
> task.

---

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الحكاية الكاملة
1. ✅ **Set the scene** — *when* and *where*
2. ✅ **Three or more past verbs**, regular and irregular
3. ✅ **One negative** with *didn't*
4. ✅ **Two connectors** — *so · but · suddenly · in the end*
5. ✅ **A feeling or an opinion**
6. ⭐ **Answer a follow-up question** without preparing it

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Board the checklist and hand out the card. Point at item 6 — the unrehearsed answer is what
> distinguishes a speaker from a reciter, and it is what the rubric rewards.

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest past sound from these three units:

- **-ed endings** — /t/ · /d/ · /ɪd/
- **irregular vowels** — *went · saw · ate · bought*
- **weak *did*** in questions, crisp **didn't** in negatives
- **narrative rhythm** — beat on the verb, pause at the connector

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Personalised from your flags across U1–U3. One target each.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
Rehearse with the checklist and a partner, then record.

Tell me about your **last holiday or day off** in 7–9 sentences. Then answer **one question** I ask you
about it, live.

> 🎙️ **Record it and keep it.** 👑

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min recording + 2 feedback.** Ask each learner one genuine follow-up — *Who did you go with? Why
> didn't you swim?* — and let them answer unscripted.
> **Success check (Unit-3):** connected 7–9 sentence narration with mostly-correct past forms, plus a
> handled follow-up (rubric: Fluency + Accuracy + Pronunciation at A2). Play them their Stage-0 graduation
> clip afterwards if you have it — three months of past tense is audible.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Units 1–3 wrap)
- **was / were** for states · **-ed** for regular actions · **a list** for irregulars.
- **did / didn't + simple verb.** The past is said once.
- **One day · Suddenly · so · but · In the end** hold a story together.
- Stay in the past from the first sentence to the last.
- *(The past block is now complete. Unit 4 turns to the future.)*

---

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 3 mini-quiz
1. *Yesterday I ___ (go) to Cairo.* → **went**
2. *I ___ (buy) a new phone.* → **bought**
3. *___ you see him?* → **Did**
4. *She ___ come to the party.* (negative) → **didn't**
5. *Did you ___ dinner?* (eat) → **eat** *(not ate)*
6. Which word warns of a surprise? → **Suddenly**

## 🎉 Unit 3 complete — الماضي خلص كله
Three units ago you could only describe **now**. You can now say where you **were**, what you **did**, what
you **didn't** do, ask someone about their past, and tell a story with a beginning and an end.

**Next → Unit 4: Future Plans.** *going to* and *will* — the stage turns from behind you to ahead of you. 👑
