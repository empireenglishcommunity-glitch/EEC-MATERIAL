> [!TEACHER]

> **Objectives:** order with *Could I have* and *I'll have* · ask for the menu and the bill · sound polite.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Order food politely: *Could I have the chicken, please?*
- Ask for the menu, and ask for the bill.
- Handle the whole visit from sitting down to paying. 🎙️

> النهاردة موقف كامل من أوله لآخره — وده أول موقف حياتي بتقدر تديره بالإنجليزي لوحدك.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Ordering a meal is a complete, self-contained win. It has a beginning, an end, and a fixed set of sentences
— which means you can be genuinely good at it after one lesson.

> والأدب في الإنجليزي مش بيتعمل بكلمات زيادة، بيتعمل بالتركيب والنبرة.

*"Give me a coffee"* is one hundred per cent correct grammar and lands badly. Learners usually have no idea
they are coming across as blunt, which is exactly why it is worth saying out loud.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A full restaurant visit (📹 in the portal).

> **Waiter:** Good evening. A table for two?
> **Customer:** Yes, please. **Could we see** the menu?
> **Waiter:** Of course. … Are you ready to order?
> **Customer:** Yes. **Could I have** the grilled chicken, please? And **I'll have** a bottle of water.
> **Waiter:** Anything else?
> **Customer:** **Could we have** some bread as well, please?
> **Waiter:** Certainly.
> *(later)*
> **Customer:** That was lovely, thank you. **Could we have the bill**, please?

**Notice:** almost every request begins with **Could I / Could we**, and almost every one ends with
**please**.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Could we see the menu? | ممكن نشوف المنيو؟ |
| Could I have the chicken? | ممكن آخد الفراخ؟ |
| Anything else? | حاجة تانية؟ |
| Could we have the bill? | ممكن الحساب؟ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners count the *Could* requests. Then say a blunt version aloud —
> *"Give me chicken"* — and let them hear the difference for themselves.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The polite request pattern: *Could I have …, please?***

| | |
|---|---|
| **Could I have** | the chicken, please? |
| **Could we have** | the bill, please? |
| **Could I see** | the menu, please? |

> **Could** هي مفتاح الأدب في الإنجليزي كله، مش في المطعم بس. استخدمها في أي طلب — في الشغل، في محل،
> مع حد ماتعرفهوش. وهي بتشتغل لأنها بتحوّل الأمر لسؤال، فبتسيب للطرف التاني مساحة يرد.

**Two other ways to order, both natural:**

| Phrase | Feel |
|---|---|
| ***I'll have** the fish.* | deciding now — this is *will* from Unit 4 |
| ***I'd like** the soup.* | slightly more formal, always safe |

**The four things that make a request polite:**

1. **Could**, not *give me*
2. **please** — at the end, or after *Could I*
3. **rising intonation** ↗
4. **thank you** afterwards

> ⚠️ **الغلطة الشائعة:** *"I want the chicken"*. مش غلط قواعديًا، بس بتوصل مثل أمر. استخدم **Could I
> have** أو **I'll have**.

**The visit, in order:** *A table for two → Could we see the menu? → Could I have…? → Anything else? →
Could we have the bill?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Teach *Could I have* as the workhorse, then the two alternatives. Make the four politeness
> markers explicit — learners often have the words but flat intonation, which undoes them.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the request must RISE at the end.** This is where politeness actually lives:

> Could I have the chicken, please? ↗

Say the same words with a **falling** tone and it becomes an instruction. The words are identical; only the
tune changed.

**Target 2 — *Could* has a silent *l*.**

> *could* = /kʊd/ — like *good*, with a **k**. Not "kold".

**Target 3 — *please* is one syllable** with a long /iː/ and a soft /z/ at the end: *pleaz*.

> النبرة الصاعدة هي اللي بتخلي الطلب مؤدب. نفس الكلمات بنبرة نازلة بتبقى أمر.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"Could I have the bill, please?"* — once rising, once falling. Hear the
> difference in yourself.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The rise-versus-fall demonstration is the single most valuable thing in this lesson. Have
> every learner produce both and identify which sounds rude.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — make it polite.**
1. *Give me a coffee.* → __________________________
2. *I want the menu.* → __________________________
3. *Bring the bill.* → __________________________

**Exercise 2 — put the visit in order.** Number 1–5.
- ______ Could we have the bill, please?
- ______ A table for two, please.
- ______ Could I have the chicken, please?
- ______ Could we see the menu?
- ______ Anything else? — No, thank you.

**Exercise 3 — complete the order.** You want soup, grilled fish and a bottle of water.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** *Could I have a coffee, please?* · *Could I see the menu, please?* ·
> *Could we have the bill, please?* **Ex2:** 5 · 1 · 3 · 2 · 4.
> **Ex3:** check *please* is present and the tone rises.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **Could I have …, please?** is the polite workhorse — everywhere, not just restaurants.
- **I'll have** = deciding now · **I'd like** = always safe.
- *I want* sounds like an order. Avoid it.
- Politeness lives in the **rising tone** as much as in the words.
- **could** rhymes with *good*; the *l* is silent.
