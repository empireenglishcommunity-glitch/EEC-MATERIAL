---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Asking a price is probably the most useful transaction sentence in any language. And containers are how you
put a number on things you cannot count.

And notice that **How much?** does two jobs: it asks about an uncountable amount, and it asks a price. One
phrase, two uses.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
At a small grocery (📹 in the portal).

> **Customer:** **How much** is a **kilo of** tomatoes?
> **Seller:** Twenty-five pounds.
> **Customer:** OK. And **how many** eggs are in the box?
> **Seller:** Thirty. **How much** rice do you want?
> **Customer:** Two **kilos**, please. And a **bottle of** water and three **pieces of** bread.
> **Seller:** That's ninety pounds altogether.

**Notice:** *How much* appears twice — once for a **price** and once for an **uncountable amount**.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| How much is a kilo? | الكيلو بكام؟ |
| How many eggs? | كام بيضة؟ |
| a bottle of water | إزازة ماية |
| altogether | الإجمالي |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners identify which *How much* is which. Also drill *altogether* —
> it appears in every real transaction.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The choice follows the noun, exactly as in L02.**

| Question | Use with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **How many** | countable | *How many eggs?* |
| **How much** | uncountable | *How much rice?* |
| **How much** | **price** | *How much is it?* |

> يعني نفس القاعدة: معدود ياخد **many**، وغير معدود ياخد **much**. والزيادة الجديدة إن السعر كمان بياخد
> **How much**.

**Container words let you count the uncountable:**

| Container | Goes with |
|---|---|
| a **bottle** of | water · milk · oil |
| a **cup / glass** of | tea · coffee · water |
| a **piece** of | bread · cake · cheese |
| a **kilo** of | rice · meat · tomatoes |
| a **packet** of | sugar · biscuits |

**Note the plural sits on the container, not the food:** *two **bottles** of water*, never *two waters*.

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** الجمع بيتحط على العلبة أو الوحدة، مش على الأكل نفسه. بنقول *three pieces of bread*،
> مابنقولش *three breads*. والسبب إن اللي بيتعد هو الحتة أو الإزازة، مش المادة نفسها.

**Asking a price, three ways:**

- *How much is it?* · *How much are they?* · *How much does it cost?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Frame as transfer, then the containers, then the price forms. The plural-on-the-container rule
> is the one worth drilling — it is where the L01 uncountable rule actually gets used.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — numbers have to be unmistakable, because money depends on them.**
The pairs that cause real trouble:

| | |
|---|---|
| thir**TEEN** vs **THIR**ty | stress at the end vs the beginning |
| four**TEEN** vs **FOUR**ty | same |
| fif**TEEN** vs **FIF**ty | same |

The **-teen** numbers stress the **end**. The **-ty** numbers stress the **beginning**. That stress is the
only difference, and getting it wrong changes the price.

> النبرة هي الفرق الوحيد بين **13** و**30**. لو حطيتها غلط، السعر بيتغير.

**Target 2 — the question tune.** *How much is it?* falls at the end ↘, like every *wh-* question.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"How much is thirteen kilos? … No, thirty."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The teen/ty contrast is the priority — it has real consequences and learners are usually
> unaware they are producing it ambiguously.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — *How much* or *How many*?**
1. ______ sugar do you want?
2. ______ people are coming?
3. ______ is this bag?
4. ______ bottles do we need?

**Exercise 2 — add the container.**
1. two ______ of water 2. a ______ of tea 3. three ______ of bread 4. a ______ of rice

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *How many rice do you want?* → __________________________
2. *Can I have three breads?* → __________________________
3. *How much eggs are there?* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — say these clearly.** 13 · 30 · 14 · 40 · 15 · 50

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** How much · How many · How much · How many.
> **Ex2:** bottles · cup · pieces · kilo. **Ex3:** *How much rice* · *three pieces of bread* ·
> *How many eggs*. **Ex4:** check the stress placement on every one.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **How many** + countable · **How much** + uncountable · **How much** for price.
- Containers let you count: *a bottle of · a piece of · a kilo of*.
- The plural goes on the **container**: *two bottles of water*.
- **thirTEEN** against **THIRty** — the stress is the price.
