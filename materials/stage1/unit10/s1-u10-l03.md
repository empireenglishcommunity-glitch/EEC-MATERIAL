# Accent Clinic: Fix Your Top Three Sounds

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 10 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: all Stage-1 units
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit10-putting-it-together.md` · **S1-U10-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Identify 4 · Perceive 5 · Re-cue 5 · Produce 9 · Passage 4 · Free speech 2 · Compare 1.
> **Objectives:** identify each learner's top three weak sounds from tracker evidence · improve them from word to connected speech · self-monitor and self-correct.
> **Personalisation is non-negotiable.** Do not give every learner the same three sounds. Use actual flags from recordings; if no tracker data exists, diagnose with the passage before selecting targets.
> **One sound improved beats three sounds named.** Support learners may work on one high-impact contrast. Stretch learners carry all three into spontaneous speech and self-correct without a prompt.
> **Honesty guardrail:** the target is clearer and more intelligible than the learner's own earlier recording — never “native.” Accent identity is not a defect.
> **The clinic cycle:** perceive → locate → produce alone → word → phrase → sentence → spontaneous speech → compare. Skipping perception or comparison turns practice into repetition without evidence.
> **Arabic ~20%** — articulation cues may use Arabic; production stays English.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
A teacher cannot follow you forever. Self-monitoring can. Once you can hear a target, feel its mouth position,
and repair it inside a sentence, improvement no longer depends on a course timetable.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (evidence first)
Open your tracker or the notes from Units 1–9. Write three **specific** targets:

> not “pronunciation” → **final /t/ in can't**
> not “th” → **voiced /ð/ in than and this**
> not “endings” → **/ɪd/ in wanted and needed**

Rank them by impact: which one most often makes a word unclear or changes meaning?

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** If no reliable flags exist, use the seeded passage in Train as a diagnosis. Do not invent data
> or copy another learner's list.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع (perception before production)
For each selected target, listen to a correct version and a common substitution. Do **not** speak yet. Mark
which one you hear.

| Common target | Contrast |
|---|---|
| past *-ed* | *worked* /t/ · *played* /d/ · *wanted* /ɪd/ |
| /θ/ and /ð/ | *think / sink* · *then / den* |
| *can / can't* | /kən/ · /kænt/ |
| /p/ and /b/ | *pack / back* |
| /v/ and /f/ | *van / fan* |
| /ŋ/ ending | *sing / sing-guh* |
| clusters | *street / se-treet* |

**If you cannot hear the contrast reliably, slow down there.** The mouth cannot consistently repair a
difference the ear does not yet track.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Use A/B listening with eyes closed. A target only advances when the learner identifies at least
> four of five contrasts. Perception failure is information, not failure.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — الطريقة (physical cue cards)
Choose only the cards that match your targets.

| Target | Physical cue | Quick test |
|---|---|---|
| /θ/–/ð/ | tongue tip lightly between teeth; /ð/ adds voice | hand on throat |
| /p/–/b/ | close both lips; /p/ releases air, /b/ adds voice | tissue moves for /p/ |
| /v/–/f/ | top teeth touch lower lip; /v/ adds voice | hand on throat |
| *can/can't* | *can't* has full /æ/ and a closed final /t/ | partner's eyes closed |
| /ŋ/ | back of tongue closes and stays; no released /g/ | hold the final sound |
| cluster | build backwards: /t/ → /tr/ → /str/ → *street* | no inserted vowel |
| *-ed* | /ɪd/ after /t,d/; otherwise voice decides /t/ or /d/ | touch throat before ending |

> وقت الكلام، استخدم إشارة جسدية.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Re-cue, do not re-lecture. Learner states the cue and demonstrates the test. The cue must be
> usable without a mirror or teacher after today.

---

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك (the repair language)

| When you notice… | Do this |
|---|---|
| the word was unclear | pause, repeat the **whole phrase**, continue |
| a missing ending | slow the final word, not the whole sentence |
| a substituted sound | reset the mouth cue, then say the phrase once |
| uncertainty | record two versions and choose by listening |
| repeated failure | step back: sentence → phrase → word → sound |

A repair should be small: *“I can go — sorry, I **can't** go — on Tuesday.”* Correct, then continue. Do not
restart the entire answer.

---

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق (the nine-minute core)
For **each** target, run the same ladder:

1. **Hear** the contrast.
2. **Set** the physical cue.
3. Say the **sound** alone five times.
4. Say three **words**.
5. Say one **phrase**.
6. Say one **sentence**.
7. **Record and compare** with the model.
8. Repeat the sentence once, changing only the target.

**Required *-ed* lane for everyone:**

> /t/: *worked · watched · stopped*
> /d/: *played · called · lived*
> /ɪd/: *wanted · needed · visited*

Then personalise lanes two and three from your tracker.

> 🎧 **Checkpoint:** save the first and final attempt. Improvement you cannot compare is only a feeling.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~9 min.** Roughly three minutes per target. Support: six minutes on the highest-impact target, three on
> *-ed*. Stretch: final sentence carries all three targets and is produced without a written cue.

---

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب (seeded passage)
Read once for meaning, once for targets:

> **Last Thursday**, three friends **visited** the new theatre. They **walked** there because the train was
> **faster than** the bus. They **wanted** to leave at ten, but the final act **finished** late. “I **can't**
> miss the last train,” said Victor, **thinking** quickly. They **thanked** the staff and **ran** to the
> station. In the end, they **caught** it.

Underline words containing your targets. Read again, then speak for one minute about a recent evening without
the passage. When you notice a slip, repair the phrase and continue.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min passage + 2 min free speech.** The passage seeds *-ed, th, can/can't, /v/, /ŋ/* and clusters.
> Score only each learner's selected targets. Count spontaneous self-repairs as a success.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Clear and intelligible is the goal; “native” is not.
- Compare with your own past recording.
- The ladder: **hear → cue → sound → word → phrase → sentence → speech → compare**.
- One physical cue survives pressure better than a paragraph of theory.
- Self-correction keeps improvement alive after the teacher is gone.
