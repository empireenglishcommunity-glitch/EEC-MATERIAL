# Big Review 2: Future, Comparison, Quantity & Advice

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 10 · **Lesson 2** — *RETRIEVAL REVIEW*
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Units 4–7
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit10-putting-it-together.md` · **S1-U10-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Retrieval 5 · Model 5 · Error check 5 · Accent 4 · Combine 7 · Record 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** retrieve future, comparison, quantity, and advice/obligation · combine at least three areas in one talk · self-correct the top error in each.
> **Combination is the assessment.** A learner who can answer four isolated grammar questions has recognition. A learner who can compare two plans, quantify the difference, and advise a friend has usable range.
> **Use error inoculation, not a lecture.** Show the five plausible wrong forms — *I going to, more big, the most biggest, much people, should to* — and make learners repair them from memory.
> **Preserve meaning distinctions:** *going to* plan / *will* prediction or instant decision; *should* advice / *have to* necessity; *many* count / *much* uncount. These are choices, not merely forms.
> **Support:** mixed retrieval deck + four-part prompt. **Stretch:** sustain two minutes and include a reasoned recommendation.
> **Arabic ~20%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Range is what lets a conversation go somewhere. You can now discuss **when**, **which is better**, **how
much**, and **what someone should do** — four jobs that ordinary decisions require every day.

> المطلوب دمج الأدوات في كلام واحد.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (six-shot retrieval)
Give one original example of each, without notes:

1. a plan with *going to*
2. a prediction with *will*
3. a comparative
4. a superlative
5. a count and an uncount quantifier
6. advice with *should*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Accept meaning first, then form. Ask one learner to explain why they chose *going to* rather
> than *will* — retrieving the distinction is stronger than repeating it.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
Choosing between two weekend plans (📹 in the portal).

> I'm **going to** take a short trip next weekend, but I haven't chosen between Alexandria and Dahab.
> Alexandria is **closer than** Dahab, and there are **more trains**, so it **will probably** be easier. Dahab
> is **more expensive**, but it has **less traffic** and the beaches are **much quieter**. I don't have **much
> time**, so I think Alexandria is **the better** choice this weekend. My friend wants Dahab, but she **has
> to** work on Sunday. I think she **should** save it for a longer holiday.

One decision naturally uses all four areas: a plan, predictions, comparisons, quantities, and advice with a
reason.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Learners tally examples by area on first listen. On second, ask which sentence actually makes
> the recommendation convincing — it is the one that connects limited time to the advice.

## 🧩 The Five-Error Card — كارت الخمس أخطاء

Repair each before reading the correction.

| Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I going to travel.* | *I**'m** going to travel.* | *going to* needs **be** |
| *This one is more big.* | *This one is **bigger**.* | short adjective + *-er* |
| *It's the most biggest.* | *It's **the biggest**.* | one superlative marker |
| *There are much people.* | *There are **many** people.* | count plural → *many* |
| *You should to rest.* | *You should **rest**.* | modal + base verb |

> خلي عينك على معنى الاختيار كمان: **should** رأي ونصيحة، لكن **have to** ضرورة. الشكل الصحيح بمعنى غلط لسه
> غلط.

**The decision map:**

| Job | Choose |
|---|---|
| planned before speaking | **be going to** |
| prediction / instant decision | **will** |
| compare two / compare with all | **comparative / superlative** |
| count plural / uncount | **many/few / much/little** |
| advice / necessity | **should / have to** |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Learners repair all five aloud, then explain one meaning choice from the map. Do not expand
> into a full re-teach; any unresolved pattern becomes an individual review target before L05.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك (combine)

| Move | Frame |
|---|---|
| state the plan | *I'm going to…* |
| predict | *I think it will…* |
| compare | *X is cheaper / more useful than Y.* |
| add degree | *much better · a little cheaper* |
| quantify | *many people · much time · a few options · a little money* |
| recommend | *I think you should… because…* |
| state reality | *You have to… · You don't have to…* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق (three carry-over targets)

**1) Long-adjective stress stays inside the adjective:**

> ex-**PEN**-sive → more ex-**PEN**-sive · com-**FOR**-ta-ble → more com-**FOR**-ta-ble

Do not stress *more* and flatten the important word.

**2) *than* begins with /ð/** — tongue lightly between the teeth, voice on:

> *cheaper **than** · better **than** · more useful **than***

**3) *have to* changes in connected speech:**

> *have to* → /ˈhæftə/ — “hafta” · *has to* → /ˈhæstə/

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"This plan is more expensive than that one, but we have to choose today."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** One minute each, then the combined sentence. The goal is intelligible connected speech, not
> a performance of isolated sounds.

## 🏋️ Train — البروفة الكاملة
Your friend has one free weekend and a limited budget. Compare **two plans** and recommend one.

Your talk must contain:
- one future plan or prediction
- one comparative and one superlative
- one count or uncount quantity
- one piece of advice with a reason
- one necessity or lack of necessity

Partner listens for **combination** and checks the five-error card. Swap, then repeat after one correction.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** Do not let learners deliver five disconnected requirement sentences. Reward language that
> connects: *because, so, but*. Ask the partner which structure helped the recommendation rather than merely
> whether all boxes appeared.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- ***be* going to** needs *am/is/are*; **will** does not.
- Short adjective + **-er**; long adjective uses **more**; never both.
- **many/few** for count · **much/little** for uncount.
- **should + base** = advice · **have to + base** = necessity.
- Range means combining choices to express one idea.
