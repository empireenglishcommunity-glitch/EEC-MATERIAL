## 2️⃣ The Summative Quiz — الاختبار
Complete the Stage-1 quiz independently. It samples recognition and short production across:

- *was/were*, regular and irregular past, *did/didn't*
- *going to / will*
- comparatives and superlatives
- count/uncount quantity and restaurant language
- symptoms, *should*, *have to / don't have to / can't*
- travel requests and booking
- feelings, adverbs, and simple-versus-continuous

> [!TEACHER]
> **~10 min.** Use the committed portal/Google Form instrument and its set band. Preserve raw result and
> production responses. A quiz command exiting successfully is not evidence of speaking ability; it is one
> component only.

## 3️⃣ Speaking Task Setup — شكل المحادثة
You will complete three connected parts:

1. **Narration:** a past event, 6–8 connected sentences, then follow-ups.
2. **Role-play:** one ordinary A2 situation with an unexpected complication.
3. **Q&A:** plans, comparison, advice, routine/current change; ask one question back.

The five dimensions are **Fluency · Accuracy · Pronunciation · Range · Interaction**.

> You may ask for repetition or clarification. That is assessed interaction, not lost marks.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~3 min.** Explain only the format and rubric. Support route may use brief preparation notes while still
> covering every criterion; record that accommodation with the result.

## 5️⃣ The A2 Speaking Exchange — المحادثة

### Part A — Narration
Tell me about **a trip, problem, or important day** from the past. Set the scene, connect events, include one
negative, and say how it ended.

### Part B — Role-play
The assessor selects one: ticket office · pharmacy · restaurant · directions. Complete the task and respond
to one complication.

### Part C — Q&A
Discuss a future plan, compare two options, give advice with a reason, describe a routine and what is different
now, then ask one question back.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~8 min, recorded.** Follow the assessment suite's prompt and scoring procedure. Do not silently simplify
> after one error or escalate after strong performance. Score the whole sample against the five rubric
> dimensions and preserve evidence notes.

---

## 6️⃣ The A2 Milestone Sample — تسجيل التطور 🎬
Record the standard prompt under the before/after protocol's comparable conditions:

> **“Tell me about yourself, your family, and your typical day.”**

Then add the Stage-1 extension:

> **“Tell me about something you did recently and one plan you have for the future.”**

Speak naturally. Do not memorise or read a script. This recording is for comparison with your Day-1 and
Stage-0 samples, not for changing the assessment result.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Capture as a separate file. Match prompt and conditions as closely as the protocol requires.
> Log file link, date, level point, and consent tier. Never publish, edit into marketing, or transfer beyond
> that tier without explicit authorisation.
