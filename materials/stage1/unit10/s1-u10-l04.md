## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (mixed retrieval)
Complete four 20-second turns:

1. something you did last weekend
2. a plan for next month
3. advice for someone who is tired
4. a comparison between two places you know

Your partner asks one follow-up after each.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Keep it moving. This warms tense switching and follow-up handling, not topic depth.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
Observe one complete miniature exchange (📹 in the portal).

> **Coach:** Tell me about a journey that did not go as planned.
> **Learner:** Last month I took a bus to Alexandria. It left late because there was a problem…
> **Coach:** What did you do while you were waiting?
> **Learner:** I called my friend and bought some coffee. I didn't get angry because I had plenty of time.
> **Coach:** Now imagine we are at a ticket office. Buy a return ticket and ask about one rule.
> **Learner:** Good morning. Could I have a return ticket to Cairo, please? What time does the last train leave?
> **Coach:** It leaves at nine, but you have to book a seat.
> **Learner:** That's fine. Do I have to pay now?
> **Coach:** Last question: are you going to travel more next year?
> **Learner:** Yes, I'm going to visit Aswan. I think it will be quieter than Cairo, and…

The learner does not deliver one perfect speech. They understand a prompt, respond, handle new information,
and keep going.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Learners identify the three parts and one example of interaction in each. Ask where the coach
> introduced information the learner could not have memorised.

## 🧩 The A2 Rubric Card — كارت التقييم
Score each dimension **0–5** using evidence from the whole exchange.

| Dimension | At-threshold evidence |
|---|---|
| **Fluency** | connected short turns; pauses do not repeatedly stop the task |
| **Accuracy** | Stage-1 forms usually carry the intended time and meaning |
| **Pronunciation** | generally intelligible; key contrasts do not invert meaning |
| **Range** | uses past, future, comparison, quantity, and modal/functions as needed |
| **Interaction** | understands prompts, asks or answers follow-ups, repairs when needed |

**A strong A2 answer is not error-free.** It is connected, understandable, responsive, and broad enough to
complete ordinary tasks.

> ركّز على الدليل، مش الانطباع. بدل "كويس" اكتب: استخدم الماضي وربط الأحداث، أو طلب توضيح وكمل.

**Useful recovery lines:**

> *Could you say that again? · Do you mean…? · Let me think for a second. · Sorry, I mean…*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Read the actual rubric bands from the assessment suite when scoring; this card explains the
> dimensions to learners. Do not invent a new threshold in the room.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق (top-one tune-up)
Choose the **one** target from L03 most likely to affect meaning or intelligibility. Run one quick ladder:

> word → phrase → assessment sentence → one unrehearsed sentence

Then stop. A three-minute tune-up should settle the mouth, not fill the learner's head with monitoring.

> 🎧 Record the assessment sentence once. No repeated perfection loop.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~3 min.** Prioritise meaning-changing targets such as *can/can't* before cosmetic ones. Do not introduce
> a new sound on mock day.

## 🏋️ Train — البروفة الكاملة (pair mock)
Take turns as learner and assessor.

### Part 1 — Narration
Tell a connected 6–8 sentence story from one prompt:
- a journey that went wrong
- a day you were proud of
- a time you helped someone

Assessor asks **two** follow-ups.

### Part 2 — Role-play
Choose one:
- buy a ticket; one option is sold out
- describe symptoms at a pharmacy and ask what to do
- order for two people; one item is unavailable
- ask directions, then clarify one step

Assessor introduces one complication.

### Part 3 — Q&A
Answer across time: one question about your routine, one about a future plan, and one comparison or opinion.
Ask one question back.

Use the rubric card for **one evidence note + one next action** per partner.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~10 min.** Five minutes each. Keep assessor behaviour consistent: two narration follow-ups, one role-play
> complication, three mixed questions. Frames are permitted for the support route, not read as a script.

## 🎤 Your Turn — دورك (coach mock)
Complete one full mock with the coach, or observe one learner while scoring independently with the rubric.

> 🎙️ **Optional recording for private comparison — this is not the milestone clip.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min + 2 feedback.** Compare observer scores to the coach's evidence. Give a maximum of **two** polish
> targets: one language/interaction target and one clarity target.
> **Success check:** sustains all three parts at or near A2 threshold, uses a recovery strategy if needed,
> and leaves with a specific, manageable L05 plan.

---

## 📜 Your Orders — مهمتك
- Complete the rubric self-assessment using evidence, not feelings.
- Prepare topic ideas, **not memorised answers**.
- Practise only your two polish targets. Last-minute review of the whole stage increases noise and reduces
  control.

---

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Format: **narration → role-play → mixed Q&A**.
- The rubric measures **Fluency · Accuracy · Pronunciation · Range · Interaction**.
- A2 is connected and intelligible, not error-free.
- Repair lines are part of interaction, not an escape from it.
- The summative is the same shape as today; there is no trap waiting.
