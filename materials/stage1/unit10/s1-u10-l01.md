# Big Review 1: Past Narration

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 10 · **Lesson 1** — *RETRIEVAL REVIEW*
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Units 1–3 and 8
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit10-putting-it-together.md` · **S1-U10-L01***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Retrieval 5 · Model 5 · Error check 5 · Accent 5 · Build 6 · Record 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** retrieve *was/were*, regular and irregular pasts, and *did/didn't* · narrate a connected past account · self-correct the three patterned past errors.
> **Retrieval, not re-teaching.** Let learners produce before you show anything. A five-second struggle to retrieve *took* strengthens memory; supplying it immediately does not.
> **Correct patterns, not every slip.** Today's three are double-marked past after *did*, missing or wrong *-ed* endings, and irregular substitutions. Note anything else for later rather than breaking the story.
> **The output must be connected.** Eight accurate but unrelated past sentences do not meet the goal. Sequence and consequence — *first, then, because, so, in the end* — make narration.
> **Support:** retrieval deck + six-slot skeleton. **Stretch:** include direct speech, a feeling, and answer a follow-up without preparation.
> **Arabic ~20%** — reassurance and the error contrast only.

At Stage 0, you could name your routine. Now you can take a listener backwards through time: set the scene,
move events in order, explain what did not happen, and say how it ended. That is a different level of control.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (retrieval sprint)
Without opening a lesson, write the past of these verbs in 60 seconds:

> **go · take · see · come · buy · eat · have · make · get · leave**

Then sort five regular verbs by ending: **worked · played · wanted · watched · called**.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Answers: *went, took, saw, came, bought, ate, had, made, got, left*. Endings: /t/ *worked,
> watched* · /d/ *played, called* · /ɪd/ *wanted*. Learners answer before any model appears.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A connected past account (📹 in the portal).

> **Last Thursday**, I **left** work late because we **had** a problem with a customer. I **didn't have**
> time to eat, so I **bought** a sandwich at the station. **While I was waiting**, I **saw** an old school
> friend. We **took** the same train and **talked** all the way home. She **told** me she **was** starting a
> new job. **In the end**, the late train **wasn't** a disaster — it **gave** me someone back.

This is not a list. **Because** explains, **so** shows the result, **while** connects two past scenes, and
**in the end** closes the account.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** First listen for the event. Second listen: learners mark irregulars, regulars, a negative, and
> four connectors. Ask what makes the final sentence feel like an ending.

## 🧩 The Past Error Card — كارت أخطاء الماضي

**Error 1 — marking the past twice.**

> ✅ *I **didn't go**.* · *Where **did** you **go**?*
> ❌ *I didn't went.* · *Where did you went?*

After **did / didn't**, the main verb returns to its base. الماضي بيتحط مرة واحدة بس.

**Error 2 — using a regular ending on an irregular verb.**

> ✅ *went · took · saw · bought* · ❌ *goed · taked · seed · buyed*

**Error 3 — losing *-ed* when the story speeds up.**

> *worked* /t/ · *played* /d/ · *wanted* /ɪd/

The ending may be small, but it tells the listener **when** the event happened.

**Six slots build the narration:**

| Slot | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 1 | When and where? |
| 2 | What was the situation? — *was/were* |
| 3 | What happened first? |
| 4 | What happened next, and why? |
| 5 | What didn't happen? |
| 6 | How did it end or feel? |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Learners self-correct the three wrong examples before you explain. Keep the six-slot skeleton
> visible for Train, then remove the error card during the final recording.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك (retrieval)

| Job | Language |
|---|---|
| set the scene | *Last… · Two years ago… · When I was…* |
| describe it | *There was/were… · It was…* |
| move forward | *first · then · after that · while* |
| explain | *because · so* |
| negative / question | *didn't + base · Did… + base?* |
| close | *in the end · finally · the best part was…* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق (past endings)

Sort by the **sound**, not the spelling:

| /t/ | /d/ | /ɪd/ |
|---|---|---|
| worked · watched · stopped | played · called · lived | wanted · needed · visited |

**Rule you can feel:**
- /ɪd/ only after a final /t/ or /d/ — it creates an extra syllable.
- /t/ after a voiceless sound.
- /d/ after a voiced sound.

Speed round: teacher says the base; learner says the past and its sound. Then put three into one connected
sentence without losing the endings.

> النطق مش إضافة تجميلية هنا. لو النهاية اختفت، المستمع ممكن يسمع الحكاية كأنها بتحصل دلوقتي.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I worked late, called my friend, and waited at the station."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Do not ask learners to recite the rule first. Make them sort and say. Include *wanted* so they
> preserve the extra syllable and *worked* so they do not invent one.

## 🏋️ Train — البروفة الكاملة
Use a picture sequence or a real prompt. Build one story through the six slots with a partner.

**Requirements:**
- one *was/were* scene
- two irregular past verbs
- two audible regular endings
- one *didn't + base*
- three connectors

Partner listens with the error card and identifies **one pattern**, not every slip. Tell the story again with
the correction.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** First round with the skeleton, second without it. If feedback becomes a list, stop it and ask
> for the one error that repeated. Patterned correction transfers; scattered correction does not.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **did / didn't + base verb** — the past appears once.
- Irregulars must be retrieved: *went · took · saw · bought*.
- *-ed* has three sounds: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/.
- A story needs sequence and consequence, not just past verbs.
- Self-correction is evidence of control, not evidence of failure.
