This unit adds almost no new language. That is deliberate. Knowledge becomes ability when it can be retrieved,
combined, heard, repaired, and used with another person. The final campaign tests exactly that.

> أنت مش داخل تجمع قواعد أكتر. أنت داخل تشغّل اللي بنيته كله مع بعض.

**Mindset:** from **“I studied English”** to **“I use English.”**
**Arabic support:** ~20% in consolidation, fading to ~10% in the English-first finale.

## What A2 Evidence Looks Like — شكل الدليل
A2 is not an error-free performance. Across the whole exchange, the learner can:

- narrate a connected past event
- discuss a future plan
- compare options and talk about quantity
- give advice and distinguish it from necessity or prohibition
- complete an ordinary role-play and handle one complication
- ask, answer, clarify, repair, and keep interaction alive
- remain generally intelligible across the stage's target sounds

The governing threshold and full bands remain in `curriculum/05-assessment-suite.md`; this wrapper does not
replace them.

## The Prize — جايزة المرحلة
- An evidence-based **A2 determination**.
- Your extended A2 exchange and standard milestone recording.
- A precise handoff to Stage 2: one language/interaction target and one residual pronunciation target.
- Proof that the learner who once named a routine can now move across time, choices, needs, and live interaction.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons; L04 ~35 min, L05 ~40 min, plus this wrapper.
> **L01 and L02 are retrieval reviews.** Struggle before support is useful. Correct repeated patterns rather
> than interrupting every slip, and demand connected meaning rather than requirement sentences.
> **L03 must be personalised from evidence.** A generic “top three” defeats the design. If tracker evidence
> is missing, diagnose from the seeded passage before choosing. The target is clarity against the learner's
> own earlier recording, never native imitation.
> **L04 previews L05 exactly.** Keep prompt types, order, and rubric stable. Its output is a maximum of two
> polish targets, not a premature determination.
> **L05 separates assessment from proof capture.** The extended exchange is scored; the standard prompt is a
> second recording for comparable progress evidence. Follow the assessment suite and before/after protocol,
> record consent tier, and never treat a green technical check as evidence that a learner met A2.
> **Be exact and warm at once.** Evidence-first scoring and humane delivery are not opposites.

## Stage-1 Completion Check — تقييم المرحلة
- **Quiz:** mixed recognition plus short production across all Stage-1 targets.
- **Speaking:** extended narration + role-play + Q&A, scored on Fluency, Accuracy, Pronunciation, Range, and
  Interaction using the committed rubric bands.
- **Proof sample:** standard prompt recorded separately under comparable conditions.
- **Tracker:** determination, score evidence, clip links, consent tier, and Stage-2 carry-over flags.
