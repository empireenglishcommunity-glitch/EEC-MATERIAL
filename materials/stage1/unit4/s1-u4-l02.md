## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Ask about plans: *Are you going to travel?*
- Ask for detail: *What · Where · When are you going to…?*
- Answer briefly: *Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.* 🎙️

> عرفت تقول خططك. دلوقتي تسأل غيرك عن خططه.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Asking about someone's plans is one of the friendliest things you can do in a language. It is how
invitations start, how arrangements get made, and how a conversation moves forward instead of stalling.

> ولاحظ إن الحركة دي إنت عارفها خلاص: نفس القلب اللي عملته في الوحدة الأولى مع **was/were**. الفعل
> بيتقدم قبل الفاعل، وخلاص.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن
From L01, say two of your own plans out loud.

Then ask the person next to you — however you can — what **their** plan is.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Let them attempt the question before it is taught. Whatever they produce becomes the material
> you correct against.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two colleagues talking about the holiday (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** **Are you going to travel** in Eid?
> **B:** Yes, I am. I **'m going to go** to Hurghada with my family.
> **A:** Nice! **When are you going to leave**?
> **B:** On Thursday. **What about you? Are you going to stay** in Cairo?
> **A:** No, I'm not. I **'m going to visit** my grandmother in Assiut.

**Notice:** the question moves *are* to the front — *You are going* becomes *Are you going*.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Are you going to travel? | هتسافر؟ |
| Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. | أيوه / لأ |
| When are you going to leave? | هتمشي امتى؟ |
| What about you? | وإنت؟ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners mark the position of *are* in the statement versus the
> question. Also flag *What about you?* — it is the cheapest conversation-extender they will learn.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Yes/no questions — move *am/is/are* to the front.**

| Statement | Question |
|---|---|
| You **are** going to travel | **Are** you going to travel? |
| She **is** going to work | **Is** she going to work? |

> نفس القاعدة بالظبط اللي اتعلمتها في الوحدة الأولى: الفعل الأول، بعدين الفاعل.

***Wh-* questions — put the question word first, then invert:**

- ***What** are you going to do this weekend?*
- ***Where** are you going to go?*
- ***When** are you going to start?*
- ***Who** are you going to travel with?*

**Short answers — repeat the *be* verb, never the whole sentence:**

- *Are you going to come?* → **Yes, I am.** / **No, I'm not.**
- *Is he going to help?* → **Yes, he is.** / **No, he isn't.**

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** في الرد القصير بنقول *"Yes, I am"* ومابنقولش *"Yes, I'm"*. الاختصار مايجيش في آخر
> الجملة خالص.

**And one phrase worth more than any grammar here:** *What about you?* — it hands the conversation back.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Anchor to the Unit-1 inversion, then drill *wh-* forms. The *"Yes, I'm"* error is universal;
> correct it on the spot, every time.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — *are you* melts together.**
In natural speech it is barely two words:

> *Are you going to…* → **"əyə-gonna…"**

You do not need to produce it, but you must **recognise** it — otherwise a perfectly normal question will
sound like noise to you.

**Target 2 — rising intonation on yes/no, falling on *wh-*.**

> Are you going to travel? ↗
> What are you going to do? ↘

> السؤال بـ **yes/no** نبرته بتطلع، والسؤال بـ **wh-** نبرته بتنزل. الفرق ده بيوصل للسامع نوع السؤال قبل الكلمات.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** both questions, with the two different tunes.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The two-tune contrast is quick and memorable. Exaggerate both, then bring to natural.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — make it a question.**
1. You are going to study tonight. → __________________________
2. She is going to call him. → __________________________
3. They are going to move house. → __________________________

**Exercise 2 — add the question word.**
1. ______ are you going to travel? (place)
2. ______ are you going to leave? (time)
3. ______ are you going to do? (activity)

**Exercise 3 — short answers.**
1. *Are you going to come?* → Yes, ________ / No, ________
2. *Is he going to drive?* → Yes, ________ / No, ________

**Exercise 4 — fix it.** *"Yes, I'm."* → __________

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** Are you going to study tonight? · Is she going to call him? · Are they going to
> move house? **Ex2:** Where · When · What. **Ex3:** Yes, I am / No, I'm not · Yes, he is / No, he isn't.
> **Ex4:** *Yes, I am.*

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Move **am/is/are** to the front — the same move as Unit 1.
- *What · Where · When · Who* + inversion.
- **Yes, I am.** — never *Yes, I'm.*
- *What about you?* keeps a conversation alive.
- Yes/no rises ↗ · *wh-* falls ↘.
