# "I'm Going To…" — Plans & Intentions

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 4 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Units 1–3 (past) · present *to be*
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit4-future-plans.md` · **S1-U4-L01***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** talk about plans with *be going to* · use future time expressions · state 3–4 real plans.
> **The stage pivots here.** Three units of past, and now the future. Say that out loud — learners feel the shape of a course and it motivates them.
> **The structural insight worth teaching:** they already own *am/is/are* from Stage 0. *going to* is bolted onto something they can already do, so this is assembly, not new grammar.
> **Support:** the frame plus a verb bank. **Stretch:** add a reason with *because*.
> **Arabic ~35%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Plans are half of ordinary conversation. *What are you doing this weekend? · When are you going to
travel? · I'm going to look for a new job.*

> والحاجة الحلوة إن التركيب ده مش جديد عليك. إنت عندك **am / is / are** من المستوى اللي فات، وكل اللي
> بنعمله إننا نضيف عليها **going to** والفعل. يعني مش قاعدة جديدة، ده تركيب بيتبني على حاجة إنت عارفها
> خلاص، وعشان كده هتلاقيه أسهل من اللي فات.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن
Say three sentences about **yesterday**, using the past.

Now notice: you have no way yet to say what happens **tomorrow**.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Deliberately create the gap before filling it. Learners take a structure faster when they
> have just felt its absence.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Someone talks about their week ahead (📹 in the portal).

> This weekend I **'m going to visit** my parents in Tanta.
> Next week I **'m going to start** a new course, and I **'m going to look for** a better job.
> My sister **'s going to travel** to Dubai next month. We **'re going to miss** her.

**Notice:** *am/is/are* changes with the person, but **going to** never changes, and the verb after it stays
simple.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I'm going to visit my parents. | هزور أهلي |
| I'm going to start a course. | هبدأ كورس |
| She's going to travel. | هي هتسافر |
| We're going to miss her. | هنوحشنا |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners mark *am*, *is*, *are* and see that only that word moves.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The formula: *am / is / are* + **going to** + simple verb.**

| Subject | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **am** (I'm) | going to | study |
| he / she / it | **is** ('s) | going to | travel |
| you / we / they | **are** ('re) | going to | start |

> الجزء الوحيد اللي بيتغير هو **am / is / are** حسب الفاعل. أما **going to** والفعل بعدها فثابتين مهما
> كان الكلام عن مين.

**Use it for things you have already decided.** *going to* carries the sense of a **plan** — you have
thought about it, maybe arranged it:

- *I'm going to buy a car next year.* → I have decided
- *We're going to get married in June.* → it is arranged

**Future time expressions** go at the start or the end:

- **tomorrow · tonight · next week / month / year · this weekend · in two days**

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** الفعل بعد **going to** بيفضل في صيغته الأصلية. بنقول *going to **study***، مش
> *going to studied* ولا *going to studying*.

**Negative:** put *not* after *am/is/are* — *I'm **not** going to work tomorrow.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Build the formula on the board with the three subject rows, drill orally, then the negative.
> The "already decided" meaning matters for the L04 contrast with *will*, so plant it now.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — link it into one smooth phrase.** Nobody says four separate words:

> *I'm going to study* → **"I'm-gonna-study"**

**Target 2 — *gonna* is what you will HEAR.**
In fast speech *going to* becomes **gonna**. You do not have to say it that way, but you must be able to
**recognise** it, or you will miss the future tense in real conversation.

> إنت مش مطالب تقول *gonna*، بس لازم تعرفها وقت السمع. أغلب الناس بتقولها كده في الكلام العادي.

**Target 3 — the stress goes on the main verb**, not on *going*:

> I'm gonna **STUDY** tonight. · She's gonna **TRAVEL**.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I'm going to study tonight and I'm going to finish early."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Frame *gonna* as receptive first — it removes the anxiety of "am I allowed to say this?" and
> fixes a real listening gap.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — am, is or are?**
1. I ______ going to call him tonight.
2. She ______ going to start school in September.
3. They ______ going to move to a new flat.
4. We ______ going to be late.

**Exercise 2 — make the sentence.**
1. (I / visit / my uncle / tomorrow) → __________________________
2. (she / not / work / next week) → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *I'm going to studied tonight.* → __________________________
2. *He going to travel next month.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — true for you.** Three real plans, with a time word in each.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** am · is · are · are. **Ex2:** *I'm going to visit my uncle tomorrow* ·
> *She's not going to work next week*. **Ex3:** *going to study* · *He's going to travel* — the missing
> *is* is the frequent one.
