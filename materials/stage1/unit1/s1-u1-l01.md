**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 1 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Stage 0 (present *to be*)

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** use *was/were* to say where someone was · use past time expressions · produce 3–4 true past sentences.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Almost every real conversation includes the past. *"How was your day?"* · *"Where were you?"* ·
*"I was at work."* Without the past tense you can only describe this exact moment — and that is not how
people talk.

> ده أكبر قفزة في المستوى الجديد. الفرق بين إنك تقول "أنا في الشغل" وإنك تحكي يومك كله.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن
From Stage 0, say these out loud in the **present**:
- *I am at home.* · *She is a teacher.* · *They are busy.*

Now hold that thought — today we move all three into the past.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Fast retrieval of present *to be* from Stage 0. Do not teach yet; you are activating the
> pattern they already own so the past forms land as a change, not as new material.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Listen to three people answer the same question (📹 in the portal).

> **Q:** Where were you yesterday?
> **A1:** I **was** at work all day.
> **A2:** I **was** at my sister's house in Alexandria.
> **A3:** We **were** at home. It was a quiet day.

**Notice:** every answer uses **was** or **were** — never *am* or *is*.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Where were you yesterday? | كنت فين امبارح؟ |
| I was at work. | كنت في الشغل |
| We were at home. | كنا في البيت |
| It was a quiet day. | كان يوم هادي |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. First for meaning, second with learners underlining every *was/were*. Ask what
> changed from the warm-up — let them discover *am/is → was* and *are → were* before you show the table.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة
The past of *to be* has only **two** forms. That is all.

| Subject | Present | **Past** |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | **was** |
| he / she / it | is | **was** |
| you / we / they | are | **were** |

> القاعدة كلها في سطر واحد: **am / is** بيبقوا **was**، و**are** بيبقى **were**. مفيش استثناءات.

**Past time expressions** — put them at the start or the end of the sentence:

- **yesterday** · *Yesterday I was at work.*
- **last** night / week / month / year · *I was in Cairo last week.*
- **… ago** · *We were there two days ago.*
- **this morning** · *This morning I was tired.*

> كلمات الزمن دي هي اللي بتوضح إن الكلام عن الماضي. حطها في أول الجملة أو في آخرها — الاتنين صح. ومن
> غيرها السامع مش هيعرف إنت بتتكلم عن امتى.

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** بنقول *we **were***، مش *we was*. الغلطة دي أشهر غلطة في المستوى ده.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Teach the two-form table in English — it is genuinely simple and they can handle it at A2.
> Reserve Arabic for the *we was* warning, which is the error that actually costs them marks.
> **Watch for:** learners over-applying *was* to plurals. Drill *we/they were* three extra times.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the /w/ in *was* and *were*.**
Round your lips before the sound. Arabic speakers often soften it toward a /v/.

- **Feel it:** *was · was · was* — lips forward, then relax.
- **Compare:** *was ✅ → vas ❌*

**Target 2 — the weak *was* /wəz/.**
In a real sentence, *was* is almost never stressed. The **adjective** carries the stress:

> I was **tired**. · She was **late**. · They were **happy**.

Say it fast and let *was* shrink. That single habit makes you sound far more natural.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"Yesterday I was tired, but today I'm fine."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Drill /w/ with rounded lips, then contrast a stressed *was* (unnatural) against the weak
> /wəz/ (natural). Praise any shrinking of the vowel — that is the A2 rhythm win.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — was or were?**
1. I ______ at the office yesterday.
2. They ______ very tired last night.
3. She ______ my English teacher last year.
4. We ______ at my mother's house.
5. It ______ a long day.

**Exercise 2 — add a time expression.** Rewrite each sentence with a past time word.
- *I am at home.* → __________________________
- *They are busy.* → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — true for you.** Complete honestly.
- Yesterday I was ______________.
- Last weekend I was ______________.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** was · were · was · were · was. **Ex2:** accept any correct past form plus a time
> expression. **Ex3:** circulate and check *was/were* choice, not content.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **am / is → was** · **are → were**. Two forms, no exceptions.
- Never *we was* — always **we were**.
- *was* is weak in a sentence; the adjective takes the stress.
