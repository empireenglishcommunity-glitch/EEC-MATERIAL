## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** by the end of Unit 1 you'll **talk about the past** — say where you and others were,
> describe a place from your memory, say how an experience felt, and ask someone about theirs.
> مهمتك في الوحدة دي: تتكلم عن اللي فات، وتوصف مكان من ذكرياتك، وتقول إحساسك عن تجربة.

**Why this is your first A2 campaign:** in Stage 0 you could describe **now**. Everything else — stories,
memories, explanations, excuses — needs the past. This unit opens all of it with a verb that has only
**two** forms.

> ده أكبر فرق بين المستوى اللي فات والمستوى ده.

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | I was, you were | use *was/were* · past time expressions (*yesterday, last week, … ago*) |
| **L02** | wasn't · weren't · Were you…? | negatives · questions by inversion · short answers |
| **L03** | There was / There were | describe what existed in a past place · singular vs plural |
| **L04** | How was it? | opinion adjectives · *because* + a reason · natural reactions |
| **L05** | Weekend account (milestone) 🎙️ | a connected 5–7 sentence past account + **record your milestone** |

**Grammar spine:** *was / were* — affirmative, negative, questions, short answers — plus
*there was / there were*.
**Accent Lab:** /w/ in *was·were* · the weak *was* /wəz/ · the final /t/ in *wasn't·weren't* · linking
*there-wuz-a* · stress on opinion words.
**Mindset:** momentum into a new stage — a new level is exactly where people quietly stop, so lock your
daily slot again.
**Arabic support:** moderate (~40%), fading to ~30% by L05.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Unit-1 weekend account** — the first recording where you talk about your past.
- A pair worth keeping: play your Stage-0 graduation clip, then this one.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (~30 min each, L05 ~35) plus this wrapper. Grammar spine is *was/were* in
> every form. The Arabic dial drops sharply from Stage 0 — explain in English first and reserve Arabic for
> the two errors that actually cost marks.
> **The two errors to hunt all unit:** subject agreement (***we was***) and existential plural agreement
> (***there was two windows***). Both are habits rather than misunderstandings, so they need repetitions in
> warm-ups, not more explanation.
> **The thing that most raises their score** is *because* + a reason, introduced in L04. Insist on it even
> when the surrounding grammar is imperfect — connected speech is the A2 threshold.
> **L05 is a proof asset.** Capture every learner's recording with consent and hold it beside their Stage-0
> graduation clip; that pair is the clearest evidence of progress the programme produces.
