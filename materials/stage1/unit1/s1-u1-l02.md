# Negatives & Questions: *wasn't · weren't · Were you…?*

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 1 · **Lesson 2**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L01
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit1-was-and-were.md` · **S1-U1-L02***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** make past *to be* negative · ask past questions with inversion · give short answers · hold a short past Q&A.
> **Watch for:** learners keeping statement word order — *"You were at home?"* with rising tone only. Accept it as a stepping stone, then model the inversion.
> **Support:** give the short-answer cards to read from. **Stretch:** add a follow-up — *"Why? Where were you?"*
> **Arabic ~40%.** The form is simple; spend the time on the Q&A drill, not on explanation.

---

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Say what **wasn't** true: *I wasn't at home.*
- **Ask** past questions: *Were you at work?*
- Answer briefly and naturally: *Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.* 🎙️

> هتعرف تنفي، وتسأل، وترد بسرعة — تلات حاجات بتخليك تعمل محادثة حقيقية عن اللي فات.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
A conversation is not one person making statements. It is questions and answers. Once you can **ask**
about the past, you can start a conversation instead of only responding to one.

> اللي بيسأل هو اللي بيقود الكلام.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
A short exchange between two friends (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** **Were** you at the party last night?
> **B:** No, I **wasn't**. I **was** at home with my family.
> **A:** Oh! **Was** it a long day at work?
> **B:** Yes, it **was**. I was really tired.

**Notice:** the question puts **were** *before* **you** — that swap is the whole grammar of this lesson.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Were you at the party? | كنت في الحفلة؟ |
| No, I wasn't. | لأ، ماكنتش |
| Was it a long day? | كان يوم طويل؟ |
| Yes, it was. | أيوه، كان |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time, learners mark where the verb sits in the question versus the
> statement. Make the inversion visible before naming it.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**1) Negative — just add *not*, then contract it.**

| Full | Contracted |
|---|---|
| I was **not** at home | I **wasn't** at home |
| They were **not** ready | They **weren't** ready |

> في الكلام العادي بنستخدم **wasn't** و**weren't** دايمًا. الصيغة الكاملة تستخدمها بس لو عايز تأكيد قوي.

**2) Question — swap the subject and the verb.**

| Statement | Question |
|---|---|
| You **were** at work | **Were** you at work? |
| It **was** cold | **Was** it cold? |

> ⚠️ **القاعدة:** الفعل الأول، بعدين الفاعل. مش بنستخدم *do* ولا *did* مع الفعل ده خالص.

**3) Short answers — never a bare *yes* or *no*.**

- *Were you tired?* → **Yes, I was.** / **No, I wasn't.**
- *Was she there?* → **Yes, she was.** / **No, she wasn't.**

> بنكرر الفاعل والفعل في الرد. *"Yes"* لوحدها بتبان جافة شوية، والرد الكامل بيبان مؤدب أكتر.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Three short blocks, each with an immediate oral drill. **Watch for:** learners answering
> *"Yes, I were"* — the short answer must agree with the subject, same as the statement.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the final /t/ in *wasn't* and *weren't*.**
Do not swallow it. The /t/ is what tells the listener the sentence is **negative** — drop it and you have
said the opposite of what you meant.

- *was ↔ **wasn't*** · *were ↔ **weren't***
- Say it slowly, then at speed, keeping the /t/ audible.

**Target 2 — rising intonation on yes/no questions.**

> Were you at work? ↗ · Was it cold? ↗

The voice goes **up** at the end. A flat tone sounds like a statement, and your listener will not know
you asked anything.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"Were you at home last night? No, I wasn't."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The final /t/ is the priority — a dropped /t/ inverts the meaning. Then drill the rising
> tone in pairs, exaggerated first, then natural.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — make it negative.**
1. I was at the meeting. → __________________________
2. They were happy. → __________________________
3. It was expensive. → __________________________

**Exercise 2 — make it a question.**
1. You were at university. → __________________________
2. She was your teacher. → __________________________
3. They were at home. → __________________________

**Exercise 3 — short answers.**
1. *Were you busy yesterday?* → Yes, ____________ / No, ____________
2. *Was the food good?* → Yes, ____________ / No, ____________

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** wasn't at the meeting · weren't happy · wasn't expensive.
> **Ex2:** Were you at university? · Was she your teacher? · Were they at home?
> **Ex3:** Yes, I was / No, I wasn't · Yes, it was / No, it wasn't.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **wasn't · weren't** — and the final **/t/** is what makes it negative.
- Questions **swap** the verb and the subject. No *do*, no *did*.
- Short answers repeat the subject and verb: **Yes, I was.**
- *(These forms come back in Unit 3 with did/didn't.)*
