## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
*"How was your weekend?"* is probably the most common question in everyday English. Answering it with one
word ends the conversation. Answering it with an opinion and a reason **continues** it.

> الرد بكلمة واحدة بيقفل الكلام. الرد بإحساس وسبب بيفتحه.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two colleagues on a Sunday morning (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Hi! **How was** your weekend?
> **B:** It **was** great, thanks! I **was** with my family in Alexandria.
> **A:** **Really?** That's nice. **How was** the weather?
> **B:** It **was** hot, but the sea **was** beautiful. **How was** yours?
> **A:** Not bad. It **was** a bit boring, actually — I **was** at home all weekend.
> **B:** Oh, **that's a pity**.

**Notice:** every answer has an **opinion word**, and the other person always **reacts** before asking again.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| How was your weekend? | إيه أخبار الويكند؟ |
| It was great, thanks. | كان جميل، شكرًا |
| Really? That's nice. | جدًا؟ حلو |
| It was a bit boring. | كان ممل شوية |
| That's a pity. | يا خسارة |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time, learners mark the three reactions. Point out that B answers **and
> asks back** — that reciprocity is what makes it a conversation.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**1) The question is just *was/were* plus *how*.**

- **How was** your weekend / your day / the food / the trip?
- **How were** the people / your exams?

> نفس الفعل اللي اتعلمته، بس بنحط **how** قبله.

**2) The answer is *was/were* plus an opinion word.**

| Positive | Negative | Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| great · fun · delicious · beautiful | boring · tiring · difficult · terrible | okay · not bad · quiet |

> *It was **tiring**.* · *The food was **delicious**.* · *It was **not bad**.*

**3) Soften or strengthen it.**

- **a bit** + negative word — *It was **a bit** boring.* (politer)
- **really** + any word — *It was **really** good.*

**4) Add *because* + a reason.** This is the step that makes you sound A2 rather than A1:

> *It was tiring **because** I worked on Saturday.*
> *It was great **because** I was with my family.*

**5) React before you reply.**

| They say | You react |
|---|---|
| something good | **Really? · That's great! · That's nice.** |
| something bad | **Oh, that's a pity. · I'm sorry to hear that.** |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Move fast — the grammar is already known. The new content is *because*, the softener *a bit*,
> and the reactions. Drill the reactions chorally so they become automatic sounds.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — stress the opinion word, hard.**
The feeling lives in the adjective, so that is where your voice rises and lengthens:

> It was **GREAT**! · It was a bit **BOR**ing. · The food was **deLIcious**.

Say the sentence flat and it sounds insincere — as if you did not mean it.

**Target 2 — the reaction *"Really?"***
Stress the **first** syllable and let the tone rise: **REAL**ly? ↗

Flat and it sounds sarcastic. Rising and warm, it sounds interested.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"It was great, because I was with my family."* — then *"Really? That's nice!"*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Have them say *"It was great"* twice — once flat, once stressed — and ask which one they
> would believe. Then drill *"Really?"* until it sounds warm rather than flat.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — choose the opinion word.**
1. I worked twelve hours. It was ______________. (tiring / delicious)
2. The restaurant was excellent. The food was ______________. (boring / delicious)
3. I was at home alone all day. It was a bit ______________. (fun / boring)

**Exercise 2 — add *because* and a reason.**
1. My weekend was great ______________________________.
2. Monday was difficult ______________________________.

**Exercise 3 — react.**
1. *"My weekend was wonderful!"* → ______________________
2. *"I was sick last week."* → ______________________

**Exercise 4 — full exchange.** Write your own three-line dialogue: question, answer with a reason,
reaction.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** tiring · delicious · boring. **Ex2:** accept any sensible reason with correct
> *was/were*. **Ex3:** *Really? That's great!* · *Oh, that's a pity. / I'm sorry to hear that.*
> **Ex4:** check the reaction is present — learners skip it first time.

> [!TEACHER]

> reaction. The reason is the thing to insist on — accept imperfect grammar around it.
