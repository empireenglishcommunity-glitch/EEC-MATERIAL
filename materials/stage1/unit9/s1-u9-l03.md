# *Usually* vs *Now*: Present Simple vs Continuous

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 9 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L02 · Stage-0 U4 & U9
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit9-people-feelings-everyday.md` · **S1-U9-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 7 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** contrast habit (present simple) with now (present continuous) · use the time markers · describe what you usually do against what you are doing these days.
> **What is new here is not the continuous — it is *temporary*.** Stage 0 taught the continuous for this second (*I'm eating*). This lesson extends it to a **temporary period**: *this week I'm working from home*. That extension is the whole lesson, and it is the use learners never acquire by themselves.
> **Anchor the choice to the time marker, not to a feeling about the tense.** Arabic speakers reliably over-use the simple for now. *Usually / every day / always* → simple. *Now / at the moment / this week / today* → continuous. Give them the marker and the tense follows; that is a rule they can actually apply under pressure.
> **The *-ing* ending is /ŋ/, with no hard /g/.** *Working*, not *"workingg"*. It appears in every sentence of this lesson, so it is worth the four minutes.
> **Support:** the sort activity plus the time-marker list. **Stretch:** add a reason and a feeling about the change.
> **Arabic ~25%.**

> ودي أول مرة تقدر تفرق بين حاجة بتعملها دايمًا وحاجة بتعملها الفترة دي بس.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
English forces a choice that Arabic does not always force: is this your **life**, or is this **today**?
*I work in an office* is who you are. *I'm working from home* is this week only. Same job, different sentence.

> والمفتاح إنك تسمع كلمة الوقت وتمشي وراها: **usually** يبقى بسيط، و**now** أو **this week** يبقى مستمر.
> متختارش بالإحساس.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Catching up with someone (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** What do you do?
> **B:** I'm a teacher. I **usually work** in a school in Giza.
> **A:** Usually?
> **B:** Well, **this month I'm working** online — they're repairing the building. **At the moment I'm
> teaching** from my bedroom.
> **A:** How is that?
> **B:** Strange! **I normally see** thirty students in a room. **These days I'm looking** at thirty little
> squares.
> **A:** And do you like it?
> **B:** Not really. But **it's only** for a few weeks.

**Notice:** *usually work · normally see* — his real life. *this month I'm working · at the moment I'm
teaching · these days I'm looking* — a temporary situation, and every one of them is marked by a time phrase.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I usually work in a school. | عادةً بشتغل في مدرسة |
| This month I'm working online. | الشهر ده بشتغل أونلاين |
| At the moment… | في الوقت الحالي… |
| These days… | الأيام دي… |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners write the time phrase from each sentence and the tense next
> to it. The pairing is the takeaway.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**1) Present simple — your life. What is generally true.**

> *I **work** in an office. · She **drinks** tea every morning.*

**Its time words:** *usually · normally · always · often · every day · never*

**2) Present continuous — *am/is/are* + *-ing*. Two jobs.**

| Job | Example |
|---|---|
| happening **this second** | *I**'m talking** to you now.* |
| happening for a **temporary period** ⭐ | ***This week** I**'m working** from home.* |

**Its time words:** *now · right now · at the moment · today · this week · these days*

> ⚠️ **الجديد في الدرس هو التاني:** المستمر مش بس للحظة دي، هو كمان لفترة مؤقتة. *This month I'm working
> online* — الشهر ده بس، مش دايمًا. ولو الحالة مدتها شهر كامل، برضه مؤقتة؛ المهم إنها مش حياتك العادية
> وهتنتهي. كلمة الوقت هي اللي بتكشف الفرق.

**3) The pair sentence — this is the shape to master.**

> *I **usually** *****drive***** to work, **but this week** I**'m taking** the metro.*
> *She **normally** *****cooks*****, **but today** she**'s ordering** food.*

**Habit + *but* + temporary.** One sentence, both tenses, and the contrast does the work.

**4) A small warning.** Some verbs describe a state, not an action, and they stay simple even with *now*:

> *I **know** him.* ✅ · *I'm knowing him.* ❌
> *I **want** coffee.* ✅ · *I'm wanting coffee.* ❌

**The common ones:** *know · want · like · need · understand · have* (own)

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Two minutes on the two tenses, two on the temporary use, two on the pair sentence. Keep the
> stative-verb note to a list of six and move on — recognition is enough at A2.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Part 1 — *-ing* is /ŋ/, and there is no /g/ at the end.**

The back of your tongue closes against the soft palate and **stays** there. You do not release it into a *g*.

> *work-ing* /ˈwɜːkɪŋ/ · *go-ing* /ˈɡəʊɪŋ/ · *do-ing* /ˈduːɪŋ/

**The test:** say *"sing"* and hold the last sound. If you hear *"sing-guh"*, you released it. Freeze instead.

> ودي بتغير شكل كلامك كله، لأن كل جملة في الدرس ده فيها **-ing**.

**Part 2 — stress the time marker when you are contrasting.** The contrast is the meaning:

> *I **USUALLY** drive, but **THIS WEEK** I'm taking the metro.*

Say it once flat, then again with those two phrases pushed. The second version makes the contrast audible
without any extra words.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I usually work in an office, but these days I'm working from home."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Two minutes on /ŋ/ with the hold-and-freeze test, two on contrastive stress. Both are
> immediately audible in the recording, so flag them there.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — simple or continuous?**
1. I ______ (get up) at six every day.
2. Look — it ______ (rain)!
3. She ______ (usually / take) the bus, but today she ______ (walk).
4. These days I ______ (study) for an exam.
5. He ______ (know) the answer.

**Exercise 2 — make a pair sentence.** Use *usually … but this week …* for:
1. drive / take the metro 2. cook at home / eat out 3. work in the office / work from home

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *I am working in a bank. It's my job for ten years.* → __________________________
2. *Every day I am going to the gym.* → __________________________
3. *I'm wanting a new phone.* → __________________________
4. *At the moment I study English.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — true for you.** Two habits, then two things you're doing **these days** that are not permanent.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** get up · is raining · usually takes / is walking · am studying · knows.
> **Ex3:** *I **work** in a bank* · *Every day I **go*** · *I **want*** · *At the moment I**'m studying***.
> Ex3.1 is the most useful — a permanent job needs the simple, however "now" it feels.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **Simple** = your life. **Continuous** = now, **or a temporary period**.
- *usually · every day · always* → simple. *now · at the moment · this week · these days* → continuous.
- The pair sentence: *I **usually** X, **but this week** I**'m** Y-ing.*
- *know · want · like · need · understand* stay simple.
- *-ing* ends in /ŋ/ — freeze it, do not release a /g/.
