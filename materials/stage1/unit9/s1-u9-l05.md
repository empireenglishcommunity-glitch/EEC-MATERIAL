# Unit Speaking Task: A Two-Minute Chat About Your Week (+ Review)

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 9 · **Lesson 5** — *UNIT MILESTONE*
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live (assessment-style) · Prerequisite: L01–L04
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit9-people-feelings-everyday.md` · **S1-U9-L05***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 5 · Model 4 · Checklist 4 · Accent clinic 4 · Rehearse 7 · Record 5 · Feedback 2 · Assign —.
> **Objectives:** sustain a roughly two-minute chat · combine feelings, adverbs, and usually-versus-now · ask back, follow up, react, and close naturally · record the Unit-9 milestone.
> **This is the most conversational task in Stage 1.** Score interaction as seriously as accuracy. A learner who produces perfect sentences without reacting is delivering parallel monologues, not having a conversation.
> **The partner must introduce one unpredictable detail.** Without it, reactions can be memorised. A genuine *Really? Why?* after new information is stronger evidence of A2 than another correct present-continuous sentence.
> **Do not rescue silence immediately.** Give learners two seconds to reach for a follow-up. Interaction needs retrieval time before it becomes automatic.
> **Support:** checklist and phrase card. **Stretch:** sustain beyond two minutes with two genuine follow-ups and no teacher prompt.
> **Arabic ~20%** — this should sound like English being used, not displayed.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain: a feeling → a personality adjective → an adverb → a *usually … but these days …* sentence → a
small-talk opener.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** No repeats. The chain retrieves every lesson before learners combine them under interaction
> pressure.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A natural chat between two classmates (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Hey, **how's it going**?
> **B:** Pretty good, thanks. **How about you?**
> **A:** A bit tired, honestly. I'm working late this week.
> **B:** **Really? Why?**
> **A:** We usually finish at five, but **these days we're preparing** a big event, so I'm leaving at eight.
> **B:** That sounds tiring. Are you enjoying it?
> **A:** Actually, yes. My team works **really well** together, and my manager explains everything **clearly**.
> What about you — how's your week going?
> **B:** Quiet. I normally study at home, but **this week I'm studying** with a friend. It's much better.
> **A:** Nice! Does your friend study quietly?
> **B:** Not at all — she talks constantly! Anyway, I have to go. **Good talking to you.**
> **A:** You too. See you tomorrow.

**Notice what makes this a conversation:** each person answers, asks back, reacts to new information, and
follows it. The grammar is accurate, but the interaction is what keeps the two minutes alive.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once without interruption. Second pass: learners count return questions, reactions, and
> follow-ups. There are no speeches longer than three sentences.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست المحادثة
1. ✅ **Open naturally** — *How's it going? · How's your week going?*
2. ✅ **Name a real feeling** and say why
3. ✅ Use one **adverb** — *well · carefully · slowly · clearly*
4. ✅ Contrast **usually** with **this week / these days**
5. ✅ **Ask back** — *How about you?*
6. ✅ **React + follow up** — *Really? Why? · That sounds… · What happened?*
7. ✅ **Close naturally** — *Anyway, I have to go. Good talking to you.*
8. ⭐ Handle one detail you did not know before the conversation

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Items 5, 6, and 8 are the assessment heart. Grammar can be rehearsed; interaction only appears
> when the learner listens and responds.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- feelings carried by pitch — *excited* should not sound *bored*
- adjective endings — *interested · worried · nervous*
- the **-ly** syllable — *carefully · clearly · slowly*
- **-ing** /ŋ/ with no released /g/
- small-talk chunk rhythm — *How's it going? · How about you?*

Then rehearse one reaction so it sounds real rather than read:

> *Really?* ↗️ · *That sounds **exhausting**.* ↘️ · *What happened?* ↗️

> 🎧 **Record & compare** one weak sound and one reaction.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** One personal target per learner. Do not run all five as a class list. The reaction line matters
> because flat reactions are the main reason a technically correct chat still sounds staged.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
With a partner, hold a chat for **about two minutes**.

Talk about how you feel, how your week is going, what you usually do, and what is different these days. Use
one adverb. **Ask back, react to something new, ask a follow-up, and close naturally.**

> 🎙️ **Record it and keep it.** 👑

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min recording + 2 feedback.** Secretly give each partner one detail to introduce — *a new job, a bad
> journey, an exam tomorrow* — so the other's reaction cannot be scripted. Allow two seconds before
> prompting a silence.
> **Success check (Unit-9):** a coherent roughly two-minute chat containing a precise feeling, one correct
> adverb, a simple/continuous contrast, and visible interaction through asking back, reacting, and following
> up (rubric: Fluency + **Interaction** + Pronunciation at A2).

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 9 wrap)
- ***What's* someone *like*?** asks about personality; ***How are* they?** asks about now.
- **-ed** is the feeling; **-ing** causes it: *bored / boring*.
- adjective + **-ly**; but **good → well · fast → fast · hard → hard**.
- **Simple** for the usual; **continuous** for now or a temporary period.
- Small talk lives on **answer + ask back + react + follow up**.
- A natural close is a phrase, not an escape: *Anyway, I have to go. Good talking to you.*

---

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 9 mini-quiz
1. *What's your manager ___?* (personality) → **like**
2. *I'm ___ because this film is ___.* (bore) → **bored / boring**
3. *She speaks English ___.* (good) → **well**
4. *He works very ___.* (hard) → **hard**
5. *I usually ___ in an office, but this week I___ from home.* (work) → **work / 'm working**
6. *At the moment she ___ for an exam.* (study) → **is studying**
7. After *"Pretty good, thanks"*, keep the talk open with: → **How about you?**
8. Ask to record information for an unavailable person: → **Can I take a message?**
