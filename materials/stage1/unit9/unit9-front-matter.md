## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** by the end of Unit 9 you'll **hold an everyday conversation** — describe people and
> feelings precisely, say how things happen, contrast ordinary life with what is temporary, manage a phone
> call, and keep small talk moving by listening and responding.
> مهمتك في الوحدة دي: متقولش جمل وخلاص؛ اسمع، رد، اسأل، وخلّي الكلام يكمل.

**Why this unit changes the game:** every earlier unit gave you language for a topic. This one teaches the
behaviour of conversation itself — asking back, reacting, following up, and closing naturally. That is the
line between someone who knows English and someone who **uses** it.

> هنا الإنجليزي بيبطل يبقى إجابات منفصلة، ويبدأ يبقى كلام بين شخصين.

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Feelings & personality | *What's he like?* · *How is he?* · **bored / boring** |
| **L02** | Adverbs of manner | say **how** — *carefully · well · hard* — in the right position |
| **L03** | *Usually* vs *now* | separate a habit from now and from a temporary period |
| **L04** | Phone & small talk | open → ask → message → close · answer **and ask back** |
| **L05** | Two-minute chat (milestone) 🎙️ | sustain real interaction + **record your milestone** |

**Grammar spine:** adverbs of manner, including *good → well* · present simple against present continuous,
extended from right now to temporary periods · phone and small-talk functions.
**Accent Lab:** feeling intonation · clear adjective and *-ly* endings · *-ing* /ŋ/ · natural rhythm in fixed
phrases and reactions.
**Mindset:** thinking in English starts by reaching for a feeling or phrase directly, before translating.
**Arabic support:** ~25% at L01, fading to ~20% by L05.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Unit-9 two-minute chat** — the most conversational proof clip in Stage 1.
- The ability to keep a real exchange alive instead of waiting for the next question.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (L05 ~35 min, the rest ~30) plus this wrapper.
> **L01 has two high-value contrasts.** *What's he like?* asks personality; *How is he?* asks current state.
> And **-ed** describes the feeling while **-ing** describes its cause. The sentence *"I'm boring"* is the
> classic embarrassing A2 error — handle it directly, not delicately.
> **L02's durable correction is *good → well*.** ❌ *"I speak English good"* is always understood, so it can
> survive for years without correction. Also make *hardly* explicit: it means almost not at all and is not
> the adverb of *hard*.
> **L03's new content is the temporary continuous.** Stage 0 already taught *I'm eating now*. The extension
> is *This week I'm working from home*. Anchor tense choice to visible time markers; it is more usable under
> pressure than an abstract tense explanation.
> **L04 is chunk-first.** Phone calls and small talk use stable phrases. Teach their social shape and fluent
> delivery, not their grammar. The key small-talk behaviour is **answer + ask back**.
> **L05 is an interaction assessment.** Perfect sentences without reaction are parallel monologues. Give
> each partner one unpredictable detail, allow retrieval time, and score asking back, reacting, and following
> up as seriously as tense accuracy.
