# "Yesterday I Worked" — Past Simple, Regular Verbs

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 2 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Unit 1 (*was/were*)

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** form the regular past with *-ed* for all persons · say 3–4 things they did yesterday · use *ago*.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Put **-ed** on a regular verb to talk about the past: *work → worked*.
- Use the **same form for everyone**: *I worked · she worked · we worked*.
- Say **3–4 true things** you did yesterday, and use *ago*. 🎙️

> في الوحدة اللي فاتت اتعلمت تقول إنت **كنت** فين. دلوقتي هتقول إنت **عملت** إيه.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
*was* and *were* only describe states. This lesson gives you **actions** — and actions are what stories are
made of.

One ending on the end of a verb, and any regular verb moves into the past. You do not memorise a new form
for each verb, and nothing changes for *he* or *she*.

> قاعدة واحدة بتفتحلك آلاف الأفعال، ودي أسهل قاعدة في الإنجليزي كله فاستغلها.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Someone describes an ordinary evening (📹 in the portal).

> Yesterday I **worked** until six. Then I **cooked** dinner and **watched** TV with my family.
> My brother **called** me at ten. I **finished** the day early — I **wanted** to sleep.

**Notice:** every action verb ends in **-ed**. Six verbs, one ending.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| I worked until six. | اشتغلت لحد ستة |
| Then I cooked dinner. | بعد كده عملت العشا |
| He called me. | كلمني |
| I finished early. | خلصت بدري |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners count the *-ed* verbs. Do not correct pronunciation of the
> endings yet — noticing the pattern is today's win.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The rule is one line: regular verb + *-ed*.**

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| work | work**ed** |
| watch | watch**ed** |
| cook | cook**ed** |
| call | call**ed** |
| finish | finish**ed** |

**And the form is the same for every person.** Say all five aloud and hear that nothing changes:

> *I worked · you worked · he worked · we worked · they worked*

Unlike the present tense, there is no extra **-s** for *he* or *she*. This is the simplest tense in English.

> مفيش تغيير حسب الفاعل زي المضارع، وده بيوفر عليك مجهود كبير.

**Compare with the present:**

- *I **work** every day.* → habit, now
- *I **worked** yesterday.* → finished, in the past

> الفكرة اللي لازم تستقر في دماغك إن الـ **-ed** دي علامة وقت، مش مجرد حرفين في آخر الكلمة. لما
> بتقولها، إنت بتقول للسامع إن الحدث خلص وانتهى. وعشان كده لو نسيتها، الجملة بتتحول من حاجة حصلت
> امبارح لحاجة بتعملها كل يوم، والمعنى بيتغير تمامًا. ودي أهم نقطة في الدرس، لأنها بتتكرر مع كل فعل
> هتتعلمه بعد كده.

> ⚠️ **الغلطة المتوقعة النهاردة:** إنك تنسى الـ **-ed**. ساعتها السامع بيفهمها عادة، مش امبارح.

**Using *ago* — count backwards from now.** It goes **after** the period of time, never before it:

- ✅ *two days **ago*** · *a week **ago*** · *three years **ago***
- ❌ *ago two days*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Teach the single form first and make them say all five persons aloud — the sameness is
> genuinely reassuring at this level. Then contrast present against past, then *ago* word order.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**A first taste of the three *-ed* sounds.** Say these and listen to how the endings differ:

> work**ed** · play**ed** · visit**ed**

They are **not** the same sound. One is a /t/, one is a /d/, and one adds a whole syllable.

**Today, one rule only: do not add an extra vowel.** *worked* is **one** syllable — not "work-ed".

> النقطة الوحيدة النهاردة: ماتزودش حركة في آخر الفعل. الكلمة تفضل مقطع واحد.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"Yesterday I worked and cooked."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Deliberately shallow — the full three-way sort is L02. Today just kill the inserted vowel,
> which is the habit that makes *-ed* verbs hard to understand.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — make it past.**
1. I ______ (work) on Saturday.
2. She ______ (cook) a big meal.
3. They ______ (watch) the match.
4. We ______ (finish) at nine.

**Exercise 2 — present or past?**
1. *I ______ English every morning.* (study — habit)
2. *I ______ English for two hours yesterday.* (study — past)

**Exercise 3 — put *ago* in the right place.**
1. I visited Cairo (three years) → __________________________
2. She called me (ten minutes) → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — true for you.** Three things you did yesterday.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** worked · cooked · watched · finished. **Ex2:** study · studied.
> **Ex3:** three years ago · ten minutes ago. **Ex4:** check the ending is present in writing — that is
> the target, not the sound.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Regular verb + **-ed**, and the form is **the same for every person**.
- Do not drop the ending: *worked*, not *work*.
- **ago** comes **after** the time: *two days ago*.
- Keep it one syllable — no extra vowel.
- *(The three -ed sounds are next lesson.)*
