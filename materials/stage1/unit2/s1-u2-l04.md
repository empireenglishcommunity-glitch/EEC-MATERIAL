# Sequencing a Day: *first · then · after that · later*

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 2 · **Lesson 4**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L03
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit2-past-regular-verbs.md` · **S1-U2-L04***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 5 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** order past actions with sequence words · narrate a 4–6 sentence sequence · put heard events in order.
> **This is the scaffold for storytelling in Unit 3.** Chaining actions is the skill; the sequence words are just the joints. Get them comfortable chaining now and U3 becomes easy.

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Put past actions **in order**: *first · then · after that · later · finally*.
- Narrate your day in **4–6 connected sentences**.
- Follow someone else's sequence and put the events in order. 🎙️

> عندك الأفعال، وعندك النطق والإملاء. ناقص إنك تربطهم في حكاية مترتبة.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
> الفرق بين حد بيقول جمل متفرقة وحد بيحكي فعلاً هو الترتيب.

Say *"First I worked, then I cooked"* and your listener walks through the time with you, step by step, at no
effort. Drop the linking words and they have to reassemble the order themselves — which makes you harder to
follow even when every single word is correct.

Sequence words are cheap to learn and they instantly make you sound organised.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن
From L02 and L03: say four regular past verbs, with the right ending and the right spelling.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Fast round. Correct endings in passing — the reps are the point.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Listen to someone describe yesterday, then **put the events in order** (📹 in the portal).

> **First** I **cleaned** the kitchen. **Then** I **studied** for an hour.
> **After that** I **called** my brother. **Later** I **watched** a film.
> **Finally** I **finished** my report and **stopped** for the night.

Events: *studied · cleaned · watched · called · finished* — what was the real order?

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| First… | الأول… |
| Then… | بعدين… |
| After that… | بعد كده… |
| Later… | بعد شوية… |
| Finally… | وفي الآخر… |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play once; learners number the events. Play again to confirm. The ordering task forces them
> to actually process the sequence words rather than skim past them.

---

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Where the sequence words go: at the front of the sentence, with a comma.**

> **First**, I cleaned the kitchen. **Then**, I studied.

In speech the comma is a **small pause**, and that pause is what makes you easy to follow.

| Word | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **First** | the opening action |
| **Then · After that · Next** | each following action |
| **Later** | something further away in time |
| **Finally** | the last action |

> ⚠️ **خلي بالك:** *then* بتربط خطوة بخطوة، لكن *finally* بتتقال **مرة واحدة بس** في آخر الحكاية. لو
> قلتها في النص السامع هيفتكر إنك خلصت، وبعدين يتلخبط لما تكمل.

> استخدم **First** مرة واحدة في الأول، وبعدها كرر **then** و**after that** زي ما تحب — مش لازم تنوّع
> في كل جملة، والتكرار هنا مش عيب.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Keep it brief — the words are transparent. The two things worth saying are the front
> position with a pause, and that *finally* is single-use.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق
**Rhythm: the beat lands on the verb, not on the sequence word.**

> First I **CLEANED** the kitchen, then I **STUDIED**.

Give a **small pause** after the sequence word, then hit the verb. Sequence words are signposts — say them
lightly and move on.

> النبرة على الفعل، والوقفة الصغيرة بعد كلمة الترتيب.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"First I worked, then I cooked, and after that I rested."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Model a version with the stress wrongly on *then* — it sounds robotic, and hearing that
> makes the correct rhythm obvious.

---

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — put the story in order.** Number 1–5.
- ______ Then I studied for an hour.
- ______ Finally, I watched a film.
- ______ First, I cleaned my room.
- ______ Later, I cooked dinner.
- ______ After that, I called my sister.

**Exercise 2 — join these into one sequence.** Add sequence words.
> *I woke up. I walked to work. I finished at five. I visited my aunt.*

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
> *First I worked. Finally I cooked. Then I studied. Finally I slept.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** 2 · 5 · 1 · 4 · 3. **Ex2:** accept any correct ordering with at least three
> sequence words. **Ex3:** *finally* appears twice — only the last action keeps it.

> pressure of longer speech. Endings often slip once sentences get longer — note who, for L05.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **First · then · after that · next · later · finally.**
- Front of the sentence, then a **small pause**.
- **finally** only once, at the end.
- The beat goes on the **verb**.
- *(Sequencing carries straight into Unit 3 storytelling and Unit 8.)*
