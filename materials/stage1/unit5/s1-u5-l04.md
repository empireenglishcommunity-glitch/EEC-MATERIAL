# *as…as* · *too* · *enough* — Degree & Preference

> [!TEACHER]

## 🎯 Your Conquest — هدفك
By the end, **you'll be able to:**
- Say two things are the same: *as expensive as*.
- Say one falls short: *not as good as*.
- Use *too* and *enough* — and give a **preference with a reason**. 🎙️

> لحد دلوقتي كنت بتقول إيه أكبر وإيه الأحسن. النهاردة بتقول إيه **زي** إيه، وإيه **زيادة**، وإيه **كفاية**.

---

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
This is the language of real decisions. *It's too expensive. The room isn't big enough. This one isn't as
good as that one, so I prefer the other.*

> و**too** و**enough** مش بيوصفوا الحاجة بس — بيقولوا إنها مناسبة أو لأ.

*too expensive* does not mean expensive. It means expensive **beyond what works for you**.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two people looking at flats (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** This flat is **as expensive as** the other one, but it's **not as big as** it.
> **B:** And it's **too far** from the metro. My car isn't **fast enough** for that traffic every morning.
> **A:** True. The kitchen isn't **big enough** either.
> **B:** So **I prefer** the first one, **because** it's closer and it has **enough space**.

**Notice the positions:** *too* comes **before** the adjective. *enough* comes **after** it.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| as expensive as | غالي زي |
| not as big as | مش كبير زي |
| too far | بعيد أوي |
| big enough | كبير كفاية |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners mark where *too* and *enough* sit relative to the adjective.
> The positions are the lesson; let them see the pattern before you state it.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**1) *as … as* — the two things are equal.**

> *This phone is **as good as** that one.* → the same

**2) *not as … as* — the first one is less.**

> *This phone is **not as good as** that one.* → the second is better

> خد بالك: **not as good as** معناها الأولى **أقل**. ودي طريقة مؤدبة تقول إن حاجة أضعف من غير ما تقولها
> بصراحة.

**3) *too* — more than you want. It goes BEFORE the adjective.**

> *It's **too expensive**.* · *It's **too far**.* → a problem

**4) *enough* — as much as you need. It goes AFTER the adjective.**

> *It's **big enough**.* · *It isn't **fast enough**.* → sufficient, or not

| | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **too** | before the adjective | *too small* |
| **enough** | after the adjective | *small enough* |

> ⚠️ **دي الغلطة المتوقعة:** *"enough big"*. الترتيب معكوس — **too** قبل الصفة، و**enough** بعدها.
> اتخيل إيدك بتشاور: **too** على الشمال، **enough** على اليمين. وثبّت الحركة دي في دماغك، لأن القاعدة
> دي مالهاش منطق تحفظه، هي مجرد ترتيب لازم يتعود عليه.

**With a noun, *enough* goes before it:** *enough **money*** · *enough **time***

**5) Give a preference:** *I **prefer** this one **because** it's cheaper.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Four short blocks, each with two oral examples. Use hands for the *too/enough* positions —
> a physical cue outperforms a second explanation here.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the rhythm of *as … as*.**
Both *as* words are **weak** — /əz/. The adjective between them carries everything:

> as **BIG** as · not as **GOOD** as

Say it as one connected run, not three separate words.

**Target 2 — *too* is long, *to* is weak.**

| Word | Sound |
|---|---|
| **too** | long /uː/, and stressed — *too expensive* |
| **to** | weak /tə/, unstressed — *going to work* |

> الفرق بينهم مهم: **too** طويلة وعليها نبرة، و**to** خفيفة ومابتتنطقش كاملة.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"It's not as big as I wanted, and it's too far."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The weak *as* is what makes the structure sound native. The *too/to* contrast prevents a real
> ambiguity.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — *too* or *enough*?**
1. I can't buy it. It's ______ expensive.
2. The room is small. It isn't big ______.
3. I can't drink this — it's ______ hot.
4. Do we have ______ time?

**Exercise 2 — rewrite with *not as … as*.**
1. *Tanta is smaller than Cairo.* → Tanta ______________________ Cairo.
2. *This film is worse than that one.* → This film ______________________ that one.

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *The bag isn't enough big.* → __________________________
2. *This is too much expensive.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — state a preference.** Two options, your choice, and a reason with *because*.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** too · enough · too · enough. **Ex2:** *isn't as big as* · *isn't as good as*.
> **Ex3:** *big enough* · *too expensive*. **Ex4:** insist on the *because* clause.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- **as … as** = equal · **not as … as** = less.
- **too** goes **before** the adjective · **enough** goes **after** it.
- With a noun: **enough money**, **enough time**.
- **I prefer … because …** is the sentence that wins arguments politely.
- Both *as* words are weak; **too** is long, **to** is not.
