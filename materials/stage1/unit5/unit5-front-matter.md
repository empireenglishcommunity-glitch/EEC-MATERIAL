## The Campaign — حملة الوحدة
> **Your mission:** by the end of Unit 5 you'll **compare and choose** — say what's bigger, better and
> the best, say what falls short, and give a reasoned preference.
> مهمتك في الوحدة دي: تقارن، وتقول رأيك، وتقول ليه.

**Why this matters more than it looks:** the past and future units gave you *facts*. This unit gives you
**opinion**. Once you can say *"I prefer this because it's cheaper and not as far"*, you can take part in
real decisions instead of only reporting them.

> ودي أول وحدة بتخليك تتكلم عن رأيك، مش بس عن اللي حصل.

## The Lessons — دروس الوحدة

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| **L01** | Comparatives (-er) | *bigger than* · the spelling rules · **than, not from** |
| **L02** | Longer & irregular | *more expensive* · *better · worse* · the **syllable test** |
| **L03** | Superlatives | *the biggest · the most beautiful* · **the** is obligatory |
| **L04** | *as…as · too · enough* | equality and degree · **too** before, **enough** after |
| **L05** | Compare two cities (milestone) 🎙️ | 6–8 sentences + a justified preference + **record it** |

**Grammar spine:** comparatives · superlatives · irregulars · *(not) as…as · too · enough*.
**Accent Lab:** /ð/ in *than* · word stress in long adjectives · the *-est* syllable · weak *as … as* ·
*too* against *to*.
**Mindset:** the only fair comparison is you-today against you-on-Day-1.
**Arabic support:** ~35% at L01, fading to ~25% by L05.

## The Prize — جايزة الوحدة
- Your **Unit-5 comparison** — the first recording where you argue a position.
- The habit of giving a **reason**, which raises every score you will ever get.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Unit overview:** 5 lessons (L05 ~35 min, the rest ~30) plus this wrapper.
> **Three predictable errors, each with a cause worth naming:**
> **1. *than* → *from*** (L01). Direct transfer from *أكبر من*. Block it actively from the first sentence;
> a warning alone will not do it.
> **2. The double comparative** — *more cheaper*, *more better* (L02). Over-application of the new form.
> Teach the **syllable test** rather than a list of "long adjectives", so they have a rule they can apply.
> **3. The missing *the*** before a superlative (L03). Arabic needs no article in *أحسن مطعم*, so the
> omission is structural, not careless. Accept no superlative without *the*, every single time.
> **The word-order split in L04 is the one thing to drill physically:** *too* **before** the adjective,
> *enough* **after** it. Hands showing left and right beats explaining it twice.
> **In L05, weight the reasoning above the forms.** A learner who justifies a preference well is performing
> at A2 even with a wrong comparative inside it.
