# Superlatives: "The Biggest / The Most Beautiful"

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 5 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L02
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit5-making-comparisons.md` · **S1-U5-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 4 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** form superlatives · always use *the* · say *the best* and *the worst*.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Superlatives are how people share what they love and recommend things. *The best restaurant in Cairo. The
worst traffic of the week. The most beautiful place I've seen.*

The difference to feel: a comparative needs **two** things, while a superlative picks **one** out of a whole
group.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Someone recommending places (📹 in the portal).

> Khan el-Khalili is **the oldest** market in Cairo, and for me it's **the most interesting** place in the
> city. The food there is **the best** I've tried. **The worst** thing is the traffic — it's **the busiest**
> area at the weekend. But it's still **the most beautiful** old part of Cairo.

**Notice:** every single one begins with **the**. Not one exception.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| the oldest market | أقدم سوق |
| the most interesting | الأكثر تشويقًا |
| the best | الأحسن |
| the worst | الأسوأ |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners count how many superlatives appear and confirm that *the*
> precedes all of them. Make the pattern feel absolute.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**The same syllable test as last lesson, plus an obligatory *the*.**

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| big | bigger | **the biggest** |
| cheap | cheaper | **the cheapest** |
| expensive | more expensive | **the most expensive** |
| beautiful | more beautiful | **the most beautiful** |

> يعني القاعدة اللي اتعلمتها امبارح هي نفسها: مقطع واحد ياخد **-est**، وتلاتة أو أكتر ياخدوا
> **the most**.

**The two irregulars again:**

| | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good | better | **the best** |
| bad | worse | **the worst** |

> ⚠️ **دي الحاجة الجديدة الحقيقية:** كلمة **the** إجبارية قبل الـ superlative، مفيش استثناء. في العربي
> بنقول "أحسن مطعم" من غير أداة، فبنميل ننسى الـ **the** في الإنجليزي. لازم تتقال دايمًا.

**Spelling follows the same three rules:** *big → biggest · easy → easiest · new → newest*

**Useful phrases that pair with it:**

- *in the world · in Cairo · in my family · of the year*
- *the best food **I've ever** tried*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Frame as transfer, then spend the time on *the*. Drill by prompting an adjective and requiring
> the full phrase including *the*, every time; accept nothing without it.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the *-est* ending is /ɪst/.**
It is a full extra syllable, and it must be audible:

> big-g**est** · cheap**est** · old**est** — two syllables each

Learners often clip it to *"bigges"*, which loses the ending entirely.

**Target 2 — *the* before a vowel sound becomes /ði/.**

> **the** biggest /ðə/ · **the** oldest /ði/ · **the** easiest /ði/

**Target 3 — in *the most*, the stress lands on the adjective**, not on *most*:

> the most ex**PEN**sive

> الـ **-est** مقطع كامل، فلازم تسمعه. ولاحظ إن **the** بتتغير لـ /ði/ قبل حرف علة.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"It's the oldest and the most beautiful part of the city."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** The clipped *-est* is the priority; the /ði/ variation is a bonus that makes them sound
> noticeably more fluent.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — make the superlative.**
1. big → ______ 2. expensive → ______ 3. good → ______ 4. easy → ______ 5. bad → ______

**Exercise 2 — complete the sentence.**
1. Cairo is ______ city in Egypt. (big)
2. This is ______ restaurant in the area. (good)
3. Monday is ______ day of the week. (bad)

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *It is biggest city in Egypt.* → __________________________
2. *This is the most cheap hotel.* → __________________________
3. *She is the goodest student.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — true for you.** Name the best thing and the worst thing about your city.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** the biggest · the most expensive · the best · the easiest · the worst.
> **Ex2:** the biggest · the best · the worst. **Ex3:** *the biggest* — the missing article is the target
> error · *the cheapest* · *the best*.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- One syllable → **the …-est** · three or more → **the most …**
- **the** is obligatory. Every time, no exceptions.
- **the best · the worst.**
- The **-est** is a full syllable — say it.
- *the* becomes /ði/ before a vowel: *the oldest*.
