# Comparatives: "Cairo Is Bigger Than…"

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 5 · **Lesson 1**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: Unit 4
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit5-making-comparisons.md` · **S1-U5-L01***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 6 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** form *-er* comparatives · use *than* · make 3 correct comparisons.
> **The spelling rules are the same three from Unit 2** — doubling, *y→i*, and just-add. Say that; it turns new material into a second use of something already learned.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
Comparing is how people explain choices. *Which phone is better? Which city is cheaper? Why did you pick
that one?* You cannot justify a decision in English without it.

> والغلطة اللي بتحصل لكل المتعلمين العرب هنا إننا بنقول *"أكبر **من**"*، فبنترجمها لـ *"bigger **from**"*.

English uses a completely different word — **than** — and it has no connection to *from*. Watch for this from
your very first sentence, because it sets hard and fast.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Two people comparing where they live (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Cairo is much **bigger than** Alexandria, but Alexandria is **cooler** in summer.
> **B:** True. And it's **cheaper than** Cairo — the flats are **smaller**, though.
> **A:** For me Cairo is **busier**, so Alexandria feels **quieter** and **calmer**.

**Notice:** every comparison word ends in **-er**, and when two things are named, **than** joins them.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| bigger than | أكبر من |
| cheaper than | أرخص من |
| cooler | أبرد |
| quieter | أهدى |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second time learners underline every *-er* word and circle each *than*. Note that
> *than* only appears when the second thing is mentioned.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**Short adjective + *-er* + *than*.**

| Adjective | Comparative |
|---|---|
| big | **bigger** |
| cheap | **cheaper** |
| old | **older** |
| fast | **faster** |

> والكلمة اللي بتربط المقارنة هي **than**، ومعناها "من" في المقارنة بس. ماتستخدمش **from** أبدًا هنا،
> وأسهل طريقة تفتكرها إنك تسمعها في جملة كاملة كتير لحد ما ودنك تستغرب لو قلت غيرها.

**The spelling rules are the three you already know from Unit 2:**

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| C-V-C → double the last letter | bi**g** → bi**gg**er · ho**t** → ho**tt**er |
| consonant + **y** → **i** | eas**y** → eas**ier** · bus**y** → bus**ier** |
| everything else | cheap → cheaper |

> نفس قواعد **-ed** بالظبط. يعني إنت اتعلمتها مرة، وبتستخدمها تاني هنا.

**You can drop *than*** when the second thing is already obvious:

- *Alexandria is **cheaper**.* → cheaper than Cairo, which we just mentioned

**Add *much* to make it stronger:** *Cairo is **much** bigger than Tanta.*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Teach the form, then link the spelling explicitly back to Unit 2. Drill *than* five times in
> isolation — the *from* substitution needs active blocking, not just a warning.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| bigger than | أكبر من | *Cairo is bigger than Tanta.* |
| cheaper / more expensive | أرخص / أغلى | *It's cheaper than that.* |
| older / newer | أقدم / أحدث | *My phone is older.* |
| faster / slower | أسرع / أبطأ | *The metro is faster.* |
| quieter / busier | أهدى / أزحم | *This street is quieter.* |
| much | بكتير | *much bigger* |
| than | من (في المقارنة) | *bigger than* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Target 1 — the /ð/ in *than*.**
The tongue tip goes lightly **between the teeth** and the sound is **voiced** — your throat vibrates.

- ✅ *than* — tongue between teeth
- ❌ *dan* — tongue behind the teeth *(the common substitution)*

Same sound as in *this*, *that*, *the*.

**Target 2 — *than* is weak and unstressed.**
It shrinks to /ðən/ and disappears into the sentence. The **adjective** takes the stress:

> Cairo is **BIG**ger than Tanta.

> صوت **/ð/** ده موجود كمان في *this* و*that*، فلو ظبطته هنا بتكسب في كلمات كتير.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"This one is cheaper than that one."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** /ð/ first with the mirror, then the weak form. Learners who stress *than* sound robotic; it
> should almost vanish.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — make the comparative.**
1. cheap → ______ 2. big → ______ 3. easy → ______ 4. hot → ______ 5. new → ______

**Exercise 2 — complete with a comparative + *than*.**
1. A car is ______ a bicycle. (fast)
2. Winter is ______ summer. (cold)
3. My flat is ______ yours. (small)

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *My brother is taller from me.* → __________________________
2. *This bag is more cheap than that one.* → __________________________
3. *Today is more hot than yesterday.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — true for you.** Compare two cities you know.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** **Ex1:** cheaper · bigger · easier · hotter · newer.
> **Ex2:** faster than · colder than · smaller than.
> **Ex3:** *taller **than** me* · *cheaper* · *hotter* — the last two are the "more + short adjective"
> error, which L02 then contrasts properly.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Short adjective + **-er** + **than**.
- **than**, never *from*.
- Spelling: the same three rules as *-ed* — doubling, *y→i*, just add.
- **much** makes it stronger: *much bigger*.
- *than* is weak; the adjective takes the stress.
