## Welcome back, Citizen — أهلاً برجوعك

You are not starting again. You arrive with A1: you can introduce yourself, describe your routine, handle
simple needs, and keep a short exchange alive. Stage 1 opens the timeline and gives that foundation range.

> إنت مش راجع للصفر. الأساس موجود؛ المرحلة دي بتخليك تتحرك للماضي والمستقبل، وتقارن، وتنصح، وتتعامل مع
> مواقف أطول باستقلال أكبر.

**By the end of Stage 1, you will be able to tell a simple story, make and discuss plans, compare choices,
give advice, and handle ordinary travel, food, health, phone, and small-talk situations in English.**

Your final evidence will not be a certificate alone. You will complete an extended A2 exchange and record a
standard milestone sample that can sit beside your Day-1 and Stage-0 recordings.

> الفرق المرة دي مش بس عدد كلمات أكبر. الفرق إنك هتربط الكلام، وتتصرف لما الموقف يتغير، وتصلّح نفسك وتكمل.

The rank path is deliberate: Recruit (A1) → **Citizen (A2)** → Legionary (B1) → **Confident** (B2, the
Coronation) → Sovereign (C1).

A Citizen does more than survive a conversation. You begin to participate in it — with a past, a plan, an
opinion, a reason, and a response to the other person.

> الرُتبة معناها إنك بقيت تستخدم اللغة في حياتك، مش بس تنفّذ تمرين عنها.

## The roadmap — خريطة المرحلة

**10 units · 50 lessons · about 10–14 weeks at roughly 7 guided hours a week.**

| Unit | Theme | What you take from it |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | The Past (1) — *Was & Were* | locate people, places, states, and experiences in the past |
| **2** | The Past (2) — Regular Verbs | say what happened and produce the three *-ed* endings |
| **3** | The Past (3) — Irregulars & Questions | ask, negate, and tell a connected simple story |
| **4** | Future Plans | distinguish plans from predictions, offers, and instant decisions |
| **5** | Making Comparisons | compare options, choose the best, and explain degree |
| **6** | Food, Quantity & Restaurants | manage amounts, containers, questions, and a polite meal order |
| **7** | Health & Advice | describe symptoms, advise warmly, and separate advice from necessity |
| **8** | Travel & Transport | get around, request help, buy a ticket, and narrate a trip |
| **9** | People, Feelings & Everyday Situations | describe people, say how, contrast usual with temporary, and sustain small talk |
| **10** | Putting It Together | retrieve the stage, complete the A2 exchange, and record your milestone |

**The grammar spine:** past *was/were* → regular and irregular past → *did/didn't* → *going to/will* →
comparatives and superlatives → count/uncount quantity → *should/have to/must/can't* → adverbs and the
simple/continuous contrast.

This stage is built around **time**. The first three units unlock the past because telling a story is the
largest A2 jump. The future follows, then the tools to compare, quantify, advise, and act in everyday domains.
Every unit retrieves language from earlier ones before adding its own layer.

**Vocabulary target:** roughly **1,000–1,500 words cumulative**, taught in context and collected in the
generated glossary at the back of this book.

**Accent Lab priorities:**
- the three past *-ed* endings: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/
- /θ/ and /ð/ consolidation
- consonant clusters without an inserted vowel
- stress in longer words
- sentence rhythm, linking, and a light introduction to weak forms
- personal weak sounds identified from recordings, not guessed from a generic list

> النطق هنا هدفه الوضوح. بنقارن تسجيلك بتسجيلك، ونشتغل على الصوت اللي فعلاً بيأثر عليك.

## The Arabic fade — الدعم العربي بيخف

Stage 1 begins with moderate Arabic support, around 40%, and fades across the course. This is not removal of
help for its own sake. Familiar lesson structure, examples, tables, and repeated English instructions gradually
take over work that translation once did.

> لو الشرح بقى إنجليزي أكتر، ده مش لأن الدعم اختفى. ده لأنك بقيت قادر تستقبل جزء أكبر من اللغة باللغة نفسها.

When a precise Arabic contrast prevents a durable error, the lesson uses it. When English is already clear,
the lesson lets English carry the meaning. The fade is planned, not random.

## How to use this book — إزاي تستخدم الكتاب

Every teaching lesson keeps the same route, while review, milestone, and assessment lessons adapt the middle
to the task.

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **Your Conquest** | name the ability you are building |
| 👑 **Why this matters** | connect the language to a real reason to use it |
| 🔁 **Warm-up** | retrieve earlier language before adding more |
| 👂 **Watch & Listen** | meet the language inside a complete situation |
| 🧩 **Decode it** | notice the form, meaning, and high-value contrast |
| 🗡️ **Your Arsenal** | collect the words and functional phrases |
| 🎙️ **Accent Lab** | perceive, cue, produce, record, and compare |
| 🏋️ **Train** | practise accurately before the speaking load rises |
| 🎤 **Your Turn** | connect the lesson into meaningful speech |
| 📜 **Your Orders** | carry one small action into real life |
| 🧠 **Remember** | compress the lesson into what must survive |
| ✅ **Self-check** | verify and correct without waiting for a teacher |

**Four rules make Stage 1 work:**

1. **Retrieve before rereading.** The effort to remember is part of the learning.
2. **Connect sentences.** A2 grows through *because, but, so, then,* and follow-up questions, not longer lists.
3. **Record before you feel ready.** Listening back turns a vague impression into specific evidence.
4. **Never miss twice.** One missed day is human; returning the next day protects the habit.

> ⭐ **القاعدة الذهبية:** الاستمرار أهم من الشدّة. ارجع بسرعة، حتى لو رجعت بعشر دقايق بس.

## What gets recorded — إيه اللي بيتسجل

Each unit ends in a speaking task. Those recordings have three jobs:

- give the coach evidence for focused feedback
- let you compare current speech with your own earlier speech
- build the proof timeline for milestone points

Recording for learning is not permission to publish. Storage, comparison, and any public use follow the
consent tier in the before/after protocol. You can participate in the course without granting marketing use.

> التسجيل دليل ليك وأداة تعليم. استخدامه خارج ده محتاج موافقة واضحة ومنفصلة.

---

## How you are assessed — التقييم

- **Entry:** A1 evidence through the Stage-0 exit or placement process.
- **End of every unit:** a short quiz plus a speaking task. These are formative checks; they direct feedback
  and the review queue.
- **Unit 10 mock:** the exact shape of the final exchange, completed without a pass/fail label.
- **Stage-1 summative:** a mixed quiz and an extended exchange — narration, role-play, and Q&A — scored on
  Fluency, Accuracy, Pronunciation, Range, and Interaction at the committed A2 threshold.
- **A2 milestone sample:** a separate standard-prompt recording for fair Day-1 → Stage-0 → A2 comparison.

A green quiz is not proof of speaking ability. A strong speech is not proof that every grammar target is
stable. The determination uses the required evidence together.

> التقييم مش كمين. شكله معروف، ومعاييره ثابتة، والنتيجة بتبدأ بالدليل قبل الاسم.

## An honest promise — وعد صادق

We promise structured practice toward **clear, confident, intelligible communication**. We do not promise a
native accent, effortless fluency in a number of days, or progress without sustained use.

> مانوعدكش بلكنة أصلية ولا بطلاقة سريعة. نوعدك بمسار واضح، تدريب حقيقي، ودليل تقدر تسمعه بنفسك.

**What Stage 1 can honestly deliver:** you can narrate familiar past events, discuss straightforward plans,
compare and choose, talk about amounts, describe symptoms and give advice, manage predictable travel and
phone tasks, and sustain a short everyday conversation. You will still pause, make errors, ask for repetition,
and need support in unfamiliar situations. That is not a failed B1; it is a real A2.

> [!TEACHER]
> **Stage overview for the coach.** 10 units, 50 lessons, ~100 guided hours, normally 10–14 weeks. Entry is
> evidenced A1. Arabic begins around 40% and fades to roughly 10–20% in the final units; do not restore
> translation merely because productive struggle appears.
> **Stage structure:** U1–U3 build the past, U4 future, U5 comparison, U6 quantity/food, U7 health/advice,
> U8 travel, U9 everyday interaction, and U10 retrieval + clinic + mock + summative.
> **Non-negotiables:** every requested recording, the Accent Lab, retrieval before support, and interaction
> evidence. A correct monologue is not a conversation; reward asking back, clarification, repair, and response
> to new information.
> **Pronunciation honesty:** clear and intelligible, never “native.” Use tracker evidence to personalise the
> Unit-10 clinic and compare each learner only with their own earlier sample.
> **Proof protocol:** the extended A2 exchange and standard milestone prompt are separate files. Use the
> assessment suite for determination and the before/after protocol for comparable capture, storage, and
> granular consent. Course participation never implies publication permission.
> **Persistent errors to recycle:** *did/didn't + base*; missing *be* in *going to*; double comparison;
> *much/many*; *should + base*; *don't have to* against *can't*; *good* against *well*; simple against
> temporary continuous; meaning-changing *can/can't* clarity.
> **The predictable dip:** learners often say they are getting worse when the Arabic fades and output grows.
> Show them two recordings, name the increased task difficulty, and compare like with like before changing
> the plan.
