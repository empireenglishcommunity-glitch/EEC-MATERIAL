# Booking & Rules: Buying a Ticket; *You Have To / You Can't*

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 8 · **Lesson 3**
⏱️ ~30 min · 📹 Recorded + live · Prerequisite: L02 · Unit 7 (*have to*)
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit8-travel-and-transport.md` · **S1-U8-L03***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 6 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 7 · Your Turn 3 · Feedback —.
> **Objectives:** buy a ticket (price, times, single/return) · state travel rules with *have to / can't* · handle a short booking exchange.
> **No new grammar.** *Have to* came from Unit 7 and *can't* from Stage 0. This lesson is assembly under pressure, which is a different skill — expect learners who were accurate in Unit 7 to drop pieces inside a live booking.
> **The three-way meaning check is worth protecting:** *have to* = necessary · *don't have to* = **not** necessary · *can't* = **forbidden**. Unit 7 taught the first two; today adds the third, and *don't have to* against *can't* is the confusion to hunt.
> ***can* against *can't* is the hardest sound in this stage for Arabic speakers** — the final /t/ is often released so weakly that the two become identical, which inverts the meaning of the sentence. Drill the minimal pair honestly; do not accept "close enough".
> **Support:** the booking-phrase card. **Stretch:** handle a complication — the train is sold out, get an alternative.
> **Arabic ~30%.**

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
A booking is a small, closed conversation — perhaps six lines, and they come in the same order every time.
The rules half matters for a different reason: *can't* and *don't have to* look similar and mean opposite
things.

> والخلطة الخطيرة هنا: **can't** معناها ممنوع، و**don't have to** معناها مش لازم. لو قلت للواحد ممنوع
> وإنت قصدك مش لازم، الكلام كله بيتقلب.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
At a ticket window (📹 in the portal).

> **A:** Hello. **A return ticket** to Alexandria, please.
> **B:** Sure. **What time** do you want to travel?
> **A:** The morning, if possible. **How much is it**?
> **B:** Two hundred and forty. The next one leaves at nine forty, from **platform** three.
> **A:** Perfect. Do I **have to** book a seat?
> **B:** Yes, you **have to** — and you **can't** change the ticket after you buy it.
> **A:** Understood. Thank you.

**Notice the order:** what you want → when → how much → which platform → the rules. It is the same every
time, which is exactly why you can prepare for it.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| A return ticket to… | تذكرة ذهاب وعودة لـ… |
| What time does it leave? | بتقوم الساعة كام؟ |
| How much is it? | بكام؟ |
| You have to book. | لازم تحجز |
| You can't change it. | ماينفعش تغيرها |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice; on the second pass learners write the five steps in order. The skeleton is the
> takeaway, not the vocabulary.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**1) Buying the ticket — four phrases cover it.**

| You need | You say |
|---|---|
| the ticket | *A **single** / a **return** ticket to Cairo, please.* |
| the time | *What time does it **leave** / **arrive**?* |
| the price | *How much is it?* |
| the place | *Which **platform** / **gate**?* |

**single** = one direction. **return** = there and back.

> وأربع جمل بس هما كل اللي محتاجه على الشباك، والباقي بتسمعه مش بتقوله.

**2) The rules — three meanings, and they are not interchangeable.**

| English | Meaning | |
|---|---|---|
| *You **have to** check in.* | necessary — required | 🔒 |
| *You **don't have to** book.* | **not** necessary — your choice | 🆓 |
| *You **can't** smoke here.* | **forbidden** | ⛔ |

> ⚠️ **أهم نقطة في الدرس:** **can't** ≠ **don't have to**. الأولى ممنوع، والتانية مش ضروري. الاتنين شكلهم
> قريب والمعنى عكس بعضه.

**3) Form, in one line:** *have to* + simple verb · *has to* for *he/she/it* · *can't* + simple verb, and
*can't* never changes for anybody.

> *He **has to** show his passport.* · *She **can't** take that bag on the plane.*

**Asking about a rule:**

> ***Do I have to*** *book a seat?* · ***Can I*** *take this bag?*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Build the three-row rules table on the board and drill by naming a situation for learners to
> classify: *required*, *your choice*, or *forbidden*. Four examples of *can't* against *don't have to*.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**This is the most important pronunciation minute in Stage 1.** *can* and *can't* are opposites, and if a
listener cannot tell them apart, your sentence means the reverse of what you intended.

| | Sound | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | /kən/ in a sentence | weak, almost swallowed |
| **can't** | /kænt/ | fuller vowel **and** a real /t/ |

Two things change together: *can't* has a **clearer vowel** and a **final /t/** you must actually release.

**Minimal pair drill** — say each pair until a partner can tell them apart with eyes closed:

> *I **can** go.* — *I **can't** go.*
> *You **can** park here.* — *You **can't** park here.*

> ودي مش تفصيلة صغيرة: لو الـ**t** مش طالعة، اللي قصادك بيسمع العكس تمامًا. اقفل الكلمة بالتاء بوضوح.

**Also keep numbers clean** in a booking — times and prices are the two things nobody can guess from context.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"I can travel on Monday, but I can't travel on Tuesday."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Eyes-closed pair testing is the only honest check. If a partner cannot tell, the learner has
> not done it, however confident they feel.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — *have to*, *don't have to*, or *can't*?**
1. You ______ show your passport at the airport. (required)
2. You ______ pay for children under six. (not necessary)
3. You ______ use your phone during take-off. (forbidden)
4. He ______ check in online. (required)

**Exercise 2 — complete the booking.**
1. ______ ticket to Aswan, please. (there and back)
2. What time ______ it leave?
3. ______ is it?
4. Which ______ ?

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *You have to check in online, isn't it?* → __________________________
2. *She have to book a seat.* → __________________________
3. *You don't have to smoke here.* (you mean: forbidden) → __________________________
4. *What time it leaves?* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — role-play.** Buy a ticket. Your partner tells you it is sold out. Ask for an alternative.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** have to · don't have to · can't · has to. **Ex2:** A return · does · How much · platform.
> **Ex3:** *…online, **don't you**?* · *She **has to***… · *You **can't** smoke here.* · *What time **does**
> it leave?* Ex4 is the stretch — do not let them settle for "OK, thank you".

- *has to* for *he/she*; *can't* never changes.
- **can** /kən/ against **can't** /kænt/ — release that /t/ or you say the opposite.
