# Unit Speaking Task: "Tell Me About a Trip You Took" (+ Review)

**Empire English** · Stage 1 — **Citizen** (A2) · Unit 8 · **Lesson 5** — *UNIT MILESTONE*
⏱️ ~35 min · 📹 Recorded + live (assessment-style) · Prerequisite: L01–L04
*Built from: `curriculum/stage1/unit8-travel-and-transport.md` · **S1-U8-L05***

> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~35 min):** Warm-up 5 · Model 4 · Checklist 4 · Accent clinic 4 · Rehearse 7 · Record 5 · Feedback 2 · Assign —.
> **Objectives:** narrate a trip in 7–9 sentences mixing past narration, travel language, and a request or a rule · answer a follow-up and ask one · record the Unit-8 milestone.
> **This is a strong candidate for a mid-A2 proof clip.** It is personal, integrative, and audibly harder than anything from Stage 0 — put it beside their Unit-0 recording and the distance is obvious to the learner, not just to you.
> **The integration is the difficulty, not the parts.** Each learner can do past narration, requests, and rules separately. Under a 7–9 sentence load, two of the three will slip. Correct the assembly.
> **Give them a real follow-up question live** — not a scripted one. Handling an unplanned question is the A2 interaction threshold, and it is the part they cannot rehearse.
> **Support:** 7 sentences with the checklist card. **Stretch:** include a request you actually made, a rule of the place, and two follow-up answers.
> **Arabic ~25%** — nearly all English.

## 🔁 Warm-up — سخّن (سلسلة)
Rapid chain across the unit: a transport word → a polite request → a travel rule with *have to* or *can't* →
a past verb.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** One item each, fast, no repeats. This is the retrieval sweep before the load goes up.

---

## 👂 Watch & Listen — النموذج
A full trip narration (📹 in the portal).

> **Two years ago** I **went** to Istanbul with two friends. We **flew** from Cairo — the flight **was** only
> two hours. From the airport we **took** the metro, and we **got off at** Sultanahmet. We **stayed** in a
> small hotel near the mosque. **On the first day** we **visited** the old market, and I **bought** a lamp for
> my mother. My bag **was** very heavy, so at the station I **asked** someone — *"**Could you** help me with
> my bag?"* — and he **did**. One thing surprised me: you **can't** take photos inside some of the mosques.
> **There were** signs everywhere. **The best part was** the food. I **want** to go back.

**Notice:** nine sentences, and it contains the whole unit — transport, *get off at*, a **request** in direct
speech, a **rule** with *can't*, past narration throughout, and a feeling at the end.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Play once for the story, once for the checklist. Ask them to find the request and the rule —
> both are folded in naturally rather than announced, which is the standard.

## 🧩 The Checklist — تشيك ليست الحكاية الكاملة
1. ✅ **When and where** — *Two years ago I went to…*
2. ✅ **How you travelled** — *We flew · we took the metro · we got off at…*
3. ✅ **Where you stayed** — *We stayed in…*
4. ✅ **What you did** — two things, in sequence
5. ✅ **A request or a rule** — *"Could you…?"* or *You can't…*
6. ✅ **How it felt** — *The best part was… · It was…*
7. ⭐ **Handle one question** you were not expecting

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Hand out the card. Item 5 is the one they forget, because it is the one that is not
> chronological — it has to be inserted deliberately.

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — عيادة النطق
Drill **your** weakest from this unit:

- ***can* against *can't*** — the final /t/, released properly
- **place names** — *Cairo · Alexandria · Dubai · Doha* — English stress
- **linking** — *get off at* → *ge-TOF-fat* · *could you* → /kʊdʒə/
- **rising intonation** on a request
- **clean *-ed*** at narrative speed

> 🎧 **Record & compare** your weakest one only.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Personalised from your L01–L04 flags. One target each. *can/can't* is the priority if it is
> still unclear — it changes meaning, and nothing else on this list does.

## 🎤 Your Turn — التسجيل (milestone)
Rehearse with a partner against the checklist, swap, then record.

Tell the story of a trip you really took, in **7–9 sentences**. Include how you travelled, where you stayed,
two things you did, **one request or one rule**, and how it felt. Then answer one question about it.

> 🎙️ **Record it and keep it.** 👑

> [!TEACHER]

> you go back? Who paid?"* — so it is genuine interaction.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر (Unit 8 wrap)
- ***How do I get to…?*** — and the answer is **take the …** → **get off at …**
- **by bus** *or* **take the bus** — never crossed. **on foot**, not *by foot*.
- **Could I…?** = permission · **Could you…?** = a request · and the rise ↗️ makes it polite.
- **have to** = required · **don't have to** = not required · **can't** = forbidden.
- Five story slots: **when/where · how · where you stayed · what you did · how it felt.**
- **can** /kən/ against **can't** /kænt/ — release the /t/.

---

## ✅ Self-check — Unit 8 mini-quiz
1. *How do I ___ to the station?* → **get**
2. *I go to work ___ bus.* → **by**
3. *I go ___ foot.* → **on**
4. *___ you help me with my bag?* (a request) → **Could**
5. *___ I sit here?* (permission) → **Can / Could**
6. *You ___ smoke here.* (forbidden) → **can't**
7. *You ___ book — there's plenty of space.* (not necessary) → **don't have to**
8. *I didn't ___ to the museum.* → **go**
9. A ticket there and back is a ___. → **return**
10. *There ___ a lot of tourists.* → **were**
