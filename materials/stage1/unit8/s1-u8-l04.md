> [!TEACHER]
> **At a glance (~30 min):** Warm-up 4 · Input 6 · Decode 5 · Accent Lab 4 · Train 7 · Your Turn 4 · Feedback 1.
> **Objectives:** narrate a past trip with past simple + sequence + travel vocabulary · say where, how, and what you did · react to a peer and ask a past question.
> **This lesson exists for the spaced-review payoff.** Units 1–3 built the past tense in isolation; this is the first time it carries real content the learner cares about. > **Six verbs do most of the work:** *went · took · stayed · visited · saw · was/were*. Four of the six are irregular, which is why the past-tense block came first.
> **The one form error to hunt:** past tense marked twice after *didn't* — ❌ *"I didn't went"*. It reappears under narrative pressure even in learners who had it right in Unit 3.
> **Support:** the trip-skeleton card plus the verb bank. **Stretch:** add a feeling and a superlative — *"the best part was…"*.

## 👑 Why this matters — ليه ده مهم
This is where the past tense stops being an exercise. You built it in Units 1, 2 and 3 — irregular verbs,
*did/didn't*, *was/were* — and today it carries something you actually want to say.

> ولاحظ حاجة: الحكاية دي كنت مستحيل تحكيها قبل ٦ أسابيع.

Telling a story is also the single most useful conversational move there is. It buys you a whole turn.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Both feed straight into the model. Keep it to four minutes — the verbs should be fast by now.

## 👂 Watch & Listen — اسمع وشوف
Someone telling a short trip story (📹 in the portal).

> **Last summer** I **went** to Luxor with my brother. We **took** the night train — it **was** long, about
> ten hours, but it **was** cheap. We **stayed** in a small hotel near the river. **On the first day** we
> **visited** the temple, and **then** we **saw** the tombs. **There were** a lot of tourists, so it **was**
> very busy. We **didn't have** much time, so we **didn't go** to the museum. **The best part was** the
> train at night — I **slept** with the window open. I **loved** it.

**Notice three things:** the time phrase comes **first** (*Last summer*), the sequence words carry you
forward (*On the first day · then*), and the story ends with a **feeling**, not a fact.

| English | بالعربي |
|---|---|
| Last summer I went to… | الصيف اللي فات رحت… |
| We took the night train. | ركبنا قطر الليل |
| We stayed in a hotel. | قعدنا في فندق |
| The best part was… | أحلى حاجة كانت… |

> [!TEACHER]
> **~6 min.** Play twice. Second pass: learners mark the time phrase, the sequence words, and the feeling —
> the three things that turn a list of facts into a story.

## 🧩 Decode it — القاعدة

**A trip story has five slots. Fill them and you have a narration.**

| # | Slot | Language |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **when + where** | *Last summer I **went** to…* |
| 2 | **how + who with** | *I **took** the train with…* |
| 3 | **where you stayed** | *We **stayed** in / at…* |
| 4 | **what you did** | *We **visited**… · we **saw**… · **There were**…* |
| 5 | **how it felt** | *It **was** amazing. · **The best part was**…* |

> والخمس خانات دي شكل أي حكاية، مش رحلة بس.

**Your six workhorse verbs:**

| Present | Past | |
|---|---|---|
| go · take · see | **went · took · saw** | irregular |
| stay · visit | **stayed · visited** | regular *-ed* |
| is/are | **was / were** | irregular |

**Sequence words that hold the story together:** *First · then · after that · in the end*

> ⚠️ **الغلطة اللي بترجع تاني هنا:** بعد **didn't** الفعل بيرجع أساسي. *I didn't **go***، مش *I didn't
> went*. الماضي مرة واحدة بس في الجملة.

**And the time phrase goes at the front:** *Last week · Two years ago · When I was a student…*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~5 min.** Board the five slots and leave them up for the whole lesson — learners build directly onto
> them in Train. Re-drill *didn't* + base verb even though Unit 3 covered it; narration reliably breaks it.

## 🗡️ Your Arsenal — ذخيرتك

| English | بالعربي | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I went to… | رحت… | *I went to Luxor.* |
| I took the train / a taxi | ركبت القطر / تاكسي | *We took the night train.* |
| we stayed in / at… | قعدنا في… | *We stayed in a small hotel.* |
| we visited… | زرنا… | *We visited the temple.* |
| There was / There were… | كان فيه… | *There were a lot of tourists.* |
| a trip / a journey | رحلة | *It was a long journey.* |
| The best part was… | أحلى حاجة كانت… | *The best part was the train.* |
| in the end | في الآخر | *In the end we went home.* |

## 🎙️ Accent Lab — معمل النطق

**Narrative rhythm — the thing that makes a story sound like a story.**

You do not stress every word. You stress the **content** — the place, the transport, the feeling — and let
the small words run underneath:

> *We **TOOK** the **NIGHT TRAIN** to **LUXOR**.*

Say that with all five words equally stressed, then again with only the capitals. The second one sounds like
someone telling you something; the first sounds like reading.

**Keep the endings clean under speed.** When learners speed up, *-ed* is the first thing to disappear:

> *stay**ed*** /steɪd/ · *visit**ed*** /ˈvɪzɪtɪd/ · *walk**ed*** /wɔːkt/

Remember the three *-ed* sounds from Unit 2: /d/, /ɪd/, /t/. *Visited* has the extra syllable; *walked* does
not.

> ولما بتسرع، أول حاجة بتضيع هي آخر الفعل. اسمع نفسك.

> 🎧 **Record & compare:** *"We took the night train and we stayed in a small hotel near the river."*

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min.** Two minutes rhythm, two on *-ed* survival at speed. The equal-stress-versus-content-stress
> contrast is worth doing as a whole group; it lands instantly.

## 🏋️ Train — تدرّب

**Exercise 1 — past forms.**
1. Last year I ______ (go) to Dubai.
2. We ______ (take) a taxi from the airport.
3. There ______ (be) a lot of people at the station.
4. I ______ (not / visit) the museum.
5. We ______ (stay) with my cousin.

**Exercise 2 — put the trip in order.**
> a) *We visited the old city.* b) *Last April I went to Amman.* c) *In the end we flew home.*
> d) *We stayed in a small flat.* e) *I took the bus from the border.*

**Exercise 3 — fix the mistake.**
1. *I didn't went to the museum.* → __________________________
2. *There was a lot of tourists.* → __________________________
3. *We stay in a hotel last week.* → __________________________
4. *I went to Luxor last summer with the train.* → __________________________

**Exercise 4 — build from the skeleton.** Use the five slots to tell a trip in five sentences — one per slot.
Then your partner asks you **one past question** about it.

> [!TEACHER]
> **~7 min.** **Ex1:** went · took · were · didn't visit · stayed. **Ex2:** b, e, d, a, c.
> **Ex3:** *didn't **go*** · *There **were*** · *We **stayed*** · ***by** train*. Ex4 is the rehearsal for
> Your Turn — make sure the partner question actually gets asked and answered.

## 🎤 Your Turn — دورك
Narrate a **real** trip in **five or six sentences**, using all five slots. End with how it felt.

> 🎙️ **Record it.**

> [!TEACHER]
> **~4 min recording + 1 feedback.** **Success check:** a coherent 5–6 sentence narration, correct past
> forms (especially after *didn't*), at least three travel words, and a closing feeling. If they only manage
> four slots, the missing one is almost always #5 — push for it, because it is what makes it a story.

## 🧠 Remember — افتكر
- Five slots: **when/where · how/who · where you stayed · what you did · how it felt.**
- **went · took · saw · stayed · visited · was/were** — six verbs carry the whole story.
- After **didn't**, the verb goes back to base: *didn't **go***.
- **There was** + singular · **There were** + plural.
- Stress the content words; keep *-ed* alive at speed.
